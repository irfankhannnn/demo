# Business Context - Example Tenant

## Company Overview

We are a premium real estate agency specializing in luxury properties in Mumbai and South Delhi.

## Target Market

- High-net-worth individuals (HNI)
- Non-Resident Indians (NRI) looking for investment properties
- Corporate clients seeking office spaces
- Institutional investors

## Value Proposition

- Exclusive property listings not available elsewhere
- Personalized concierge service
- Deep market expertise and local knowledge
- Complete legal and financial support
- Transparent pricing and no hidden charges

## Property Portfolio

### Luxury Residential
- Budget Range: ₹5 Cr - ₹50 Cr+
- Locations: Bandra, Worli, South Delhi, Gurugram
- Focus: Premium 3-4 BHK apartments, villas, penthouses

### Mid-Range Residential
- Budget Range: ₹2 Cr - ₹5 Cr
- Locations: Andheri, Powai, Dwarka, Noida
- Focus: 2-3 BHK apartments, townhouses

### Commercial & Office
- Budget Range: ₹1 Cr - ₹20 Cr
- Locations: CBD areas, business districts
- Focus: Office spaces, retail, co-working spaces

## Pricing Strategy

- Transparent pricing with no hidden charges
- Flexible payment plans for qualified buyers
- Special discounts for bulk purchases
- Referral commissions for agents

1. **Exclusive Access**: Direct relationships with builders and developers

3. **Technology**: Advanced CRM and virtual tour capabilities
