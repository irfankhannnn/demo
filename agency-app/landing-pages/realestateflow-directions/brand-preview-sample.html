🎨 Creative Rules

Left side: Light red tint (#FEE2E2) signals pain. Emojis replace screenshots for universal readability.

Right side: Dark slate gradient signals authority and solution. Logo mark with blue-to-green gradient = brand identity.

Contrast: Warm pain vs cool solution. Stops the scroll.

Text: Max 6 words per line. Readable on mobile.

📐 Layout Rules

Nav: Logo left, minimal links, one CTA right.

Hero: H1 max 8 words. Subhead = one sentence. Two buttons max.

Social proof: One line of trust signal below buttons.

Metrics: 3 numbers max. Readable at a glance.

📝 Copy Notes

Headline: Problem-aware — calls out WhatsApp directly.

Subhead: Positions as "operating system" (not just CRM).

CTAs: "Get Started Free" (low friction) + "Watch Demo" (education).

No jargon: "Deals, payments, clients, documents" — every broker understands.
