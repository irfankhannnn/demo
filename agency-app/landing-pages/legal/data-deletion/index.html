Instagram Data Deletion — RealEstateFlow

RealEstateFlow lets real estate agencies connect their Instagram professional account to read and answer messages and comments, and to see how their posts perform. This page explains how to stop that access and have the Instagram data we store deleted. What we store and why is described in Section 3.8 of our Privacy Policy.

1. If you connected your Instagram account

You can use any of these, in any order.

Remove RealEstateFlow from Instagram

  1. In the Instagram app, open your profile and tap the menu, then Settings and activity.
  2. Open Website permissions, then Apps and websites.
  3. Under Active, choose RealEstateFlow and tap Remove.

Instagram tells us straight away. We stop using the connection, and we can no longer read or send anything for that account. If you also ask Instagram to delete your data, Instagram sends us that request; we delete the Instagram data stored for your account and Instagram shows you a confirmation code you can use to check the request.

Disconnect in RealEstateFlow

An agency admin can open Instagram console → Instagram accounts and choose Disconnect. This stops syncing immediately. Messages and enquiries already in the console stay available to the agency until it asks for them to be deleted.

Email us

Write to info@realestateflow.in with the subject Instagram data deletion and the Instagram username. We will confirm who you are, delete the Instagram data stored for that account within 30 days, and reply when it is done.

2. If you messaged or commented on an agency

If you sent a message to, or commented on, an agency that uses RealEstateFlow, that agency controls your conversation. Ask the agency first. You can also email info@realestateflow.in with your Instagram username and the agency's name, and we will pass the request on and help the agency complete it.

3. What is deleted

  • The Instagram account connection and its encrypted access token
  • Stored messages, conversations and enquiries that came from that Instagram account
  • Stored comments, posts, reels and insights for that account

Records we must keep by law, such as billing and tax records, are kept for the periods set out in our Privacy Policy. They do not contain Instagram messages.
