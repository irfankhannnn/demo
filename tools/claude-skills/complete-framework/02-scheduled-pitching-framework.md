## 🎯 Delivery Tips for Scheduled Pitches

### Before the Pitch:
1. **Research your audience:** Know their portfolio, thesis, investment stage
2. **Customize the deck:** Emphasize what THEY care about (traction for VCs, ROI for angels)
3. **Practice out loud:** Rehearse 5+ times with a timer
4. **Prepare for objections:** Have backup slides for common questions

### During the Pitch:
1. **Own the room:** Stand, don't sit. Use confident body language.
2. **Tell stories:** Numbers are facts, stories are memorable.
3. **Pause for emphasis:** After big numbers or bold claims, pause 2 seconds.
4. **Watch the clock:** If you only have 10 minutes, skip or speed through slides 6-7.
5. **Read the room:** If they're engaged, slow down. If distracted, speed up or ask a question.

### After the Pitch:
1. **Ask for feedback:** "What concerns do you have?" (not "What did you think?")
2. **Handle objections calmly:** Never get defensive. Acknowledge, then reframe.
3. **Always close with next steps:** "When can we schedule a follow-up?"
4. **Send a thank-you + recap:** Within 24 hours, email a summary with attachments.

## 🧪 Testing Your Scheduled Pitch

### The 5-Question Test (Good signs if they ask):
1. "What's your current burn rate?"
2. "Can I talk to one of your beta customers?"
3. "What's your plan for Series A?"
4. "How defensible is your AI moat?"
5. "When do you need a decision by?"

### The Red Flags (Bad signs):
- ❌ They interrupt with basic questions you already answered
- ❌ They spend more time on their phone than listening
- ❌ They don't ask about next steps or follow-up
- ❌ They say "We'll get back to you" without a specific timeline

---

## 📋 Pre-Pitch Checklist

Before any scheduled pitch, ensure:
- [ ] Deck is under 13 slides (12 content + 1 appendix)
- [ ] You can deliver it in 10 minutes (leaving 5 min for Q&A)
- [ ] Every number is accurate and sourced
- [ ] You have a backup plan if demo fails (screenshots/video)
- [ ] You've anticipated the top 5 questions and have answers ready
- [ ] You have a clear, specific ask
- [ ] You've researched the audience and customized content
