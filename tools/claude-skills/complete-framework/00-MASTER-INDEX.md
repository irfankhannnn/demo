## 🔑 Daniel Priestley's Core Frameworks Applied to RealtyFlow

### 1. Social Pitch (30 seconds): NAME-SAME-FAME-PAIN-AIM-GAME

```
NAME: "I'm [Name] from RealtyFlow."
SAME: "You know how agents use Excel and WhatsApp?"
FAME: "We built India's first AI-powered CRM with AI calling agents."
PAIN: "Agents lose 60% of leads due to poor tracking."
AIM:  "We're onboarding 3,000 agents in India and Dubai."
GAME: "We're building the operating system for every real estate transaction in India."
```

### 2. Scheduled Pitch (10-15 min): PROBLEM-SOLUTION-TRACTION-MARKET-MODEL-VISION-ASK
Full 13-slide deck structure in `02-scheduled-pitching-framework.md`

### 3. Sales Pitch (30-45 min): RAPPORT-DISCOVER-DEMONSTRATE-DIFFERENTIATE-CLOSE-FOLLOW-UP
Complete conversation flow in `03-sales-pitching-framework.md`

## 📊 RealtyFlow at a Glance (Key Numbers for Any Pitch)

### The Problem
- **5 lakh+** real estate agents in India
- **70%** use Excel + WhatsApp (no CRM)
- **60%** of leads lost due to poor tracking
- **15+ hours/week** wasted on manual follow-ups
- **₹2,400 crore** in commissions lost annually

### The Solution
- **AI-powered CRM** built exclusively for real estate agents
- **AI Calling Agent** that follows up with leads 24/7
- **Smart Property Matching** in 30 seconds (vs. 20 minutes manual)
- **Khata Book** for financial tracking (replace paper ledgers)
- **Complete Lifecycle:** Buyers, Sellers, Owners, Tenants, Properties

### The Traction
- **3,000 agents** in lead generation pipeline
- **287 beta users** active on the platform
- **12,500+ leads** managed in the CRM
- **8,700+ properties** listed
- **1,200+ AI calls** made

### The Business
- **₹1,499/month** (Solo Plan)
- **₹4,999/month** (Team Plan)
- **₹14,999/month** (Agency Plan)
- **LTV:CAC Ratio:** 8.6x
- **Gross Margin:** 85%

### The Market
- **TAM:** ₹900 crore/year
- **SAM:** ₹270 crore/year
- **SOM (3-year):** ₹18 crore/year
- **India RE market:** ₹12 lakh crore (15% CAGR)

## 🗣️ Go-To Pitch Lines (Memorize These)

### English (Professional)
```
"RealtyFlow helps Indian real estate agents close 50% more deals
by automating follow-ups with AI calling and WhatsApp agents."
```

### Hinglish (Casual/Marketing)
```
"RealtyFlow se real estate agents 50% zyada deals close karte hain—
AI automatically follow-ups karta hai, aap bas closing pe focus karo."
```

