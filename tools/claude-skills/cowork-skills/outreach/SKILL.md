# Multi-Channel Outreach Skill

## Overview
This skill enables building multi-channel outreach campaigns across email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and cold calling for RealtyFlow lead generation and nurture. All templates use **Hinglish** (70% English + 30% Hindi romanized) optimized for Indian real estate agents.

## 2. Trigger-Based Sequences

### Website Visitor → No Form Submission (3 emails, 7 days)
**Trigger:** Page visit recorded, no lead capture

**Email 1 (Day 0, 2 hours after):** "You checked out RealtyFlow"
- Hook: "Saw you exploring our platform today"
- Value: One pain-point they likely care about based on pages visited
- CTA: Book 15-min call

**Email 2 (Day 3):** Social proof angle
- Case study relevant to their page journey
- Success metric they'd care about
- CTA: Short demo video link

**Email 3 (Day 7):** Last chance angle
- Special offer or limited availability
- Simple one-click CTA
- Clear exit: "Not interested? No prob—removed from list"

### Downloaded Resource → No Lead Info (2 emails, 5 days)
**Trigger:** Downloaded PDF (lead management guide, case study, etc.)

**Email 1 (Day 0):** Contextual follow-up
- Reference which resource they downloaded
- Offer deeper resource or next step
- CTA: Chat with us

**Email 2 (Day 3):** Personal touch
- Share relevant success story
- Specific solution based on resource topic
- CTA: Short intro call

### Meeting Scheduled → Pre-Call Prep (1 email, Day before)
**Subject:** "Tomorrow at [TIME]—here's what we'll show"

- Quick agenda (3-4 bullet points)
- One key insight to prime them
- Ask one question to understand their biggest problem
- Zoom link + preparation request

### Demo Attended → No Purchase (3 emails, 14 days)
**Email 1 (Day 1 after):** "Thanks for the demo"
- What we learned about their use case
- Why we think RealtyFlow fits
- Next step: Trial or pricing discussion

**Email 2 (Day 5):** Objection handling
- Address likely objection (price, fit, integration, etc.)
- Case study from similar agent/agency
- CTA: Consultation call

**Email 3 (Day 12):** Urgency + trial offer
- Limited time trial offer
- Countdown (10 days remaining)
- Simple yes/no question

## Cold Call Script (Hinglish)

### Script Framework: AIDA (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action)

```
OPENING (10 seconds):
"Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] from RealtyFlow.
Got 2 minutes?

[PAUSE FOR YES - if no, ask for best time to call back]

ATTENTION (20 seconds):
Quick reason for calling:
Hum dekh rahe hain ki Indian agents like you are losing 3-4 leads every week because WhatsApp par chaos hota hai.
We built RealtyFlow to solve exactly that.

Do you see the same problem in your business?

[LISTEN - let them talk 60% of the call]

INTEREST (30 seconds - IF YES):
Nice. So what happens when you miss a follow-up?

[Let them talk about impact]

What would change if every lead got automatic follow-up in 30 minutes?

DESIRE (40 seconds):
Here's what other agents are seeing:
- 2 hours/day time saved
- 25% faster response to leads
- Way better team coordination

All automated. Zero manual work.

OBJECTION HANDLING:
[See common objections below]

ACTION (20 seconds):
Next step: 3-minute demo on Zoom?
[Offer: Tomorrow 4pm or Thursday 10am?]

Or if you prefer, just try 7-day free trial on your own.

What works better?

CLOSE:
Great! I'll send you the demo link.
Or check it out at [link] anytime.

Thanks for the time, [Name]. Talk soon! 👍
```

## 7-Touch Multi-Channel Sequence (14 Days)

This sequence touches a prospect **7 times across email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and call** over 2 weeks.

### Day 1
- **Touch 1 (Email):** Value-first email (Email 1 from framework)
- **Touch 2 (LinkedIn):** Connection request + first message

### Day 2
- **Touch 3 (WhatsApp):** Intro message (if phone available)

### Day 3
- **Touch 4 (Email):** Social proof email (Email 2)
- **Touch 5 (LinkedIn):** Engage with their recent post

### Day 7
- **Touch 6 (Phone):** Cold call + objection handling

### Day 10
- **Touch 7 (Email or WhatsApp):** Demo offer + video walkthrough

**Success Metrics:**
- Touch 3-4: 30% open rate target
- Touch 5-6: 15% reply rate target
- Touch 7: 8% meeting scheduled target
