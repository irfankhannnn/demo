## 3. Free Trial (Solo, Team, Team+)

### 3.1 Trial terms

- **Duration:** 14 days from Account creation
- **Credit card:** Not required to start
- **Applies to:** Solo, Team, and Team+ only
- **Does not apply to:** AI Employee add-on

### 3.2 During trial

You have full access to plan features. No charge is made during the trial unless you voluntarily upgrade before trial expiry.

### 3.3 After trial

- Your data is retained for **30 days** for export
- No charge is made

## 4. Paid Subscription Refund Window

### 4.1 30-day money-back guarantee

For **first-time paying subscribers** on Solo, Team, or Team+:

- You may request a **full refund** within **30 calendar days** of your **first paid charge** (not trial start date)
- Refund covers subscription fees only (excluding GST already remitted to government — GST component refunded where legally permissible)
- One refund per customer entity (agency/GSTIN/email/phone)

### 4.2 Anti-abuse conditions

A refund request will be **denied** if any of the following apply:

1. You exported **more than 100 CRM records** during the trial or refund window
2. You sent **more than 50 outbound WhatsApp messages** through the Service during the trial or refund window
3. You have previously received a refund from RealEstateFlow
4. You signed up multiple times to abuse the refund policy (same agency, GSTIN, or principal contact)
5. Your Account was terminated for Terms of Service violation
6. The request is for **AI Employee** (always non-refundable — see Section 5)

We log usage metrics to enforce these conditions fairly. You may appeal a denial by contacting [info@realestateflow.in](mailto:info@realestateflow.in) with your Account details.

## 5. AI Employee — No Trial, No Refund

The **AI Employee** add-on (₹7,999/month + 18% GST) is subject to special terms:

- **No free trial.** Billing starts on Day 1 of activation.
- **No refund.** Each AI Employee requires manual concierge configuration by our support team (24-hour SLA). This configuration cost is sunk at purchase.
- **Exception:** If your AI Employee is not live within **30 days** of purchase due to our failure (not yours — e.g., you provided required WhatsApp access), we will refund on a **pro-rata basis** for the non-delivered period.

AI Employee requires an active Solo, Team, or Team+ Subscription. Cancelling your base plan does not entitle you to an AI Employee refund.

## 7. How to Cancel

### 7.1 Self-service cancellation

1. Log in to [https://app.realestateflow.in](https://app.realestateflow.in)
2. Go to **Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription**
3. Confirm cancellation
4. You will receive an automatic acknowledgement email within 60 seconds

### 7.2 Effect of cancellation

- **Monthly plans:** Access continues until the end of the current billing period. No further charges.
- **Annual plans:** Access continues until the annual period ends (unless refund window applies).
- **AI Employee:** Cancels at end of current billing period; no partial refund.

### 7.3 Data after cancellation

- Export your data via **Settings → Data Export** within **30 days**
- After 30 days, data is deleted per our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy)

## 8. How to Request a Refund

### 8.1 Steps

1. Cancel your Subscription (Section 7) if not already cancelled
2. Email [info@realestateflow.in](mailto:info@realestateflow.in) with subject: **"Refund Request — [Your Agency Name]"**
3. Include: registered email, phone number, plan name, payment date, Razorpay payment ID (from invoice)
4. Brief reason for refund (optional but helpful)

### 8.2 Processing timeline

| Step | Timeline |
|------|----------|
| Acknowledgement | Within 1 business day |
| Review and decision | Within 5 business days |
| Refund initiation (if approved) | Within 7 business days of approval |
| Credit to your account | 7–10 working days (bank/UPI/card dependent) |

Refunds are processed through **Razorpay** to the **original payment method**. We cannot refund to a different account for fraud-prevention reasons.

### 8.3 GST on refunds

Where GST was collected, the refund amount includes the GST component. We issue a credit note for GST compliance. Your CA may require this credit note for input tax credit adjustment.

### 10.1 Failed payment

If a recurring payment fails:

- We retry up to 3 times over 7 days
- You receive email notification after each failure

### 10.2 Chargebacks

Filing a chargeback without contacting us first may result in immediate Account suspension. Contact [info@realestateflow.in](mailto:info@realestateflow.in) before initiating a chargeback — we resolve most issues within 5 business days.
