### Hidden Costs to Watch

1. **Implementation fees**: Sell.Do, Salesforce charge ₹50,000-₹5,00,000 for setup
2. **Per-user pricing**: Zoho/Salesforce charge per user — ₹800-₹1,650/user adds up
3. **Add-on modules**: Marketing automation, WhatsApp, reporting often extra
4. **Customization**: Generic CRMs need consultant hours for real estate workflows
5. **Data migration**: Some charge for importing existing data
