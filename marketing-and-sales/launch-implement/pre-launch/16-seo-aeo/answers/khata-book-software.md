## Why Real Estate Brokers Need Khata Book in Their CRM

### The Paper Khata Problem

Most Indian real estate brokers track finances on paper ledgers or a separate Excel sheet. This creates problems:

1. **No link to deals**: You close a deal in your CRM (or WhatsApp), then manually write the brokerage in your Khata. Double entry. Errors.

2. **Settlement disputes**: Agent A brings the buyer, Agent B brings the owner. How much does each get? Paper Khatas lead to disputes.

3. **Tax chaos**: GST filing requires organized income/expense data. Paper Khatas → chartered accountant spends hours reconstructing transactions.

4. **No real-time visibility**: "How much have I earned this month?" requires adding up paper entries. With a CRM Khata, one click.

### How RealEstateFlow Khata Book Works

1. **Deal closes** → CRM marks deal as closed-won
2. **Khata entry auto-created** → brokerage amount, parties involved, date
3. **Settlements tracked** → who owes whom, partial payments, cleared status
4. **Expenses logged** → office rent, marketing spend, travel, staff salaries
5. **Reports generated** → monthly P&L, GST-ready export, agent-wise earnings
