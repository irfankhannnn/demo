## The WhatsApp Problem for Mumbai Brokers

Mumbai's real estate market runs on WhatsApp. A typical broker receives 20-50 WhatsApp messages daily from potential buyers, owners, and other agents. The problem: WhatsApp was built for personal messaging, not for managing a business pipeline.

### Common Pain Points

1. **Lost leads**: A buyer messages at 9 PM. You're asleep. By morning, they've messaged 3 other brokers. The fastest responder wins.

2. **No tracking**: "Which buyer was interested in the 2BHK in Andheri?" Good luck finding that conversation from 2 weeks ago.

3. **Manual follow-ups**: You remember to follow up with some buyers, forget others. The forgotten ones go to competitors.

4. **Team chaos**: You forward leads to team members via WhatsApp. No way to track who followed up, what was said, or where the deal stands.

5. **No Khata tracking**: The deal closes. What was the brokerage? When did the owner pay? Which amount went to the team member? All in your head.
