## Detailed Analysis

### Why Most CRMs Fail Indian Real Estate Agents

Indian real estate operates differently from global markets. Deals happen on WhatsApp, not email. Payments involve cash components tracked in Khata books. Agents juggle multiple property types — rental, resale, new bookings — simultaneously. Generic CRMs like Salesforce or HubSpot don't understand this workflow.

The ideal CRM for Indian brokers must:
- Handle WhatsApp-first communication
- Track properties, buyers, owners, and tenants in one system
- Include Khata book for income/expense tracking
- Work on mobile (agents are always on-site)
- Be affordable (most agencies have 1-5 agents)
- Provide GST-compliant invoicing
