# Positioning Wedge — RealEstateFlow

**Last updated:** 2026-06-11  
**Audience:** Mumbai real estate brokers, solo to 5-agent agencies

---

## One-Line Wedge

**RealEstateFlow is an AI Employee that runs your broking agency on WhatsApp + Telegram.**

Not a CRM you log into once a week. Not a developer sales platform. An AI teammate that qualifies leads, follows up buyers, and updates your Khata book — while you close deals.

---

## Why This Wedge Wins (Mumbai-Specific)

### 1. WhatsApp is the office

70%+ of Mumbai brokers never open a desktop CRM. They live in WhatsApp groups, forwarded messages, and voice notes. RealEstateFlow meets them where work actually happens — not where software vendors wish it happened.

### 2. Small teams, not sales floors

The modal Mumbai agency is 1 owner + 2 agents in Andheri or Bandra. They don't need Sell.do's CP module or LeadSquared's call-centre workflows. They need shared Khata, lead follow-up, and someone (or something) to reply at 9pm when they're at a site visit.

### 3. AI priced as a junior agent, not a chatbot

At ₹7,999/month, AI Employee costs less than half a junior broker — and works 24×7. Solo plan at ₹999 gets them into the CRM; AI Employee is the upsell when they're ready to stop doing follow-ups manually.
