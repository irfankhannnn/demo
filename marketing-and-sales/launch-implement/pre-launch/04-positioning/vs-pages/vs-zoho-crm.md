## When Zoho CRM Is the Right Choice

Choose Zoho when you:

- Need a **generic CRM** for multiple business lines (not just real estate)
- Already use the **Zoho ecosystem** (Books, Mail, Campaigns)
- Have an admin who can **build custom modules** and workflows
- Run a **non-real-estate sales team** alongside your brokerage
- Need **deep enterprise customisation** and global support scale

Zoho is an excellent general-purpose CRM. It's not optimised for brokerage out of the box.

## When RealEstateFlow Is the Right Choice

Choose RealEstateFlow when you:

- Are a **real estate broker or agency** in Mumbai (or India)

- Have **1–5 agents** and want flat team pricing
