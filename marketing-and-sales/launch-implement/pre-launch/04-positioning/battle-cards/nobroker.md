## 3 Things They Do Well

1. **Consumer brand** — owners and tenants know NoBroker
2. **Lead volume** — inbound demand in major metros
3. **Marketing spend** — heavy ads = awareness

---

## 3 Things They Do Poorly (for Independent Brokers)

1. **You don't own the lead** — platform controls access; lead goes to multiple agents
2. **Race to the bottom** — same buyer contacted by 5 agents; commission pressure
3. **No CRM** — NoBroker is a marketplace, not your agency operating system
