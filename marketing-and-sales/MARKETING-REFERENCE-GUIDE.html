hormozi-meats.md

5 Meat Formats

The body of the ad — 20-60 seconds. Educates customer on offer/product/solution/problem.

📱
Meat 1: Demonstration
Screen recording of CRM in action. Lead → AI call → CRM update → agent notified. 45s full · 15s cut.
🎙️
Meat 2: Testimonial
Real agency owner or agent shares before/after story. Highest trust. Agency (50s) + Agent (35s) versions.
📊
Meat 3: Educational
Teach something valuable. "Lead Leakage Explained": 32% stat → ₹20L → unified pipeline. 55s.
📖
Meat 4: Story
"Priya's Story": 7 agents → manual midnight work → setup in 2hrs → ₹4.2L month 1. 60s.
🖥️
Meat 5: Faceless
Screen recording + timer overlay. "Setup 0:00 to 2:00:00." No people. Fastest to produce.
