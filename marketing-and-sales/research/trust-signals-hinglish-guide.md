## Part 4: Trust-Killing Language to AVOID

### What NOT to Say

❌ "Limited time offer" - Sounds like pressure tactics
✅ "Currently showing 3 available units" - Factual

❌ "Act fast or miss out" - Scammy
✅ "High demand property, many interested buyers" - Honest

❌ "Unbelievable deal" - Too good to be true
✅ "Market-rate pricing with premium location" - Credible

❌ "This is confidential" - Hiding something
✅ "Fully transparent process with documentation" - Trustworthy

❌ "Contact me only by WhatsApp" - Avoids accountability
✅ "Reach us by phone, email, or office visit" - Professional

❌ "I know the builder personally" - Conflict of interest
✅ "Independent real estate advisor" - No bias

❌ "Free consultation" (but they try to sell hard) - Misleading
✅ "15-min free consultation. No strings." - Clear

❌ "Highest prices guaranteed" - Impossible
✅ "We'll get you competitive market rates" - Realistic

## Part 5: By-Segment Trust Checklist

### Solo Broker Trust Checklist

Essential:
- [ ] RERA license clearly displayed (header or footer)
- [ ] Personal phone number + office address
- [ ] Photo of self on website
- [ ] 5+ testimonials with client names
- [ ] "Est. YYYY" badge (if 5+ years experience)
- [ ] List of credentials (years experience, deals closed)
- [ ] Google My Business with reviews
- [ ] WhatsApp profile optimized (bio + profile picture)
- [ ] Simple, clean website design (no flashy elements)
- [ ] Direct call CTA (not "Book a meeting")

Nice to Have:
- [ ] Video testimonials
- [ ] Published blog (5-10 articles)
- [ ] LinkedIn with 500+ connections
- [ ] Instagram with regular posts
- [ ] Property walkthrough videos

## Part 7: Hinglish Style Guide

### Proper Hinglish Ratio

**Good (70% English, 30% Hindi):**
```
"Real estate buying ka process confusing ho sakta hai.
Hamara team step-by-step guide kar sakta hai.
Property selection se registration tak sab kuch.
Easy ho gaya apke liye."
```

**Bad (50% English, 50% Hindi - too much):**
```
"Real estate khareedna mushkil hota hai.
Hamara team aapko sab kuch samjha sakta hai.
Ghar dhundne se registration tak.
Sab kuch asaan kar diya humne."
```

**Bad (100% English - loses Indian appeal):**
```
"Real estate purchasing can be confusing.
Our team can guide you step-by-step.
Property selection to registration.
Everything simplified."
```
