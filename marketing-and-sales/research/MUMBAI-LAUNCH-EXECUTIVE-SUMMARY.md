## 👥 Three Core Personas

### 1. **PRIYA PATEL** — "The Ambitious Broker Owner"
- **Profile:** 35-45, manages 3-8 agents, ₹50L-5Cr revenue
- **Pain:** Lead leakage (losing 30-40%), manual commission tracking, admin overload
- **Solution Fit:** Lead accountability, auto-follow-up, commission settling
- **Winning Message:** *"Stop losing ₹20L in commissions per year to spreadsheets."*
- **Sales Channel:** LinkedIn, warm intros, industry associations
- **Decision Cycle:** 60-90 days

### 2. **ARJUN DESAI** — "The Tech-Savvy Young Agent"
- **Profile:** 26-35, top performer (15% close rate), self-reliant
- **Pain:** 20 hours/week in admin, clients expect quick response, wants performance proof
- **Solution Fit:** AI calling (saves 10 hours/week), call recording, personal dashboard
- **Winning Message:** *"AI does the follow-up. You do the selling."*
- **Sales Channel:** WhatsApp, LinkedIn DM, peer referrals
- **Decision Cycle:** 14-30 days

### 3. **SURESH SHARMA** — "The Old-School Brokerage Boss"
- **Profile:** 50-60, founder, 8-15 agents, ₹1-5Cr revenue
- **Pain:** No succession plan, commission disputes, aging tech, agent churn
- **Solution Fit:** Business continuity, settlement automation, transparency
- **Winning Message:** *"Pass the torch (without losing control)."*
- **Sales Channel:** Warm intros only, peer brokers, family members
- **Decision Cycle:** 90-180 days

## 👤 Sales Team Playbook

### Three-Step Sales Process

#### Step 1: Discovery (15 min call)
**Goal:** Qualify if they're ICP fit + identify main pain

**Questions:**
- How many agents/leads per month?
- What's your current lead management system?
- What's your biggest challenge right now?
- How much revenue are you losing to lead leakage?

**Persona Assignment:**
- Listening cues indicate if Priya, Arjun, or Suresh type

#### Step 2: Demo (30 min web call)
**Goal:** Show solution to their specific pain

**For Priya:** Focus on lead tracking + commission settling
**For Arjun:** Focus on AI calling + time saved
**For Suresh:** Focus on operations transparency + succession

**Demo Script:**
1. "Here's how we prevent lead loss..."
2. "Here's what your team would see..."
3. "Here's the result most customers get..."
4. "Questions?"

#### Step 3: Trial Setup (5 min)
**Goal:** Get them started on free trial

**Steps:**
- Import 10-20 real leads
- Set up 1 AI call
- Schedule 1-week check-in

**Success Metrics:**
- Trial activation: >90%
- Feature adoption: 5+ features in first week
- Conversion: 40-50% trial-to-paid

## 💡 Key Insights

1. **Lead Leakage is the #1 Pain:** Most brokers lose 30-40% of leads in cracks. This is our biggest sales hook.

2. **Priya is Primary Target:** Broker owners decide budgets and have highest LTV. Prioritize this persona for Q1.

3. **Arjun is the Advocate:** Young agents drive adoption. Get them using it → they convince brokers.

4. **Suresh Wants Succession:** Old-school owners are desperate for succession solutions. Emphasize next-gen capability.

5. **Hinglish Resonates:** 70% English + 30% Hindi (romanized) is critical for Mumbai market adoption.

6. **AI Calling is Differentiator:** No competitor offers built-in AI calling. This is our advantage.

7. **Affordable Pricing is Key:** ₹5K-25K/month vs ₹50K+ for competitors. Price is a major selling point.

8. **Fast Implementation Matters:** 2-hour setup vs 4+ weeks for competitors. Speed wins deals.
