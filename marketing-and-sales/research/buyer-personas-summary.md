# Buyer Personas - One-Page Summary

## Persona 1: Priya Patel - "The Ambitious Broker Owner"

### Quick Profile
- **Title:** Broker/Agency Owner
- **Age:** 35-45
- **Team Size:** 3-8 agents
- **Tech Level:** Medium
- **Decision Power:** HIGH (final approver)
- **Buying Cycle:** 60-90 days

### Pain Points
- Lead leakage (leads forgotten or assigned to wrong person)
- No visibility into agent activity
- Manual commission tracking (errors & disputes)
- Admin overload (can't hire more without cash crunch)
- Agent turnover (lack of professional tools)

### What Success Looks Like for Priya
- ✅ Leads followed up within 24 hours (currently: 48-72h)
- ✅ 25% increase in pipeline visibility
- ✅ Admin time reduced from 6 hours/day to 3-4 hours/day
- ✅ Fewer agent complaints about "lost leads"
- ✅ Commission settlements automated

### Sales Hook for Priya
**"Your best agents are getting poached by competitors using better tools."**

- Lead source: LinkedIn (broker associations)
- Best outreach: Warm intro + 15-min call
- Demo angle: Show exact team size data entry, live lead tracking demo
- Objection handler: "Your admin will have 2 hours/day back. What would they focus on instead?"

## Persona 2: Arjun Desai - "The Tech-Savvy Young Agent"

### Quick Profile
- **Title:** Senior Agent / Rising Star
- **Age:** 26-35
- **Performance:** Top 20% of office (12-15% close rate)
- **Tech Level:** High (early adopter)
- **Decision Power:** MEDIUM (can advocate internally)
- **Buying Cycle:** 14-30 days

### Pain Points
- Manual follow-ups drain 15-20 hours/week
- Client response delays (competitors are faster)
- Broker's tools don't support his success
- Can't prove his individual performance (comp disputes)
- Wants tools to build personal brand for future agency

### What Success Looks Like for Arjun
- ✅ Response time to clients: <30 minutes
- ✅ Auto-generated call notes (saves 2 hours/week)
- ✅ AI calling handles qualification (frees him for negotiation)
- ✅ Personal dashboard showing his metrics (15+ deals/month proof)
- ✅ Testimonial video credibility for his future brand

### Sales Hook for Arjun
**"The AI calling alone will save you 10 hours/week—imagine what you can close with that time."**

- Lead source: LinkedIn DM + peer referrals
- Best outreach: WhatsApp/DM (matches his preference)
- Demo angle: Show call recording, transcript, lead scoring
- Objection handler: "Free 7-day trial. Set your first AI call tomorrow. See it work live."

## Persona 3: Suresh Sharma - "The Family-Run Brokerage Boss"

### Quick Profile
- **Title:** Founder/Owner (20+ years)
- **Age:** 50-60
- **Team Size:** 8-15 agents
- **Tech Level:** Low (prefers calls)
- **Decision Power:** HIGHEST (founder call)
- **Buying Cycle:** 90-180 days

### Pain Points
- Can't delegate operations (no one understands the "system")
- Worried about agent accountability
- Commission settling takes 10+ hours/month (manual excel)
- No clear succession plan (son doesn't want to learn "old way")
- Fear of technology replacing his value

### What Success Looks Like for Suresh
- ✅ His son can manage operations (succession ready)
- ✅ Settlement process: <1 hour/month (automated)
- ✅ Agent performance tracked (transparency)
- ✅ Fewer lead conflicts & commission disputes
- ✅ Business scales without him being involved in every deal

### Sales Hook for Suresh
**"Your successor won't manage the business the way you do—give them the tools to do it better."**

- Lead source: Warm intro from trusted peer or family
- Best outreach: In-person coffee meeting
- Demo angle: Show Khata Book (settlement), agent tracking, hierarchy view
- Objection handler: "Your son runs it. You oversee strategy. That's how successful firms grow."
