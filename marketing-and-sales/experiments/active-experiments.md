## Operational Rules

1. **Max simultaneous tests per funnel step:** 2 (more = interaction effects)
2. **Max total tests at once:** 5 (more = team capacity overload)
3. **Mandatory cooldown after creative fatigue:** 7 days before re-testing similar creative
4. **No mid-test changes:** changes invalidate statistical validity, restart instead
