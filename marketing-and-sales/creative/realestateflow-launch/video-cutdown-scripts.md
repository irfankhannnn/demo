## VIDEO PRODUCTION NOTES

### General
- All videos: Add burnt-in captions (80% of Reels watched without sound)
- Font for on-screen text: **Poppins ExtraBold** (headlines) / **Inter Medium** (body)
- Background: Dark navy `#07111E` for all screen/text frames
- UI elements: Navy cards `#0C1E3A` with green `#22C55E` highlights and badges
- Music: Upbeat but professional instrumental — not cheesy, not loud

### Pricing Disclosure Rules (must follow)
- Bot pricing (+₹5K/mo, no free trial) MUST appear on screen as text (not just VO)
- Minimum 2 seconds display on screen for pricing frames
- Font size: readable at mobile (minimum 24px equivalent in video)
- Color: Amber `#F59E0B` for pricing so it stands out from all other text

### Platform-specific Aspect Ratios
| Platform | Ratio | Duration |
|----------|-------|---------|
| Instagram Reels | 9:16 | 15s, 30s |
| Instagram Feed | 1:1 or 4:5 | 30s |
| YouTube | 16:9 | 30s, 60-90s |
| YouTube Bumper | 16:9 | 6s |
| LinkedIn | 16:9 or 1:1 | 30s |
| Facebook Stories | 9:16 | 15s, 30s |
| WhatsApp Status | 9:16 | 15s |

### Export Settings
- Format: MP4 (H.264)
- Resolution: 1080p minimum
- Frame rate: 30fps
- Audio: 128kbps AAC stereo
- Captions: Burnt-in as SRT + export with captions baked in

### Color Check (before export)
- [ ] No sky-blue `#0EA5E9` anywhere
- [ ] No saffron `#F97316` anywhere
- [ ] Background is `#07111E` (not `#060B18`)
- [ ] Poppins used for all headlines (not Inter-only)
- [ ] Logo is house+wave SVG (not the old "R" lettermark)
