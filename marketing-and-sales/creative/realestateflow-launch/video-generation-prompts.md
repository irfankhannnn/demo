## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Rendering specs
- **Resolution:** 1080×1920 (9:16) or 1920×1080 (16:9)
- **Frame rate:** 30fps (ads), 24fps (cinematic)
- **Codec:** H.264 MP4
- **Audio:** Add background music in editor (upbeat instrumental, not cheesy)
- **Captions:** Always add burnt-in captions (80% of Reels watched muted)

### Post-production checklist per video
- [ ] Add navy-to-green gradient lower third
- [ ] Add house+wave logo watermark (bottom-right, 15% opacity)
- [ ] Add captions (Poppins Bold, white with dark navy shadow)
- [ ] Add pricing disclosure frames (minimum 2 seconds for bot pricing, amber text)
- [ ] Export in all required aspect ratios
- [ ] Create thumbnail from best frame
