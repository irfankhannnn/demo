# RealEstateFlow — WhatsApp Automation Graphics & AI Employee Storyboard (v2)

## A) WhatsApp Chat Mockup Spec (for AI Employee LP)

### Design Requirements
- **Tool:** Figma, Canva Pro, or screen recording + crop
- **Format:** 9:16 (mobile) primary, 16:9 cut for desktop
- **Background:** Dark navy `#07111E` with phone frame overlay
- **Chat UI style:** Realistic enough to look familiar, but NOT infringing WhatsApp trademark
- **Bot bubble color:** `#22C55E` tinted — distinct from exact WhatsApp green (#25D366)

### Messages to Show (in order)
```
CLIENT: "Hi, I'm looking for a 3BHK in Andheri under 1.5Cr"

BOT (seconds later):
  "Hi! Found 3 matching properties for you 👇
   
   🏠 Property 1 – Andheri West
   3BHK | 850 sqft | ₹1.42Cr
   
   🏠 Property 2 – Andheri East  
   3BHK | 920 sqft | ₹1.38Cr
   
   🏠 Property 3 – Versova
   3BHK | 780 sqft | ₹1.29Cr
   
   Want details on any of these?"

CLIENT: "Tell me more about Property 2"

BOT:
  "Property 2 – Andheri East 📋
   Area: 920 sqft | Floor: 8th | Parking: 1
   Society: Sunrise Heights
   Ready possession ✅
   
   Would you like to schedule a site visit?"

CLIENT: "Yes, can I visit Sunday?"

BOT:
  "✅ Visit confirmed!
   📅 Sunday, 11:00 AM
   📍 Sunrise Heights, Andheri East
   👤 Agent Arjun will meet you
   
   You'll receive a reminder 2 hours before."
```

### Timestamp Notes
- Show realistic timestamps (10:47 PM, 10:47 PM, 10:48 PM, 10:48 PM, 10:48 PM)
- Shows "all this at 10:48 PM while the owner sleeps" angle
- KEY: Late-night timestamps = most powerful hook for this product

### Visual Overlays to add (motion)
- Green `#22C55E` checkmarks appearing when CRM updates
- Small "CRM Updated ✓" toast notification in corner (navy `#0F3A66` bg)
- Agent phone screen showing "Hot Lead: Andheri East" ping (green badge)

## C) Motion Storyboard — AI Employee Intro (45s Video)

### Scene 1: Hook (0–3s)
- **Visual:** Dark navy screen. A WhatsApp-style notification slides in.
- **Text:** "Client message. 2:00 AM."
- **Sound:** Notification ping sound
- **Color:** Dark `#07111E` bg, green notification badge

### Scene 2: AI Response (3–12s)
- **Visual:** Chat mockup — bot replies instantly with property listings
- **Text overlay:** "AI Employee replies in 30 seconds" (white Poppins)
- **Animated:** Messages appearing one by one, green `#22C55E` ticks
- **Bot bubbles:** Navy-tinted green, not exact WhatsApp green

### Scene 3: CRM Update (12–20s)
- **Visual:** Split screen — chat on left, CRM pipeline updating on right (navy cards)
- **Text overlay:** "Lead automatically added to CRM pipeline"
- **Animated:** Card sliding into "New Lead" column (navy `#0F3A66` card with green badge)

### Scene 4: Agent Alert (20–28s)
- **Visual:** Agent's phone (in bed or at dinner) receives notification
- **Text overlay:** "Agent gets notified only when lead is HOT"
- **Animated:** Green notification badge + vibration effect

### Scene 5: Visit Booked (28–38s)
- **Visual:** Calendar view, visit slot confirmed (navy card, green checkmark)
- **Text overlay:** "Site visit booked. Agent calendar updated."
- **Animated:** Calendar card appearing with green `#22C55E` checkmark

### Scene 6: Pricing Disclosure (38–45s)
- **Visual:** Clean slide, dark navy background `#07111E`
- **Text (large, white):** "Optional Add-on"
- **Text (medium, white):** "+₹5,000/month"
- **Text (small, amber `#F59E0B`):** "No free trial — paid from day 1"
- **CTA:** "Book a demo. See it live." (green button)
