## Video Production Standards

### Visual Style
- **Background:** `#07111E` (dark navy — all UI screens and text frames)
- **UI cards:** `#0C1E3A` navy cards with `#22C55E` green badges/CTAs
- **Headlines:** Poppins Black (900 weight), white, large
- **Body text:** Inter Medium (500 weight), slate-300
- **Gradient text:** White → green (`#ffffff` → `#22C55E`) for hero lines
- **Timeline lines:** Navy `#0F3A66` → Green `#22C55E` gradient

### Pricing Disclosure Rules (non-negotiable)
- Bot pricing (+₹5K/mo, no free trial) **MUST appear on screen as text** (not just VO)
- **Minimum 2 seconds** display on pricing frames
- Font color: Amber `#F59E0B` — stands out from all other text colors
- Font size: Readable at mobile (minimum equivalent of 24px in video)

### Platform-specific Aspect Ratios
| Platform | Ratio | Duration |
|----------|-------|---------|
| Instagram Reels | 9:16 | 15s, 30s |
| Instagram Feed | 1:1 or 4:5 | 30s |
| YouTube | 16:9 | 30s, 60-90s |
| YouTube Bumper | 16:9 | 6s |
| LinkedIn | 16:9 or 1:1 | 30s |
| Facebook Stories | 9:16 | 15s, 30s |
| WhatsApp Status | 9:16 | 15s |

### Export Settings
- Format: MP4 (H.264)
- Resolution: 1080p minimum
- Frame rate: 30fps
- Audio: 128kbps AAC stereo
- Captions: Burnt-in (Poppins Bold, white, dark navy shadow/bg)
