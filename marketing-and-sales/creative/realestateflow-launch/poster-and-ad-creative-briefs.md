# RealEstateFlow — Poster + Ad Creative Briefs (v2 — APPROVED & ACTIVE)

## Brand Identity Quick-Ref
- **Colors:** Navy `#0F3A66`, Green `#22C55E`, Dark BG `#07111E`, Card `#0C1E3A`
- **Fonts:** Poppins Black/ExtraBold (headlines), Inter Regular/Medium (body)
- **Primary hook:** "Apna business WhatsApp se baahar nikalo."
- **Logo:** House outline + 4 wave lines (green accent wave) + "RealEstateFlow" wordmark

## Target Audiences
- **Agency owners (Priya):** 3–15 agents, wants control + visibility — lead with WhatsApp chaos pain
- **Agents (Arjun):** wants time back + higher close rate — lead with time-saving angle
- **Enterprise:** data control + white-label — lead with authority/scale angle (navy-dominant)

## Core Claims (must remain consistent)
- CRM: **2 months free** (no card)
- CRM: **6-month money-back guarantee**
- Bot add-on: **+₹5,000/month, no free trial**
