## Style
- **Page background:** `#07111E` (dark navy — richer, more premium than pure midnight)
- **Card background:** `#0C1E3A`
- **Primary highlight:** `#0F3A66` (Navy)
- **Accent highlight:** `#22C55E` (Green — pipeline stage wins, checkmarks, CTAs)
- **Warning/disclosure:** `#F59E0B` (Amber — bot pricing, "no free trial" messages)
- **Error/pain:** `#EF4444` (Red — lead loss stats, pain metrics)
- **Gradient (text):** White `#ffffff` → Green `#22C55E` (135deg)
- **Gradient (timeline):** Navy `#0F3A66` → Green `#22C55E` (left to right)
- Rounded corners (12px–16px cards), soft shadows, clean modern UI motion
- Poppins ExtraBold for all animated headline text
- Inter Medium for body/label text in motion

## Why Navy + Green Works
- **Navy** (`#0F3A66`) = authority, trust, premium — conveys serious business software
- **Green** (`#22C55E`) = growth, money, deals closed — deeply resonant for real estate
- **Dark navy BG** = premium tech aesthetic, same language as top Indian fintechs
- **White→green gradient** = progress, from standard to winning
- No Indian PropTech competitor uses this exact combination at this dark premium execution

## Notes
- Keep motion minimal so it feels premium, not gimmicky.
- Always keep pricing disclosures readable for at least 2 seconds when shown.
- Amber warning text for bot pricing must be minimum 16px and at 100% opacity.
- "WhatsApp" can be shown as a concept (green chat bubble) — don't use the exact WhatsApp logo.
