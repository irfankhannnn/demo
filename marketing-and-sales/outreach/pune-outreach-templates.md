## Why Pune is Different from Mumbai

- **Smaller teams:** Average agency 3–8 agents (vs Mumbai 5–15)
- **IT crowd overlap:** Many part-time agents are IT professionals
- **New construction heavy:** Baner, Wakad, Hinjawadi, Kharadi dominate
- **Price-sensitive:** Lower deal sizes (₹40L–1.5Cr avg vs Mumbai ₹80L–5Cr)
- **Tight-knit community:** Brokers know each other — referrals matter more
- **Language mix:** More Marathi + English vs Mumbai's Hindi + English
