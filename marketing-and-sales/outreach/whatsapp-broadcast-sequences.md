## BROADCAST RULES

1. **Always personalize** [Name] and [City/Area] — never send generic blasts
2. **Never send more than 1 message/day** to the same person
3. **Stop immediately** if someone says "stop" / "unsubscribe" / "not interested"
4. **Track responses** in CRM — mark hot/warm/cold
5. **Reply within 5 minutes** when someone responds (this is critical)
6. **Never send bot pricing** without disclosure: "+₹5K/mo, no free trial, paid from day 1"
7. **Best days:** Tuesday–Thursday
8. **Best times:** 10 AM–12 PM and 3 PM–5 PM IST
9. **Weekend rule:** Never send on Sundays. Saturday only if urgent/time-sensitive
10. **Language:** Default English. Switch to Hindi/Hinglish if the prospect replies in Hindi
