## ✅ Quality Checklist for All Creatives

**Before finalizing any creative, check:**

- [ ] Does it position against WhatsApp? (primary enemy)
- [ ] Does it highlight one of 4 pillars? (Control, Visibility, Risk, Growth)
- [ ] Does it follow P-A-S-O framework? (Problem → Agitation → Solution → Outcome)
- [ ] Is tone direct & practical? (no fluff, outcome-focused)
- [ ] Are brand colors used correctly? (tokens from design-tokens.json)
- [ ] Is Hinglish ratio correct? (70% English + 30% Hindi)
- [ ] Is it one of allowed 7 angles? (not a disallowed angle)
- [ ] Does it show real product UI? (not generic CRM imagery)
- [ ] Is it mobile-responsive? (check on phone)
- [ ] Does it avoid technical jargon? (WhatsApp not "CRM platform")
