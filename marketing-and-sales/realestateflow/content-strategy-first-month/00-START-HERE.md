# RealEstateFlow - Month 1 Instagram + Facebook Content Pack

**Campaign window:** Mon 10 Aug 2026 → Sun 6 Sep 2026 (4 weeks, 28 feed posts)
**Prep window:** Thu 6 Aug → Sun 9 Aug 2026 (batch production before Day 1)
**Stage:** Pre-launch. Zero paying customers. Zero testimonials. This pack is built for that reality.
**Brand system:** v3 "Bazaar Signal" (`marketing-and-sales/creative/realestateflow-launch/brand-kit.md`)

---

## 1. What this pack is

A complete, production-ready first month of organic content for the RealEstateFlow Instagram
and Facebook accounts. Every post in the calendar has a script, a design spec, an owner, and a
caption. Nothing here says "TBD."

Paid ads are **out of scope** - planned separately, as you asked.

---

## 2. Who does what

There are three producers on this campaign. Every asset in the calendar is tagged with exactly one.

| Tag | Producer | Tools | What they make |
|-----|----------|-------|----------------|
| **`[AI]`** | You, driving Claude | Higgsfield MCP, HyperFrames skill, nano-banana-pro | Animated kinetic-type reels, WhatsApp chat-thread reels, all carousels, all posters, AI-presenter raw footage + voice |
| **`[REC]`** | You, manually | OBS / phone screen record / Mac-Win screen capture | Raw screen recordings of the real CRM and the real WhatsApp AI Employee |
| **`[ED]`** | Your video editor | Whatever he already uses (Premiere / CapCut / AE / Resolve) | Multi-shot narrative reels, assembling AI-presenter footage with b-roll, cutting screen recordings, sound design, caption burn-in |

**The rule:** the editor only touches work that needs human judgement - pacing a narrative, cutting a
screen recording down to its 3 best seconds, sound design. Everything templated goes to `[AI]`.

**Editor load:** ~13 videos across 4 weeks = **~3 videos/week.** Deliberately kept low.

### ★ What to send the editor

**Send him the `EDITOR-JOBCARDS/` folder. Nothing else.**

It is written in simple English + Hinglish, one file per video. He opens `R01.md` and everything for
that one reel is there: which files you owe him, what b-roll to shoot (with AI prompts if he wants to
generate it), a second-by-second timeline, the exact on-screen text to copy, the special rules, and a
checklist. No scrolling between documents, no strategy to read.

```
EDITOR-JOBCARDS/
├── 00-READ-FIRST.md   ← he reads this once (brand colours, fonts, export, safe zone, 8 rules)
├── R01.md  R03.md  R04.md  R05.md  R08.md  R09.md  R10.md
└── R12.md  R13.md  R14.md  R15.md  R17.md  R18.md
```

Files 06, 07 and 10 stay here as **your** reference. They hold the full creative reasoning and the
captions; the job cards are the stripped-down build instructions cut from them. If you change a
script, change it in both places.

## 4. Asset naming convention

Every file the editor or Claude produces uses this name. No exceptions - the tracker depends on it.

```
REF-<TYPE><NN>-<slug>-v<N>.<ext>

REF-R01-lead-kho-gaya-v1.mp4
REF-C03-paisa-leak-slide2-v1.png
REF-P02-followup-stat-v1.png
REF-S09-khata-screenrec-RAW.mp4
```

| Code | Type | Delivery spec |
|------|------|---------------|
| `R` | Reel | 1080×1920, 9:16, 30fps, H.264, ≤ 90s, audio −14 LUFS |
| `C` | Carousel slide | 1080×1350, 4:5, PNG |
| `P` | Poster / static | 1080×1350, 4:5, PNG |
| `S` | Screen recording (raw) | 1080×1920 preferred, or 1920×1080 to be reframed |
| `ST` | Story frame | 1080×1920, PNG or MP4 |

## 5. Reel safe zones (non-negotiable - burn into the editor's template)

Instagram's UI covers the frame. Anything important outside the safe box gets hidden.

```
1080 × 1920 frame
├─ Top 0-220px      ── DEAD. IG profile/UI. No text, no logo.
├─ 220-1500px       ── SAFE. All hook text, captions, key visuals live here.
│                      Sweet spot for burned-in captions: y ≈ 1050-1250
└─ 1500-1920px      ── DEAD. IG caption, username, audio, CTA buttons.
```

Right edge: keep the last **140px** clear (like/comment/share rail).

## 7. Posting times (IST)

Indian agency owners check their phone in three windows. Post at the third - it is by far the best.

| Slot | Time | Use for |
|------|------|---------|
| **Primary** | **9:30 PM IST** | Every feed post. Day is done, site visits are over, they are on the sofa scrolling. |
| Secondary | 8:30 AM | Stories, and any post you double-slot |
| Tertiary | 1:45 PM | Stories only |

Do **not** post at 11 AM-1 PM or 4-7 PM. They are on site visits or driving.

## 8. Three hard rules for month 1

1. **No fabricated proof.** We are pre-launch. No testimonials, no "200+ agencies," no invented
   customer quotes, no AI-generated face presented as a real customer. See `04` §7 and `14` §2.
2. **Every number must survive the claims audit** in `14-METRICS-CLAIMS-REVIEW.md` §2 before it
   goes on screen.
3. **Reply to every single comment and DM within 60 minutes.** At zero followers, the reply is the
   product. This is what converts a scroll into a waitlist signup.
