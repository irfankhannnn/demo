## STEP 4 - Special rules (BAHUT DHYAN SE PADHO)

1. **★ 6.0 se 8.0 second wala WAIT ko SPEED UP MAT KARO.**
   Jab message send hota hai aur AI ka jawaab aane mein 2 second lagte hain - **wo 2 second asli hain aur waise hi rehne chahiye.** Agar aap usko fast kar doge, video fake lagne lagegi. Wo wait hi proof hai ki ye asli hai.

2. **★ 13.0 se 17.0 second - jawaab pe 4 second hold.**
   Dekhne wale ko asli numbers padhne ka time chahiye. Ye is video ka poora proof hai. **Yahan music mat lagao.** Chup raho.

3. **Recording asli phone pe hai, haath mein.** Agar usme haath thoda hil raha hai - **wo achha hai. Stabilize mat karo.** Hilna hi batata hai ki asli hai.

4. **Notification aur message ki asli awaazein use karo** agar recording mein hain. Nakli sound effect mat lagao.

5. **Personal data blur karo** - agar kisi asli client ka naam ya number dikh raha hai.

6. **PRICING WALA TEXT LAGANA ZAROORI HAI.** End card pe ya caption mein. `₹5,000/month add-on. Free trial nahi.` **Isko chhota karke chhupana mat.** Padhne layak hona chahiye.

7. Agar recording mein AI ka jawaab galat ya adhoora hai - **Kalim ko batao, dobara record karwao. Fake jawaab kabhi mat banao.**
