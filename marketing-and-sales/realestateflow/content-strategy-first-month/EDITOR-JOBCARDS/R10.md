## STEP 5 - Special rules

1. **ZOOM-PUNCH KARO, SLOW PAN MAT KARO.**
   Har voice line ke beat pe ekdum se zoom in karo us hisse pe, fir wapas. Screen recording bina zoom-punch ke bilkul boring lagti hai. **Ye is video ka main kaam hai.**

2. **Har number pe orange annotation lagao** jab voice usko bolti hai. Halka circle ya underline, jo beat pe animate hoke aaye. Orange `#FF7A1A`.

3. **Agar recording mein kisi asli client ka naam ya phone number dikh raha hai to blur kar do.** Ya Kalim ko bolo dobara record kare.

4. Recording mein loading spinner, error, ya khali screen nahi dikhna chahiye. **Agar dikhe to Kalim ko turant batao.**

5. End card ORANGE hai, text INK `#1C1512` mein.
