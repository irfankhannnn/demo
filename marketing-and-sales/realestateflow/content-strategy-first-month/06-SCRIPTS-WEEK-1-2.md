# 06 - Reel Scripts, Weeks 1 & 2 (R01-R08)

**How to read a script**

- **VO** = voiceover line. Written in TTS-safe spelling (`04` §5). `|` short beat, `||` hard pause,
  **bold** = stressed word.
- **TEXT** = burned-in on-screen text. Uses digits and normal spelling. Unbounded 800 unless noted.
- **VIS** = what is on screen.
- **[ED]** = a note for the video editor only.
- All reels: 1080×1920 · 30fps · captions burned · safe zone y 220-1500 · audio −14 LUFS.
