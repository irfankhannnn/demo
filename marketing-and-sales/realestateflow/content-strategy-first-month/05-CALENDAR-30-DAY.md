## Contingency rules

1. **If a reel breaks out (>25k views):** immediately produce a follow-up on the same pain within
   48 hours. Push the scheduled post back a day. Momentum beats the plan.
2. **If the editor slips:** promote a `[AI]` kinetic reel (format C) into the slot - you can produce
   one in under an hour. Never post nothing.
3. **If the product isn't demo-ready by Aug 26:** swap R10 and R12 for kinetic reels and move the
   demos to week 4. **Do not fake a demo.**
4. **Festival hooks:** Independence Day (Aug 15) is locked into R04. Ganesh Chaturthi falls in
   September and is a major Mumbai/Pune moment - **verify the exact 2026 date** and plan it as the
   month-2 opener.
