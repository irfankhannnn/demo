## CONTEXT — Yeh video kyun banaya, aur sources kya hain

Bahut saare Instagram/WhatsApp forwards keh rahe hain ki "2025 mein naya rent law aa gaya, deposit sirf 2 mahine ka legal hai" — ye baat **galat** hai. Asli kahani:

- **Model Tenancy Act, 2021** sirf ek *model* (sujhaav) tha — Centre ne states ko bheja tha adopt karne ke liye, lekin apne aap kisi state pe force nahi hota. [Source: PRS India Bill Track →](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-model-tenancy-act-2021)
- Cabinet ne isko sirf **circulation** ke liye approve kiya tha, states/UTs ko — ek advisory ki tarah, ek binding law ki tarah nahi. [Source: PM India press release →](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/cabinet-approves-model-tenancy-act-for-circulation-to-the-states-union-territories-for-adoption/)
- Ek dedicated fact-check bhi exist karta hai jo exactly yehi "naya rent agreement act 2025" wala claim baseless bata raha hai. [Source: LawStreet Journal fact-check →](https://lawstreet.co/executive/fact-check-centre-has-not-notified-any-new-rent-law-claims-of-a-new-rent-agreement-act-2025-baseless)
- Sirf **UP, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu aur Assam** ne is model ko apni state mein adopt kiya hai. **Maharashtra ne nahi.**

**Editor ke liye note:** in links ko khud check kar sakte ho agar shot list ya script confusing lage — but text mein koi change mat karo bina humse confirm kiye.
