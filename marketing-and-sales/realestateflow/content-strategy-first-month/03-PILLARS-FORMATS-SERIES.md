## 1. The six content pillars

| # | Pillar | Share of month | Job | Success signal |
|---|--------|----------------|-----|----------------|
| **1** | **CHAOS MIRROR** - pain reflection | **32%** (9 posts) | Reach. Make them feel seen. | Shares, "yeh mera hi haal hai" comments |
| **2** | **THE SYSTEM** - education / authority | **18%** (5 posts) | Credibility. We are operators. | Saves, follows |
| **3** | **AI EMPLOYEE** - product magic | **18%** (5 posts) | Differentiation. The wow. | Shares, DMs, "kaise?" comments |
| **4** | **KHATA & PAISA** - money | **14%** (4 posts) | Urgency. Money is the hottest nerve. | Comments, magnet claims |
| **5** | **BUILD IN PUBLIC** - pre-launch trust | **11%** (3 posts) | Trust substitute while we have no proof | Follows, replies |
| **6** | **BAZAAR CULTURE** - humour / relatability | **7%** (2 posts) | Shares, algorithm warmth, humanity | Shares, tags |

**Why CHAOS MIRROR is a third of the month:** at zero followers, reach is the constraint. Pain
recognition is the only thing that travels between brokers ("bhai yeh dekh"). Education gets saved
but not shared. Product gets ignored by strangers.

**Why BUILD IN PUBLIC exists:** we have no testimonials. Honest pre-launch narration -
*"we're building this, here's why, here's what's broken"* - is the only trust mechanism available to
us that isn't fabricated. Use it instead of faking proof.

## 2. The eight formats

| Code | Format | Length | Producer | Cost | Notes |
|------|--------|--------|----------|------|-------|
| **A** | **AI Presenter reel** - avatar talking head + b-roll + burned captions | 20-35s | `[AI]` gen → `[ED]` assemble | High | Our "face." See `04`. |
| **B** | **Screen-demo reel** - real product recording + VO + captions | 15-30s | `[REC]` → `[ED]` | Medium | Highest-trust format we have |
| **C** | **Kinetic-type reel** - text only, no face, HyperFrames | 8-15s | `[AI]` only | Very low | Volume filler. Surprisingly high save rate. |
| **D** | **WhatsApp chat reel** - animated chat thread | 15-25s | `[AI]` only | Low | The AI Employee's natural format |
| **E** | **Carousel** - 5 slides, fixed brand rhythm | - | `[AI]` only | Low | Saves engine |
| **F** | **Poster / static** - one idea, one number | - | `[AI]` only | Very low | Grid anchors |
| **G** | **Story set** - 3-5 frames, polls/questions | - | `[AI]` | Very low | Daily, off-calendar |
| **H** | **Culture / meme post** | - | `[AI]` | Very low | Shares |

### 2.1 Format specs

**Reels (A-D)** - 1080×1920, 30fps, H.264, audio −14 LUFS.
Hook must land **inside 1.5 seconds** - first frame carries text, not a logo, not a fade-in.
Burned-in captions always (they scroll on mute in public, on sound at night - cover both).
Loop-friendly: last frame should visually rhyme with the first.

**Carousel (E)** - 1080×1350 (4:5). Fixed 5-slide rhythm from the brand kit:

```
Slide 1  GULAL ground (#FF3D7F)   - the hook. Question or claim. Nothing else.
Slide 2  INK ground   (#1C1512)   - point 1, marigold bullet dot
Slide 3  INK ground   (#1C1512)   - point 2
Slide 4  INK ground   (#1C1512)   - point 3
Slide 5  MARIGOLD     (#FF7A1A)   - the CTA, ink text
```
Never mix marigold and gulal as accents on the same slide.

**Poster (F)** - 1080×1350. One ground colour, one idea, one accent. Readable at thumbnail size.

## 3. Brand application cheat-sheet

Pulled from `brand-kit.md` v3 so producers don't have to switch files.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|
| Ink | `#1C1512` | Default ground - warm near-black |
| Ink 2 | `#251C16` | Cards |
| Paper | `#FBF2E4` | Light ground, testimonial-style posts |
| **Marigold** | `#FF7A1A` | Primary accent, CTAs, prices, highlights |
| **Gulal** | `#FF3D7F` | Secondary pop - **once per screen** |
| Tulsi | `#1FAA59` | Checkmarks / verified **only** |
| Dust | `#C9BBA8` | Secondary text on ink |
| Error | `#EF4444` | Pain, loss, red numbers |

**Type:** Unbounded 800/900 for headlines and big numbers **only** (never a paragraph).
Manrope 400-800 for everything else. CTA button labels: Manrope 800.

**Per-screen colour ratio:** ~42% ink · 18% paper · 22% marigold · 14% gulal · 4% tulsi.

**Feed grid rule:** across any 9 tiles, no more than 3 share a ground colour. Alternate
ink / marigold / paper / gulal in a loose checkerboard so the feed reads as a set from arm's length.

**Gradient (hero headlines on dark):** `linear-gradient(100deg, #FF7A1A 15%, #FF3D7F 85%)`

## 4. The four recurring series

Recurring series build viewing habit and make production cheaper every week - the editor builds the
template once and reuses it. Each series has a fixed slot.

### ★ Series 1 - "WhatsApp Se Puchho" (Ask WhatsApp)
**The flagship.** Every Friday from week 4 onward. A real Hinglish query typed into the WhatsApp AI
Employee, screen-recorded, real answer.
Format B/D · Slot: Friday · Template: fixed cold open → type → 1s pause → answer reveal → CTA
*Long-term this is the series the account becomes known for. Protect it: never fake a result.*

### Series 2 - "₹ Leak of the Week"
One specific way agencies lose money, with a mechanism and a fix. Carousel.
Format E · Slot: Tuesday · Ends with `KHATA` or `AUDIT` keyword CTA

### Series 3 - "Broker Reality Check"
8-12 second kinetic-type truth bomb. No face, no product, no CTA. Pure shareable statement.
Format C · Slot: Wednesday · Zero editor cost - you generate these in minutes

### Series 4 - "Agency OS"
How to actually structure an agency: roles, SOPs, handoffs, what to write down.
Format E · Slot: Sunday · The authority pillar

## 6. Copy rules (apply to every asset)

1. **70% English / 30% romanized Hindi.** The Hindi carries the emotion; the English carries the
   information. Never write a full sentence in Devanagari - this audience reads romanized.
2. **Short sentences. Strong verbs.** If a line runs past 9 words on screen, cut it.
3. **Second person, always.** "Aapka employee," not "brokers often."
4. **One idea per asset.** If a reel has two points, it has zero.
5. **Numbers beat adjectives.** "₹15,000" beats "a lot of money."
6. **Never "AI-powered" without immediately showing what it does.**
7. **No motivational-guru register.** No "hustle," no "grind," no "10x your life." This audience is
   already exhausted; we are the calm operator, not the hype man.
8. **The villain is the situation, never the viewer.** Never make the agency owner feel stupid -
   make the *system he inherited* the stupid thing.

### Hinglish spelling standard (keep consistent across all assets)
`nahi` · `nahin` (emphatic) · `kyunki` · `hai` · `hain` · `aapka` · `aapki` · `kitne` · `kitni` ·
`dikhao` · `chahiye` · `paisa` · `jhagda` · `yaad` · `bhool` · `mahina` · `raat` · `subah`

Numbers in on-screen text: use digits with Indian formatting - `₹15,000`, `₹2.5 lakh`, `₹1.2 Cr`.
In voiceover scripts: **write numbers as words** so TTS reads them correctly (see `04` §5).
