## 1. The positioning problem, and the fix

### 1.1 The contradiction

The approved positioning documents say:

- `BRAND-POSITIONING.md` §3.1 - **Enemy: WhatsApp-based business operations**
- `BRAND-POSITIONING.md` §15 - **Differentiator: "WhatsApp replacement (not WhatsApp addon)"**
- `brand-kit.md` - **Primary hook: "Apna business WhatsApp se baahar nikalo."** (Get your business
  out of WhatsApp)

But the product's single most differentiated feature is an **AI employee that lives inside
WhatsApp.** The agency connects their own WhatsApp number by QR, and the AI reads messages, runs
real CRM operations, and replies - in Hinglish - inside WhatsApp.

So the flagship feature is literally a WhatsApp addon, and the headline tells people to leave
WhatsApp. On Instagram, where a reel has 1.5 seconds to make sense, this reads as a lie. Worse:
the moment you demo the AI Employee, an alert viewer notices the contradiction and trust drops.

### 1.2 Why the old frame is also strategically weak

"Leave WhatsApp" asks the customer for a **behaviour change** - and behaviour change is the exact
reason Indian brokers abandon every CRM they have ever bought. They buy it, log in twice, and go
back to WhatsApp because WhatsApp is where the client already is. Telling them to leave WhatsApp
puts us in the same graveyard as every CRM that failed before us.

### 1.3 The fix - same enemy, sharper target

**Do not change the enemy. Change what part of WhatsApp is the enemy.**

| | Old frame | **New frame (recommended)** |
|---|---|---|
| Enemy | WhatsApp, the app | **WhatsApp as your database, memory, ledger and filing cabinet** |
| Ask | Leave WhatsApp | **Keep WhatsApp. Put a system behind it.** |
| Behaviour change | Total | **Near zero** |
| Our AI Employee | Contradicts the message | **Is the proof of the message** |

### 1.4 The new lines

**Core reframe line (use everywhere):**
> ## "WhatsApp rahega. Chaos jayega."
> *(WhatsApp stays. The chaos goes.)*

**The month-1 thesis - the single idea this entire month exists to plant:**
> ## "Har CRM India mein fail hota hai - kyunki broker WhatsApp nahi chhodta.
> ## Toh humne CRM ko WhatsApp ke andar daal diya."
>
> *(Every CRM fails in India because the broker won't leave WhatsApp. So we put the CRM inside
> WhatsApp.)*

This is the strongest asset the company has and it is currently buried under a generic "we are a
CRM" message. Month 1 is built to make this idea land.

**Supporting lines (approved for use):**

- "WhatsApp aapka darwaza hai. Aapka daftar nahi." *(WhatsApp is your door. Not your office.)*
- "Aapka business WhatsApp mein nahi rehna chahiye. Lekin WhatsApp se chalna chahiye."
- "Ab CRM kholna nahi padega. CRM aapko WhatsApp pe milega."
- "Ek naya employee. Wahi WhatsApp. Zero training."

### 1.5 What survives unchanged

Everything else in the approved positioning stays exactly as written:

- Category: **Real Estate Operating System** (not "a CRM")
- Pillars: **Control · Visibility · Risk Reduction · Growth Enablement**
- Structure: **Problem → Agitation → Solution → Outcome**
- Tone: direct, practical, confident, 70% English / 30% romanized Hindi
- Core belief: *"A real estate business without a system is a risky business."*
- Banned: technical jargon, generic CRM language, income claims, legal guarantees

### 1.6 What must be retired immediately

| Retire | Reason |
|--------|--------|
| "Apna business WhatsApp se baahar nikalo" as the **primary** hook | Contradicts the hero feature. Demote to a *secondary* line used only about data/files, never about the app itself. |
| "WhatsApp replacement, not WhatsApp addon" | Factually wrong about our own product. |
| Trust bar: **"200+ Agencies · Mumbai · Delhi · Pune · Dubai"** | We are pre-launch with zero customers. **Delete from every template.** |

## 2. Month-1 objective

This month is **not** a sales month. We have no customers, no proof, and no reviews. Trying to sell
would produce a handful of low-intent leads and a dead account.

**The job of month 1 is to become the account that Indian agency owners recognise as "the one that
gets it," and to convert that recognition into a named waitlist.**

### 2.1 Targets (realistic for a cold account, no ad spend)

| Metric | 30-day target | Why this number |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| Followers | **1,200 - 2,500** | Achievable cold with 28 posts if 2-3 reels break out |
| Median reel views | **3,000 - 8,000** | Niche B2B; a 40k+ outlier is the goal, not the plan |
| Saves per carousel | **40+** | Saves are the truest signal for B2B utility content |
| Comments/post | **15+** | Driven by the keyword-comment mechanic (§4) |
| **Waitlist signups** | **150 - 300** | ★ **The real KPI.** Everything else is vanity. |
| Qualified demo conversations | **20 - 40** | Month-2 revenue pipeline |
| Testimonial-ready pilot agencies | **5 - 10** | Unlocks month-2 proof content |

### 2.2 Anti-goals

- Do **not** chase generic real-estate audiences. 10,000 home-buyer followers is worth less than
  400 agency owners.
- Do **not** post listings. We are not a broker. See §3.2.
- Do **not** buy followers or engagement pods. It destroys the reach we need.

## 3. Audience & channel posture

### 3.1 Who we are talking to

Straight from the approved ICP, sharpened for social:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Owner / principal of a real-estate agency. The person whose money it is. |
| **Not** | Salaried agents, solo beginners, enterprise developers |
| **Team size** | 2-15 |
| **Geography** | Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Pune (month 1 focus). Then Bengaluru, Delhi NCR. |
| **Age** | 28-45 |
| **Volume** | 30-200 listings, 50-500 clients |
| **Device** | Android phone, one hand, at night, sound often ON (unlike B2C - they listen) |
| **Language** | Speaks Hinglish. Reads English. Feels in Hindi. |
| **Buying trigger** | The moment they lose money or face to a mistake they can't explain |

### 3.2 The critical channel insight

**Our audience is itself an Instagram creator.** Indian brokers post listings, walkthroughs and
"just closed" posts all day. So:

> If our account looks like a broker's account, we look like a competitor and they will not follow.

Our account must look like **the operator's account** - the one person on their feed talking about
the *business* of broking rather than about property. Dark ink ground, big Unbounded type, numbers,
systems, blunt truths. Visually the opposite of the glossy property carousel.

This is also why the brand's dark "Bazaar Signal" palette is a genuine competitive advantage on
this feed: every other real-estate account in India is white, blue, and full of building photos.

## 4. Funnel & CTA architecture (pre-launch)

We have no product proof, so the funnel converts on **specificity and scarcity**, not on social
proof.

```
Reel / carousel (pain recognition)
   ↓  they comment a keyword
Auto-DM with the lead magnet
   ↓  they receive real, useful value for free
Soft ask: "Want the founding-50 slot?"
   ↓
WhatsApp conversation with a human (you)
   ↓
Waitlist / pilot onboarding
```

### 4.1 The keyword-comment mechanic

Every CTA asks for a **comment**, not a link click. Comments feed the algorithm and open a DM
window. This also demos our own category - an automated chat reply - which is thematically perfect.

| Keyword | Delivers | Used in |
|---------|----------|---------|
| **`SYSTEM`** | Founding-50 waitlist link | Hero + closing posts |
| **`KHATA`** | Free Khata Book tracker template (Google Sheet) | Money pillar posts |
| **`AUDIT`** | "Agency Leakage Audit" - 12-question scorecard PDF | Pain/cost posts |
| **`DEMO`** | 90-second product walkthrough video + booking link | Product pillar posts |

### 4.2 The three lead magnets (build these in prep week)

**1. Agency Leakage Audit (PDF, 2 pages)** - 12 yes/no questions, scored out of 100.
*"Can you name, right now, every person who owes your agency money?"* Ends with a band:
0-40 "Aapki agency memory pe chal rahi hai" / 41-70 / 71-100. Ends with the founding-50 offer.
This is the highest-converting magnet because it makes them **self-diagnose** - we never have to
claim a loss number ourselves. (See `14` §2 for why that matters.)

**2. Khata Book Tracker (Google Sheet)** - a free manual version of our Khata Book: To Give /
To Take, party, property, category, due date, settled Y/N. Deliberately good enough to be useful
and manual enough that maintaining it hurts. The product is the upgrade. This is the strongest
magnet we have - give away the workflow, sell the automation.

**3. Follow-Up SOP (1 page)** - the follow-up cadence a 5-person agency should run. Positions us as
operators, costs nothing, and seeds the pipeline conversation.

### 4.3 The offer (must be stated identically everywhere)

- **CRM: 2 months free. No credit card.**
- **6-month money-back guarantee. No questions.**
- **WhatsApp AI Employee: optional add-on, +₹5,000/month, NO free trial - paid from day one.**
- **Founding 50:** first 50 agencies get the 2 free months **plus** founding pricing locked for
  12 months.

> **Honesty rule:** the AI Employee's "no free trial, ₹5,000/month" line must appear on any creative
> that demos the AI Employee. Do not let the free-CRM-trial message imply the AI is free. If you
> commit to "Founding 50," you must honour all 50.

## 5. The 4-week narrative arc

The month is one story, not 28 unrelated posts. Each week has one job and hands off to the next.

| Week | Dates | Name | Job | Product shown? | Emotional target |
|------|-------|------|-----|----------------|------------------|
| **1** | Aug 10-16 | **THE MIRROR** | Reflect their daily chaos so precisely they feel watched | **None** | *"Yeh toh mere baare mein hai."* |
| **2** | Aug 17-23 | **THE COST** | Attach a rupee number to the chaos - their number, self-calculated | None (magnets only) | *"Ruko… main kitna kho raha hoon?"* |
| **3** | Aug 24-30 | **THE SYSTEM** | Reveal the category answer + first real product demos | Yes - CRM | *"Achha, aisa bhi ho sakta hai."* |
| **4** | Aug 31-Sep 6 | **THE AI EMPLOYEE** | Detonate the differentiator + drive the waitlist | Yes - AI Employee | *"Yeh maine pehle nahi dekha. Mujhe chahiye."* |

**Why product is withheld until week 3:** a cold audience that has not yet admitted it has a problem
will scroll past a product demo. Two full weeks of pain-mirroring buys the right to sell. It also
means our two strongest assets - the CRM demo and the AI Employee reveal - land on an audience that
is already 2,000 people deep instead of on an empty account.

**The two hero posts of the month:**
- **R09 (Mon 24 Aug)** - the thesis reel: *"Har CRM fail hota hai kyunki broker WhatsApp nahi
  chhodta."*
- **R14 (Mon 31 Aug)** - the AI Employee reveal.

Both are `[ED]` builds. Give the editor extra time on these two.
