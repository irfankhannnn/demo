## 1. Caption architecture

Every caption follows the same four-part shape. Instagram truncates at roughly 125 characters, so
the first line has to work alone.

```
LINE 1   The hook, restated. Must stand alone before "...more".
         Never start with "In this reel..." or "Are you a broker who..."

BODY     3-5 short paragraphs. One idea each. Blank line between every one.
         Hinglish, same voice as the reel. Not a transcript - an extension.

TURN     The reframe or the useful takeaway. This is what earns the save.

CTA      One ask. Never two.
         👇 emoji + the keyword in caps.
```

**Caption rules**
- Line length short. Broken into single-thought paragraphs. Nobody reads a block on a phone.
- Emoji: maximum 2 per caption. `👇` for the CTA, `🧡` after the brand line. Nothing else.
- Never repeat the reel's VO verbatim - add something the video didn't say.
- **No hashtags in the caption.** First comment only (see §2).
- Every caption that mentions the AI Employee must carry the pricing disclosure.

## 2. Hashtags

**Post them in the first comment, immediately after publishing.** Keeps the caption clean and lets
you edit the set without touching the post.

### Why not #realestate

`#realestate` has hundreds of millions of posts, almost all US agents marketing to home buyers. We
would be invisible and the audience would be wrong. Every tag below is chosen to reach **Indian
brokers and agency owners**, not property buyers.

### Set A - Core (use on every post, 8 tags)
```
#realestateindia #propertydealer #realestateagentindia #brokerlife
#realestatebusiness #propertyconsultant #realestateagent #realtorindia
```

### Set B - Geography (rotate 3, match the post)
```
#mumbairealestate #punerealestate #thanerealestate #navimumbai
#mumbaiproperty #puneproperty #realestatemumbai #pcmc
```

### Set C - Category / product (use 3 on System, AI Employee and Khata posts)
```
#realestatecrm #proptech #proptechindia #realestatetech
#crmsoftware #realestatetools #whatsappbusiness #businessautomation
```

### Set D - Business/owner (use 2-3 on authority and Build-in-Public posts)
```
#smallbusinessindia #businessowner #indianbusiness #entrepreneurindia
#salestips #followup #businessgrowth
```

### The formula
**8 core + 3 geo + 3 category/business = 14 hashtags.** Do not exceed 15.

### Per-pillar sets

## 3. Stories - daily, off-calendar

Stories keep the audience you already have warm while reels do the acquiring. **3-5 frames every
single day**, including days with no feed post.

### Format
1080×1920 · Ink `#1C1512` ground · Unbounded 800 for headlines, Manrope for body · Marigold accents.
Text in the middle third only - Instagram's UI eats the top and bottom.

### The weekly pattern

| Day | Frames | Content |
|-----|--------|---------|
| **Mon** | 3 | (1) Re-share today's reel to Story (2) Poll: *"Aaj kitne WhatsApp messages ka reply diya? 50 se kam / 50+"* (3) Result reaction next morning |
| **Tue** | 3 | (1) Today's carousel (2) *This or That*: two agency habits, tap to choose (3) A one-line takeaway |
| **Wed** | 4 | (1) Reality Check reel (2) **Question box: "Aapki agency ka sabse bada leak kya hai?"** (3-4) Answer 2 responses on screen |
| **Thu** | 3 | (1) Today's reel (2) **Behind the build** - a real screenshot of something shipped this week (3) One line about why it matters |
| **Fri** | 4 | (1) Today's post (2) Best comment of the week, screenshot, replied to (3) Reminder of the active keyword CTA (4) Weekend question |
| **Sat** | 3 | (1) Today's poster (2) Quiz: *"Guess: ek average agency mahine mein kitne leads bina teesre follow-up ke chhod deti hai?"* (3) Reveal - framed as "aap khud nikaalo" |
| **Sun** | 4 | (1) Week recap - 4 posts as a montage (2) The one idea of the week (3) The CTA (4) Ask: *"Agle hafte kis cheez pe baat karein?"* |

### Story rules
- **Every story set ends with an interaction sticker** - poll, question, quiz or slider. Story
  replies are the single fastest way to open a DM thread with a real agency owner.
- **Reply to every story response.** In month 1 this is where the pipeline actually comes from.
- Screenshot good question-box answers and turn them into feed posts. Free content, and the person
  who wrote it becomes an advocate.
- Add the keyword CTA to at least one frame per day.

### Highlights (set these up in prep week)
`Kya hai?` (what the product is) · `AI Employee` · `Khata` · `Founding 50` · `Reviews` *(empty for
now - fill in month 2, do not fake it)*

## 4. Facebook

### Why Facebook matters here more than usual

For Indian real estate brokers, **Facebook Groups are a genuinely active professional channel** -
property dealer groups, city broker networks, rental listing groups. This audience skews older and
more Facebook-native than a typical B2B SaaS audience. Do not treat FB as an afterthought.

### 4.1 Cross-posting rules

Post the **same creative**, but never the same caption.

| | Instagram | Facebook |
|---|---|---|
| Caption length | Short, punchy, truncates at 125 chars | **Longer.** FB rewards a fuller story. Expand the caption by 2-3 paragraphs. |
| Links | Not clickable - use keyword CTAs | **Clickable.** Put the waitlist link directly in the post. |
| CTA | "Comment SYSTEM" | "Comment SYSTEM **ya link pe click karo →**" both work |
| Hashtags | 14, first comment | **3-5 maximum, in the caption.** FB hashtags barely function; more looks spammy. |
| Format | Reels-first | Reels + also post the carousel as a **native photo album** |
| Timing | 9:30 PM IST | **8:00 PM IST** - FB peak is slightly earlier |

### 4.2 Facebook-specific caption pattern

```
[Hook line - same as IG]

[2-3 paragraphs, expanded. On FB you can be more explicit and more direct
 than on IG. Say the thing plainly.]

[The takeaway]

👉 [Direct link to the waitlist]
Ya comment karo "SYSTEM" - main DM kar doonga.

#realestateindia #propertydealer #mumbairealestate
```

### 4.3 Group strategy - the real FB channel

The Page is not the point. The Groups are.

**Week 1 - join and observe.** Find 10-15 active Indian real estate broker groups (search:
"property dealers Mumbai", "real estate brokers India", "Pune property network"). Join. **Post
nothing.** Read what people complain about - it is free research for month 2's content.

**Week 2 - participate, don't promote.** Answer questions genuinely. No links, no product mentions.
Build recognition of the profile name.

**Week 3-4 - share value, sparingly.** Post the *free* assets only - the Khata template, the
Leakage Audit, the commission-rules carousel. Frame as *"maine ye banaya hai, free hai"*, not as a
product pitch.

**Rules:**
- **Read each group's rules before posting.** Most ban promotion outright. Getting banned from a
  group of 20,000 brokers is an expensive mistake for one post.
- Never drop a link cold as your first contribution.
- Never post the same message into multiple groups on the same day - it flags as spam.
- Post from a real personal profile, not the Page. Groups distrust Pages.

### 4.4 What not to bother with on FB
- Stories - negligible reach for this audience
- FB-only original content - cross-post and adapt, don't create twice
- Boosting posts - that's the paid plan, out of scope here

## 5. Comment & DM playbook

At zero followers, **the reply is the product.** This section matters more than any single post.

### 5.1 Response SLA
- Comments: **within 60 minutes** of posting, then twice daily
- DMs: within 2 hours during 9 AM-11 PM IST
- Story replies: within 1 hour

### 5.2 The keyword flow

When someone comments a keyword:

1. **Reply publicly** to the comment: *"DM check karo 👍"* - this makes the mechanic visible to
   everyone else scrolling the comments, which drives more comments.
2. **Send the DM within 15 minutes.**
3. Deliver the asset **first**, with no ask attached.
4. **Wait for a reply.** Only then ask a question.

### 5.3 DM templates

**On `KHATA`:**
> Ye lo - Khata Book tracker template: [link]
>
> To Give / To Take, party wise, property wise. Bas copy karke apna bana lo.
>
> Koi catch nahi hai. Use karke batana kaisa laga.

*(Then wait. If they reply:)*
> Ek cheez pooch sakta hoon - abhi aap ye track kaise karte ho? Diary, Excel, ya bas yaad rakhte ho?

**On `AUDIT`:**
> Ye raha Agency Leakage Audit - 12 sawaal, 2 minute lagenge: [link]
>
> Apna score nikaal ke batana. Main aapko batata hoon ki us score pe zyadatar agencies kya karti
> hain.

**On `DEMO`:**
> Ye 90-second walkthrough hai: [link]
>
> Agar aur detail mein dekhna ho toh bata dena - 15 minute mein aapki agency ke hisaab se dikha
> doonga. Koi pitch nahi, sirf screen share.

**On `SYSTEM`:**
> Founding 50 ke liye ye link hai: [link]
>
> Short version: 2 mahine free CRM, no credit card. 6-month money-back. Aur founding price 12 mahine
> lock.
>
> (WhatsApp AI Employee alag optional add-on hai - ₹5,000/month, uska free trial nahi hai. Bata raha
> hoon taaki baad mein surprise na ho.)
>
> Aapki team mein kitne log hain?

### 5.4 Comment reply rules
- **Reply to every comment.** Even "🔥". Reply with a word, but reply.
- Disagreement is good - engage with it seriously, never defensively. R08 is designed to provoke it.
- If someone shares their own war story in the comments, **ask permission to turn it into a post.**
  That is how month 2's content gets written, and how the first real testimonials appear.
- Never argue with a competitor. Never mention a competitor by name.

### 5.5 Honesty in DMs
Pre-launch, the temptation in a DM is to imply more traction than exists. Don't.

- ✅ *"Hum abhi launch nahi hue - aap early access mein aayenge"*
- ❌ *"Bahut saari agencies already use kar rahi hain"*

If someone directly asks how many customers you have, tell them the truth. Pre-launch honesty is a
sales asset with this audience - they have been oversold by every CRM company already.
