## 💡 Pro Tips for Best Results

### ✅ DO:
- ✅ Be specific about dimensions (1080x1080, 1920x1080, etc.)
- ✅ Include brand colors in hex format
- ✅ Specify format (PNG, MP4, HTML, PPTX)
- ✅ Reference BRAND-POSITIONING.md messaging
- ✅ Include Hinglish copy examples
- ✅ Specify frame rate for videos (25fps or 60fps)
- ✅ Ask for iterations and refinements
- ✅ Request dark mode/light mode variants
- ✅ Include expected completion time in brief

### ❌ DON'T:
- ❌ Use vague briefs ("make it look good")
- ❌ Ask for generic designs without context
- ❌ Skip specifying dimensions/format
- ❌ Forget to include brand colors
- ❌ Use technical jargon (CRM, API, database)
- ❌ Request multiple outputs simultaneously (do one at a time)
- ❌ Forget mobile responsiveness requirement
- ❌ Skip context about target audience
