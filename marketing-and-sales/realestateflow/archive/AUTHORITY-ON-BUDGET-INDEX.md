## ❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: Can I change the colors?**
A: Not recommended. The Navy/Blue/Green palette is carefully chosen for trust and professionalism. If you must change, follow the brand guidelines.

**Q: What if I need a new component?**
A: Build it using the existing classes in brand-styles-authority.css as a template. Follow the spacing and color rules.

**Q: How do I update the brand?**
A: Any changes require review of brand guidelines. Create a v1.1 with updated date. Never change primary colors without approval.

**Q: Can I add other colors?**
A: No. Stick to Navy, Blue, Green, and neutrals only. This maintains consistency.

**Q: What if my logo doesn't match?**
A: Logo should be Navy #0F3A66. If different, update it to match the brand.

**Q: How often should I post on Instagram?**
A: 2-3 times per week minimum. Use the post templates provided.

**Q: When should I update the brand?**
A: Next major review in August 2026 (v1.1). Minor fixes anytime.
