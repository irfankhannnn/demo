## 3. TYPOGRAPHY

### Font Family
- **Headlines (H1-H3):** Poppins (sans-serif)
  - Weight: 700-800 (Bold to Extra Bold)
  - Letter-spacing: Normal
  - Line-height: 1.2-1.3
  
- **Body Text:** Inter (sans-serif)
  - Weight: 400-500
  - Letter-spacing: Normal
  - Line-height: 1.5-1.6

- **CTA/Labels:** Poppins
  - Weight: 600 (Semi-Bold)
  - Text-transform: Uppercase (optional)
  - Letter-spacing: 0.5-1px

### Typography Hierarchy

| Element | Font | Size | Weight | Line-Height | Color |

| **H1** | Poppins | 40px | 800 | 1.2 | #111827 |

## 4. DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### Principle 1: 60% Whitespace Minimum
- Every design should have significant breathing room
- Don't crowd elements
- Use whitespace strategically for focus
- Signals: Premium, uncluttered, professional

### Principle 2: Minimalist Approach
- Remove unnecessary elements
- Only include what serves a purpose
- Clean lines, minimal borders
- Focus on clarity over decoration

### Principle 3: Trust-First Design
- RERA license badge always visible (header or prominent)
- Years of experience displayed
- Real testimonials with names
- Direct contact information
- Transparent process documentation

### Principle 4: Direct Communication
- Short, clear headlines
- No marketing jargon
- Action-oriented language
- Focus on outcomes, not features
- Hinglish for cultural resonance

**Standard Card**
- Background: White

- Border-radius: 8px
- Padding: 20px
- Box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)

### Forms

**Input Field**
- Border: 1px solid #E2E8F0
- Border-radius: 4px
- Padding: 10px 12px

### Trust Signals (Critical)

**RERA Badge**
- Background: #0F3A66
- Text: White
- Placement: Header or top-left (always visible)
- Format: "RERA #[License Number]"
- Importance: NON-NEGOTIABLE

**Establishment Badge**
- Format: "Est. [Year]"
- Placement: Near logo or header
- Conveyed: Experience, longevity, stability

**Testimonial Card**
- Include: Client name, quote, rating
- Placement: Multiple on page
- Format: Real names preferred (no anonymous)

## 6. LAYOUTS & SPACING

### Spacing Scale
- **4px:** Micro spacing (icon gaps, small padding)
- **8px:** Element spacing (button padding)
- **12px:** Component spacing (card padding)
- **16px:** Section padding, standard gaps
- **24px:** Large gaps, component separation
- **32px:** Major section separation
- **48px:** Page-level separation

### Layout Rules
- Max-width for content: 1200px
- Left/right padding on mobile: 20px
- Column gap: 24px
- Row gap: 20px

## 7. MESSAGING & TONE

### Tone of Voice
- **Direct:** Say what you mean, no fluff
- **Transparent:** Honest about process and pricing
- **Expert:** Show knowledge and experience
- **Approachable:** Not corporate, not casual

### Messaging Framework: P-A-S-O
1. **Problem:** Solo brokers lose credibility competing with big agencies
2. **Agitation:** Prospects don't trust inexperienced-looking branding
3. **Solution:** Professional design that's affordable and easy to implement
4. **Outcome:** Solo agent = established professional

### Key Messages
- "Direct broker. Trustworthy. Established."
- "20+ years of expertise. Personal attention. No middlemen."
- "Professional branding for solo agents."
- "Transparent process. Proven results."

### Hinglish Messaging Examples
- "Direct broker से बात करो। Sab kuch transparent।"
- "Apke dream property। Personal service। Expert guidance।"
- "RERA certified। 20+ years experience। Trust guaranteed।"

## 8. VISUAL STYLE

### Photography Style
- Real-life broker photos (not stock)
- Professional headshots
- Office environment shots
- Client testimonial photos
- Property showcase images

### Imagery Rules
- Always show real people (trust signal)
- High quality but authentic (not overly styled)
- Include RERA badge in broker photos
- Show client satisfaction through real testimonials

### Icon Usage
- Minimal icons
- Only used to clarify text
- Navy or Blue color
- Simple, clean style
- Avoid decoration icons

### Patterns & Graphics
- Avoid patterns (minimalist principle)
- Solid colors only
- Gradient accents (optional): Navy to Blue
- No textures or complex backgrounds

## 9. WEBSITE SPECIFICATIONS

### Homepage Structure
1. Navigation (logo + links + CTA button)
2. Hero Section (headline + subheadline + CTA + image)
3. Trust Signals Section (RERA, years, testimonials)
4. Properties Section (showcase)
5. About/Expertise Section
6. Testimonials (with names)
7. CTA Section
8. Footer

### Key Pages
- **Homepage:** Hero + Trust signals + CTA
- **Properties:** List/grid view with details
- **About:** Experience, RERA license, team (if applicable)
- **Contact:** Phone + WhatsApp + Email + Form
- **Testimonials:** Full page with client names/photos

### Mobile-First Approach
- Minimum font-size: 13px
- Touch-friendly buttons: 40px+ height
- Full-width layouts on mobile
- Vertical stacking of content
- Large tap targets

- Consistent posting times
- Engagement-focused captions

## 17. COMPLIANCE & LEGALS

### RERA Compliance
- RERA license number must be visible
- License number must link to official registry (if possible)
- Include "RERA certified" in appropriate places
- Never make false claims about certifications

### Testimonials
- Include full client name (if permission given)
- Include photo if possible
- Get written permission before publishing
- Be truthful in quotes

### Contact Information
- Phone number must be real and monitored
- WhatsApp must be accessible
- Email must be checked regularly
- Response time target: <2 hours
