**CTA:** "Swipe to see solution"

## 🎨 Design Consistency Across All Posts

### Colors Used:
- **Primary:** #0F172A (Dark Slate) - Professional, authoritative
- **Blue:** #2563EB (Brand Blue) - Trust, action, features
- **Accent:** #22C55E (Growth Green) - Success, growth, positivity
- **Red:** #EF4444 - Urgency, pain, problems
- **Backgrounds:** #FFFFFF, #F8FAFC, Dark gradients

### Typography:
- **Headings:** Poppins (800 weight) - Bold, confident
- **Body:** Inter (400-600 weight) - Readable, professional
- **Sizes:** 36-48px headlines, 13-16px body text

### Layout Principles:
- All posts are 1080x1080px (Instagram optimal)
- Vertical centering for mobile viewing
- Clear visual hierarchy (headline → visual → CTA)
- RealtyFlow logo subtle (top right, low opacity)
