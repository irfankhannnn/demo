# Day 17 — Initiate Cold Outreach: List 50 + Templates Ready

## Objective
Compile a list of 50 high-fit Indian real estate businesses (separate from Day 8 beta prospects), prepare 3 channel templates (WhatsApp, LinkedIn, email), and set up the multi-touch sequence for Day 18-19 execution.

## Why This Matters for RealtyFlow
Direct outbound is the fastest, most reliable way to land your first 5 paying customers. Inbound (directories, press, content) takes 3+ months to compound. Outbound delivers replies in 24-48 hours. 50 prospects × 10% reply rate = 5 conversations × 30% conversion = 1-2 paying customers from Week 3 outbound alone. Compound that with Week 4 follow-ups and you hit your Month 1 paid customer target.

## User Story
As a founder, I want a list of 50 cold outreach prospects (real estate agencies in target cities, NOT beta testers), with personalization research done for each, and a 3-touch sequence prepared across WhatsApp/LinkedIn/email, so that Day 18-19 execution is rapid and high-quality, producing 5+ booked discovery calls by Day 21.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] 50 prospects in spreadsheet — completely separate from Day-8 beta list
- [ ] Each prospect: name, agency, city, role, channel preference, hook
- [ ] Mix of agencies: 30% solo agents, 50% small teams (2-5), 20% larger (6-15)
- [ ] Geographic spread: Mumbai (20), Bangalore (15), Pune (8), Delhi NCR (5), Hyderabad (2)
- [ ] All prospects have valid contact info (verified)
- [ ] 3 message templates drafted: WhatsApp, LinkedIn DM, email
- [ ] Each template has 2-3 personalization variables
- [ ] Sequence designed: Touch 1 (Day 18-19) → Touch 2 (Day 23 follow-up) → Touch 3 (Day 26 final)
- [ ] Cold outreach tool (Instantly/Smartlead) loaded with email list
- [ ] WhatsApp Business broadcast lists prepared (NOT broadcast — manual send)
- [ ] Calendly demo slots open and visible (Tue-Fri 11am-5pm IST)
- [ ] Day 18 outreach plan: 25 prospects, allocated across channels and time slots

## Implementation Steps

### Step 1: Source 50 new prospects (do NOT reuse Day 8 beta list)
Channels — same as Day 8 but fresh names:
- **LinkedIn search** (20 prospects): "Real Estate Agent / Broker" + city filter
- **99acres / MagicBricks / Housing.com directories** (15 prospects): Top-rated agents by city
- **JustDial / Sulekha** (5 prospects): Indian small-business directories
- **Local Facebook real estate groups** (5 prospects): Active posters
- **Quora / Reddit answerers** (5 prospects): People posting about real estate India

### Step 2: Build the spreadsheet
Same columns as Day 8 beta list, plus:
- Touch 1 sent (date + channel)
- Touch 2 sent
- Touch 3 sent
- Response (positive/neutral/negative/none)
- Demo booked (Y/N + slot)
- Outcome (trial signup / paid / declined / silent)

### Step 3: Research personalization hooks
Lower-effort than Day 8 (volume is higher) but still concrete. For each:
- Glance at LinkedIn — note recent post if any
- Check their 99acres listings — note focus area
- Pick ONE specific thing per prospect

Examples:
- "Saw your 5 Andheri listings on 99acres — looks like you focus on resale"
- "Noticed your LinkedIn post about agent commission challenges"
- "Saw your team handles Whitefield + Sarjapur — that's a lot of inventory to track"

If you can't find a hook within 90 seconds → still send, but with a city/specialty-based hook ("Saw your agency focuses on Bangalore residential — wanted to share something built for that").

### Step 7: Multi-touch sequence
Touch 1 (Day 18-19) — initial outreach
Touch 2 (Day 23) — follow-up to non-responders ("Did this get buried?")
Touch 3 (Day 26) — final value-add ("Closing this thread — wanted to share one resource: [link to relevant blog/case study]")

After Touch 3 with no reply → archive. Don't beg.

### Step 8: Tool prep
**For email (Instantly / Smartlead):**
- Upload prospect list
- Set up sequence: Email 1 → wait 5 days → Email 2 (follow-up) → wait 3 days → Email 3 (final)
- Use rotating inboxes (from `pre-launch-prep/03`)
- Daily send cap: 30-40/inbox
- Open tracking ON, click tracking OFF (for deliverability)

**For WhatsApp:**
- WhatsApp Business app on phone
- Quick replies saved (3 message templates)
- DO NOT broadcast — send manually one by one (WhatsApp flags broadcasts to non-contacts)
- Save each prospect as contact BEFORE messaging

**For LinkedIn:**
- Manual sending only (LinkedIn flags automation tools)
- Use Sales Navigator if you have it (better targeting)
- Daily cap: 15-20 DMs

Calendly slots prepared:

## Risks & Mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| 50 prospects too few — Week 3 conversion target needs more | Add 20 more Tier 2 prospects for Day 19 evening if Day 18 mornings convert well |
| Templates feel generic | Force personalization hook in first 2 sentences; reject template feel-test |
| Email deliverability low — Day 17 surprise | Run Mail-Tester one more time today. If <8/10, halt cold sends until fixed. |
| WhatsApp ban risk (mass messaging) | Max 30/day; always save contact first; never use broadcast |
| LinkedIn account flagged | Max 15-20 DMs/day; connection requests separate count (~30/day OK) |
| You feel awkward about cold outreach | Reframe: "I'm offering free 3-month CRM to people losing leads. They'll thank me." It's offer-driven, not spammy. |

## India-Specific Notes
- WhatsApp is the highest-reply channel for Indian B2B (60%+ open rate vs 30% email)
- Hinglish messages outperform pure English for tier-2/3 city agents
- Indian agents prefer phone calls > video > text for trust-building. Offer WhatsApp call as alternative to Zoom.
- Diwali / festival weeks: low response. Plan timing if Day 18-19 overlaps.
- Mumbai agents respond faster than Bangalore (general pattern). Hyderabad slowest. Calibrate expectations per city.
- Saturday morning WhatsApp = high response rate (agents on phones during weekend showings)
