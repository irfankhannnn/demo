# Day 16 — Launch on Software Directories + Indian Real Estate Press

## Objective
Submit RealtyFlow to 10+ directories — Product Hunt, BetaList, Indian-specific listings, and real estate trade press — to generate the first wave of organic discovery, social proof backlinks, and inbound signups for Week 3 traffic.

## Why This Matters for RealtyFlow
Directories give you free distribution, dofollow backlinks for SEO, social proof ("seen on Product Hunt"), and the chance of going viral on a launch day. Indian real estate trade press (ET Realty, PropTiger Insights, MagicBricks blog) reaches your exact ICP — way more targeted than Product Hunt. Even if no single directory delivers a deluge, the aggregate of 10 listings produces ~50-100 quality visits across Week 3.

## User Story
As a founder, I want to submit RealtyFlow to 10+ launch directories and pitch 3-5 Indian real estate publications, with a transparent launch post explaining why I built RealtyFlow and what specific problem it solves, so that Week 3 receives 50-100 organic visitors and the brand gets discoverable in directories + press searches.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Product Hunt launch submitted (or scheduled for upcoming Tuesday — best PH day)
- [ ] BetaList submission live
- [ ] Indie Hackers post published
- [ ] 5+ Indian/global SaaS directories submitted (G2, Capterra, SaaSHub, AlternativeTo, SoftwareSuggest, etc.)
- [ ] 2+ Indian PropTech directories (PropTiger blog inquiries, MagicBricks Insights)
- [ ] 3-5 Indian real estate publications pitched (ET Realty, Inman India, MagicBricks Insights, 99acres trade desk, IRESC member list)
- [ ] Each submission has unique-ish copy (not pure copy-paste)
- [ ] All submissions link to `realtyflow.in` with UTM parameters for tracking
- [ ] Launch announcement post on LinkedIn + X
- [ ] Internal launch tracker maintained (submission, status, response)
- [ ] At least 1 directory featured listing OR press response by EOD

## Implementation Steps

### Step 1: Decide on Product Hunt timing
Product Hunt launches start at 12:01am PT (1:31pm IST). Best days:
- **Tuesday/Wednesday** — highest community engagement
- Avoid: Friday-Sunday (lower traffic)
- Avoid: major holiday launches (US holidays especially)

If you can pick the day, schedule for next Tuesday. Otherwise launch today.

### Step 2: Prepare Product Hunt assets
- **Logo:** square, 240×240px, transparent PNG
- **Gallery:** 5-7 screenshots (1270×760px) — dashboard, AI call, WhatsApp, pricing, mobile
- **Tagline:** ≤60 chars: "AI-powered CRM for Indian real estate agents"
- **Description:** ~250 chars + bullet points of features
- **Maker comment** (you posting first comment as creator):
  > "Hey PH! Kalim here. I built RealtyFlow because my cousin in Mumbai kept losing deals to dropped WhatsApp follow-ups. Indian real estate has 80M+ transactions/year but no CRM built for the way Indian agents actually work (WhatsApp-first, Hindi/Hinglish calls, ₹999 pricing). Spent 18 months building this. We're 2 weeks into beta with 10 agents — would love your feedback. Honest critique > polite praise. AMA below!"

Track which directory drives signups. Add UTMs to every directory URL:

Check PostHog / GA4 by UTM source on Day 21 to see what actually drove traffic.

## Risks & Mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Product Hunt launch flops (0-10 upvotes) | Don't depend on PH alone — 10 directories spread risk |
| Press doesn't reply | Follow up Day 18. Indian press is slower than US. |
| Directory listings rejected for incomplete info | Pre-check requirements before submitting. Most accept on resubmission. |
| Spike of low-quality signups overwhelms support | Auto-trial limits (per `pre-launch-prep/01`). Day 5 helpdesk handles volume. |
| LinkedIn launch post underperforms | Tag testers + ask them to share — multiplies reach 3-5x |
| Negative PH comment ("just another CRM") | Reply genuinely, don't argue. Often turns into respectful conversation. |

## India-Specific Notes
- Product Hunt skews US-heavy. Don't expect viral Indian signups from PH alone.
- Indian directories (SoftwareSuggest, ET Realty) deliver smaller volume but better fit
- LinkedIn India has very active SaaS founder community — your launch post can travel within builder circles
- WhatsApp groups (Indian SaaS founders, Indian real estate brokers) share launches — get into 3-5 by Day 16
- Press cycle: pitch Tue-Thu morning, follow up Thu/Fri, get coverage Mon/Tue next week
