# Day 18 — Execute Cold Outreach Batch 1 (25 Prospects)

## Objective
Send the first 25 personalized outreach messages across WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and email — handle replies in real time, book demos as positive responses come in, and track every interaction for Day 23 follow-ups.

## Why This Matters for RealtyFlow
Day 18 is the moment ideas meet reality. 25 cold messages will produce signal: how strong is your wedge, your message, your offer? Expect 2-5 replies on Day 18, 5-8 over the next 3 days. Each conversation is a learning opportunity AND a potential paying customer. Speed matters: replies within 30 min convert 2x replies within 4 hours.

## User Story
As a founder, I want to send 25 personalized cold messages on Day 18 across 3 channels with appropriate timing and pacing, monitor for replies in real-time, book demos for positive responses within 30 minutes, and update tracking, so that by EOD I have 2-3 booked demos and clean data on which channel/message is working.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] 25 outreach messages sent by EOD
- [ ] Channel split: 10 WhatsApp, 10 LinkedIn, 5 email
- [ ] All messages personalized per Day 17 hooks
- [ ] Send timing observed (10am-12pm + 4-6pm IST)
- [ ] All replies acknowledged within 30 minutes
- [ ] 2-3 demos booked
- [ ] At least 1 demo COMPLETED today (if Calendly slots filled today)
- [ ] Spreadsheet updated: sent date, opened, replied, sentiment, next step
- [ ] Open / click / read rates tracked (per channel)
- [ ] End-of-day reflection: what's working / not working

## Implementation Steps

### Step 1: Morning warmup (9-10am IST)
- Coffee, calm head
- Review yesterday's tool setup
- Re-read your 3 templates (cold becomes stale fast — fresh eyes catch issues)
- Open: spreadsheet, WhatsApp Business, LinkedIn, Instantly/Smartlead, Calendly

### Step 2: WhatsApp batch — 10 messages (10am-12pm IST)
Why first: highest reply rate, busiest morning window for agents.

Per prospect:
1. Save their phone as a contact (NAME + agency)
2. Open chat, paste personalized message
3. Send
4. Move to next
5. Pace: 1 message every 2 min (don't blast)

WhatsApp etiquette:
- Single message, not 4 split
- Don't open with "Hi" alone (looks lazy)
- Always reference their work specifically

### Step 3: LinkedIn batch — 10 DMs (11am-1pm IST)
Mix:
- 6 to 1st-degree connections (DM directly)
- 4 to 2nd-degree (send personalized connection request first — DM after accept)

LinkedIn etiquette:
- Personalize first 2 sentences
- Don't paste a wall of text
- One link max (Calendly or landing)
- Send during business hours

### Step 4: Email batch — 5 messages (sent via Instantly/Smartlead)
Already configured in tool from Day 17. Day 18:
- Verify scheduled send time (between 10am-11am IST)
- Watch for bounce/error notifications
- Don't let bounces leave inboxes unhealthy — pause if bounce rate >5%

### Step 5: Reply monitoring
Throughout the day, keep WhatsApp + LinkedIn + email tabs open. Goal: reply within 30 min during peak hours.

When a reply comes:
1. Read it twice
2. Identify sentiment: positive / neutral / curious / negative / objection
3. Respond appropriately (next step)
4. Update spreadsheet

### Step 6: Reply handling — positive ("Yes, interested!")
> "Awesome — quick demo this week?
> Wed 3pm or Thu 11am IST work? Or pick a slot here: calendly.com/kalimq
>
> 10-min walkthrough, I'll show you the AI calling and WhatsApp threading. After, you decide if a free 3-month trial makes sense."

Confirm in their channel (don't bounce them to email).

### Step 7: Reply handling — curious ("Tell me more")
> "Happy to. Quick context: RealtyFlow is a CRM specifically for Indian residential brokers.
>
> Wedge: AI calling in Hindi + auto-WhatsApp follow-up + owner-buyer matching.
>
> Live demo (no signup): realtyflow.in/demo
> 90-sec video: [link]
>
> Best way to see if it fits your workflow is a 10-min walkthrough. Want one?"

Don't info-dump. Open the door for a demo.

### Step 8: Reply handling — objection ("How is this different from Sell.do?")
Use battle cards from `pre-launch-prep/04`:
> "Good question. 3 differences:
>
> 1. AI calling built-in (Sell.do is just pipeline tracking)
> 2. Tiered pricing not per-user — saves ~50% for 3-5 agent teams
> 3. WhatsApp threading native, not bolt-on
>
> But honestly — best way to compare is 10 min on a call. Want one this week?"

Brief. Confident. Bridge to demo.

### Step 9: Reply handling — neutral ("Not now / busy")
Two responses:
- If "interested but bad timing": "No worries — should I check back in 2-3 weeks? Or want trial access now to explore at your pace?"
- If "not interested": "Totally fine — appreciate the honest reply. Mind if I ask what made it not a fit? Helps me improve the pitch."

Even "no" replies give you data.

### Step 10: End-of-day debrief (7-8pm IST)
Numbers to log in tracker:
- Messages sent (by channel): __
- Open rate (email): __% (Instantly reports)
- Read rate (WhatsApp): __% (blue tick count)
- Read rate (LinkedIn): __% (LI shows read status for some plans)
- Replies received: __
- Reply rate by channel: __% / __% / __%
- Positive responses: __
- Demos booked: __
- Demos completed: __

Reflection (10 min):
- Which channel produced the most replies?
- Which template variant worked?
- Any common objection / theme?
- Adjust for Day 19's batch based on findings

If reply rate <5% across the board → diagnose tomorrow morning. Don't send Day 19 batch on a broken approach.

## Risks & Mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Low open/reply rate | Diagnose: subject line? Wedge unclear? Re-tweak before Day 19 |
| Inbox / number gets flagged | Stay under volume caps. WhatsApp: save contact first. LinkedIn: personalize every DM. |
| Reply explosion overwhelms you | Triage: positive first, objections second, neutral last. Quick template responses ready. |
| Demo no-shows | Send 1-hour reminder via WhatsApp with Calendly link |
| Negative reply ("you're spamming me") | Apologize, offer to unsubscribe, take feedback. Don't argue. |
| You spend too long perfecting each reply | Time-box: 2 min per reply. Trust your templates. |

## India-Specific Notes
- WhatsApp Business reads (blue ticks) are highest-signal: if read but no reply in 4h, it's a soft no
- Hindi/Hinglish replies = high engagement signal; respond in same language
- Indian agents send voice notes for replies — listen carefully (often more info-rich than text)
- LinkedIn read receipts only available on Premium — not always visible
- Festival days / Saturdays-late: lower send rate, higher weekend response rate
- Mumbai responds fastest (often within 1h); Tier-2 cities take 4-24h
