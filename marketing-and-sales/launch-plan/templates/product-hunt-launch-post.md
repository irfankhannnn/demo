# Template — Product Hunt Launch Post

## When to Use
Day 16 — public launch on Product Hunt. Also adaptable for: BetaList, Indie Hackers, LinkedIn launch post, X thread.

## Pre-Launch (1-2 weeks before Day 16)
- Create Product Hunt account (if don't have)
- Build "Coming Soon" page on PH (gathers email list pre-launch)
- Tease in LinkedIn / X 5-7 days before
- Line up "hunters" who'll comment + upvote launch day
- Notify beta testers — they'll upvote / comment

## Maker Comment (First Comment You Post)

This is the MOST IMPORTANT piece of launch copy. Treated as a mini-story by hunters.


## Comment Engagement Playbook

When comments come in:

### Positive comment
> "Thanks [name]! What made you click? Always curious where the wedge resonates."

### Question
Answer specifically + with context. Don't be defensive.

### Critique ("just another CRM")
> "Fair pushback! What's the angle that would make this NOT 'just another CRM' to you? Genuinely asking — the AI calling is what I bet on, but happy to hear what's missing."

### Self-promo from competitors
> "Welcome [name]! [Their product] does X well — different angle, more enterprise-focused. Glad PH has us both."

### Request for feature
> "Noted — adding to backlog. We're shipping [related feature] next month. Anything specific you'd want in this?"

Reply within 30 min during launch day (1:31pm - midnight IST).

## Tone Rules
- Founder voice (first person, not company)
- Story-led (Rohit's deal lost, not "we built X")
- Specific numbers (₹85L deal, 10 brokers, 2 weeks)
- Acknowledge competitors honestly (don't trash; differentiate)
- Indian context (Hindi, ₹, Mumbai/Bangalore — not "western default")
- Vulnerability OK ("18 months of building", "not perfect")

## What NOT To Do
- Hype words: "revolutionary", "game-changing", "disrupting"
- Inflate numbers (10 brokers ≠ "thousands of users")
- Trash competitors by name
- Auto-respond to comments
- Disappear after launch hour (engage 8+ hours)
- Forget to tag beta testers (they multiplied your reach)

## Expected Performance
- Product Hunt: 50-150 upvotes for first-time founders (no guarantee of going viral)
- LinkedIn launch post: 5k-20k views, 50-200 likes, 20-50 comments
- X thread: 500-5k impressions, 10-50 likes
- New signups Day 16: 5-30 depending on traffic
- Long-tail backlink value: high (PH product page = dofollow backlink for SEO)

- UTM all links: `?utm_source=producthunt&utm_medium=launch&utm_campaign=launch-day`
- Bookmark PH page; check ranking hourly
- Track signups by UTM in PostHog Day 21 metric review
