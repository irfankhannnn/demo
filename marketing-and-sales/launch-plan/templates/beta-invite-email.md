# Template — Beta Invite Email

## When to Use
Day 9 — personalized invitation to 30-40 ICP-fit real estate agents inviting them to a 3-month free beta in exchange for honest feedback and a 10-min walkthrough.

## Channel
Email — only when you have a verified professional email address for the prospect. For WhatsApp/LinkedIn, use `beta-invite-linkedin.md` instead.

## Personalization Variables
- `[FIRSTNAME]` — prospect first name
- `[CITY]` — Mumbai / Bangalore / Pune / Delhi NCR
- `[HOOK]` — specific thing you noticed about them (post, listing, comment)
- `[AGENCY]` — their agency name if applicable
- `[FOCUS]` — their specialty (resale / new project / rentals)

## Subject Lines (A/B test 2-3)
- "Saw your [HOOK] post"
- "Quick question about [CITY] real estate"
- "Free 3-month beta — Indian real estate CRM"
- "Built RealtyFlow for agents like [AGENCY]"

## Body (under 120 words)

```
Hi [FIRSTNAME],

[HOOK opening — 1-2 sentences referencing something specific. Example: "Saw your 99acres listings in [CITY] — looks like you focus on [FOCUS]. Your recent LinkedIn post about lead follow-up really resonated."]

I'm Kalim, building RealtyFlow — a CRM specifically for Indian real estate agents. AI calling in Hindi, auto WhatsApp follow-up, owner-buyer matching. Built for solo agents and 2-15 person agencies (not enterprise overkill like LeadSquared).

Looking for 10 agents to test it completely free for 3 months in exchange for honest feedback (positive or negative — both useful).

Kalim Q.
```

## Variations

### Variation A — More direct (for high-confidence ICP fit)
Replace the hook + ask:
> "[FIRSTNAME] — your work in [CITY] is exactly the audience I'm building for. Want a 10-min demo this week? Free 3-month beta access included."

### Variation B — Question-led
> "Hi [FIRSTNAME], quick question: how do you handle weekend WhatsApp follow-ups? Most [CITY] agents I talk to say they lose 2-3 leads/week that way..."

### Variation C — Hinglish (for Tier-2/3 prospects)
> "Hi [FIRSTNAME], [CITY] mein property handle karte hain — saw your listings. Quick question: WhatsApp follow-ups kabhi miss ho jate hain? Most agents tell me ki Saturday-night leads Monday tak cold ho jate hain..."

## Tone Rules
- DON'T: "Hope you're doing well", "Quick question", "I wanted to reach out"
- DO: Lead with the hook (a specific observation), keep it under 120 words, end with a concrete CTA + time slot
- AVOID: Buzzwords ("synergy", "leverage", "ecosystem"), salesy framing ("game-changing", "revolutionary")
- USE: Founder voice ("I built this because..."), specific details ([CITY], [HOOK]), honest framing ("looking for 10 testers — honest feedback")

## What NOT To Send
- Long emails (>150 words) — agents skim mobile inbox
- Multiple links — distracting
- Marketing-heavy tone — feels like Mailchimp
- Generic "Hello!" openers — auto-deleted

## Follow-up
If no reply by Day 11, send Touch 2 (use `cold-outreach-followup.md` template).

## Tracking
After sending:
- Log in beta-prospects.csv with sent date, channel = email
- Email tool (Instantly/Smartlead) tracks opens automatically
- Reply within 30 min if positive response
