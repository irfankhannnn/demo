# Template — Cold Outreach Email (Day 18-19)

## When to Use
Days 17-19 — Touch 1 cold email to ICP-fit prospects who are NOT beta testers. Goal: book a 10-min demo and offer free trial.

## Channel
Email via warmed-up secondary domain (per `pre-launch-prep/03`). Sent via Instantly.ai / Smartlead.ai.

## Subject Lines (A/B test 4)
1. `Re: your [CITY] listings`
2. `Saw your 99acres listings — quick question`
3. `Indian real estate CRM — free 3-month beta`
4. `Built for [AGENCY] — would love your feedback`

Avoid: "BREAKTHROUGH!", "FREE!", "AMAZING OFFER", anything trigger-word for spam filters.

## Body Template (under 130 words)

```
Hi [FIRSTNAME],

[HOOK — 1-2 sentences. Example: "Saw your residential listings in [LOCALITY] on 99acres — looks like you handle solid inventory."]

Quick question: how do you handle weekend WhatsApp follow-ups? Most agents I talk to say they lose 2-3 leads/week to that gap.

I'm building RealtyFlow — a CRM specifically for Indian real estate. AI calling in Hindi/English, auto-WhatsApp threading, owner-buyer matching. Tiered pricing (not per-user) — starts at ₹999/month.

Launched publicly Monday. 10 Mumbai/Bangalore agencies in beta. One closed a ₹85L deal Week 2 from auto Saturday follow-up.

Want a 10-min demo? Free 3-month trial if you sign up this week.

Watch demo: realtyflow.in
Calendly: calendly.com/kalimq

Cheers,
Kalim
Founder, RealtyFlow
+91-XXXXX-XXXXX

P.S. Live demo (no signup): realtyflow.in/demo
```

## Personalization Variables
- `[FIRSTNAME]`
- `[CITY]` — Mumbai / Bangalore / Pune / Delhi NCR
- `[LOCALITY]` — Andheri / Whitefield / Koramangala / Gurgaon
- `[HOOK]` — specific observation
- `[AGENCY]` — their agency name if known

## Variations by Persona

### Variation A — Solo agent
> "[HOOK]. Solo agents I talk to say WhatsApp follow-up alone takes 3-4 hours/day. RealtyFlow's AI calling handles that for ₹999/month..."

### Variation B — Small team (2-5 agents)
> "[HOOK]. Your team must be juggling [N] active leads at any time. RealtyFlow's pipeline + WhatsApp threading is built for exactly this — Tier 2 at ₹2,499 covers up to 5 agents..."

### Variation C — Larger agency (6-15)
> "[HOOK]. Managing a [N]-person team's pipeline is where most CRMs break. RealtyFlow's Team tier (₹6,499) handles up to 15 agents with custom workflows..."

### Variation D — Hinglish (Tier-2/3 city prospects)
> "Hi [FIRSTNAME], [CITY] mein property handle karte hain — saw your work. Quick question: weekend leads kabhi miss ho jate hain? Most agents tell me Saturday-night messages Monday tak cold ho jate hain. Built something for exactly this..."

## P.S. Variations
- "P.S. Live demo (no signup): realtyflow.in/demo"
- "P.S. 90-sec walkthrough: [video link]"
- "P.S. I'm in [CITY] next month — happy to do a coffee instead of a video call."

P.S. lines often get read MORE than the email body. Make them count.

## Anti-Spam Triggers To Avoid
- "FREE" in subject line (use "Free 3-month beta" in body instead)
- ALL CAPS
- Multiple exclamation marks
- Excessive links (>2 in body)
- Spammy phrases: "Act now", "Limited time", "Guarantee", "Risk-free"
- Images embedded in body (most cold emails are text-only)

## Tone Rules
- DO: Lead with their context, frame as offer not pitch, keep it under 130 words
- DON'T: Talk too much about RealtyFlow features upfront — save for demo
- USE: Specific numbers (10 agencies in beta, ₹85L deal, Tier 2 at ₹2,499)
- AVOID: Marketing-speak ("game-changing", "revolutionary", "AI-powered")
