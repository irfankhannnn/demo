# Template — Cold Outreach Follow-Up (Day 23)

## When to Use
Day 23 — Touch 2 follow-up to Day 18-19 cold outreach prospects who didn't reply. Polite, low-pressure, adds new value.

## Channel
Same as Touch 1 (email/WhatsApp/LinkedIn) — OR switch channels for those who didn't open the original.

## Variant Decision Tree
```
Day 18 outreach → no response by Day 22 →
   ├─ Was original message READ/OPENED?
   │   ├─ YES → Send "polite bump" follow-up (Variant A)
   │   └─ NO  → Switch channel + lead with "tried earlier" (Variant B)
   │
   └─ Did they reply with "not now" or "interested but busy"?
        └─ Send "low-pressure re-engagement" (Variant C)
```

## Variant C — Low-Pressure Re-Engagement (for "not now" responses)

For prospects who replied with "interested but bad timing" or "not the right time":

```
Hi [FIRSTNAME], totally get the timing thing — wanted to follow up briefly.

Two low-pressure options:

1. Want trial access NOW so you can explore at your pace, no commitment? Just reply with your email and I'll set it up — no demo call needed unless you want one.

2. Should I ping you again in 3-4 weeks when things ease up?

Either works. Thanks for the original honesty about timing.

Cheers,
Kalim
```

This recovers 30%+ of "not now" prospects without being pushy.

## Tone Rules — All Variants
- Acknowledge the bump openly ("wanted to follow up", "tried reaching you earlier")
- ONE new value angle (case study mention, urgency, channel switch)
- Explicit permission to say no
- Demo link as low-commitment alternative
- Keep shorter than Touch 1
- Sign off personal — your name, not company name

## What NOT To Do in Follow-up
- Don't be aggressive ("Have you reviewed my proposal?")
- Don't pretend it's a fresh message ("Hi, I wanted to introduce myself")
- Don't send images / attachments
- Don't follow-up >2 times to same prospect (after Touch 3 on Day 26, accept the silence)

## Final Touch (Day 26 — Touch 3)
If still no reply by Day 26, send ONE final message — value-give, not pitch:

```
Hi [FIRSTNAME], last note from me on RealtyFlow — promise.

Sharing a free resource that might be useful regardless: [link to your Day 25 case study OR a free PDF / guide].

Quick takeaway from our beta data: Indian real estate agents who follow up within 4 hours of a lead close at 3x the rate of 24-hour responders. Hope it's useful.

If you ever want to try RealtyFlow, the demo is at realtyflow.in/demo — no email needed.

Best of luck with [CITY] real estate this year,
Kalim
```

This is the "kind goodbye". After this, archive the prospect. No more outreach for 90 days.
