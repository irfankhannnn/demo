# Template — Beta Invite LinkedIn DM

## When to Use
Day 9 — personalized LinkedIn DM to ICP-fit real estate agents who appear in your LinkedIn network or 2nd-degree connections. Pairs with `beta-invite-email.md` for prospects without LinkedIn presence.

## Channel
LinkedIn DM (1st-degree) OR connection request with 300-char note (2nd-degree)

## Personalization Variables
- `[FIRSTNAME]` — first name
- `[CITY]` — Mumbai / Bangalore / Pune / Delhi NCR
- `[HOOK]` — specific post / comment / observation
- `[AGENCY]` — their agency name
- `[FOCUS]` — residential / new project / rental

## Length Targets
- Connection request note: ≤300 characters (LinkedIn limit)
- First DM after acceptance: 80-110 words
- Don't paste a wall of text — looks spammy

## Connection Request Note (for 2nd-degree)
≤300 characters:
```
Hi [FIRSTNAME], saw your [HOOK] post — building a CRM specifically for Indian real estate agents and would love to connect with [CITY] brokers. No pitch, just keen to learn from your work.

— Kalim, Founder RealtyFlow
```

## First DM (after they accept OR if already 1st-degree)

```
Hi [FIRSTNAME],

[HOOK opening — example: "Saw your post last week about lead nurturing challenges in [CITY] — resonated. Built a tool to fix exactly that."]

I'm building RealtyFlow — CRM specifically for Indian residential real estate agents. AI calling in Hindi, WhatsApp threading, owner-buyer matching. Founder-built, not enterprise-bolted-on.

Looking for 10 [CITY] agents to test it free for 3 months. Honest feedback in exchange — that's the only ask.

Open to a 10-min walkthrough this week? Pick a slot: calendly.com/kalimq

(Or watch a 90-sec demo first: realtyflow.in)

Either way — would love to swap notes about how you handle [PAIN POINT, e.g., weekend leads].

Cheers,
Kalim
```

## Variations

### Variation A — Founder-to-founder framing (for agency owners)
> "[FIRSTNAME] — fellow founder reaching out. Saw your [AGENCY] is doing solid residential work in [CITY]. I'm building RealtyFlow — would love to get your eyes on what we've built for 2-15 person agencies. 10-min demo + 3 months free?"

### Variation B — Curiosity-led (for active LinkedIn posters)
> "[FIRSTNAME], read your [HOOK] post twice. The bit about [SPECIFIC POINT] is exactly what RealtyFlow solves..."

### Variation C — Hinglish (for tier-2/3 prospects)
> "Hi [FIRSTNAME], [CITY] mein real estate handle karte hain — saw your work. Quick context: building a CRM specifically for Indian agents..."

## Tone Rules
- LinkedIn is slightly more formal than WhatsApp, less formal than email
- Lead with the hook in first sentence
- One link max (Calendly OR landing — not both)
- DON'T send 5 split messages — single, cohesive DM
- DO sign off with name + role (LinkedIn shows your headline anyway)

## What NOT To Send
- Generic copy-paste (LinkedIn detects this)
- "Hope you're well" openers
- Multiple links + attachments in first DM
- Auto-DM via tools (LinkedIn flags + bans)

## LinkedIn-Specific Etiquette
- Wait for connection acceptance before DM (for 2nd-degree)
- Don't send 3 follow-ups within a week
- Limit: 15-20 DMs/day to avoid algorithmic flagging
- Personalize EVERY DM — LinkedIn detects copy-paste

## Follow-up Strategy
- If no read after 4 days: send via different channel (WhatsApp or email)
- If read but no reply after 4 days: one follow-up message ("Did this get buried?")
- After that: archive, don't ping again
