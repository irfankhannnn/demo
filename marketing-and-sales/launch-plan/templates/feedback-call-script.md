# Template — Feedback Call Script

## When to Use
Day 10 — onboarding calls with beta testers where they share their screen and use RealtyFlow live. Also useful for: Day 19 prospect demos, Day 24 stalled user screen-shares, Day 26 trial wrap-ups.

## Call Setup (Pre-Call — 5 min)
- Open this script in another window for reference
- Open the prospect's spreadsheet row
- Have demo tenant URL ready (per `pre-launch-prep/05`)
- Calendar reminder set
- Test audio + video
- Notepad open for live notes

---

## Opening (1 min)

```
"Hey [FIRSTNAME], thanks so much for hopping on. Really appreciate the 15 min.

Quick frame: my goal here is to watch YOU sign up and use RealtyFlow as if I'm not in the room. Your 'this is weird' or 'this is confusing' is way more valuable than 'this is great'.

I might stay quiet for stretches — that's intentional. I want to see where you'd naturally figure things out vs get stuck. Cool?

Mind if I record? Just for my notes — won't share it externally. [Wait for yes]

Great. Can you share your screen and pull up realtyflow.in?"
```

**Key tone:** Curious, observer, NOT salesperson. Set expectation that you'll be quiet.

---

## Phase 1: Landing → Signup (5 min)

Watch them:
- Read the hero (do they get what RealtyFlow is in 5 sec?)
- Click CTA (or hesitate?)
- Fill signup form (any confusion?)

**Stay silent.** Don't help. Take notes:
- Where do they hover and not click?
- What do they say out loud while reading?
- Where do they back-button?
- Time to signup completion

**If asked direct questions:** Answer briefly. Don't volunteer info.

**If stuck >2 min:** Ask open-ended:
- "What are you looking for right now?"
- "What would you click next, if you had to guess?"

---

## Phase 2: Onboarding (5 min)

Watch them complete the in-app onboarding flow. Same rules — observe, don't intervene.

**Common reactions to log:**
- Confusion about "Tenant" vs "Buyer" terminology
- Skipping a step that needs to be done
- Looking for a feature in the wrong menu
- Phone number format errors

---

## Phase 3: Three Core Tasks (5-7 min)

After onboarding, prompt:
> "Cool, you're in. Now I'd like to see you do three things — go at your own pace, narrate what you're thinking if it's helpful.
>
> 1. Try adding a buyer — someone looking for a 2BHK in Andheri, budget ₹1.5 Cr.
> 2. Try starting an AI call to that buyer.
> 3. Try sending a WhatsApp message about a new project to them.
>
> No rush. If you get stuck, I'm here."

For each task, observe:
- Steps they take vs steps you expected
- Where they pause / scroll / look around
- Verbal reactions ("oh!", "wait...", "ah, found it")
- Time-to-completion
- Whether they succeed or partially complete

---

## Phase 4: Q&A + Standard Questions (5 min)

After they've tried the tasks, transition:
> "Awesome — okay, can I ask you a few questions while it's fresh?"

### Standard Question 1 (PMF — Sean Ellis Test)
> "On a scale of 1-10, how disappointed would you be if RealtyFlow disappeared tomorrow?"

Listen for: explanation behind the number, not just the number.

### Standard Question 2 (Wedge validation)
> "What's the ONE thing that would make this indispensable to your daily work?"

This tells you what feature/UX investment matters most.

### Standard Question 3 (Pricing reaction)
> "If I told you Tier 1 is ₹999/month and Tier 2 is ₹2,499/month, what would your reaction be? Fair? Too high? Too low?"

Get their gut. Don't argue.

### Optional Question 4 (Comparison)
> "Have you used Sell.do, Zoho, or other CRMs? How does this compare?"

If they have, capture the comparison.

### Optional Question 5 (Word of mouth)
> "If you stayed with RealtyFlow, who would be the first person you'd tell?"

Tests virality + reveals their network.

---

## Phase 5: Wrap-Up (2 min)

```
"Two more things, then I'll let you go:

1. I'd love to add you to the beta tester WhatsApp group — agents are sharing tips. Cool to add you?

2. Quick gut check: anything I should have done differently in this call?

Thanks again for the time. You'll get free access for 3 months — go play with it for a week or two. I'll message you mid-week to see how it's going.

If you hit ANY roadblock, message me directly at +91-XXXXX. Same with feature ideas — I'm building this with my testers, so don't hold back."
```

---

## During Call — When You CAN Intervene
- Answer direct questions briefly
- Help if they're TRULY stuck >2 min (escalate to gentle hint)
- Confirm permission for recording / WhatsApp group
- Bridge between phases ("now let's try...")

## During Call — When You CAN'T Intervene
- Apologize for friction ("sorry, that's confusing")
- Pitch features they didn't ask about
- Defend a design choice they criticize
- Steer them away from a flow you know is broken
- Talk to fill silence (silence is a feature, not a bug)

## Post-Call (within 5 min)
- Save recording to labeled folder
- Review notes — anything missed?
- Add new friction items to backlog
- Update prospect's spreadsheet row
- Send 1-line WhatsApp thank-you:
  > "Thanks again [name]! Couple of fixes going out this week based on what we saw. Anytime you hit a snag, ping me."

---

## Call Quality Indicators
- 80%+ of time you were silent? — GOOD
- They asked you to fix something during the call? — Friction signal
- They volunteered a verbatim quote that could go on the landing page? — Testimonial pipeline
- They asked "when can I start paying?" — Strong conversion signal
- They asked "is there a referral program?" — Plant the seed for Day 30

---

## Anti-Patterns To Avoid
- Sales mode (closing instead of learning)
- Over-explaining when they're confused
- Skipping the silence moments (they're data)
- Asking leading questions ("Wouldn't it be great if...")
- Spending >30 min (call quality drops; book 2 if needed)

---

## Expected Outcomes Per Call
- 5+ specific friction points identified
- 1-2 verbatim quotes for marketing
- 1 Sean Ellis score logged
- Beta tester engaged in WhatsApp group
- Conversion likelihood scored
