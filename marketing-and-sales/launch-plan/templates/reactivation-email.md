# Template — Trial User Re-Activation

## When to Use
Day 24 — to trial signups (Weeks 2-3) who haven't logged in for 3+ days. Goal: get them back into the app or get honest feedback about why they stalled.

## Channel
Email or WhatsApp — match the channel they originally engaged via.

## Segmentation (4 stall types)

### Type 1: Never Logged In After Signup

```
Subject: Did the welcome email get buried?

Hi [FIRSTNAME],

Quick check-in — you signed up Tuesday but haven't had a chance to log in yet. No judgment, real estate is busy.

Two options:

1. Want me to walk you through the basics in 10 min? Calendly: calendly.com/kalimq
2. Or I can send a 60-sec video showing how to add your first lead — just reply "yes, video".

If timing's just off, totally fine — happy to ping again in 2 weeks.

Or if it's not the right fit, a quick "not for me" reply helps me know.

Cheers,
Kalim
```

### Type 2: Logged In Once, Stalled in Onboarding

```
Subject: Got tripped up somewhere in RealtyFlow?

Hey [FIRSTNAME],

Saw you signed up and explored Tuesday but haven't been back. Curious: did the onboarding flow trip you up somewhere?

Happy to do either:

1. 15-min screen share to unblock anything live: calendly.com/kalimq
2. Send me a screenshot of where you got stuck — I'll send a quick Loom walkthrough.

No pressure — just want to make sure the product isn't broken before you decide it's not for you.

Cheers,
Kalim
```

### Type 3: Onboarded, Empty Dashboard

```
Subject: Want me to populate your account?

Hey [FIRSTNAME],

You're set up but haven't added any buyers yet. Two things that might help:

1. Want me to drop 5 sample buyers into your account so you can see RealtyFlow populated? Takes me 2 min on your behalf. You can replace with real data later.

2. Or want a 10-min walkthrough showing the AI calling feature? That's the part most agents say "okay this is different".

Reply "samples please" or "walkthrough" and I'll set it up.

Cheers,
Kalim
```

### Type 4: Added Some Data, Stalled Mid-Trial

```
Subject: Have you tried the AI calling yet?

Hey [FIRSTNAME],

Saw you've added [N] buyers (nice!). Quick question: have you tried the AI calling feature yet?

That's the part most agents say is game-changing — auto-call your leads in Hindi/English, schedule site visits, all hands-off.

I can demo it to you live on YOUR leads in 10 min. DM me a time or pick: calendly.com/kalimq

Otherwise, anything in the way? Bugs? Confused about something? Reply with whatever's on your mind.

Cheers,
Kalim
```

Reply with details + I'll book a slot to walk you through what happens.

This is the highest-effort intervention but recovers stuck prospects at 60%+ rate.

## Tone Rules
- Empathetic, not desperate
- Non-judgmental about lack of usage
- Specific, helpful offers (not generic "let me know if you need help")
- Permission to opt out
- Founder-signed, personal
- Channel-match (email looks long; WhatsApp looks short)

## Anti-Patterns
- "Just checking in!" (generic, ignored)
- "Last chance!" (pressure)
- "Don't miss out!" (salesy)
- "Click here to..." (vague CTA)

## Expected Performance
- 30-50% reply rate (warm — they signed up after all)
- 20-40% of replies result in re-engagement (logged back in)
- 1-3% convert to paid from re-activation
- Highest ROI of any Week-4 activity per minute spent

## Tracking
- Update beta-prospects.csv / trial users tracker
- Note: re-activation attempted, response, status change
- If re-engaged: monitor activity for next 7 days
- If still silent: accept and move on (don't ping a 3rd time)
