# Template — Testimonial Request

## When to Use
Day 14 — collect 5+ testimonials from active beta testers. Also useful Days 26-30 for paying customers post-conversion.

## Channel
WhatsApp first (highest reply rate). Email if WhatsApp not preferred.

---

## Initial Ask Template

### WhatsApp / Short Channel
```
Hey [FIRSTNAME] — quick favor.

Putting RealtyFlow's landing page together for our public launch next Monday. Would you be open to sharing a 1-2 sentence quote about your experience so far?

Something specific you can speak to — like "AI calling saved me 3 hours yesterday" or whatever's actually true.

Happy to DRAFT something for you to approve and edit — would just take 30 sec of your time to OK it.

Also — okay to include your name + city + [AGENCY]? Or anonymous works too. Either way!
```

### Email Version (Slightly Longer)
```
Subject: Quick favor for RealtyFlow's launch page

Hi [FIRSTNAME],

Hope you're doing well. Quick favor — we're launching RealtyFlow publicly next Monday and putting together a landing page with real beta tester feedback.

Would you be open to sharing a 1-2 sentence quote about your experience so far? Something specific you can speak to — a feature that helped, a stat (like "saved 3 hours/day"), a moment that clicked.

Two ways to make this easy:

1. I'll DRAFT something based on what you mentioned on our call — you just OK it (or edit it). Should take 30 sec.

2. Or send me your own version — your words, your tone.

Also: okay to include your name + [AGENCY] + city? Or fully anonymous if you prefer ("Founder, Mumbai agency" works too).

No pressure either way — appreciate you being part of the beta regardless.

Cheers,
Kalim
```

## Drafted Quote (Send With Initial Ask If Possible)

The fastest way to get approval is to draft for them. Sample drafts based on what they mentioned on Day 10 call:

```
Hey [FIRSTNAME] — here's a draft based on what you mentioned the other day:

"RealtyFlow's AI calling is the only reason I haven't missed a single weekend lead in 2 weeks. It's like having an assistant who works Saturday mornings."

Cool to use as-is, or edit it however feels right? Or send me your own version — totally up to you.
```

**Why drafting works:**
- Lowers their effort from "compose original" → "approve/edit"
- Captures verbatim feel from their actual words
- Most testers respond within an hour

## Permission Confirmation Message

After they approve, lock in permission in writing (DPDP compliance):

```
Awesome, thanks [FIRSTNAME]. Confirming I can use:

✓ Your name: [FIRSTNAME LASTNAME]
✓ City: [CITY]
✓ Agency: [AGENCY NAME]
✓ Photo: [yes/no]
✓ Quote: "[final approved version]"

On realtyflow.in landing page + LinkedIn + occasionally on outreach. Reply "yes" to confirm.

(Save your reply as my consent record. Standard stuff.)
```

Save their "yes" reply (screenshot WhatsApp / archive email) to your testimonial-permissions folder.

## Tone Rules
- Ask is LOW-friction (draft for them, easy approve)
- Permission is EXPLICIT (saved in writing)
- Confirmation is SPECIFIC (name, city, agency, photo, quote)
- Thanks is PERSONAL (founder voice, not corporate)
- Permission can be revoked anytime (mention this if asked)

## What NOT To Do
- Use a testimonial without explicit permission
- Edit a quote AFTER permission (re-confirm if changed)
- Use a quote outside agreed contexts (e.g., for paid ads when only landing was agreed)
- Pressure for response — they're doing you a favor
- Make their permission contingent on continued use ("only if you sign annual")

- 80% give name + city; 60% include photo
