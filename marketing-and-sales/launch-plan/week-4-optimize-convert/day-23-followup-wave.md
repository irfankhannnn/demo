# Day 23 — Send Follow-Up Wave to Non-Responders

## Objective
Send a polite, value-add follow-up message to all Day 18-19 outreach prospects who opened/read your initial message but did NOT reply — re-surfacing the offer with a soft angle ("Did this get buried? Even a 1-line reply helps") to win back 20-30% of silent prospects.

## Why This Matters for RealtyFlow
Follow-up doubles cold outreach reply rates. Most founders skip it ("they'd reply if interested"). They wouldn't — most emails get buried in 50+ daily messages. A second-touch reply rate of 10-15% is normal. Combined with Day 18-19's 10% reply rate, you're now at 20-25% cumulative — which is the difference between 5 conversations and 12.

## User Story
As a founder, I want to send a thoughtful follow-up message to every Day-18/19 prospect who didn't reply — using the right channel, the right tone, and ONE new value angle (case study, demo link, urgency) — so that 5-10 additional conversations open by EOD Day 23 and pipeline expands without new sourcing.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] All non-responders from Day 18-19 identified (likely 35-40 of 50)
- [ ] Segmented: "Opened/Read no-reply" vs "No-open / No-read"
- [ ] Each segment receives appropriate follow-up message
- [ ] Channel optimization: try a DIFFERENT channel if original didn't open
- [ ] All messages sent by EOD Day 23
- [ ] Each message has new value (not just "bumping this up")
- [ ] Total expected outcomes: 3-6 new replies, 2-3 new demos booked
- [ ] No spam-tone or "pushy" language
- [ ] Tracker updated: touch 2 sent date + outcome
- [ ] Day 26 final touch list prepared (those who don't reply by Day 25)

## Implementation Steps

### Step 1: Segment non-responders (30 min)
Open Day 18-19 tracker. Three buckets:

**Bucket A: Read/Opened, No Reply** (15-20 prospects)
- These saw your message and chose not to reply
- They're a borderline case — possibly interested but busy, possibly polite ignore
- Best target for follow-up

**Bucket B: Not Read / Not Opened** (15-20 prospects)
- Possibly didn't see (email in spam, WhatsApp not active, LinkedIn buried)
- Best for CHANNEL SWITCH (try different channel)

**Bucket C: Replied negatively** (1-3 prospects)
- Skip these — respect the no
- Note for future ("not now" can turn into "yes in 3 months")

### Step 2: Bucket A follow-up message draft (WhatsApp/LinkedIn)
Soft, polite, ONE new value angle.

Sample (90 words):
> "Hey Rahul, hope this isn't bad timing — wanted to bump up my message from Monday.
>
> Quick context update: Skyline Properties (Mumbai broker, 4 agents) closed a ₹85L deal Week 2 of using RealtyFlow — auto Saturday-night follow-up that they would have missed.
>
> Worth a 10-min demo? I'm aiming to onboard 3 more agencies this week before launch incentives end.
>
> Live demo (no signup): realtyflow.in/demo
>
> Even a "not interested" reply helps me know whether to retry later. Thanks!"

Key elements:
- Open with the bump acknowledgment
- New value: case study mention
- New urgency: launch incentives ending
- Explicit permission to say no
- Demo link as low-commitment alternative

### Step 3: Bucket A email follow-up
Slightly different than WhatsApp (more space, less casual):

Subject line: "Re: RealtyFlow demo — quick bump"

Body (110 words):
> Hi Rahul,
>
> Following up on my Monday note about RealtyFlow — wanted to share an update.
>
> One of our Mumbai beta testers (Skyline Properties, 4 agents) closed a ₹85L deal in Week 2 via auto WhatsApp follow-up on a Saturday lead they would have otherwise missed.
>
> Still aiming to onboard 3 more agencies before launch pricing ends. Worth a 10-min demo?
>
> Try the live demo (no signup needed): realtyflow.in/demo
>
> If this isn't a fit, no worries — a quick "not now" reply helps me close the loop. Either way, thanks for considering.
>
> Best,
> Kalim

### Step 4: Bucket B — channel switch
For prospects who didn't open your Day 18-19 outreach, try a DIFFERENT channel.

| Original | Switch To |
|----------|-----------|
| Email | WhatsApp |
| WhatsApp | LinkedIn DM |
| LinkedIn DM | WhatsApp |

Use Day 17 templates as base, but lead with: "Hi Rahul, tried reaching you earlier this week — different channel in case my message got lost."

### Step 5: Send mechanics
- Stagger sends: 10 in morning (10am-12pm IST), 15 in afternoon (3-5pm IST)
- Manual sends for WhatsApp + LinkedIn
- Email tool can auto-fire Bucket A follow-ups (set as Email 2 in your sequence)

Volume guideline:
- Email follow-ups: up to 40 (tool-handled)
- WhatsApp: 20-25 (manual)
- LinkedIn: 15-20 (manual)

### Step 6: Reply handling (same as Day 18-19)
Same playbook. Speed matters. Use Day 18-19 templates for positive/neutral/objection responses.

If someone replies positively after your follow-up:
- Acknowledge: "Thanks for the reply — glad you saw the bump!"
- Move to demo booking quickly

### Step 7: Run any new demos (booked from Day 18-22)
By Day 23, some Day 18-19 booked demos may fall on today. Run them with the same playbook (Day 19, Step 5).

### Step 8: Pricing-based urgency (use sparingly)
You can mention "launch incentives end Friday" or "founding-member discount ends Day 30" — but only if true:
- Did you actually plan an end-of-Month-1 launch promo end?
- If yes, mention it. If no, don't fake it. Trust > false urgency.

Honest urgency you can use:
- "I'm aiming to onboard X agencies before [day]"
- "Free 3-month trial for the next 5 signups"
- "Founder-direct setup ends when my workload allows" (true — you only have so much time)

### Step 9: Update tracker
For each prospect:
- Touch 2 date + channel
- Read / opened (where measurable)
- Response (Y/N + sentiment)
- Updated likelihood + next step

### Step 10: Final-touch plan for Day 26
Prospects who didn't respond to Touch 1 AND Touch 2:
- Save list for Day 26 final attempt
- Final touch will be value-give, not pitch (article link, free resource)
- After Day 26 → archive, no more outreach

## Risks & Mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Prospects mark as spam after follow-up | Polite tone + new value angle + "no worries if not" exit |
| Email follow-ups auto-fire to people who replied yesterday | Pause/exit them from sequence manually upon reply |
| WhatsApp ban risk on second-touch volume | Stagger over 2-3 hours; never broadcast |
| Negative response ("you're being annoying") | Apologize, offer to remove, move on. Mark "do not contact" |
| Time drains into follow-up at expense of new outreach | Day 23 is follow-up day. Day 24-25 you'll do other work. Don't conflate. |

## India-Specific Notes
- Indian agents often reply 2-3 days late — give Day 18 messages time before declaring "non-responder"
- WhatsApp's blue tick double-check is high-signal: read but no reply = soft no
- Hinglish in follow-ups feels more personal than formal English
- Mumbai prospects respond faster; tier-2 cities benefit more from third-touch (Day 26)
- Avoid Saturday follow-ups (family time for most agents)
- Sunday evenings can work — agents prep for week ahead
