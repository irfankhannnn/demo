# Day 25 — Customer-Centric Content + SEO Foundation

## Objective
Write and publish ONE detailed case study or operational guide (1,500-2,500 words) — based on a real beta tester outcome — and commit to a 3-5 article SEO content calendar for the following 60 days, planting evergreen content seeds that compound into Month 2-3 inbound.

## Why This Matters for RealtyFlow
Cold outreach is acquisition; content is gravity. One detailed case study about "How Skyline Properties closed a ₹85L deal using RealtyFlow" gets shared on WhatsApp groups, cited in LinkedIn comments, and ranks for "real estate CRM case study India" over time. Outbound dries up if you stop sending; content keeps working. Day 25's content piece is the first deposit in that compounding bank.

## User Story
As a founder, I want to publish ONE detailed case study or operational guide (1,500-2,500 words) based on a real beta tester outcome — using their actual words, numbers, and screenshots — AND commit to a 3-5 article SEO content calendar for the next 60 days, so that organic search and social shares begin compounding into Week-5+ inbound traffic.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] One 1,500-2,500 word piece published at `realtyflow.in/blog/[slug]`
- [ ] Content type: case study OR operational deep-dive (both work)
- [ ] Real beta tester featured (with permission, from Day 14 testimonials)
- [ ] Quantitative outcomes included (deals closed, hours saved, leads added)
- [ ] At least 3 screenshots embedded
- [ ] SEO-optimized: H1 + 5-8 H2s, meta description, target keyword
- [ ] Internal links to: landing page, pricing, demo, features
- [ ] CTA at end: "Try the demo / Start free trial"
- [ ] Shareable: Open Graph image + Twitter card configured
- [ ] Shared on LinkedIn, X, beta tester WhatsApp group
- [ ] 3-5 article editorial calendar drafted for next 60 days
- [ ] Featured beta tester thanked + sent live link

## Implementation Steps

### Step 1: Pick the piece type
Two strong options for Day 25:

**Option A: Case study (recommended if you have a strong beta outcome)**
Title: "How [Agency Name] Closed a ₹85L Deal Using RealtyFlow's AI Calling"
- Story arc: problem → trying RealtyFlow → first 2 weeks → specific outcome
- Quantitative + emotional
- Strong CTA potential

**Option B: Operational deep-dive (recommended if no clear case study yet)**
Title: "The Indian Real Estate Agent's Lead Follow-Up Bible: A 9-Step Framework"
- Practical, evergreen, SEO-friendly
- Position RealtyFlow as the tool that automates Steps 5-9
- Lower direct-conversion but higher long-term SEO

**Pick Option A if you have a willing beta tester.** Otherwise Option B.

### Step 2: Interview the beta tester (if case study)
Schedule 30-min call with your most successful Day-14 tester (e.g., Rohit Sharma / Skyline Properties).

Questions:
1. What was the deal? (size, location, type, buyer profile)
2. What was your previous process for similar leads?
3. Where would the deal have died without RealtyFlow?
4. What specifically did RealtyFlow do that you couldn't have?
5. What's the metric you'd measure to prove this was different?
6. Quote: "Without RealtyFlow, this deal would have ____."

Record the call (with consent). Pull verbatim quotes.

### Step 3: Outline the piece (30 min)
Case study outline (1,800-2,200 words):

```

[Hero image — agent photo or city skyline]

[Lead — 80 words] Real estate is high-stakes. Skyline Properties, a 4-agent Mumbai brokerage in Andheri-Bandra, was losing 2-3 deals per month to follow-up gaps. In their first 2 weeks with RealtyFlow, they closed an ₹85L deal that — by their own admission — would have died without the platform. Here's how.

H2: The Brokerage Profile
- 4 agents, founded 2019
- ~₹2 Cr/month average GMV
- Focus: residential resale in Mumbai western suburbs
- Tools they were using: Excel + WhatsApp + Sell.do (sometimes)

H2: The Problem — Saturday Night Leads
[300 words on the specific pain point]

H2: Trying RealtyFlow — Week 1

- Day 1: Signed up, populated 47 active leads

H2: What Changed Beyond This One Deal
```

### Step 4: Write the draft (4-6 hours)
- Use the outline strictly
- Lead with story, support with numbers
- Quote heavily — readers trust voices more than narrators
- Embed screenshots: WhatsApp thread, pipeline view, AI call transcript
- Hyperlink to: feature pages, pricing, demo
- Short paragraphs (Indian mobile readers — 3-4 sentence max)

### Step 5: SEO optimization
Target keyword: "real estate CRM India" or "real estate agent software India"

Check:
- H1 contains target keyword
- First paragraph mentions keyword naturally
- 3-5 H2s with related keywords
- Meta description: 150 char compelling summary
- URL slug: short, keyword-rich (`/blog/how-skyline-properties-closed-85l-deal`)
- Alt text on all images
- Internal links to 5+ pages (landing, pricing, features, demo, signup)
- External links to 1-2 authority sites (RERA gov.in, ET Realty)

### Step 6: Open Graph + Twitter cards
- OG image: 1200×630px, includes title + agency logo + photo
- Twitter card: same
- Test via Twitter Card Validator + Facebook Sharing Debugger

### Step 7: Publish + distribute
- Publish on your blog at `realtyflow.in/blog/[slug]`
- Tweet from your X account
- LinkedIn post (long-form): 3-paragraph teaser + link
- Share in beta tester WhatsApp group: "Story I wrote about Skyline Properties — feel free to share if useful"
- Email the featured tester with live link + thanks
- Submit to relevant subreddits (r/IndianRealEstate) IF the community accepts content posts

### Step 9: Document referrals + repurposing
Plan to repurpose this single piece into:
- LinkedIn carousel (10 slides — key stats)
- X thread (12 tweets)
- Email to your list (excerpt + link)
- Reel/short video (case study summary)

Each repurpose = 1 day's work spread across Month 2-3.

- Conversions from page (signup CTA clicks)

## Risks & Mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Featured beta tester declines | Have backup tester ready (from Day 14's 5+) |
| Content writing takes 2 days | Acceptable — Day 26 is buffer-friendly |
| Article gets zero views Week 1 | Distribution is the fix, not more writing. LinkedIn, WhatsApp groups, communities, Reddit. |
| SEO won't show results in Month 1 | True — SEO is Month 2-3 payoff. Day 25 plants seeds. |
| Article reads like an ad | Quote heavily; lead with story not pitch; ask 2 readers (non-customers) to check tone |

## India-Specific Notes
- Indian B2B readers prefer narrative over data tables — lead with story
- Specific Indian context (Mumbai locality, ₹ amounts, Hindi quotes) increases shareability
- "How [X] saved Y crore" headlines outperform generic ones
- Cross-share to LinkedIn India real estate groups for amplification
- Hinglish in quotes feels authentic; full Hinglish article works for tier-2/3 audiences
