# Pre-Launch Prep — 06: Founder Personal Brand (LinkedIn + X)

## Objective
Build minimum founder authority signals — optimized LinkedIn profile, 5 published posts, X account — so that Week 3 cold outreach prospects see a credible human, not a bare profile that pattern-matches to spam.

## Why This Matters for RealtyFlow
Cold message reply rates correlate strongly with sender credibility. When a real estate agent in Mumbai gets a LinkedIn message from "Kalim Q., Founder at RealtyFlow", their next action is to check your profile. If they see: photo, headline, posts about real estate / SaaS, India context → they reply. If they see: empty profile → ignore. This isn't vanity. It's a 2-5x reply rate multiplier.

## User Story
As a founder, I want a complete LinkedIn profile, at least 5 published posts establishing credibility in Indian real estate + SaaS, and a basic X presence, so that prospects who research me during cold outreach in Week 3 see a credible founder and reply at 2-3x the rate they would otherwise.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] LinkedIn profile photo (professional, smiling, clear face)
- [ ] LinkedIn banner image (RealtyFlow branding or city skyline)
- [ ] Headline: "Founder @ RealtyFlow | Building India's smartest real estate CRM | ex-[prior role]"
- [ ] About section: 3-4 paragraphs — what you do, why you built RealtyFlow, what you believe about Indian real estate
- [ ] Featured section: pin demo video + landing page + best post
- [ ] Experience section updated with RealtyFlow as current role
- [ ] 5 LinkedIn posts published (before Day 17 cold outreach starts)
- [ ] 200+ LinkedIn connections (relevant — real estate, SaaS founders, India tech)
- [ ] X account created (optional but recommended): @KalimQ or @RealtyFlowIN with 3-5 posts
- [ ] Calendly link in headline for "Book a Demo"

## Implementation Steps

### Step 1: LinkedIn profile audit
Use [Resume Worded LinkedIn Review](https://resumeworded.com/linkedin-review) — free tool, scores your profile.

Target score: 80+/100.

### Step 2: Profile photo
- High-res (1000x1000px minimum)
- Smiling, facing camera, professional but not stiff
- Background: neutral or workplace, not selfie-style
- If you don't have one: pay ₹2,000-5,000 for a 30-min portrait shoot from a local photographer

### Step 3: Banner image
Two options:
- **Branded:** RealtyFlow logo + tagline + Mumbai/Bangalore skyline photo
- **Cityscape only:** Just Mumbai/Bangalore skyline (cleaner)

Use Canva (free) — search "LinkedIn banner real estate".

### Step 4: Headline (most-read field)
Format: `Role + Company + Mission + Authority signal`

Examples:
- "Founder @ RealtyFlow | Building India's smartest real estate CRM | Helping 1000+ agents close more deals"
- "Co-Founder, RealtyFlow.in | AI-powered CRM for Indian real estate agents | Mumbai + Bangalore"

Avoid:
- Generic ("Entrepreneur" / "CEO" alone)
- Buzzwords ("Visionary", "Thought leader")
- Multiple unrelated titles

### Step 5: About section (most-skipped, most-effective)
Structure:
1. **Hook (1-2 sentences):** Why you do this work
2. **What RealtyFlow does (2-3 sentences):** Plain language
3. **Who it's for (1 sentence):** Specific persona
4. **Why now (1-2 sentences):** Market timing / pain
5. **Call to action:** "Book a demo: calendly.com/yourname"

Sample:
> Real estate agents in India lose 40% of their leads to dropped follow-ups. Not because they don't care — because Excel + WhatsApp + 50 phone calls a day is impossible to scale alone.
>
> I'm building RealtyFlow — a CRM specifically for Indian real estate agents. It includes AI calling (in Hindi, English, regional languages), WhatsApp automation, owner-buyer matching, and project management — out of the box. No 6-week customization. No per-user pricing tax.
>
> Built for solo agents and 2-15 person agencies in Mumbai, Bangalore, Pune, Delhi NCR.
>
> If you're losing leads to follow-up gaps, let's talk. Book a 15-min demo: calendly.com/kalimq

### Step 6: Featured section
Pin 3 items at the top:
1. **Demo video** (60-90 sec) — built Day 15, but you can use a prototype version now
2. **Landing page link** — `realtyflow.in`
3. **Your best post** (from Step 7)

### Step 7: Publish 5 LinkedIn posts (1 post / 2-3 days, starting Day -14)

**Post 1 — "Why I'm building RealtyFlow"** (founder story)
> 18 months ago, I watched my cousin (a real estate agent in Mumbai) lose a ₹2.4 Cr deal because he forgot to follow up with a buyer who'd asked for floor plans on a Saturday night...
> [Build emotional context. End with: "That's why I'm building RealtyFlow."]

**Post 2 — "What's broken in Indian real estate CRM"** (problem post)
> Sell.do is expensive. Zoho needs 4 weeks of customization. Excel doesn't WhatsApp. Most agents I've talked to use 4 tools to do what should be 1...
> [List the gaps. Position yourself as the fixer.]

**Post 3 — "How AI calling works in Hindi"** (feature highlight + demo)
> Yesterday I watched an AI agent call a Mumbai buyer in Marathi, schedule a site visit, send a WhatsApp confirmation — all in 90 seconds. Zero human in the loop.
> [Embed a 30-sec video clip.]

**Post 4 — "I talked to 30 real estate agents — here's what I learned"** (insight post)
> Top 5 things Indian real estate agents told me they'd pay for:
> 1. Auto-follow-up on WhatsApp
> 2. Lead source attribution (which portal is converting?)
> 3. ...
> [Demonstrates customer research. Builds trust.]

**Post 5 — "Looking for 10 beta testers in Mumbai/Bangalore"** (CTA post)
> Building a CRM for solo and small-team real estate agencies. Looking for 10 founders who want 3 months free in exchange for honest feedback...
> [Drive Day 8-9 beta invitations.]

**Posting cadence:** 1 post every 2-3 days. Use LinkedIn native scheduler.

**Format tips:**
- First line is the HOOK (3-7 words, intriguing)
- Use line breaks aggressively (each thought = new line)
- Add a P.S. with a question to drive comments
- No more than 1 link per post (LinkedIn deboosts external links)
- Post Tuesday-Thursday 9-11am IST for max reach

### Step 8: Connection building
Send 20-30 LinkedIn connection requests per day to:
- Real estate agents in Mumbai/Bangalore/Pune/Delhi
- Real estate agency owners
- PropTech founders in India
- SaaS founders in India (community)
- Real estate investors

Personalize each request:
> "Hi [Name], saw your post on [topic]. I'm building a CRM specifically for Indian real estate agents — would love to connect."

Goal: 200+ connections by Day 17.

### Step 9: X (Twitter) — optional but high-leverage for India SaaS
Indian SaaS Twitter is active (#buildinpublic, #IndianSaaS). Many founders launch here.

- Create @KalimQ or @RealtyFlowIN
- Bio: same as LinkedIn headline
- Pinned tweet: "Building RealtyFlow — CRM for Indian real estate agents. Looking for beta testers. DM me."
- Follow: @nikhilkamath @kunalshah @harnidh @VarunMayya — Indian builder voices
- Post 1 weekly thread mirroring your LinkedIn content

### Step 10: Calendly setup
Create a free Calendly account with:
- 15-min "RealtyFlow Demo Call" slot
- Available Tue-Fri 11am-5pm IST
- Auto-send Google Meet link
- Notifications to your phone + email
- Buffer: 15 min between meetings

Link this in: LinkedIn headline, X bio, About section, email signature.

## Risks & Mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Posts feel "salesy" → low engagement | Lead with stories and insights, not pitches. 4:1 value:pitch ratio. |
| Connection request rate flagged | Stay under 30/day, always personalize, decline accept-all-back invitations |
| Profile photo looks unprofessional | Pay ₹3-5k for proper portrait. ROI > 10x. |
| No time to post 5 times | Batch-write all 5 on a Sunday. Schedule via LinkedIn native. |
| Posts get zero engagement | Comment on others' posts 2x daily for 2 weeks to build network reciprocity |

## India-Specific Notes
- Indian LinkedIn is more active than US. Posts about Indian topics outperform global ones for Indian audience.
- Hinglish in posts is FINE for Indian audience — feels authentic.
- Tag relevant builders/founders ONCE per post to extend reach (don't spam).
- Real estate topics on LinkedIn India: heavy interest. Even mundane "I sold a flat" posts get 1000+ views.
- Sunday evening posts work well in India (Mon morning consumption).

- Day 17 cold outreach reply rate >10% (vs ~3% for "no-name" profile)
- 200+ LinkedIn connections by Day 17
