# Pre-Launch Prep — 04: Competitive Positioning

## Objective
Define RealtyFlow's wedge — the one thing you do better than Sell.do, Zoho CRM, LeadSquared, and Salesforce for an Indian real estate agent — so every page, email, and pitch leads with that wedge.

## Why This Matters for RealtyFlow
Indian real estate CRM is a crowded category. Sell.do has thousands of customers. Zoho is the default for SMB India. LeadSquared raised $100M+ and dominates enterprise. When a prospect reads your cold email or lands on your homepage, they have ONE question: "Why this and not Zoho?" If you can't answer in one sentence, they bounce. This document forces the answer.

## User Story
As a founder, I want a documented competitive position — wedge, target persona, differentiator phrases, and battle cards for 4 competitors — so that every piece of marketing copy on the landing page, in cold outreach, and during demo calls leads with a sharp, credible "why us".

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] One-sentence wedge defined (the "we're the only X that does Y" statement)
- [ ] Target persona narrowed (industry, role, company size, geo, pain)
- [ ] Battle cards written for: Sell.do, Zoho CRM, LeadSquared, Salesforce Essentials
- [ ] 5 differentiator phrases drafted (for landing page, ads, emails)
- [ ] "Why not [competitor]" objection responses prepared
- [ ] Pricing comparison chart drafted
- [ ] 3 anti-positions defined (what you are NOT — to repel wrong customers)
- [ ] Validated with 3+ real estate agents ("does this resonate?")

## Implementation Steps

### Step 1: Pick a wedge (this is the hardest decision)
A wedge is the one capability you'd bet the company on. Examples of possible RealtyFlow wedges:

| Wedge | Audience | Sound Bite |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| **AI calling built-in** | Solo agents who can't hire callers | "The only Indian real estate CRM with built-in AI that calls leads for you" |
| **Owner-buyer matchmaking** | Resale-focused agencies | "Auto-match property owners with the right buyer in seconds" |
| **WhatsApp-first follow-up** | Agents in tier-2/3 cities | "The only CRM where lead-to-deal happens entirely on WhatsApp" |
| **Multi-city project network** | Bangalore/Mumbai agents handling NRI deals | "Manage Mumbai + Dubai inventory in one CRM" |
| **Built-in compliance (RERA)** | Larger agencies | "Auto-generate RERA-compliant docs from your pipeline" |

**Pick ONE.** Don't try to lead with all five. Test which resonates in Step 8.

### Step 2: Target persona — get specific
A persona statement should fit in one sentence:

> "Solo or 2-5 person residential real estate agencies in Mumbai/Bangalore/Pune/Delhi-NCR who currently manage leads in Excel + WhatsApp, are losing deals to dropped follow-ups, and want a CRM that does the calling/messaging for them — not just track it."

Specific = excludes wrong customers (which is the goal). Avoid: "all real estate agents" or "anyone selling property".

### Step 3: Battle cards — research the competitors

Build a battle card per competitor with these fields:

#### Sell.do
- **Their wedge:** Real estate-specific CRM, integrations with 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com, established brand
- **Their pricing:** ~₹1,500-3,000/user/month
- **Strengths:** Brand trust, portal integrations, established workflows
- **Weaknesses:** Expensive at scale, dated UI, weak AI/automation, slow support, per-user model penalizes small teams
- **How you win:** Modern UI + AI calling + tiered (not per-user) pricing
- **Their objection vs you:** "We have more customers, more integrations"
- **Your response:** "More integrations means more cost and complexity. RealtyFlow ships what you actually use — pipeline, calls, messages — at half the price."

#### Zoho CRM
- **Their wedge:** Cheap, integrated suite, India brand trust
- **Their pricing:** ₹1,300-4,200/user/month
- **Strengths:** Mature, integrated with Zoho Books/Mail/Workplace, brand
- **Weaknesses:** Generic CRM (not real estate-specific), no built-in calling for real estate context, requires heavy customization
- **How you win:** Industry-specific workflows out of the box, no setup time
- **Their objection vs you:** "Zoho works for any business, why pay more for vertical?"
- **Your response:** "Generic CRM = 40 hours of customization. RealtyFlow has buyer/seller/owner/tenant pipelines pre-built. You sell on Day 1, not Day 40."

#### LeadSquared
- **Their wedge:** Lead lifecycle automation, enterprise-grade
- **Their pricing:** ₹2,500-5,000+/user/month, often custom
- **Strengths:** Strong automation, enterprise sales motion, large customer base
- **Weaknesses:** Overkill for solo/small agencies, complex onboarding, premium pricing
- **How you win:** Solo agent and small team focus
- **Their objection vs you:** "We're trusted by big names"
- **Your response:** "Great if you have a 50-person sales team. If you're 1-10 agents, RealtyFlow ships faster, costs less, and doesn't need a 6-week onboarding."

#### Salesforce Essentials
- **Their wedge:** Salesforce brand, AppExchange ecosystem
- **Their pricing:** ~₹2,000/user/month for Essentials
- **Strengths:** Brand, ecosystem
- **Weaknesses:** Generic, US-centric, no India-specific (no WhatsApp native, no Razorpay native, no RERA), expensive at scale
- **How you win:** India-first, real estate-first
- **Their objection vs you:** "Global standard"
- **Your response:** "Built for Mumbai, not San Francisco. Native WhatsApp, INR billing, RERA-ready."

### Step 4: Five differentiator phrases (copy bank)

Write 5 sharp phrases you'll reuse everywhere:

1. "The CRM that calls your leads for you — in Hindi, English, or Marathi."
2. "Built for Indian real estate agents, by people who've sold property in India."
3. "Stop losing deals to dropped WhatsApp follow-ups."
4. "Solo agent to 15-person agency — one CRM, no per-user pricing tax."
5. "Pipeline + calls + WhatsApp + reports. No 40-hour Zoho customization."

These go on landing page, in outreach, on Product Hunt copy, on LinkedIn.

### Step 5: "Why not X" objection responses
Document 3-5 word answers to:
- "Why not Sell.do?" → "Half the price, modern AI calling, tier pricing"
- "Why not Zoho?" → "Pre-built real estate workflows, no customization needed"
- "Why not LeadSquared?" → "Built for small teams, not enterprise"
- "Why not stick with Excel?" → "Excel doesn't call leads or follow up while you sleep"
- "Why not free?" → "AI calling alone costs ₹3 per minute — we include 50 free mins"

### Step 6: Pricing comparison chart
Build a visual side-by-side. Sample:

| Feature | RealtyFlow | Sell.do | Zoho | LeadSquared |
|---------|-----------|---------|------|-------------|
| Starting price (₹/mo) | 999 | 1,500 | 1,300 | 2,500 |
| Pricing model | Tiered | Per-user | Per-user | Per-user |
| Real estate-specific | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | Partial |
| AI calling built-in | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| WhatsApp native | ✅ | Partial | Add-on | Add-on |
| RERA workflow | ✅ | Partial | ❌ | ❌ |
| Setup time | 1 day | 1 week | 4-6 weeks | 6-8 weeks |
| Free trial | 14 days | 7 days | 15 days | Demo only |

This chart goes on your `/pricing-comparison` or `/vs-sell-do` page.

### Step 7: Anti-positions (who you AREN'T for)
Repel wrong customers actively:
- "Not for: 50+ agent enterprises with custom workflow needs (try LeadSquared)"
- "Not for: commercial leasing teams (we focus on residential)"
- "Not for: international real estate beyond India (India + Dubai only)"

Counter-intuitive, but anti-positioning attracts the right ICP more strongly.

### Step 8: Validate with 3+ agents
Show the wedge + battle cards + differentiator phrases to 3-5 real estate agents. Ask:
- "If I said this to you cold, would you reply?"
- "Which phrase would make you click?"
- "Anything that sounds fake or generic?"

Adjust based on feedback.

## Risks & Mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Wedge is too broad ("better CRM for real estate") | Force a specific X-only-we-do-Y format |
| Wedge can be copied easily | That's fine for Month 1. Wedge is for messaging, not moat |
| Battle cards are based on outdated competitor info | Update quarterly. Re-check competitor pricing pages every 90 days. |
| You feel "boring" picking one wedge | Boring = focused = converts. Generic = bounced. |

## India-Specific Notes
- Indian agents pattern-match on "built in India / by Indians" — lean into this in copy
- WhatsApp-first messaging is a strong wedge that US competitors can't replicate easily
- "Per-user pricing" is a pain point — many agencies feel taxed. Tiered pricing is a wedge.
- RERA compliance is unique to India — leverage it
- Hindi/regional language support is a wedge
