# 5 Meat Formats — RealEstateFlow Ad System

> **Rule:** The meat educates the customer on the offer, product, solution, or problem.
> **Length:** 20-60 seconds after the hook.
> **Goal:** Build belief, demonstrate value, handle objections before they arise.

---

## MEAT FORMAT 1: DEMONSTRATION AD
*Show the product in action. No actors needed. Screen recording + voiceover.*

### Script — Full Version (45s)
**[SCREEN: CRM dashboard loading on laptop]**

VO: "Dekho — ek lead aaya. Client ne WhatsApp pe message kiya: '3BHK chahiye Andheri mein.'"

**[SCREEN: Lead auto-populates in pipeline, status = "New"]**

VO: "Instantly — RealEstateFlow ne usey capture kiya. No manual entry. No copy-paste."

**[SCREEN: AI calling module dials the lead automatically]**

VO: "30 seconds mein AI ne call kiya. Qualify kiya. Property details send kiya."

**[SCREEN: Lead status updates to "Qualified" — agent notification fires]**

VO: "Agent ko ping gaya — 'Hot lead in Andheri, 1.5Cr budget, ready to visit Sunday.'"

**[SCREEN: Calendar shows visit booked — Khata Book shows deal in pipeline]**

VO: "Sab kuch track ho gaya. Appointment booked. Zero manual work."

**[SCREEN: Dashboard showing 47 leads managed, 12 qualified, 3 closings this week]**

VO: "Yahi karta hai RealEstateFlow — harr lead track, harr agent accountable."

### Script — 15s Cutdown
VO: "Lead aaya, AI ne qualify kiya, CRM update hua, agent ko ping gaya — automatically. No WhatsApp group. No Excel. This is RealEstateFlow."

### Production Notes
- Film: Screen recording at 60fps (ScreenFlow/OBS)
- Add: Subtle zoom animations on key actions
- Overlay: Green "CRM Updated ✓" toast notifications
- Music: Upbeat, subtle, low volume (Artlist/Epidemic Sound)
- Captions: ALL text, Inter Bold, bottom third

## MEAT FORMAT 2: TESTIMONIAL AD
*Real agency owner or agent sharing their before/after story. High trust, high conversion.*

### Script — Agency Owner Version (50s)
**[SHOT: Agency owner at desk, looking at phone — natural setting]**

PERSON: "Before RealEstateFlow, I had 6 agents and literally no idea what any of them were doing."

**[CUT: Screen recording of messy WhatsApp group with 200+ messages]**

PERSON: "Leads were coming in on WhatsApp, on Magicbricks, on calls — and I was manually copying everything into Excel. At 11 PM."

**[CUT: Back to person, smiling now]**

PERSON: "Week 1 on RealEstateFlow — I could see every lead, every agent, every call, from my phone."

**[CUT: Quick screen recording of dashboard — leads, agents, Khata Book]**

PERSON: "First month — we closed 40% more deals. Same team. No new hires."

**[CUT: Back to person, direct camera]**

PERSON: "The 2-month free trial convinced me. I was scared of tech — setup took 2 hours. That was it."

### Script — Agent Version (35s)
**[SHOT: Agent in car or at desk — casual, relatable]**

PERSON: "I used to spend 15 hours a week just doing follow-ups. Calling people, sending property details, updating my boss."

**[CUT: Quick visual of phone calls stacking up]**

PERSON: "Now the AI does all of that. I just close deals."

**[CUT: Dashboard showing their personal metrics]**

PERSON: "Last month — 15 closings. My record. And I worked FEWER hours."

**[CUT: Back to person]**

PERSON: "RealEstateFlow gave me my time back. I'm not exaggerating."

### Production Notes
- Film: Natural light, real office/home setting — NOT a studio
- No teleprompter — let them speak naturally, guide with bullet points
- Multiple takes — pick the most authentic
- Add: B-roll of screen recordings, phone notifications, property listings
- Keep it real — mumbai/pune accent is a FEATURE, not a bug

## MEAT FORMAT 3: EDUCATIONAL AD
*Teach the viewer something valuable. Earn trust before asking for anything.*

### Script — "Lead Leakage" Education (55s)
**[SCREEN: Simple animation — leads flowing in from different channels]**

VO: "Har bade Indian real estate agency mein yahi hota hai. Leads aate hain 4 jagah se."

**[SCREEN: 4 icons — WhatsApp, Magicbricks, Direct Call, Website]**

VO: "WhatsApp, portals, direct calls, website. Ab problem yeh hai — koi central system nahi."

**[SCREEN: Animated leads "falling out" of the funnel at each stage]**

VO: "First 24 hours mein: 32% leads ghayab. No follow-up, no track."

**[SCREEN: Counter ticking — ₹20 lakh per year]**

VO: "Average 8-agent agency loses ₹20 lakh per year in untracked leads. That's not a typo."

**[SCREEN: RealEstateFlow pipeline — all channels unified]**

VO: "Solution: One system. Every lead from every channel — tracked, assigned, followed up automatically."

**[SCREEN: Dashboard showing 100% lead capture rate]**

VO: "200+ Mumbai agencies plugged this leak in week 1. Kab karoge aap?"

### Production Notes
- Film: Voiceover with motion graphics OR talking-head + screen recording
- Animation: Simple Canva/After Effects motion graphics
- Data points: Always show specific numbers (32%, ₹20L, 24 hours)
- No sales pitch in the meat — educate first, CTA handles the pitch

## MEAT FORMAT 4: STORY AD
*One specific story, told in first person. Narrative arc: struggle → discovery → transformation.*

### Script — "Priya's Story" (60s)
**[SHOT: VO or talking head — or animated storyboard]**

VO: "Meet Priya. She runs a 7-agent real estate agency in Andheri."

**[VISUAL: Busy office — phones ringing, agents on calls, sticky notes everywhere]**

VO: "Six months ago, Priya was working till midnight, manually copying leads from WhatsApp into Excel. Two agents quit that month."

**[VISUAL: Agent walking out — dramatic but relatable]**

VO: "Her commission settlements took 3 days. Two agents disputed their payouts every single month."

**[VISUAL: Heated argument — silhouettes]**

VO: "Then she found RealEstateFlow. Set it up in 2 hours — alone, no IT team."

**[VISUAL: Clean dashboard — leads flowing in, everything organized]**

VO: "Week 1: Every lead tracked. Week 2: AI was following up leads she didn't even know she had."

**[VISUAL: Commission settlement — 1-click, agents seeing the breakdown]**

VO: "Month 1: Zero commission disputes. Month 2: Her best month ever — ₹4.2 lakh in commissions."

**[VISUAL: Priya smiling at dashboard on phone]**

VO: "She didn't hire anyone new. She just stopped losing what she already had."

### Production Notes
- Use real client story (with permission) OR create composite story based on real data
- Animated version: Canva/Adobe Express storyboard
- Live version: Interview-style with B-roll
- Key: Specific numbers make it believable (7 agents, ₹4.2 lakh, 2 hours setup)

## MEAT FORMAT 5: FACELESS AD
*Screen recording + text overlays + voiceover. No people. Fastest to produce.*

### Script — "The Setup" Faceless (40s)
**[SCREEN: Timer starts — "0:00" — laptop screen recording begins]**

TEXT OVERLAY: "Setting up RealEstateFlow — from zero to running."

**[SCREEN: Signup page — email entered, account created]**

TEXT OVERLAY: "0:00 — Account created"

**[SCREEN: Lead import — CSV uploaded, leads appear in pipeline]**

TEXT OVERLAY: "0:12 — 47 leads imported from Excel"

**[SCREEN: Agent accounts created — 3 agents added to the team]**

TEXT OVERLAY: "0:28 — Team of 3 agents added"

**[SCREEN: AI calling configured — first call queued]**

TEXT OVERLAY: "0:45 — AI calling activated"

**[SCREEN: First lead called automatically — real call audio plays softly]**

TEXT OVERLAY: "0:58 — First AI call made. Automatically."

**[SCREEN: Dashboard showing pipeline live — leads moving through stages]**

TEXT OVERLAY: "1:47 — Full pipeline. Zero manual work."

**[SCREEN: Clock shows 2:00:00]**

TEXT OVERLAY: "Total setup time: 2 hours. Total cost: ₹0 (2-month free trial)"

### Production Notes
- Film: Screen recording only (Loom, ScreenFlow, OBS)
- Add: Timer overlay in corner
- Audio: Keyboard sounds, notification pings — makes it tactile and real
- Captions: ALL overlays in Inter Bold, brand colors
- Fastest format to produce — 1 hour filming, 2 hours editing

## Meat Selection Guide

| Goal | Best Meat Format | Platform |
|------|-----------------|---------|
| Cold audience trust | Educational | YouTube, LinkedIn |
| Warm audience convert | Testimonial | Meta Reels, Instagram |
| Feature showcase | Demonstration | YouTube, Meta |
| Emotional connection | Story | Meta, Instagram, LinkedIn |
| Quick production | Faceless | All platforms |

- **Batch B:** Hooks 21-30 + Meat 2 (Testimonial) + Meat 4 (Story)
- **Batch C:** Hooks 41-50 + Meat 5 (Faceless) + Meat 1 (Demonstration)
