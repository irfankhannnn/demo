# 3 CTAs — RealEstateFlow Ad System

> **Rule:** A good CTA tells the viewer FIVE things:
> 1. WHAT to do next
> 2. WHERE to go
> 3. WHY do it now
> 4. WHAT they'll get
> 5. WHAT it costs (or removes the cost concern)

---

## CTA Matching Guide

| Audience | Temperature | Best CTA |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| Complete cold | Ice cold | CTA 3 (Lead Magnet — low ask) |
| Engaged with content | Warm | CTA 1 (Demo Book) |
| Visited website/video | Hot | CTA 2 (Free Trial) |
| Trial user | Very Hot | CTA 2 (push to paid conversion) |
| Agency owners | Any | CTA 1 (demo — they want to see it) |
| Individual agents | Warm/Hot | CTA 2 (trial — low commitment) |
