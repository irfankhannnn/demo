## WEEKLY AD PRODUCTION SCHEDULE

### Week 1 — FILMING SESSION (1 day)
**Morning (9 AM - 12 PM): Film All Hooks**
- Set up: Desk setup, brand colors background, ring light
- Film: Hook 1-10 (one take each, max 3s per hook)
- Film: Hook 41-50 (UGC style — phone camera, casual)
- Total: 20 hooks filmed in ~2 hours

**Afternoon (1 PM - 4 PM): Film All Meats**
- Screen recording: Meat 1 (Demo) — 45 min
- Screen recording: Meat 5 (Faceless setup) — 30 min
- Interview style: Meat 2 (Testimonial) — 1 hour
- VO recording: Meat 3 (Educational) — 30 min

**Late Afternoon (4 PM - 5 PM): Film All CTAs**
- Film: CTA 1 (Demo book) — 10 min
- Film: CTA 2 (Free trial) — 10 min
- Film: CTA 3 (Calculator) — 10 min

### Week 2 — EDITING SESSION (3 days)
- Day 1: Edit Batch 1A (10 ads — agency owners, cold)
- Day 2: Edit Batch 1B + 1C (15 ads)
- Day 3: Edit Batch 1D + review all 40 ads

### Week 3 — LAUNCH + TEST
- Upload to Meta Ads Manager (5 ad sets, 8 ads each)
- Budget: ₹500/day per ad set = ₹2,500/day total
- Let run: 72 hours minimum before optimization
- Kill: Any ad with CTR < 1% after 72 hours
- Scale: Any ad with CTR > 3% — double budget

### Week 4 — ANALYZE + NEXT BATCH
- Identify top 5 hooks (by CTR and CPL)
- Identify top 2 meats (by watch time and conversion)
- Write 10 more hooks based on winning patterns
- Film Batch 2: 10 new hooks × 3 meats × 2 CTAs = 60 ads

---

## WINNING AD INDICATORS (Kill/Scale Rules)

### Kill if (after 72 hours):
- CTR < 1% (hook not working)
- CPL > ₹800 (too expensive)
- Watch time < 15% at 5 seconds (hook failing)
- 0 leads after ₹2,000 spent

### Scale if:
- CTR > 3% (great hook)
- CPL < ₹200 (cost efficient)
- 50%+ watch time at 10 seconds
- ROAS > 3x

### Iterate if:
- Good CTR, low conversion → Fix CTA
- Good watch time, no click → Fix CTA relevance
- Good CTR on hook, drops off at 10s → Fix Meat
