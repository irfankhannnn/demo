## Hook Writing Rules
1. **3-second rule:** If it doesn't grab in 3 seconds, rewrite it.
2. **One idea only:** Don't combine two hooks into one.
3. **Hinglish ratio:** Hooks 1, 3, 12, 22, 42 use Hinglish — test both Hindi and English versions.
4. **Specificity wins:** "₹20 lakh" beats "a lot of money." "32%" beats "many."
5. **Pain or curiosity:** Every hook is either a pain point or a curiosity gap — never just a feature.
6. **Audience match:** Match hook awareness level to the targeting (cold = unaware/problem aware, retargeting = solution/product aware).
