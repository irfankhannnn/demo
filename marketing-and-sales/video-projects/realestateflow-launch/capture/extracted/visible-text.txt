REALESTATEFLOW — SOURCE MATERIAL (no-capture path)
==================================================

CATEGORY
Real Estate Operating System. Not "a CRM".

CORE REFRAME (approved for this build)
"WhatsApp rahega. Chaos jayega."
(WhatsApp stays. The chaos goes.)

THE THESIS
"Har CRM India mein fail hota hai — kyunki broker WhatsApp nahi chhodta.
Toh humne CRM ko WhatsApp ke andar daal diya."
(Every CRM fails in India because the broker won't leave WhatsApp.
So we put the CRM inside WhatsApp.)

SUPPORTING LINES (approved)
- "WhatsApp aapka darwaza hai. Aapka daftar nahi."   (WhatsApp is your door. Not your office.)
- "Ab CRM kholna nahi padega. CRM aapko WhatsApp pe milega."
- "Ek naya employee. Wahi WhatsApp. Zero training."
- "Apni agency ka Real Estate OS."

THE ENEMY
Not WhatsApp the app — WhatsApp as your database, memory, ledger and filing cabinet.
The ask is near-zero behaviour change: keep WhatsApp, put a system behind it.

PILLARS
Control · Visibility · Risk Reduction · Growth Enablement

AUDIENCE
Owner/principal of an Indian real-estate agency. 2-15 staff. Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Pune.
Age 28-45. Android, one hand, at night, sound often ON. Speaks Hinglish, reads English,
feels in Hindi. Buying trigger: the moment they lose money or face to a mistake they can't explain.

VERIFIED FEATURE SET — the only features this video may show
(checked against the codebase; see 14-METRICS-CLAIMS-REVIEW.md §2.3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

LEADS
  Buyer · Seller · Owner · Tenant leads · Enquiries · B2B leads
  Lead status: new, contacted, qualified, negotiating, converted, lost
  Search by name, phone or area. Convert lead to buyer/owner/tenant/customer.

PEOPLE
  Buyers · Owners · Tenants · Customers · Contacts

INVENTORY
  Properties · Projects · Developers · Areas · Rented properties · Buildings

KHATA BOOK
  To Give / To Take, per party, per property, with settlement status and reminders.
  Categories: Brokerage · Maintenance · Deep Cleaning · Repair · Security Deposit ·
  Rent · Utility Bills

OPERATIONS
  Calendar & meetings · Notes · Documents · Call recordings · Team hierarchy ·
  Business analytics · Billing

INTELLIGENCE
  Daily brief · Pipeline summary · Priority leads · Follow-up summary ·
  Business health · Next-action suggestions

WHATSAPP AI EMPLOYEE (the differentiator)
  Connect your OWN WhatsApp number by QR — no Meta Business API needed.
  Runs real CRM operations from inside WhatsApp: create and search leads, add buyers,
  owners, tenants and contacts, log properties, schedule meetings, write notes,
  attach documents.
  Understands Hinglish: kharidar, kirayedar, makan, malik, sampark, milan, kitne, dikhao.
  Replies in Hinglish, inside WhatsApp, 24x7.
