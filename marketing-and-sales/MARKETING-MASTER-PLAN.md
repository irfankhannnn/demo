## PART 4: AD TESTING PLAYBOOK

### Meta Ads A/B Testing Framework

**Phase 1: Creative Testing (Week 5–6)**
Test 9 ad variants across 3 ad sets:

| Ad Set | Creative A | Creative B | Creative C |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Agency Owners Mumbai | Lead leakage stat poster | Dashboard mock | Hinglish pain headline |
| Agents Mumbai | "Save 10 hrs/week" | AI calling demo clip | Time audit chart |
| Agency Owners Pune | "Pune agencies losing ₹15L/yr" | OS positioning | Commission disputes |

**Test parameters:**
- Budget: ₹500/day per ad set (₹1,500/day total)
- Duration: 5 days minimum per test
- Primary metric: Cost per lead (CPL)
- Kill threshold: CPL > ₹500 after 1,000 impressions
- Winner criteria: Lowest CPL with >3 conversions

**Phase 2: Audience Testing (Week 7–8)**
Keep winning creatives, test audiences:

| Test | Audience A | Audience B | Audience C |
|------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Test 1 | Interest: Real Estate + CRM | Lookalike: Website visitors | Job title: Broker/Agent |
| Test 2 | Mumbai only | Mumbai + Pune | Mumbai + Pune + Delhi |
| Test 3 | Age 25–35 | Age 35–50 | Age 25–50 broad |

**Phase 3: Offer Testing (Week 9–10)**
Keep winning creative + audience, test offers:
- Offer A: "2-Month Free Trial"
- Offer B: "Book Free 30-Min Demo"
- Offer C: "Download Free Lead Audit Template"
- Offer D: "Watch 2-Min Product Video"

**Phase 4: Scale (Week 11+)**
- Take winning combination → increase daily budget 20%/day
- Launch retargeting (website visitors, video viewers, page engagers)
- Create Lookalike audiences from converters

## PART 5: OUTREACH EXECUTION PLAN

### Email Outreach (Cold)

**Daily volume:** Start 200/day → scale to 500/day by Week 8
**Tool:** Instantly.ai (₹5K/month, unlimited accounts)
**Warm-up:** 2 weeks on 3 email accounts before blasting

**Sequence structure (per persona):**

```
Day 0:  Email 1 — Pain hook (lead leakage / time wasted / succession)
Day 3:  Email 2 — Follow-up with proof point
Day 7:  Email 3 — Social proof (testimonial)
Day 14: Email 4 — Breakup email with free resource
```

**Persona split:**
- 50% Priya (agency owners) — highest LTV
- 35% Arjun (agents) — fastest conversion
- 15% Suresh (senior owners) — warm intro only

### WhatsApp Outreach

**Tool:** WhatsApp Business API (via Interakt or Wati — ₹2K/month)
**Daily volume:** 100 messages/day (to avoid bans)

**Broadcast sequence:**
```
Day 0:  "Hi [Name], saw you manage [X] agents in [City]. Quick question — what % of your leads actually get followed up?"
Day 3:  "Most agencies we audit lose 32% of leads. We built a tool that fixes this. 2-min video: [link]"
Day 7:  "200+ agencies in Mumbai already use RealEstateFlow. Book a free 30-min demo: [Calendly link]"
Day 14: "[Testimonial screenshot] — This broker closed 3 extra deals in month 1. Want to see how? Reply YES"
```

### LinkedIn DM Outreach

**Tool:** Dripify (₹4K/month) or manual
**Daily volume:** 50 connection requests + 30 DMs/day

**Sequence:**
```
Step 1: Connect (personalized note mentioning mutual connection or their agency)
Step 2: Day 2 after accept → Value message (share a blog post or video)
Step 3: Day 5 → Soft pitch ("We help agencies like yours reduce lead leakage by 80%")
Step 4: Day 10 → Direct CTA ("Book a 15-min call?")
```

### AI Calling Outreach

**Use your own AI calling product to prospect!**
- Call 100 leads/day from scraped lists
- AI qualifies: "Do you use a CRM? How many agents? Interested in a demo?"
- Hot leads → SDR callback within 5 minutes
- This doubles as a **product demo** — "BTW, this is the same AI calling we sell"

## PART 7: CUSTOMER RETENTION STRATEGY

### Onboarding (Days 0–14)

```
Day 0:  Welcome email + setup guide + Calendly for onboarding call
Day 1:  Onboarding call (30 min) — import leads, set up team, first AI call
Day 2:  Email: "Your first 3 actions" checklist
Day 3:  WhatsApp: "Need help? Reply here"
Day 5:  Email: "How [Agency] used RealEstateFlow in their first week"
Day 7:  Check-in call (15 min) — any blockers?
Day 14: Email: "Your first 2-week report" (auto-generated from CRM data)
```

### Retention Loops (Month 1–6)

| Touchpoint | Frequency | Channel | Owner |
|-----------|-----------|---------|-------|
| Product update email | Monthly | Email | Marketing |
| Feature adoption nudge | Weekly | In-app | Product |
| Customer success call | Monthly (top 20%) | Phone | CS |
| NPS survey | Quarterly | Email | CS |
| Community WhatsApp group | Ongoing | WhatsApp | CS |
| Product webinar | Monthly | Zoom | Marketing |
| Referral program nudge | After 30 days | Email + WhatsApp | Marketing |

### Referral Program

**Reward structure:**
- Referrer: ₹5,000 CRM credit per successful referral (paid customer for 1+ month)
- Referee: 1 extra month free (3 months total free trial)
- Double rewards first 90 days after launch

**Promotion:**
- In-app banner after Day 30
- Email at Day 30 + Day 60
- WhatsApp message from CS team
- LinkedIn post template for customers to share

### Churn Prevention

**Early warning signals:**
- No login for 7+ days → automated "We miss you" email
- <3 features used after 30 days → CS call
- Support ticket unresolved >48 hours → escalation
- Trial ending in 7 days → reminder sequence (3 emails)

**Save plays:**
- Offer 1 extra free month if churning due to "not enough time to evaluate"
- Offer free onboarding call if churning due to "too complex"
- Offer discount (20% for 3 months) if churning due to "too expensive"
- Offer feature request fast-track if churning due to "missing feature"
