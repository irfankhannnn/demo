# Content Types

**10 content types**, each a ready-to-run recipe. A content type (`CT-*`) says which frameworks, cast, language, CTA and Higgsfield workflow to default to. [`production-pipeline.md`](production-pipeline.md) consumes these recipes.

> Read alongside [`framework-library.md`](framework-library.md) (`FW-*`), [`cast-and-presenter.md`](cast-and-presenter.md) (`CH-*`), [`visual-system.md`](visual-system.md) (`VP-*`), [`higgsfield-guide.md`](higgsfield-guide.md) (`HF-*`), [`hooks/hook-library.md`](hooks/hook-library.md), [`ctas/cta-library.md`](ctas/cta-library.md) and [`../10-audience-and-voice/language-and-tone.md`](../10-audience-and-voice/language-and-tone.md).

**Two types are switched off pre-launch.** `CT-CASE` and `CT-PROOF` both need real customers and real permission. We have neither. They stay documented so they can be switched on the day we do — see [`../10-audience-and-voice/claims-and-proof-policy.md`](../10-audience-and-voice/claims-and-proof-policy.md).

**Which cast members are available is open.** Every "Best cast" line below assumes the June roster; the roster itself is gated.
> Open decision D25 — see `marketing-and-sales/launch-plan-v2/00-OPEN-DECISIONS.md`

**Marathi (`mr-dominant`) options are parked.** M1 is Mumbai-only.
> Open decision D24 — see `marketing-and-sales/launch-plan-v2/00-OPEN-DECISIONS.md`

## CT-EDU — Educational
- **Purpose:** Teach 1 useful thing; earn saves + authority.
- **Best frameworks:** FW-MISTAKE, FW-CRM, FW-MYTH, FW-LEAD-LEAKAGE.
- **Best cast:** CH-PRESENTER (authority), or CH-OWNER / CH-SALESMGR / CH-BROKER in labelled fiction.
- **Best language:** mixed → hi-dominant for emotion, en-leaning for data.
- **Best CTA category:** SAVE, then COMMENT / DEMO.
- **Best Higgsfield workflow:** HF-TALKING-HEAD + HF-DEMO-SCREEN inserts · preset `VP-EDU-DESK`.
- **Format:** Reel (talking + screen) or Carousel. **Cadence:** highest-volume pillar.

## CT-DRAMA — Drama / Skit
- **Purpose:** Entertain to teach; reach + relatability + series.
- **Best frameworks:** FW-DRAMA, FW-WHATSAPP-CHAOS, FW-SKIT, FW-CONVO.
- **Best cast:** labelled-fiction duos — CH-OWNER↔CH-SALESMGR, CH-SALESMGR↔CH-BROKER, CH-NEWBIE↔CH-TEAMLEAD, CH-LOSTLEAD.
- **Best language:** hi-dominant; mr-dominant for Pune.
- **Best CTA category:** FOLLOW, COMMENT, then DEMO.
- **Best Higgsfield workflow:** HF-DRAMA-DIALOGUE · preset `VP-DRAMA-OFFICE`.
- **Format:** Reel 20–45s. **Cadence:** the flagship reach engine.

## CT-UGC — UGC
- **Purpose:** Authentic creator-style trust; great for paid too.
- **Best frameworks:** FW-UGC, FW-PROPERTY, FW-OBJECTION.
- **Best cast:** CH-PRESENTER in selfie POV, speaking for the brand — never claiming to be a broker who uses the product.
- **Best language:** hi-dominant / regional (mr for Pune).
- **Best CTA category:** TRIAL, DEMO, DM.
- **Best Higgsfield workflow:** HF-UGC-SELFIE · preset `VP-UGC-FIELD`.
- **Format:** Reel, handheld. **Cadence:** steady; spin many variants for ads.

## CT-FOUNDER — Founder
- **Purpose:** Brand trust, mission, "why we built this".
- **Best frameworks:** FW-FOUNDER, FW-AUTHORITY, FW-CONTRARIAN.
- **Best cast:** CH-PRESENTER (Arjun) speaking for the brand — **never an AI face posing as the founder**, and the founder is never on camera. A text post or carousel under the founder's name is the honest alternative.
- **Best language:** hi-dominant (warmth) → en for vision.
- **Best CTA category:** COMMENT, FOLLOW (COMMUNITY is held under D23).
- **Best Higgsfield workflow:** HF-TALKING-HEAD cinematic · preset `VP-FOUNDER-CABIN`.
- **Format:** Reel 30–60s, carousel, or text post. **Cadence:** 1–2/week.

## CT-CASE — Case Study — **DISABLED PRE-LAUNCH**
> **Precondition:** a real customer with written permission for their name, agency and numbers. No AI character may stand in for that customer.
- **Purpose:** Believable proof with numbers.
- **Best frameworks:** FW-CASE, FW-BAB, FW-LEAD-LEAKAGE.
- **Best cast:** the real customer, on camera or quoted, with permission. Nobody else.
- **Best language:** en-leaning (credibility) + hi quotes.
- **Best CTA category:** DEMO, TRIAL, DM.
- **Best Higgsfield workflow:** HF-DEMO-SCREEN for the product half · preset `VP-DEMO-SCREEN`. (`VP-PROOF-HOME` and `HF-PROOF-HOME` are disabled with this type.)
- **Format:** Reel or Carousel. **Cadence:** post-launch, 1/week.
- **Substitute today:** CT-DEMO — show the product handling a sample lead end to end.

## CT-DEMO — Product Demo
- **Purpose:** Show the product solving the pain on screen.
- **Best frameworks:** FW-DEMO, FW-AI, FW-CRM. The AI shown is the WhatsApp AI Employee; AI calling is not on the approved-claims list (**D26**).
- **Best cast:** CH-PRESENTER lead-in, then real hands on a real phone.
- **Best language:** mixed.
- **Best CTA category:** DEMO, TRIAL.
- **Best Higgsfield workflow:** `[REC]` screen recording of the real product, cut by `[ED]`, with an optional AI-presenter lead-in · preset `VP-DEMO-SCREEN`. The UI on screen is always the real UI.
- **Format:** Reel 15–30s. **Cadence:** 2/week (feature spotlights).

## CT-PROOF — Social Proof — **DISABLED PRE-LAUNCH**
> **Precondition:** real customers. We have zero. "X agencies trust us", customer counts, star ratings and testimonials are all banned until they are true and consented — that includes the "200+ Agencies" trust bar, which is fabricated and must not be reused from any older file.
- **Purpose:** Testimonials, counts, ratings.
- **Best frameworks:** FW-CUSTOMER, FW-CASE, FW-AUTHORITY.
- **Best cast:** the real customer, with permission.
- **Best language:** authentic Hinglish.
- **Best CTA category:** DEMO, TRIAL, DM.
- **Best Higgsfield workflow:** none pre-launch.
- **Format:** Reel / Story / Carousel. **Cadence:** post-launch.
- **Substitute today:** product proof — a `[REC]` screen recording of the real CRM or the real WhatsApp AI Employee, which proves the thing works without claiming anyone bought it.

## CT-NEWS — News / Newsjacking
- **Purpose:** Timely RERA/market/portal news with a broker angle.
- **Best frameworks:** FW-NEWS, FW-CONTRARIAN, FW-CRM.
- **Best cast:** CH-PRESENTER commentary.
- **Best language:** mixed → en for facts.
- **Best CTA category:** FOLLOW, COMMENT, SAVE.
- **Best Higgsfield workflow:** HF-TALKING-HEAD + lower-third graphics · preset `VP-EDU-DESK`.
- **Format:** Reel 20–40s, fast turnaround. **Cadence:** reactive.

## CT-MEME — Memes
- **Purpose:** Relatable humor, cheap reach, shareability.
- **Best frameworks:** FW-SKIT, FW-CURIOSITY, FW-WHATSAPP-CHAOS.
- **Best cast:** any fictional figure, exaggerated and labelled; CH-NEWBIE and CH-LOSTLEAD are the strongest.
- **Best language:** hi-dominant / mr-dominant.
- **Best CTA category:** SHARE, FOLLOW, COMMENT.
- **Best Higgsfield workflow:** HF-IMAGE-CHARACTER (meme stills) / short HF-DRAMA-DIALOGUE · preset `VP-DRAMA-OFFICE`.
- **Format:** Reel / single image / Story. **Cadence:** opportunistic, frequent.

## CT-AUTHORITY — Authority
- **Purpose:** Deep expertise; thought leadership; LinkedIn cross-post.
- **Best frameworks:** FW-AUTHORITY, FW-CONTRARIAN, FW-CRM, FW-MYTH.
- **Best cast:** CH-PRESENTER, or a text post under the founder's name.
- **Best language:** en-leaning + Hinglish hooks.
- **Best CTA category:** COMMUNITY, FOLLOW, SAVE.
- **Best Higgsfield workflow:** HF-TALKING-HEAD cinematic · preset `VP-FOUNDER-CABIN`.
- **Format:** Reel + Carousel + LinkedIn. **Cadence:** 1–2/week.
