# Cast and Presenter

**One brand presenter, plus a fictional supporting cast.** Every recurring figure has a stable ID (`CH-*`) and a **character consistency prompt** that locks appearance across Higgsfield generations.

> **The presenter comes first.** The month-1 pack defines a single AI narrator, **Arjun**, who speaks *for the brand* and never plays a broker, an owner or a customer — see `marketing-and-sales/realestateflow/content-strategy-first-month/04-AI-PRESENTER-BIBLE.md`. The bio and the pinned post must disclose "AI presenter". The founder never appears on camera.

> **How big the cast should be is not settled.** Option A retires the supporting cast and lets Arjun carry everything, with this file surviving as an archetype reference for writing. Option B keeps two or three fictional figures for clearly-labelled comedy skits. Both options are compatible with everything below; the roster is what is open.
> Open decision D25 — see `marketing-and-sales/launch-plan-v2/00-OPEN-DECISIONS.md`

### Rules that hold whichever way D25 lands

1. **No AI face plays a real person.** Not a customer, not a broker giving testimony, not the founder, not a named agency's staff. That is why the "happy customer" couple that used to sit in this file has been removed outright, and why no casting row routes to "proof" or "testimonial" any more. See [`../10-audience-and-voice/claims-and-proof-policy.md`](../10-audience-and-voice/claims-and-proof-policy.md).
2. **Skits are labelled.** Any scene with a fictional figure carries an on-screen "dramatised" label. Fiction is honest; fiction passed off as evidence is not.
3. **Proof comes from the product**, i.e. `[REC]` screen recordings of the real CRM and the real WhatsApp AI Employee — never from an actor.
4. **Names do not collide with the presenter.** The tech-savvy broker below was called "Arjun" in the June draft; he is now **Sameer**, because Arjun is the brand narrator.
5. **Consistency:** reuse the consistency prompt verbatim as the character anchor; keep wardrobe, age and face descriptors identical; vary only scene, emotion and camera. Use a saved Higgsfield Character (Soul ID) per figure — see [`higgsfield-guide.md`](higgsfield-guide.md).

**Universe (fiction):** "Apna Properties", a 12-agent brokerage with a Mumbai head office in Andheri. Warm, slightly chaotic, ambitious. It is an invented company and is never presented as a customer.

## CH-PRESENTER — Arjun (the brand narrator)
- **Role:** the single AI presenter. Speaks for RealEstateFlow in the first person plural ("hum", "we"), explains, demonstrates and asks questions.
- **Never:** claims to be a broker, an agency owner or a customer; gives a testimonial; states a figure that is not on the approved-claims list or that the viewer did not supply.
- **Disclosure:** the profile bio and the pinned post say "AI presenter". Any creative naming the AI Employee carries its price and no-trial line.
- **Spec, voice, wardrobe and consistency prompt:** `marketing-and-sales/realestateflow/content-strategy-first-month/04-AI-PRESENTER-BIBLE.md` is the source of truth. Do not fork it here.

## CH-OWNER — Rajesh Bhai (The Agency Owner)
- **Age:** 44. **Appearance:** Stocky, salt-and-pepper hair, trimmed moustache, gold ring, expressive eyes, warm but tired.
- **Clothing:** Crisp light-blue formal shirt (sleeves folded), dark trousers, leather watch; sometimes a navy blazer for "owner" shots.
- **Voice:** Deep, warm, authoritative; speaks slowly when serious.
- **Personality:** Decisive, ROI-driven, fatherly to team, hates wasting money, secretly anxious about losing control.
- **Goals:** Grow the agency, stop lead/commission leakage, sleep peacefully.
- **Pain points:** "Pata hi nahi chalta kitne leads hain", commission disputes, team chaos.
- **Expressions:** Pinching the bridge of his nose; satisfied nod; sharp "kya?!" look.
- **Speaking style:** Boss-elder mix, uses "beta", "dekho", "business hai bhai".
- **Hindi:** "Mujhe har lead ka hisaab chahiye." **Marathi:** "Mala pratyek lead cha hisob pahije." **English:** "I need visibility into every single lead."
- **Camera:** Mid shots, slight low angle (authority), desk/cabin setting.
- **Scene:** Owner's cabin, glass-walled, city view, laptop + chai.
- **Thumbnail:** Face + the viewer's own question ("KITNE LEADS?") + worried-to-relieved. Never a ₹ figure we cannot source.
- **Consistency prompt:** `Indian man, 44, stocky build, salt-and-pepper hair, trimmed grey-black moustache, warm tired brown eyes, gold ring, crisp light-blue folded-sleeve formal shirt, gold leather-strap watch, real-estate agency owner, photorealistic, soft office lighting`

## CH-LOSTLEAD — Mr. Mehta (The Lost-Lead Customer)

- **Consistency prompt:** `Indian man, 41, casual polo shirt, smartphone in hand, mildly impatient expression, ordinary property buyer, photorealistic, home lighting`
