# GTM OS — 30/60/90 Day Roadmap (Phase 14)

Highest-leverage actions first. Content + distribution start Day 1 (cheap, compounding); engineering instrumentation runs in parallel. Workstreams: **Content · Distribution · Sales · Engineering.** KPI gates per phase.

## Days 0–30 — Launch & Measure
**Theme:** content live, conversations started, funnel visible.
| Workstream | Actions | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Content | Run 14-day launch plan → weekly cadence (5–6 reels/wk); establish 9 characters + 5 franchises | Content Factory |
| Distribution | IG daily + stories; founder-engine daily (comments/connects/DMs); WhatsApp lead-nurture; LinkedIn authority started | Founder + community |
| Sales | objections + qualification + demo-script in use; book first demos | SDR |
| Engineering | **EP-1 attribution + EP-2 events + minimal dashboard** (Sprint 1); UTM capture; start Meta/WhatsApp app submissions | Builders |
**KPI gate:** 1k IG followers · hook rate >40% · 10+ DM convos · 3–5 demos · attribution live (sheet ok).

## Days 31–60 — Convert & Automate
**Theme:** turn reach into demos; remove manual friction.
| Workstream | Actions |
|---|---|
| Content | double down winning franchises; add CT-AI + CT-CASE weekly; launch IG broadcast |
| Distribution | FB-group seeding; light paid on proven reels; YouTube Shorts repurpose |
| Sales | follow-up + closing + onboarding scripts; demo-followup automation |
| Engineering | **EP-3 inbound capture** (IG/FB webhooks, keyword auto-reply, Meta Lead Ads, AI auto-qualify) + start **EP-4 sequences** + WhatsApp API (Sprints 2–3) |
**KPI gate:** 5k followers · speed-to-lead <5 min · 15–20 demos · 5+ trials · no-show <30%.

## Days 61–90 — Optimize & Scale
**Theme:** predictable, measured engine.
| Workstream | Actions |
|---|---|
| Content | lock theme-day calendar; batch by character; weekly testimonial |
| Distribution | scale winning paid; WhatsApp community + monthly founder live |
| Sales | referral motion live post-activation; win-back for inactive trials |
| Engineering | **EP-5 scoring + EP-6 referral + EP-7 full dashboard** (CAC/LTV/cohorts/content-ROI) + observability (Sprints 4–5) |
**KPI gate:** 10k+ followers · predictable demos/week · CAC defined + LTV:CAC ≥3:1 · content-`OPP-*` ROI visible · referral % tracked.

## Prioritization principle
Cheapest compounding first (content + founder engine), then conversion automation, then optimization. **Never build scoring/dashboards before attribution exists.** Re-prioritise monthly off `growth-dashboard.md`.

## Dependencies & risks
Meta/WhatsApp app review can take weeks → submit Day 1. WhatsApp template approval gates sequences → submit in Phase A. Content quality gates everything downstream → protect production capacity.
