# 04 — Character System

**9 permanent, recurring characters.** Use ONLY these — never random people. Each has a stable ID (`CH-*`) and a **character consistency prompt** to lock appearance across Higgsfield generations. These are the *cast of the RealEstateFlow universe* (a Mumbai/Pune brokerage called "Apna Properties").

> Consistency rules: always reuse the consistency prompt verbatim as the character anchor; keep wardrobe, age, and face descriptors identical; vary only scene/emotion/camera. Use a saved Higgsfield Character (Soul ID) per person — see `higgsfield/06-higgsfield-production-guide.md`.

**Universe:** "Apna Properties" — a 12-agent brokerage with a Mumbai HO (Andheri) and a Pune branch. Warm, slightly chaotic, ambitious. RealEstateFlow is the system that brings order.

---

## CH-OWNER — Rajesh Bhai (The Agency Owner)
- **Age:** 44. **Appearance:** Stocky, salt-and-pepper hair, trimmed moustache, gold ring, expressive eyes, warm but tired.
- **Clothing:** Crisp light-blue formal shirt (sleeves folded), dark trousers, leather watch; sometimes a navy blazer for "owner" shots.
- **Voice:** Deep, warm, authoritative; speaks slowly when serious.
- **Personality:** Decisive, ROI-driven, fatherly to team, hates wasting money, secretly anxious about losing control.
- **Goals:** Grow the agency, stop lead/commission leakage, sleep peacefully.
- **Pain points:** "Pata hi nahi chalta kitne leads hain", commission disputes, team chaos.
- **Expressions:** Pinching the bridge of his nose; satisfied nod; sharp "kya?!" look.
- **Speaking style:** Boss-elder mix, uses "beta", "dekho", "business hai bhai".
- **Hindi:** "Mujhe har lead ka hisaab chahiye." **Marathi:** "Mala pratyek lead cha hisob pahije." **English:** "I need visibility into every single lead."
- **Camera:** Mid shots, slight low angle (authority), desk/cabin setting.
- **Scene:** Owner's cabin, glass-walled, city view, laptop + chai.
- **Thumbnail:** Face + bold number ("₹20L LOST?") + worried-to-relieved.
- **Consistency prompt:** `Indian man, 44, stocky build, salt-and-pepper hair, trimmed grey-black moustache, warm tired brown eyes, gold ring, crisp light-blue folded-sleeve formal shirt, gold leather-strap watch, real-estate agency owner, photorealistic, soft office lighting`

## CH-SALESMGR — Priya Madam (The Sales Manager)
- **Age:** 33. **Appearance:** Sharp, shoulder-length straight hair, minimal jewellery, confident posture, expressive hands.
- **Clothing:** Smart kurti or formal blazer over top, ID lanyard, smartwatch.
- **Voice:** Crisp, fast, organized; the "operator".
- **Personality:** Hyper-organized, deadline-driven, protective of her team, allergic to chaos and excuses.
- **Goals:** Hit team targets, clean reporting to owner, no leads slipping.
- **Pain points:** Manual reporting, chasing agents for updates, site-visit scheduling mess.
- **Expressions:** Raised eyebrow, decisive point at screen, relieved exhale.
- **Speaking style:** "Chalo focus", "report ready hai?", efficient.
- **Hindi:** "Pipeline ka update abhi chahiye." **Marathi:** "Pipeline cha update aata pahije." **English:** "I need the pipeline update now."
- **Camera:** Eye-level mids, over-shoulder at dashboard, walking-and-talking.
- **Scene:** Open floor, whiteboard, team desks, dashboard on monitor.
- **Thumbnail:** Pointing at glowing dashboard + "TEAM = VISIBLE".
- **Consistency prompt:** `Indian woman, 33, shoulder-length straight black hair, minimal jewellery, confident posture, smart navy blazer over a top, office ID lanyard, smartwatch, real-estate sales manager, photorealistic, bright office lighting`

## CH-BROKER — Arjun (The Tech-Savvy Broker)
- **Age:** 29. **Appearance:** Lean, fashionable fade haircut, light stubble, AirPods, energetic.
- **Clothing:** Fitted polo or smart-casual shirt, chinos, white sneakers, slim watch.
- **Voice:** Quick, upbeat, mobile-native slang.
- **Personality:** Ambitious top-performer, early adopter, hates admin work, status-conscious.
- **Goals:** Close more, look pro to clients, automate the boring stuff.
- **Pain points:** 20 hrs/week admin, missed follow-ups, clients expecting instant replies.
- **Expressions:** Confident smirk, "let's go" fist, phone-in-hand gesture.
- **Speaking style:** "Bro", "scene yeh hai", mixes English freely.
- **Hindi:** "Mujhse follow-up miss ho jaata hai, yaar." **Marathi:** "Maza follow-up rahun jato." **English:** "Follow-ups keep slipping through."
- **Camera:** Handheld selfie, dynamic walk-talk, car shots, on-site.
- **Scene:** Property site, car, café, street — mobile life.
- **Thumbnail:** Selfie + phone + "AI kar raha hai follow-up".
- **Consistency prompt:** `Indian man, 29, lean build, fashionable fade haircut, light stubble, wireless earbuds, fitted polo shirt, chinos, white sneakers, slim smartwatch, young real-estate broker, photorealistic, natural daylight`

## CH-CONSULTANT — Sneha (The Property Consultant)
- **Age:** 26. **Appearance:** Friendly, neat ponytail, warm smile, small earrings, approachable.
- **Clothing:** Pastel kurti or formal blouse, sling bag, lanyard.
- **Voice:** Warm, clear, reassuring — great with clients.
- **Personality:** Empathetic, detail-oriented, client-first, eager to grow.
- **Goals:** Match buyers to the right property, delight clients, build a name.
- **Pain points:** Client info scattered, recalling requirements, juggling site visits.
- **Expressions:** Genuine smile, attentive nod, "got it" thumbs-up.
- **Speaking style:** Polite Hinglish, "ji", "bilkul", customer-service warmth.
- **Hindi:** "Client ki saari requirement ek jagah." **Marathi:** "Client chi sagli requirement ek thikani." **English:** "All client requirements in one place."
- **Camera:** Soft eye-level, client-meeting two-shots, warm tones.
- **Scene:** Meeting room, model flat, with a buyer couple.
- **Thumbnail:** Warm smile + phone showing match + "PERFECT MATCH".
- **Consistency prompt:** `Indian woman, 26, neat ponytail, warm friendly smile, small stud earrings, pastel kurti, office lanyard, sling bag, property consultant, photorealistic, soft warm lighting`

## CH-OPS — Kiran (The Operations Manager)
- **Age:** 38. **Appearance:** Calm, glasses, neat side-part, methodical.
- **Clothing:** Plain formal shirt, simple watch, pen in pocket.
- **Voice:** Steady, precise, process-minded.
- **Personality:** The fixer; loves systems, hates duplicate data and disputes.
- **Goals:** Clean data, smooth khata/settlements, zero process leaks.
- **Pain points:** Commission disputes, duplicate records, manual reconciliation.

- **Camera:** Stable mids, screen-share inserts, document close-ups.
- **Scene:** Back-office desk, ledger screen, files.
- **Thumbnail:** Calm face + "₹ HISAAB = ZERO JHAGDA".
- **Consistency prompt:** `Indian man, 38, neat side-part hair, rectangular glasses, plain light formal shirt, simple watch, calm methodical operations manager, photorealistic, even office lighting`

## CH-NEWBIE — Rohan (The New Joiner)
- **Age:** 23. **Appearance:** Fresh, slightly nervous, neat but budget clothes, eager eyes.
- **Clothing:** Simple shirt slightly oversized, backpack, basic phone.
- **Voice:** Hesitant, curious, learns fast.
- **Personality:** Eager, overwhelmed, asks the "dumb" (relatable) questions.
- **Goals:** Not mess up, learn the system, make first sale.
- **Pain points:** Doesn't know the process, scared of deleting things, info overload.
- **Expressions:** Wide-eyed, scratching head, proud first-win grin.
- **Speaking style:** "Sir, yeh kaise?", polite, learner.
- **Hindi:** "Sir, lead kaise add karu?" **Marathi:** "Sir, lead kashi add karaychi?" **English:** "Sir, how do I add a lead?"

- **Scene:** Training desk, being shown the app.
- **Thumbnail:** Confused→delighted + "DAY 1 vs DAY 7".
- **Consistency prompt:** `Indian man, 23, fresh-faced, slightly oversized simple shirt, backpack, eager nervous expression, new real-estate trainee, photorealistic, office lighting`

## CH-LOSTLEAD — Mr. Mehta (The Lost-Lead Customer)
- **Age:** 41. **Appearance:** Ordinary buyer, casual, mildly impatient.
- **Clothing:** Polo/casual shirt, phone always in hand.
- **Voice:** Neutral, becomes annoyed when ignored.
- **Personality:** Ready-to-buy but impatient; goes to whoever responds first.
- **Goals:** Buy a home/property without chasing the broker.
- **Pain points:** No callback, no follow-up, treated as "just another number".
- **Expressions:** Checking phone, frowning at no-reply, shrug-and-leave.
- **Speaking style:** "Bhai reply hi nahi aaya", customer-frustration.
- **Hindi:** "2 din ho gaye, koi call nahi aaya." **Marathi:** "2 divas zhale, konacha call nahi." **English:** "Two days, not a single callback."
- **Camera:** Reaction shots, phone close-ups, "walking to competitor".
- **Scene:** Home/sofa with phone, or outside a rival's office.
- **Thumbnail:** Frustrated buyer + "LEAD GONE 👋".
- **Consistency prompt:** `Indian man, 41, casual polo shirt, smartphone in hand, mildly impatient expression, ordinary property buyer, photorealistic, home lighting`

- **Thumbnail:** Couple + keys + "SMOOTH DEAL ✅".
- **Consistency prompt:** `Indian couple, woman 35 and man 38, smart-casual festive clothes, holding new house keys, warm satisfied smiles, happy property buyers, photorealistic, golden-hour lighting`

## Casting Cheat-Sheet (which character for which job)

| Need | Lead character(s) |
|---|---|
| ROI / money / control | CH-OWNER |
| Team visibility / reporting | CH-SALESMGR, CH-TEAMLEAD |
| Daily agent life / AI / mobile | CH-BROKER |
| Client empathy / matching | CH-CONSULTANT |
| Khata / disputes / data clean | CH-OPS |
| Onboarding / "easy to learn" | CH-NEWBIE |
| Lead leakage / fear / loss | CH-LOSTLEAD (+ CH-OWNER reaction) |
| Proof / testimonial | CH-HAPPY, CH-CONSULTANT |
| Drama/skit duos | CH-OWNER↔CH-SALESMGR, CH-SALESMGR↔CH-BROKER, CH-NEWBIE↔CH-TEAMLEAD |

> **Per-business reuse:** keep these 9 *roles*; re-skin name/look/language for a new business in `workspaces/<business>/casting.md`.
