# Attention OS — Virality Engine

**What makes RealEstateFlow content spread in the Mumbai/Pune broker niche.** Reach is fuel for the whole funnel. Integrates with `CT-DRAMA`, `CT-MEME`, `FW-CONTRARIAN`, characters (`CH-*`).

## 1. Share & Save Triggers (the two reach multipliers)
**Shares come from:** "this is SO us" recognition (drama), status flex ("send to your team"), useful-to-others ("send to a broker who forgets follow-ups"), humour (skits/memes), regional pride (Marathi for Pune).
**Saves come from:** lists ("3 galtiyan"), how-to, templates, math/ROI, "I'll need this later" reference content.

## 2. The "Tag a Broker" Mechanic
Every relatable-pain piece ends with a tag prompt: "Tag a broker jo aaj bhi WhatsApp pe leads sambhalta hai." Each tag = a free new viewer + social proof. Works best on CT-DRAMA / CT-MEME / FW-WHATSAPP-CHAOS.

## 3. Repeatable Viral Formats (franchises)
| Franchise | Engine | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **"Apna Properties" office drama** | CH-OWNER↔CH-SALESMGR skits (FW-DRAMA) | 2–3/week |
| **"Broker ki Galti #N"** | numbered mistakes (FW-MISTAKE) | weekly |
| **"AI ne kya kiya aaj"** | AI wow moments (FW-AI) | weekly |
| **"WhatsApp Chaos Files"** | relatable lost-lead stories (FW-WHATSAPP-CHAOS) | weekly |
| **"Owner vs Reality"** | contrarian owner truths (FW-CONTRARIAN) | weekly |
Series compound: recurring characters + format = predictable reach + retention.

## 4. Controversy / Contrarian Angles (comment + reach)
- "CRM choti agency ke liye nahi" — MYTH bust.
- "Aur leads nahi, better follow-up chahiye."
- "Tumhari team ek mehnga WhatsApp group hai."
Contrarian = comments = reach. Keep it true to business memory, never insulting the viewer.

## 5. Trend-Jacking SOP
1. Spot a trending audio/format (reels) or RE news (RERA, portal price hike, market data).
2. Map to a `FW-*` (usually FW-NEWS or FW-SKIT) within 24–48h.
3. Localise (Mumbai/Pune, Hinglish/Marathi) + attach our pain angle.
4. Ship fast; perfect is the enemy of timely.

## 6. Collab & Cross-Pollination Loops
- Co-create with broker influencers / CP community admins.
- Founder appears on local RE pages/podcasts (see founder-engine).
- "Duet/stitch" broker hot-takes.
- FB-group seeding of the best reels.

## 7. Virality Checklist (score before posting, 0–2 each, ship if ≥8/12)
- [ ] Hook lands in <1s
- [ ] One clear emotion
- [ ] Relatable to broker daily life
- [ ] Share/tag trigger present
- [ ] Save trigger or quotable line
- [ ] Recurring character/format (familiarity)
- [ ] Subtitles (sound-off)
- [ ] Strong, simple CTA

> Reach without conversion is vanity — every viral piece still carries a CTA and feeds `content-to-conversation.md`.
