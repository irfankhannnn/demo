# Attention OS — Virality Engine

**What makes RealEstateFlow content spread in the Mumbai/Pune broker niche.** Reach is the fuel for the whole funnel. Integrates with `CT-DRAMA`, `CT-MEME`, `FW-CONTRARIAN`, characters (`CH-*`), and the content plan (`OPP-*`).

---

## 1. The Two Reach Multipliers — Shares & Saves
**Shares come from** (sending to others):
- "This is SO us" recognition (office drama).
- Status flex ("send to your team / your boss").
- Useful-to-a-peer ("send to a broker who forgets follow-ups").
- Humour (skits/memes).
- Regional pride (Marathi reel for Pune brokers).

**Saves come from** (keeping for self):
- Lists ("3 galtiyan"), how-tos, step-by-steps.
- Templates / frameworks / scripts.
- Math / ROI ("leakage calculator").
- "I'll need this later" reference content.

**Design rule:** every reel should be engineered for EITHER a share trigger OR a save trigger (ideally name it in the script: "send this to…" / "save this for…").

---

## 2. The "Tag a Broker" Mechanic
Every relatable-pain piece ends with a tag prompt:
> "Tag a broker jo aaj bhi WhatsApp pe leads sambhalta hai 👇"

Each tag = a free new viewer + social proof + a notification that pulls the tagged person in. Works best on `CT-DRAMA`, `CT-MEME`, `FW-WHATSAPP-CHAOS`. Rotate the tag target (forgetful-follow-up broker, Excel-loyal owner, AI-skeptic, etc.).

## 4. Controversy / Contrarian Angles (comment + reach)
Comments signal the algorithm → more reach. Contrarian truths spark comments:
- "CRM choti agency ke liye nahi" — MYTH bust.
- "Aur leads nahi, better follow-up chahiye."
- "Tumhari team ek mehnga WhatsApp group hai."
- "AI tumhe replace nahi karega — AI-wala broker karega."

**Rule:** attack the *belief/old-way*, never the *viewer*. Stay true to business memory; no fake claims.

---

## 5. Trend-Jacking SOP
1. **Spot** a trending reel audio/format OR RE news (RERA rule, portal price hike, market data, festival).
2. **Map** to a `FW-*` (usually FW-NEWS or FW-SKIT) within 24–48h.
3. **Localise** — Mumbai/Pune, Hinglish/Marathi, our pain angle.
4. **Ship fast** — timely beats perfect. Keep a "trend-ready" character shoot on hand.

## 7. Virality Checklist (score before posting — ship if ≥9/16)
Score 0–2 each:
- [ ] Hook lands in <1s
- [ ] One clear emotion
- [ ] Relatable to broker daily life
- [ ] Explicit share OR tag trigger
- [ ] Save trigger or quotable line
- [ ] Recurring character/format (familiarity)
- [ ] Subtitles (sound-off)
- [ ] Strong, single CTA

---

## 8. Metrics
Shares/reach, saves/reach, tag count, follower-conversion on viral pieces, reach percentile vs your median. A viral piece with no CTA is vanity — every reach play still carries a CTA and feeds `content-to-conversation.md`.
