# Attention OS — Retention Engine

**Keeping attention over time — turning one-time viewers into daily followers and warm pipeline.** Reach without retention is a leaky bucket. Integrates with characters (`CH-*`), `distribution-os/instagram/content-cadence.md`, and broadcast/community.

---

## 1. Profile as a Landing Page
A viral reel sends thousands to your profile in 3 seconds — it must convert them to followers.
- **Bio:** "Real estate CRM jo samjhe aapka kaam | Leads · AI Calling · Khata | Mumbai + Pune 🏙️" + CTA link (demo.realtyflow.in).
- **Pinned 3 reels:** (1) best drama (reach + "what is this"), (2) AI-calling wow (differentiation), (3) case study (proof).
- **Highlights (named):** Demos · AI Calling · Khata · Reviews · Galtiyan · Free Trial.
- **Grid coherence:** consistent covers (`VP-THUMB-*`), `#2563EB` accent, Inter font, logo bug → one-brand feel that signals "worth following."

---

## 2. Series & Recurring Characters = Return Visits
The "Apna Properties" cast (CH-OWNER, CH-SALESMGR, CH-BROKER, CH-NEWBIE, CH-LOSTLEAD…) makes content a *show*. People follow people, not products. Each franchise (virality-engine §3) trains the audience to expect the next episode → habitual viewing.

---

## 3. The Binge Path
Engineer a path a curious new visitor can binge in one sitting:
```
Viral reel → profile → pinned drama → "more like this" via a named highlight
→ a save-worthy carousel → DM CTA
```
Cross-reference within content ("Part 2 profile pe", "iska full breakdown highlights mein").

---

## 4. Posting Rhythm (consistency > intensity)
- Reels 5–6/wk · Stories daily (3–5 frames) · Carousels 2/wk · Broadcast 2–3/wk.
- **Theme-days = appointment viewing** (see content-cadence): Galti Monday, Demo Tuesday, Drama Wednesday, Proof Thursday, AI Friday, Meme Saturday, Founder Sunday.
- Same series, same day → the audience learns the schedule and comes back.

---

## 5. Broadcast Channel & Community Rituals
- **IG broadcast channel "RealEstateFlow Brokers":** behind-the-scenes, tips, early features, polls (off-feed retention that survives algorithm dips).
- **WhatsApp community** for warm followers (see `distribution-os/whatsapp/community.md`).
- **Weekly rituals:** "Monday Pipeline Cleanup" tip · "Friday AI win." Predictability = retention.

---

## 6. Story Consistency (the daily touch)
Stories keep you in the top tray. Daily mix: 1 value tip + 1 BTS/poll + 1 social proof + (some days) 1 CTA. Interactive stickers train the algorithm to surface you and create micro-conversations (→ DMs).

---

## 7. Retention Loops
| Loop | Mechanism | Fuel |
|---|---|---|
| **Save loop** | reference content re-opened/shared | CT-EDU carousels |
| **Series loop** | "next episode" expectation | franchises |
| **Reply loop** | 1:1 DM/comment replies → loyalty → early engagement on new posts | comment SOP |
| **Community loop** | broadcast/WhatsApp → off-feed retention | rituals |
| **Streak loop** | daily stories → habitual check-ins | story cadence |

---

## 8. Metrics
| Metric | Why | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Follower retention (unfollow rate) | are we keeping them? | low/falling |
| Returning-viewer % | habit forming? | rising |
| Story completion rate | daily hold | >70% |
| Broadcast open rate | off-feed retention | >30% |
| Profile→link CTR | profile converting? | rising |

Tracked in `growth-dashboard.md`. Falling hold on a series = refresh the *format*, keep the *characters*.
