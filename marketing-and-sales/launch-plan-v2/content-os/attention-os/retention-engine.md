# Attention OS — Retention Engine

**Keeping attention over time — turning one-time viewers into daily followers and warm pipeline.** Integrates with characters (`CH-*`), content cadence, and broadcast channels.

## 1. Profile as a Landing Page
- **Bio:** "Real estate CRM jo samjhe aapka kaam | Leads · AI Calling · Khata | Mumbai+Pune" + CTA link (demo.realtyflow.in).
- **Pinned 3 reels:** (1) best drama (reach), (2) AI-calling wow (differentiation), (3) case study (proof).
- **Highlights:** Demos · AI Calling · Khata · Reviews · "Galtiyan" · Trial.
- **Grid coherence:** consistent covers (VP-THUMB), blue accent, Inter — one-brand feel.

## 2. Series & Recurring Characters = Return Visits
The "Apna Properties" cast (CH-OWNER, CH-SALESMGR, CH-BROKER…) makes content a show. Viewers follow people, not products. Each franchise (see virality-engine §3) trains the audience to expect the next episode.

## 3. The Binge Path
New follower journey on profile: pinned drama → "more like this" series via highlights → save-worthy carousel → DM CTA. Design so a curious viewer can binge 4–5 pieces in one sitting.

## 4. Posting Rhythm (consistency > intensity)
- Reels: 5–6/week. Stories: daily (3–5 frames). Carousels: 2/week. Broadcast: 2–3/week.
- Same series on the same day (e.g. "Galti Monday", "Drama Wednesday", "AI Friday") → appointment viewing.
- Full cadence math in `distribution-os/instagram/content-cadence.md`.

## 5. Broadcast Channel & Community Rituals
- IG broadcast channel "RealEstateFlow Brokers" — behind-the-scenes, tips, early access, polls.
- Weekly ritual: "Monday Pipeline Cleanup" tip; "Friday AI win" story.
- WhatsApp community for warm followers (see `distribution-os/whatsapp/community.md`).

## 6. Story Consistency (daily touch)
Stories keep you in the top tray. Daily mix: 1 value tip + 1 BTS/poll + 1 social proof + (some days) 1 CTA. Interactive stickers train the algorithm to show you more.

## 7. Retention Loops
- **Save loop:** reference content they re-open → repeated profile visits.
- **Series loop:** "next episode" expectation.
- **Reply loop:** DM/comment replies build 1:1 relationship → loyalty.
- **Community loop:** broadcast/WhatsApp → off-feed retention even when algorithm dips.

## 8. Metrics
Follower retention (unfollows), returning-viewer %, story completion, broadcast open rate, profile→link CTR. Tracked in `growth-dashboard.md`. Falling hold rate on a series = refresh the format, keep the characters.
