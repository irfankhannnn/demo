# ATTENTION OS — 01 — Audience Attention Research

> **Purpose:** The market-research file (`workspaces/realestateflow/02-market-research.md`) tells us *who brokers are and what they consume*. This file applies the **attention lens** on top of it: for each surface (Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook groups, LinkedIn) and each persona (Mumbai owner, Pune owner, broker), it answers four operational questions:
>
> 1. **What gets attention** (the thumb stops)
> 2. **What gets ignored** (the thumb keeps moving)
> 3. **What causes engagement** (save / share / comment)
> 4. **What causes a DM / demo request** (the money action)
>
> This is the demand-side intelligence that powers `attention-model.md`, `content-to-conversation.md`, `virality-engine.md`, and `retention-engine.md`. It does **not** restate market research verbatim — it converts it into attention rules. Read alongside `02-market-research.md` (§3, §9, §10, §12) and `03-language-strategy.md`.

**Inputs:** `02-market-research.md`, `03-language-strategy.md`, framework library (`FW-*`), content types (`CT-*`).
**Owner:** deep-researcher + trend-hunter.
**Status:** Source of truth for *attention behaviour*. Numbers are directional estimates synthesised from market research, not measured analytics.

---

## 0. The one-line attention thesis

> **Brokers don't scroll to learn — they scroll to feel seen, to feel ahead of rivals, and to feel safe about money.** Attention is granted to content that says *"I know your exact Tuesday-afternoon pain"* and is denied to content that says *"transform your workflow."* Every rule below is a corollary of this.

## 2. Instagram consumption — the attention lens

IG is ~60% of broker content consumption (market research §9.1). Sound-off, one-handed, between site visits.

### 2.1 What gets attention on IG (stop the thumb)

| Stop-trigger | Why it works for brokers | Map to |
|---|---|---|
| A **₹ number in the first frame** ("₹5L gaya") | Money = survival; instantly relevant | FW-LEAD-LEAKAGE, FW-FEAR |
| **POV of their exact pain** ("POV: client 3rd time pooch raha hai 'koi update?'") | Recognition reflex; feels like a mirror | FW-WHATSAPP-CHAOS, FW-DRAMA |
| **WhatsApp-chaos visual** (200 unread, frantic scroll) | The #1 daily pain, instantly legible sound-off | FW-WHATSAPP-CHAOS |
| **Marathi hook card** (Pune geo) | In-group "apna banda" signal | FW-DRAMA, `mr-dominant` |
| **Open loop / contrarian claim** ("CRM matlab Excel nahi") | Curiosity gap they must close | FW-CURIOSITY, FW-CONTRARIAN |
| **Recognisable office archetype** (CH-NEWBIE fumbling, CH-LOSTLEAD slipping away) | Casting familiarity = instant comprehension | CT-DRAMA, CT-MEME |

### 2.2 What gets ignored on IG (thumb keeps moving)

| Scroll-past trigger | Why it dies |
|---|---|
| Logo / brand intro in first 2s | Burns the 3-second window on nothing |
| "Transform your business / digital revolution" | Generic SaaS — pattern-matched as ad, ignored |
| Stock photos, foreign offices, suited westerners | "Not for me" / inauthentic; brokers smell it |
| Talking head with no on-screen hook text (sound-off) | Mute = zero message; subtitle-less = invisible |
| >3 min in-feed, slow build | Exhausted audience won't invest |
| Jargon ("lead qualification pipeline", "omnichannel") | Makes them feel dumb → defensive scroll |

### 2.3 What causes engagement (save / share / comment) on IG

| Action | What earns it | Frameworks / CT |
|---|---|---|
| **Save** | Usable tips, mistake lists, ROI math, templates | FW-MISTAKE, FW-CRM, FW-LEAD-LEAKAGE → CT-EDU |
| **Share (WhatsApp-forward)** | Funny/relatable validation of frustration; "tag a broker who…" | FW-DRAMA, FW-SKIT → CT-DRAMA, CT-MEME |
| **Comment** | Contrarian/debate bait, "agree?" prompts, keyword triggers | FW-CONTRARIAN, FW-MYTH → CT-AUTHORITY |

### 2.4 What causes a DM / demo request on IG

| DM/demo trigger | Mechanism | CTA category |
|---|---|---|
| "DM 'DEMO' / comment 'CRM'" keyword loop after a pain reel | Low-friction, stays in-app | CTA-DM, CTA-COMMENT |
| ROI-calculator / lead-leakage lead magnet | Trades value for a conversation | CTA-LEAD-MAGNET |
| Peer testimonial from a *named* local agency | Trust transfer → "if it worked for them…" | CTA-DEMO |
| AI-calling "magic-but-real" reveal | Curiosity strong enough to ask "how?" | CTA-DEMO, CTA-DM |

## 3. WhatsApp usage — the attention lens

WhatsApp is **100% reach, 0% native publishing** (market research §9.1). It is not a posting platform — it is the **forwarding layer** where IG content goes to spread and where DMs convert to demos. Attention here is *relationship-gated*, not algorithm-gated.

| Question | Answer for brokers |
|---|---|
| **What gets attention** | A forward from a *trusted peer* ("dekh ye, apne kaam ka hai"); a reply to *their own* message; a voice note from a real person |
| **What gets ignored** | Cold broadcast blasts, link-only messages, anything that smells automated/bulk |
| **What causes engagement** | A reel forwarded with a one-line personal endorsement; a poll/question in a broker group |
| **What causes a demo** | 1:1 chat that answers a pricing/objection in *their* language within minutes; a voice note demo offer |

**Attention implication:** IG content must be **forward-optimised** — legible as a thumbnail in a WhatsApp chat list, self-explanatory without caption, and emotionally "shareable to my broker group." The DM→WhatsApp→demo handoff (see `content-to-conversation.md`) is where WhatsApp's relationship-attention is monetised.

## 4. Facebook group behaviour — the attention lens

FB groups ("Mumbai Real Estate Agents", "Real Estate Entrepreneurs") = ~20% of consumption (§9.1). Tone is raw, peer-to-peer, less polished.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **What gets attention** | Raw text confessions ("aaj ek ₹8L deal haath se nikal gaya, kyun?"), questions seeking peer advice, commiseration posts |
| **What gets ignored** | Polished branded creatives (read as "ad, report it"), overt self-promo, anything corporate |
| **What causes engagement** | Vulnerability + a question; "kisi ne ye face kiya?"; mini-polls; controversy ("portals are looting us, agree?") |
| **What causes a DM** | A genuinely helpful comment on someone's pain post → they DM the helper. **Value-first, never pitch-first.** |

**Attention implication:** In FB groups, RealtyFlow shows up as a **helpful peer voice** (founder/CH-OWNER persona), not as a brand page. Native text > branded video here. This is a **soft-attention / trust-seeding** surface, not a direct-response surface.

## 5. LinkedIn behaviour — the attention lens

LinkedIn = ~5% of consumption, used for **recruiting agents**, not lead-gen (§9.1). But it is the **credibility mirror** for the *buyer* (owner) and B2B partners.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **What gets attention** | Owner-flavoured authority: "How a 15-agent Pune brokerage cut lead leakage to near-zero"; data, RERA changes, growth stories |
| **What gets ignored** | Hinglish memes, drama skits (wrong register), pure entertainment |
| **What causes engagement** | Numbered insights, contrarian B2B takes, named case studies |
| **What causes a demo** | A "serious, modern product" signal that de-risks the buyer's reputation → DM/booking |

**Attention implication:** LinkedIn = `en-leaning` only (per language strategy §5b). It carries **CT-AUTHORITY, CT-CASE, CT-FOUNDER** cross-posts. It does not carry the reach engine. Its job is to make the owner feel safe choosing a "real" product.

## 7. Attention timing — when the thumb is available

From market research §3.3 & §12, re-stated as an attention-windows table the calendar can enforce.

| Window | Attention state | Best content energy | Best CT / goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **6–8 AM** (coffee scroll) | Fresh, doom-scrolling WhatsApp/IG | Pain-recognition, validation | CT-DRAMA, CT-EDU (save) |

| **6–8 PM** (wind-down) | Reflective, planning tomorrow | Aspiration, proof, demo asks | CT-CASE, CT-PROOF, CT-DEMO |

| **Fri eve** | Relaxed, high engagement | Best reach bets | CT-DRAMA, CT-MEME |
| **Mon AM** | Work mode, low scroll | Avoid heavy launches | — |

City overlay: Pune skews slightly later-evening and more receptive to growth/tech content; Mumbai's 6–8 AM WhatsApp window is the single richest.

## 10. Quick-reference (pin this)

- **Brokers grant attention to:** ₹-loss, mirror-of-my-pain, peer name-drop, WhatsApp chaos, Marathi handshake (Pune).
- **Brokers deny attention to:** jargon, stock photos, logo intros, "transformation", long slow builds.
- **Saves** ← useful (mistake lists, math). **Shares** ← funny/relatable ("tag a broker"). **DMs/Demos** ← peer proof + keyword loops + AI magic.
- **WhatsApp = forwarding layer; FB groups = trust seeding; LinkedIn = buyer credibility; IG = the engine.**
- **One persona, one attention state, one framework per piece.**
