# Instagram — Content Cadence

> **Phase 3 · Instagram Subsystem · RealEstateFlow (brand: RealtyFlow) · @realtyflow_india**
> The weekly/monthly posting calendar that balances **reach (drama) · saves (edu) · demos (demo/case)** while honouring the language strategy mix and the peak-hour windows. This is the operating rhythm that turns the 520-opportunity content plan (`04-content-plan-500.md`, OPP-*) into a predictable feed. Integrates with `reels.md`, `carousels.md`, `stories.md`, `broadcast-channels.md`, and the content factory (`10-content-factory.md`).

**Audience reality:** peak windows **6–8 AM (chai/edu) · 12–1 PM (lunch/meme) · 9–11 PM (drama)**; **Friday evening strongest**, Monday morning weakest (research §12). Android, sound off, Hinglish 70/30 + Marathi for Pune.

## 4. Language-Mix Balancing (tie to language strategy)

Target portfolio (per `03-language-strategy §2` + `04-content-plan-500.md`): **Hindi/Hinglish spine 55% · Marathi-led 30% (Pune) · English-leaning 15%**.

Per week (of ~8 pieces): **~4 hi-dominant/mixed, ~2–3 mr-dominant (Pune slots), ~1 en-leaning (owner-credibility / LinkedIn cross-post)**. Practically:
- Wed drama + Fri AI alternate hi-dominant ↔ mr-dominant week to week (OPP-002/OPP-006 are Pune cuts of the same angle).
- Thu case + Sun founder carry the en-leaning credibility pieces (OPP-240, OPP-089).
- Tag every piece's `language`; pipeline-manager enforces the monthly split (`04-content-plan-500.md §5.5`).

## 5. Batch-Production Workflow

Produce a **week in one sitting** to keep the cast consistent and the factory efficient.

1. **Pull** ~8 OPP-* for the week from `04-content-plan-500.csv` (by theme-day CT + language + city).
2. **Group by character/preset** — shoot all CH-OWNER cabin pieces together, all CH-BROKER selfies together (one Higgsfield session per Soul ID, one VP preset).
3. **Run the factory** (`10-content-factory.md` Steps 1–9): hook → framework → dialogue → VO/subtitles → CTA-id.
4. **Render** in `my-video/`; export covers (`VP-THUMB-*`).
5. **Schedule** the whole week via Blotato to IG+FB+YT at the slot table below.
6. **Stories + broadcast** batched separately (`stories.md §7`, `broadcast-channels.md §3`).

## 8. Sourcing from the Content Plan

Every slot is filled from `04-content-plan-500.csv`, not invented:
- **Default:** pull the highest-priority OPP-* whose `content_type` matches the theme-day's CT and `language`/`city` match the slot.
- **Rule of thumb** (`04-content-plan-500.md §5`): ~60% from the **Strong (6.5–7.9)** band, ~25% high-reach memes/dramas, ~15% deep lead-gen (demo/case/UGC with DEMO/TRIAL CTAs).
- New OPP-* sourced from comment research (`comments.md §6`) feed back into the CSV.
- The 14-day launch sprint (`05-14-day-launch-plan.md`) is a worked example of exactly this selection for Month 1.

> Cadence is judged by **consistency + balance**: did we ship the quota, hit the language split, and keep each franchise on its weekday? Misses break appointment-viewing and the series loop.
