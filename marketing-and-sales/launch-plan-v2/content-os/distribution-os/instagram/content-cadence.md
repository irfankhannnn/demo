# Instagram — Content Cadence

The weekly/monthly posting calendar that balances reach, saves, and demos while honouring the language strategy mix.

## Weekly quota
| Format | Qty | Primary goal |
|---|---|---|
| Reels | 5–6 | reach + demos |
| Carousels | 2 | saves |
| Stories | daily (3–5 frames) | retention + DMs |
| Broadcast | 2–3 | warm retention |

## CT-* mix (per ~8 reels/carousels)
- CT-DRAMA ×2 (reach), CT-EDU ×2 (saves), CT-DEMO ×1 (demos), CT-AI ×1 (differentiation), CT-CASE/PROOF ×1 (trust), CT-FOUNDER ×1 (brand).

## Weekly theme-days (appointment viewing)
| Day | Series | CT-* |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | "Broker ki Galti #N" | CT-EDU |
| Tue | Demo/feature spotlight | CT-DEMO |
| Wed | "Apna Properties" drama | CT-DRAMA |
| Thu | Case study / proof | CT-CASE |
| Fri | "AI ne kya kiya" | CT-AI |
| Sat | Meme/relatable + UGC | CT-MEME/UGC |
| Sun | Founder/authority | CT-FOUNDER |

## Language mix (match language strategy: ~55% Hindi/Hinglish, 30% Marathi-led for Pune, 15% English)
Per week: ~3 hi-dominant, ~2 mr-dominant (Pune slots), ~1 en-leaning (LinkedIn cross-post), rest mixed. Tag each piece's `language`.

## Slot times (IST, from market research)
Reels/drama → 9–11 PM; educational/carousels → 7–8 AM; demos/BOFU → Fri/Sat 1–2 PM & 9 PM. Stories spread morning+evening.

## Sourcing
Pull pieces from `04-content-plan-500.csv` top priority OPP-* per theme day; run the content factory.
