# Instagram — Broadcast Channel

A 1-to-many retention + warm-lead layer that survives algorithm dips. Channel: "RealEstateFlow Brokers".

## Launch
Promote via reels/stories ("Join the channel for daily broker tips + early AI features"). Pin the channel link in bio/stories.

## Content
- Daily/3x-week: one quick tip (follow-up, lead, khata, AI).
- Behind-the-scenes building updates (founder).
- Polls (what feature next? biggest pain?).
- Early access / beta invites / exclusive lead magnets.
- Soft demo nudges (1 in 5 messages max).

## Cadence
2–3 messages/week minimum; never pure promo. Value:promo ratio ≥ 4:1.

## Driving to demo/community
Periodic: "Demo dekhna hai? 👉 {link}" + invite to WhatsApp community for deeper engagement.

## Metrics
Subscribers, open/reaction rate, channel→demo clicks. Tracked in `growth-dashboard.md`.
