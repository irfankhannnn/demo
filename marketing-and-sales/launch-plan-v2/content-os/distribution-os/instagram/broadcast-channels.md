# Instagram — Broadcast Channel

> **Phase 3 · Instagram Subsystem · RealEstateFlow (brand: RealtyFlow) · @realtyflow_india**
> A **1-to-many warm-retention layer** that survives algorithm dips. Reels/carousels chase cold reach; the broadcast channel is a push line straight to the brokers who already raised their hand — no feed, no algorithm gatekeeping. Channel name: **"RealEstateFlow Brokers"**. Integrates with `retention-engine.md`, `content-cadence.md`, `follower-to-demo.md`, and the community/WhatsApp layers.

**Audience reality:** brokers already get 200 WhatsApp messages/day — a broadcast must be *glanceable, useful, low-frequency, never pure promo*. Value:promo ratio ≥ 4:1. Hinglish 70/30 + Marathi for Pune.

## 2. Launch Plan

1. **Create** the channel "RealEstateFlow Brokers" (Meta broadcast).
2. **Seed:** announce across 3 reels + daily stories for the first week — "Daily broker tips + early AI features — join 👇" (`CTA-COMMUNITY` energy).
3. **Pin** the channel link in bio + a permanent story Highlight ("AGENTS 🤝", `stories.md §6` #8).
4. **First message** (welcome): "Swagat hai 🙌 yahan roz ek quick tip (follow-up, lead, khata, AI) + sabse pehle naye features. Pakka useful, zero spam. Reply karke apni biggest lead-problem batao."
5. **Convert existing DMs:** invite every nurture-stage lead from `dm-workflows.md §6`.

## 5. Growth Tactics

- **Reel/story CTA:** end value-heavy posts with "full tips channel mein — join (link bio)."
- **Exclusivity:** drop lead magnets + beta access **channel-first** ("yeh sirf channel pe hai").
- **DM rescue:** auto-invite cold DM leads (the +7d nudge) instead of dropping them.
- **Cross-channel:** mention in WhatsApp community + LinkedIn for owners.
- **Reply prompts:** ask a question in ~1/3 of drops to keep reactions/reach high.

---

## 6. Conversion to Demo / Community

The channel is a **slow trust drip**, not a hard funnel. Periodically (≤1 in 5):
- **To demo:** "Demo dekhna hai? 👉 {link}" (`CTA-DEMO`).
- **To community:** invite to WhatsApp community for deeper engagement (`CTA-COMMUNITY`).
- **To live:** monthly founder Q&A / Pune live (Month 3, `90-day-growth-strategy.md`).
Hand any "reply 'DEMO'" to `dm-workflows.md`.

---

## 7. Cadence

2–3 messages/week minimum; never pure promo; **value:promo ≥ 4:1**. Drop at peak windows (morning tip 7–8 AM, evening proof 6–8 PM). Don't over-post — an over-active channel gets muted, and a muted channel is dead.

---

## 8. Metrics

| Metric | What it tells you | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Subscribers | warm-audience size | grows with each reel CTA week |
| Open / reaction rate | message relevance | reactions > vanity opens |
| Reply rate | conversation pull | rises with poll/question drops |
| Channel → demo clicks | funnel pull | track per drop |
| Mute / leave rate | over-posting signal | keep low (≤4:1 protects this) |

Tracked in `growth-dashboard.md`. Judge the channel by **demo clicks + replies**, not subscriber count.
