# Instagram — Carousels (The Save Engine)

> **Phase 3 · Instagram Subsystem · RealEstateFlow (brand: RealtyFlow) · @realtyflow_india**
> Carousels win **saves + dwell-time → algorithm boost + reference value**. Where Reels grab cold reach and Stories nurture, carousels are the *bookmarkable utility* a broker re-opens before a client call. Saves are the strongest IG ranking signal alongside comments (`08-cta-library.md` cheatsheet). Best for `CT-EDU`, `CT-CASE`, `CT-AUTHORITY`. Integrates with `FW-*`, `CH-*`, `HK-*`, `CTA-*`, `VP-*`, and the content plan (`04-content-plan-500.md`, OPP-*).

**Audience reality:** Android, scrolling at 6–8 AM / 12–1 PM, sound off. They save what helps their business (how-to, templates, ₹-math) and share what makes them look smart in a peer WhatsApp group (research §9.2). Hinglish 70/30 + Marathi for Pune. Carousels also cross-post natively as **LinkedIn documents** + Facebook.

## 1. Slide Architectures (5 reusable blueprints)

Pick ONE architecture per carousel; never blend. 6–9 slides is the sweet spot (enough to earn the swipe, not so long they bail).

### A. Mistake list — `FW-MISTAKE` (highest saves)
| Slide | Job |
|---|---|
| 1 cover | "5 galtiyan jo har broker karta hai" + number + CH-* worried face |
| 2 | "Galti agar ki, toh ₹X gaya" (raise stakes) |
| 3–6 | one mistake per slide + its ₹/time cost |
| 7 | the fix (often the product, shown) |
| 8 | recap card (all 5 in one glance) |
| 9 CTA | `CTA-SAVE` + "save & share with your team" |

### B. How-to / steps — `FW-CRM`, `FW-DEMO`
Cover (promise) → why-it-matters → Step 1…N (one per slide, screenshot insert) → recap → CTA-SAVE/DEMO.

### C. Case breakdown — `FW-CASE`, `FW-BAB`
Cover (result hook: "8% → 12% close rate") → who+city → before metric → what they changed (2–3 slides) → after metric → CTA-DEMO.

### D. Myth-bust — `FW-MYTH`
Cover ("Myth: CRM mahanga hai") → why people believe it → the truth + proof → 2–3 reframes → CTA-COMMENT/DEMO.

### E. Data / math — `FW-LEAD-LEAKAGE`
Cover ("100 leads aaye, 12 convert. Baaki?") → the leakage math step-by-step (₹ per stage) → total annual loss → the plug → CTA-LEAD-MAGNET ("comment 'LEAK' for the calculator").

## 2. Cover Design Rules (slide 1 does 80% of the work)

- Big Hinglish **promise or number** (Inter Bold, `#2563EB` accent on white/cream).
- One CH-* face with emotion (worried → curious), or a bold stat card.
- A visible **"swipe →"** affordance + the slide count ("1/8").
- Use `VP-THUMB-EDU` style so the cover matches the reel grid (one-brand look).
- The cover is also the **grid thumbnail** — it must stop a scroll on the cold feed exactly like a reel hook. Pull the line from `07-hook-library.md` (CURIOSITY/REVENUE/CRM categories).

---

## 3. Swipe-Retention Tactics

Each slide must *pull* to the next, or the swipe dies and saves drop.

- **Open the loop on the cover**, close it on the last slide ("slide 8 pe asli number").
- **Number the slides** ("Galti #2 →") so progress is visible.
- **One idea per slide, big text** — no paragraph walls (research §9.2: nobody reads long blocks on phone).
- **Cliffhanger slide-ends:** end a slide mid-thought so the thumb keeps moving.
- **Penultimate recap** slide is the most-saved frame — make it screenshot-worthy.
- **Visual rhythm:** alternate text-card / screenshot / CH-* face so it doesn't feel like a PDF.

---

## 4. Caption-as-Blog (SEO Hinglish)

The caption is a **mini-blog** that earns search discovery over time (the SEO caption loop, `growth-loops.md §5`).

- **Opener** repeats the hook (first line shows in feed before "…more").
- **Body:** 80–150 words Hinglish expanding the carousel, seeded with keywords: *real estate CRM, lead leakage, lead management software, AI calling for brokers, Mumbai/Pune broker*.
- **Ender:** the single CTA line (`CTA-SAVE` primary) + a soft secondary ("share with your team").
- **Hashtags:** 8–12 from the pillar set (`growth-loops.md §hashtags`).
- Log the OPP-id + CTA-id for attribution.

---

## 5. Design Specs

- **Size:** 1080×1350 (4:5, more feed real-estate) or 1080×1080. Consistent across the whole carousel.
- **Font:** Inter (700 headlines / 500 body). **Accent:** `#2563EB`. Background: white/cream for readability on Android.
- **Numbers:** ₹/lakh/crore, large. One idea per slide. Logo bug bottom-corner every slide.
- **Template:** save a master in `my-video/` (Remotion stills) / brand kit so every carousel is one tap to skin a new OPP-*.
- **Cross-post:** export same slides as a LinkedIn document (en-leaning caption) + Facebook.

## 6. Carousel vs Reel (when to choose)

| Use a CAROUSEL for | Use a REEL for |
|---|---|
| lists, frameworks, step-by-steps | emotion, drama, skits |
| data / ₹-math breakdowns | product demo in motion |
| case study with metrics | AI-wow reveal |
| reference content (save/re-open) | cold reach / shares |

Many top OPP-* ship **both** ways — e.g. OPP-296 (carousel) and OPP-443 (demo reel) are the same "₹5L deal gaya kahan" angle in two formats.

## 8. Cadence & Metrics

**Cadence:** 2 carousels/week (`content-cadence.md` weekly quota), typically Mon (Broker ki Galti) + Thu (case/proof), posted 7–8 AM. Caption = mini-blog. Cross-post to LinkedIn + FB same day.

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| **Save rate** (saves / reach) | the #1 carousel metric — beat reels on this |
| **Swipe-through** (slide-8 views / cover views) | > 45% reaches the recap |
| **Comments** (keyword triggers) | spikes on FW-MYTH/LEAD-LEAKAGE covers |
| **Shares** to peer groups | "send to team" CTAs drive this |
| **Carousel → DM** (lead-magnet keywords) | log by OPP-id + CTA-id (`follower-to-demo.md`) |

> Judge carousels by **saves + lead-magnet DMs**, not reach. A carousel that earns 400 saves and 12 "LEAK" DMs beats one with 20k reach and zero saves.
