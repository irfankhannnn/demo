# IG Stories — Daily Story System

> **Phase 3 · Instagram Subsystem · RealEstateFlow (brand: RealtyFlow) · @realtyflow_india**
> Stories are the **warm-layer + funnel-to-DM engine**. Reels/carousels win cold reach; Stories nurture the audience reach already earned and convert it into 1:1 conversations. This is where a follower becomes a DM, and a DM becomes a demo (see `follower-to-demo.md`, `dm-workflows.md`).

**Audience reality (from `02-market-research.md`):** Mumbai/Pune brokers scroll at **6–8 AM (chai), 12–1 PM (lunch), 6–8 PM (wind-down)**, on Android, sound mostly OFF, between 200 WhatsApp messages. Stories must be **glanceable in 2 seconds, tappable, Hinglish 70/30 + Marathi for Pune.**

## 2. Daily Story Slots (the fixed grid)

Run **4–7 frames/day**, batched, posted across the 3 peak windows. Don't dump 7 at once — space them.

| Slot | Window | Frame type | Goal | Sticker | CTA category |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | 6–8 AM | **Re-share today's Reel** ("naya reel live 👇") | Drive reel views | Reaction slider / "watch" tap | — (internal) |
| S2 | 8–10 AM | **Poll / This-or-That** (broker pain) | Engagement signal | Poll | CTA-COMMENT energy |
| S3 | 12–1 PM | **BTS / cast moment** (Apna Properties) | Warmth, recall | Quiz or emoji slider | CTA-FOLLOW / COMMUNITY |
| S4 | 4–6 PM | **Mini-tip / 1 stat card** | Save + authority | Quiz | CTA-SAVE / DM keyword |
| S5 | 6–8 PM | **Question sticker** ("biggest lead-pain?") | Open DMs | Question | CTA-DM |
| S6 | 6–8 PM | **Funnel frame** (demo/trial link sticker) | Lead-gen | Link sticker | CTA-DEMO / TRIAL / WHATSAPP |
| S7 | flex | **Proof drop** (testimonial screenshot, count) | Trust | "DM us" | CTA-DM / DEMO |

> Minimum viable day = **S1 + S5 + S6** (re-share reel, open a question, drop the funnel). Friday evening: add a heavier funnel push (highest engagement per research §12).

## 3. Interactive Sticker Playbook

Stickers are the engagement currency of Stories. Each maps to a goal and feeds a downstream funnel.

### 3.1 Poll (binary) — fastest engagement
- **Use for:** validating a pain, soft objection surfacing, reach warm-up.
- **Frame formula:** Hinglish question + 2 options + a CH-* face reacting.
- **Examples:**
  - "Leads kahan rakhte ho? 👉 **WhatsApp** / **Excel**" (CH-BROKER confused face)
  - "Follow-up yaad rehta hai? 👉 **Haan** / **Bhool jaata hoon**" (CH-OWNER)
  - **Pune (mr):** "Lead track karta? 👉 **Ho** / **Visarto**"
- **Funnel:** whoever taps "Excel / bhool jaata hoon" = warm pain → next frame offers the fix + link sticker.

### 3.2 Quiz (multi-choice, has a "right" answer) — teach + save
- **Use for:** CT-EDU micro-lessons, myth-busting (FW-MYTH), stat reveals.
- **Frame formula:** "Guess karo: 100 leads aaye, kitne convert hote hain?" → options 5 / 12 / 30 → reveal "sirf 12 — baaki leak (OPP-013)".
- **Funnel:** reveal frame ends with CTA-LEAD-MAGNET ("free leakage checklist — DM 'LEAK'").

### 3.3 Emoji Slider — emotion + low-friction tap
- **Use for:** reactions to drama/BTS, "kitna relate kiya" gauge.
- **Frame formula:** "WhatsApp chaos kitna relatable? 🔥 slide karo" over a CH-DRAMA still.
- **Funnel:** high slide → retarget with the matching reel; feeds comment.md engagement-signal read.

### 3.4 Question sticker — the DM opener (most important)
- **Use for:** opening conversations, sourcing content ideas, surfacing objections.
- **Frame formula (prompts):**
  - "Apni sabse badi lead-problem batao — main agle reel mein solve karunga." (CTA-COMMENT-003 energy)
  - "Konsa CRM use karte ho abhi? Sach bolo, judge nahi karunga."
  - "Ek sawaal poocho RealtyFlow ke baare mein — sab answer karunga."
- **Funnel (critical):** **every answer = a warm DM thread.** Reply privately → run the DM script in `dm-workflows.md`. Re-share good answers (anonymized) as the next story = social proof loop.

### 3.5 Link sticker — the conversion frame
- **Use for:** demo, trial, WhatsApp, lead-magnet, community.
- **Rule:** one clear link per frame, with a CH-* pointing + 3-word Hinglish label.
  - "Demo dekho 👆 2 min" → `demo.realtyflow.in` (CTA-DEMO-001)
  - "Free trial 👆 no card" → `app.realtyflow.in/signup` (CTA-TRIAL-001)
  - "WhatsApp pe baat 👆" → wa link (CTA-WHATSAPP-001)
  - "Leakage checklist 👆 free" → lead-magnet (CTA-LEAD-MAGNET-001)

> **One primary action per frame.** Never stack demo + trial + community on the same story.

## 4. Story → DM Funnels (the money path)

Three repeatable funnels. Each is a 2–3 frame sequence ending in a DM thread that hands to `dm-workflows.md`.

### Funnel A — "Pain Poll → Fix → DM"
1. **Poll frame:** "Leads WhatsApp mein kho jaate hain? Haan / Nahi" (FW-WHATSAPP-CHAOS).
2. **Fix frame:** "Jinhone 'Haan' kiya — yeh 30-sec demo dekho 👇" + link sticker (CTA-DEMO).
3. **DM bait frame:** "DM mein 'LEAK' likho, free leakage checklist bhej deta hoon" (CTA-DM-001 / LEAD-MAGNET).
→ Keyword `LEAK` triggers auto-reply (see dm-workflows §2).

### Funnel B — "Question Sticker → Private Reply → Demo"
1. **Question frame:** "Konsi cheez sabse zyada time khaati hai?" (CH-SALESMGR).
2. Broker answers → **reply privately**, empathize, mini-value, soft demo offer.
3. Hand to DM qualify→demo flow.

### Funnel C — "Proof → Curiosity → WhatsApp"
1. **Proof frame:** testimonial screenshot / "200+ brokerages already on RealtyFlow" (CT-PROOF, OPP-378).
2. **Curiosity frame:** "Andheri ke ek broker ne 60 din mein leakage ~0 kiya — kaise?" (OPP-372).
3. **WhatsApp frame:** "Poori kahani WhatsApp pe — 👆 tap" (CTA-WHATSAPP).

## 5. Behind-the-Scenes (BTS) System

BTS builds the **parasocial trust** brokers need before they DM. Star the recurring cast (`CH-*`) so the "Apna Properties" universe feels real and continuous (the same trick reels use).

| BTS type | Cast | Frame idea | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Office life | CH-SALESMGR, CH-TEAMLEAD | "Monday standup — leaderboard reveal" | 2×/wk |
| Founder voice | CH-OWNER | "Owner ki cabin se — aaj kya seekha" (FW-FOUNDER) | 1–2×/wk |
| Newbie POV | CH-NEWBIE | "Rohan ka Day 3 — pehla lead add kiya 🎉" | 1×/wk |
| Field/UGC | CH-BROKER | selfie at site, "scene yeh hai bro…" | 2×/wk |
| Build-in-public | CH-OWNER | "Naya feature aa raha hai — kya chahiye? (question sticker)" | 1×/wk |
| Customer love | CH-HAPPY | repost a customer story / keys handover | opportunistic |

> BTS frames keep stickers light (emoji slider / question) — goal is warmth + recall, not conversion. Always logo bug bottom-left (visual-system non-negotiable #9), except raw selfie UGC.

## 6. Story Highlights Architecture (evergreen funnels)

Highlights are the **pinned, always-on mini-funnels** a new profile visitor taps after a reel hooks them. Design covers in the brand system (Inter 700, blue/amber, `VP-THUMB` style). Order them left→right as a funnel.

| # | Highlight | Cover label | Contents | Funnel job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Shuru Karo** | "START 👋" | who we are, what RealtyFlow does in 3 frames, link | Orient cold visitor |
| 2 | **Demo** | "DEMO 🎬" | 2-min demo clips, link sticker | MOFU → demo (CTA-DEMO) |
| 3 | **Proof** | "RESULTS 📈" | testimonials, before/after, counts (CT-PROOF/CASE) | Trust |
| 4 | **Leakage** | "LEAK ₹" | leakage calculator + checklist (OPP-163) | Lead-magnet capture |
| 5 | **AI Calling** | "AI 📞" | AI call demos (FW-AI, OPP-481) | Wow + demo |
| 6 | **Pricing** | "PRICE 💸" | Free/₹999/₹2,999/₹5,999 + ROI | BOFU objection (research §8.2) |
| 7 | **Pune** | "PUNE 🟧" | Marathi-flavored proof + local team | Geo funnel |
| 8 | **Community** | "AGENTS 🤝" | community invite (CTA-COMMUNITY) | Belonging/nurture |

> Refresh Highlights monthly; keep covers visually identical for one-brand look. Every Highlight ends on a link/DM frame so it always exits to action.

## 7. Frequency & Production SOP

- **Volume:** 4–7 frames/day, 6 days/week (Sunday lighter). ~30–40 frames/week.
- **Batch:** produce a week of stories in one sitting; upload manually at peak windows (Meta Business Suite / IG app).
- **Language mix (per `03-language-strategy`):** ~55% Hindi/Hinglish spine, ~30% Marathi (Pune frames + Pune Highlight), ~15% English-leaning (pricing/proof). Subtitles burned for sound-off.
- **Pune rule:** at least 1 mr-dominant frame/day; the Pune Highlight is always-on.
- **Rule of one:** one primary CTA per frame; log the CTA-ID used for tracking (per cta-library §"How the factory assigns").

### Daily checklist
1. [ ] Re-share today's reel/carousel (S1).
2. [ ] 1 interactive sticker frame (poll/quiz/slider).
3. [ ] 1 question sticker (open DMs).
4. [ ] 1 funnel frame (link sticker → demo/trial/WA).
5. [ ] Reply to **every** sticker response + DM within the window → hand to dm-workflows.
6. [ ] Note which frame drove the most taps/DMs → feed growth-loops + content-cadence.

## 8. KPIs (Stories)

| Metric | What it tells you | Target (steady state) |
|---|---|---|
| Story reach / follower base | Recall + algorithm warmth | ≥ 25% of followers |
| Tap-forward rate | Frame quality (lower = better) | < 12% |
| Sticker interaction rate | Engagement-signal strength | ≥ 8% of viewers |
| Question/DM replies per day | Top-of-conversation volume | ≥ 10/day by Month 2 |
| Link sticker taps | Funnel pull | track per CTA-ID |
| Story→DM→demo conversions | The actual objective | see follower-to-demo.md |

> Stories are measured by **conversations started**, not views. A story day that produced 12 DM threads beats one with 5k views and zero replies.
