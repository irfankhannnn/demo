# Instagram — 90-Day Growth Strategy

Concrete 3-month plan. Pulls content from `04-content-plan-500.csv` (top OPP-*). KPIs feed `growth-dashboard.md`.

## Month 1 — Foundation (0 → 1k followers)
**Goal:** establish brand, characters, series; learn what hooks land.
- Set up profile (bio, pinned reels, highlights), broadcast channel, hashtag sets.
- Ship the 14-day launch plan (`05-14-day-launch-plan.md`) then continue cadence.
- 5–6 reels/week (heavy CT-DRAMA + CT-EDU for reach), daily stories, 2 carousels/week.
- Launch all 5 franchises; find the 1–2 that pop.
- Founder engine daily (comments/connects/DMs).
- **KPIs:** 1k followers, avg hook rate >40%, 10+ DM convos, 3–5 demos.

## Month 2 — Reach (1k → 5k)
**Goal:** scale the winning series; turn reach into DMs.
- Double down on top 2 franchises; cut what flops.
- Add weekly collab/trend-jack; seed best reels in FB groups.
- Introduce CT-AI + CT-CASE weekly (differentiation + proof).
- Tighten comment→DM and DM→WhatsApp flows; add keyword auto-replies.
- Start light FB/IG lead ads on proven reels.
- **KPIs:** 5k followers, 1–2 reels >50k reach, 40+ DM convos, 15–20 demos, 5+ trials.

## Month 3 — Conversion (5k → 10k+)
**Goal:** make content a predictable demo engine.
- Lock the weekly theme-day calendar; batch-produce by character.
- Weekly case study/testimonial (CT-PROOF) as social proof compounds.
- Scale winning paid creatives; retarget engagers.
- Launch WhatsApp community; monthly founder live Q&A.
- Optimise by content attribution — produce more of what books demos.
- **KPIs:** 10k+ followers, consistent demos/week, defined CAC, repeatable factory output.

## Weekly milestones (every week, all months)
Ship quota · reply all comments/DMs in 60 min · 1 founder long-form · review top/bottom content · update attribution.

## What to double down on
Whatever drives DMs→demos (usually CT-DRAMA for reach + CT-AI/CT-CASE for conversion). Kill low hook-rate formats fast; keep the characters, change the format.
