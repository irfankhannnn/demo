# Instagram — 90-Day Growth Strategy

Concrete week-by-week 3-month plan. Pulls content from `../../workspaces/realestateflow/04-content-plan-500.csv` (top `OPP-*`). KPIs feed `../../growth-dashboard.md`. Goal arc: **Foundation → Reach → Conversion.**

## Month 1 — Foundation (0 → 1k) — "establish the show"
**Goal:** brand + characters + series live; learn which hooks land.
| Wk | Focus | Quotas | Milestones |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Setup + launch | profile/bio/pinned/highlights/broadcast set; run 14-day launch plan Day 1–7 | profile live; Day-1 founder reel (OPP-383) |
| 2 | Launch cont. | finish 14-day plan; 6 reels, daily stories, 2 carousels | first 5 franchises seeded |
| 3 | Find winners | 6 reels (heavy CT-DRAMA + CT-EDU); founder engine daily | identify top 1–2 franchises |
| 4 | Double down | scale winning series; cut flops; 2 carousels | 1k followers; 10+ DM convos |
**Month-1 KPIs:** 1k followers, hook rate >40%, 10+ DM convos, 3–5 demos, attribution live (manual sheet if eng not ready).

## Month 2 — Reach (1k → 5k) — "scale what works"
**Goal:** turn reach into DMs.
| Wk | Focus | Quotas | Milestones |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Scale winners | double top 2 franchises; add weekly collab/trend-jack | first reel >25k reach |
| 6 | Differentiate | introduce CT-AI weekly (OPP-481 type) + CT-CASE | AI series traction |
| 7 | Convert | tighten comment→DM + DM→WhatsApp; keyword auto-replies | DM rate rising |
| 8 | Seed + paid | FB-group seeding; light FB/IG lead ads on proven reels | 5k followers |
**Month-2 KPIs:** 5k followers, 1–2 reels >50k reach, 40+ DM convos, 15–20 demos, 5+ trials.

## Month 3 — Conversion (5k → 10k+) — "predictable demo engine"
**Goal:** make content a measurable pipeline.
| Wk | Focus | Quotas | Milestones |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Lock cadence | theme-day calendar locked; batch by character | consistent output |
| 10 | Proof | weekly CT-PROOF/testimonial; case studies compound | social proof live |
| 11 | Optimise paid | scale winning creatives; retarget engagers | defined CAC |
| 12 | Community | launch WhatsApp community; monthly founder live Q&A | repeatable engine |
**Month-3 KPIs:** 10k+ followers, consistent demos/week, defined CAC + LTV:CAC tracked, content-`OPP-*` ROI visible.

## Weekly milestones (every week, all months)
Ship the quota · reply ALL comments/DMs in 60 min · 1 founder long-form · review top/bottom content by hook rate · update content attribution.

## What to double down on
Whatever drives DMs→demos — usually CT-DRAMA for reach + CT-AI/CT-CASE for conversion. Kill low-hook-rate formats fast; **keep the characters, change the format.** Re-prioritise weekly off the dashboard.
