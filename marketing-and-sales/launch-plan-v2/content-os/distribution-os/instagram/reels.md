# Instagram — Reels (The Reach Engine)

Reels drive 80% of reach. Integrates with `CT-DRAMA/EDU/DEMO/AI`, `FW-*`, `HK-*`, `CTA-*`, visual presets `VP-*`.

## Anatomy (sound-off)
| Time | Job |
|---|---|
| 0–1s | `HK-*` hook + on-screen text + face/reaction (scroll-stop) |
| 1–3s | Name the pain in Hinglish ("this is me") |
| 3–18s | One idea via `FW-*` structure |
| last 2–4s | `CTA-*` |

## Formats by content type
- **Drama/skit** (CT-DRAMA) — duos, office scenes, 20–45s. Reach + follows.
- **Educational** (CT-EDU) — talking + screen, "3 galtiyan", 20–35s. Saves.
- **Demo** (CT-DEMO) — screen-record + face, 15–30s. Demos.
- **AI wow** (CT-AI) — "AI ne kya kiya", 15–30s. Differentiation.
- **UGC** (CT-UGC) — selfie, 20–40s. Trust + ads.

## Length & cadence
15–45s (drama up to 60s). **5–6 reels/week.** Same series on same weekday (appointment viewing).

## Audio
Trending audio for drama/meme; clear VO for edu/demo (ElevenLabs per Higgsfield guide). Always subtitles.

## Covers/thumbnails
`VP-THUMB-*`: big Hinglish phrase + character face + number. Consistent caption bar + logo bug → grid coherence.

## Series (franchises)
"Apna Properties drama", "Broker ki Galti #N", "AI ne kya kiya aaj", "WhatsApp Chaos Files", "Owner vs Reality" (see virality-engine §3).

## CTA strategy
TOFU reels → FOLLOW/SAVE/tag; MOFU → COMMENT keyword/DM; BOFU → DEMO/TRIAL. One CTA per reel.

## Publishing workflow
Content factory (Steps 1–9) → render (`my-video/`) → schedule Blotato (IG+FB+YT Shorts) at peak slot → reply to comments first 60 min.
