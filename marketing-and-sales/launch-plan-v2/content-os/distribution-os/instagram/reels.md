# Instagram — Reels (The Reach Engine)

> **Phase 3 · Instagram Subsystem · RealEstateFlow (brand: RealtyFlow) · @realtyflow_india**
> Reels drive ~80% of cold reach and ~60% of all broker content consumption (`02-market-research.md §9.1`). This is the **scroll-stop → follow → save → DM** engine. Carousels earn saves, Stories nurture, but Reels are how a Mumbai/Pune broker who has never heard of us first meets the brand at 9 PM on the couch. Integrates with `CT-DRAMA/EDU/DEMO/AI/UGC/CASE/MEME/FOUNDER`, `FW-*`, `CH-*`, `HK-*`, `CTA-*`, visual presets `VP-*`, and the content plan (`04-content-plan-500.md`, OPP-*).

**Audience reality:** Android, **sound OFF**, scrolling between 200 WhatsApp messages at **9–11 PM (drama), 6–8 AM (chai/edu), 12–1 PM (lunch/meme)**. They share to peer WhatsApp groups when content *validates frustration* or *makes them look smart*. Hinglish 70/30 + Marathi for Pune. Money in ₹/lakh/crore, never "improve close rate by 50%".

## 1. Reel Anatomy (second-by-second, sound-off-first)

A 30-second reel has a fixed skeleton. Every second has a job. Subtitles are **always burned in** (sound off).

| Time | Job | What's on screen | Mapped to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0.0–1.0s** | Scroll-stop. Hook line + a face mid-reaction | Big Hinglish text (top third) + CH-* face/motion | `HK-*` (line 1) |
| **1.0–3.0s** | "This is me" — name the pain in their words | CH-* doing the painful thing (scrolling WhatsApp, fumbling) | research §6 pain, §10 moment |
| **3.0–6.0s** | Agitate / open the loop | cost in ₹, or "kaise?" tease | `FW-*` setup beat |
| **6.0–22.0s** | One idea, delivered via the framework structure | drama escalation OR 3 teaching beats OR 2–3 demo taps | `FW-*` body |
| **22.0–27.0s** | Payoff / twist / result | product brings order; before→after flip | `FW-*` resolution |
| **27.0–30.0s** | ONE CTA + end-card | spoken + on-screen CTA, logo bug, handle | `CTA-*` (single) |

**The 3 non-negotiables:**
1. **Hook in frame 1** — text must be readable before the audio loads. Pull a hook from `07-hook-library.md` matching the framework (every hook is tagged `best_frameworks`).
2. **One idea per reel** — combining two frameworks dilutes; the hook may borrow a second framework's *energy*, the structure follows one (`03-framework-library.md` pairing rule).
3. **One CTA** — log the `CTA-ID` used so attribution knows which line drove the DM (`content-to-conversation.md`).

## 2. Reel Format Templates (by content type)

Each template = a repeatable shoot recipe. Default character, framework, length, primary metric, and a worked OPP-* example.

### 2.1 Drama / Skit — `CT-DRAMA` (the flagship reach engine)
- **Frameworks:** FW-DRAMA, FW-WHATSAPP-CHAOS, FW-SKIT, FW-CONVO · **Cast:** duos (CH-OWNER↔CH-SALESMGR, CH-SALESMGR↔CH-BROKER, CH-NEWBIE↔CH-TEAMLEAD, +CH-LOSTLEAD) · **Length:** 20–45s (up to 60s) · **Win:** reach + follows + shares.
- **Structure:** relatable office scene → conflict (pain bites) → twist (system saves) → punchline → CTA-FOLLOW/COMMENT.
- **Example — OPP-001** "POV broker scrolling 200 unread WhatsApp, dhoondh raha ek lead": CH-BROKER frantically scrolling (0–6s) → CH-LOSTLEAD message buried → "buyer already bought" (twist) → cut to dashboard, calm → `CTA-FOLLOW`. Pune cut = OPP-006 (mr-dominant). Hook: `HK-WHATSAPP-CHAOS-*`.

### 2.2 Educational — `CT-EDU` (the save engine in reel form)
- **Frameworks:** FW-MISTAKE, FW-CRM, FW-MYTH, FW-LEAD-LEAKAGE · **Cast:** CH-OWNER/CH-SALESMGR/CH-BROKER talking + screen inserts · **Length:** 20–35s · **Win:** saves + authority.
- **Structure:** "3 galtiyan jo har broker karta hai" → mistake+consequence ×3 → fix → CTA-SAVE.
- **Example — OPP-013** "100 leads aaye, sirf 12 convert hue, baaki kahan gaye": CH-OWNER at desk, on-screen counter 100→12, leakage math, plug → `CTA-SAVE-014`. Hook: `HK-LEAD-MANAGEMENT-*` / `HK-REVENUE-*`.

### 2.3 Product Demo — `CT-DEMO` (the conversion engine)
- **Frameworks:** FW-DEMO, FW-AI, FW-CRM · **Cast:** CH-BROKER/CH-SALESMGR hands on phone · **Length:** 15–30s · **Win:** demo clicks + trials.
- **Structure:** pain in 1 line → "dekho kaise" → 2–3 taps screen-record → result → CTA-DEMO.
- **Example — OPP-443** "WhatsApp mein jo ₹5L deal laga tha woh gaya kahan (demo)": pain line → one-tap WhatsApp-lead-to-system capture → reminder fires → `CTA-DEMO-001`. Hook: `HK-CURIOSITY-*`.

### 2.4 AI Wow — `CT-AI` (differentiation reach)
- **Frameworks:** FW-AI, FW-CURIOSITY · **Cast:** CH-BROKER selfie / CH-OWNER · **Length:** 15–30s · **Win:** viral curiosity + demos.
- **Structure:** "AI ne raat 2 baje mere client ko call kiya" → play/describe → result (booked visit) → CTA-DEMO/DM.
- **Example — OPP-481** "AI ne mere 100 buyers ko call kiya, 30 qualified nikle" (top-priority 8.0): authority deep-dive cut + demo cut (OPP-042/OPP-336). Hook: `HK-AI-*`.

### 2.5 UGC Selfie — `CT-UGC` (trust + paid fuel)
- **Frameworks:** FW-UGC, FW-PROPERTY, FW-OBJECTION · **Cast:** CH-BROKER/CH-CONSULTANT selfie POV · **Length:** 20–40s · **Win:** trust + ad creative.
- **Structure:** hand-held "main ek broker hoon" → 1 benefit shown → casual CTA-TRIAL.
- **Example — OPP-467** "Response within 15 min = 60% close, after 1 hr = 20%" (UGC, 7.8). Spin 3–5 variants for Meta ads (ab-optimizer pulls these).

### 2.6 Case Study — `CT-CASE` (believable proof)
- **Frameworks:** FW-CASE, FW-BAB, FW-LEAD-LEAKAGE · **Cast:** CH-OWNER/CH-SALESMGR (the customer), CH-HAPPY · **Length:** 30–45s · **Win:** demos + trust.
- **Structure:** who+city → before metric → what they did → after metric → CTA-DEMO.
- **Example — OPP-280** "Arjun ne 5 deal se 12 deal kiye system se" · **OPP-372** "Andheri broker ne lead leakage near-zero kiya 60 din mein."

### 2.7 Meme / Relatable — `CT-MEME` (cheap reach + shares)
- **Frameworks:** FW-SKIT, FW-CURIOSITY, FW-WHATSAPP-CHAOS · **Cast:** any, exaggerated; CH-NEWBIE/CH-LOSTLEAD gold · **Length:** 8–20s · **Win:** shares.
- **Example — OPP-435** "2026 aa gaya bhai aur tu abhi bhi Excel pe" · **OPP-430** "POV jab client puche mera lead kahan hai." CTA-SHARE.

### 2.8 Founder — `CT-FOUNDER` (brand trust)
- **Frameworks:** FW-FOUNDER, FW-AUTHORITY, FW-CONTRARIAN · **Cast:** CH-OWNER as founder proxy · **Length:** 30–60s · **Win:** follows + community.
- **Example — OPP-383** "Launch day, RealEstateFlow ab live hai" · **OPP-088** "Indian brokers ko US CRM nahi chahiye, desi chahiye." CTA-COMMUNITY/FOLLOW.

## 3. Hook · Retention · CTA Mechanics

**Hook (0–1.5s):** Pull from `07-hook-library.md` by category matching the angle — FEAR/REVENUE for leakage, WHATSAPP-CHAOS for drama, AI for AI-wow, CURIOSITY for demos. Use the **number/loss** pattern ("100 leads aaye, 95 ka pata hi nahi") and **POV/story** pattern for highest stop-rate. Burn the hook as on-screen text.

**Retention levers (1s–27s):**
- **Open loops:** tease the payoff ("dikhau kahan?") and resolve only at the end.
- **Pattern interrupts every 3–5s:** cut, zoom, caption-pop, b-roll insert — sound-off scrollers need visual motion.
- **Visual progress:** counters, "Galti #1 / #2 / #3" labels, before→after split.
- **No slow intro:** never "Hi, aaj hum baat karenge…" — the value starts in frame 1.

**CTA (27–30s):** ONE category, matched to funnel stage (`08-cta-library.md` cheatsheet). TOFU reels → FOLLOW/SAVE/SHARE/COMMENT; MOFU → COMMENT-keyword / DM-keyword / DEMO; BOFU → TRIAL. Pinned comment restates it (see `comments.md`).

## 4. Audio Strategy

| Reel type | Audio | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Drama / Meme | **Trending audio** (lip-synced or background) | algorithm boost + culturally current; sound-on viewers get bonus |
| Educational / Demo / Case | **Clear VO** (ElevenLabs per `higgsfield/06-higgsfield-production-guide.md`) | clarity > trend; teaching needs a voice |
| AI Wow | VO + an actual AI-call snippet | the call audio *is* the proof |

**Rules:** subtitles ALWAYS (sound-off default). For trending audio, check it's not falling (use within first ~2 weeks of trend). VO in the piece's `language` tag (hi-dominant / mr-dominant for Pune / mixed). Keep music under VO at ~15% volume.

## 5. Editing & Pacing Rules

- **Cut rhythm:** a cut/zoom/text-change every **2–4 seconds**. Dead air = scroll.
- **Aspect:** 9:16, 1080×1920, safe-zone text away from top 250px (handle) and bottom 350px (caption/CTA UI).
- **Text:** Inter Bold, `#2563EB` accent on white/cream cards, max ~6 words per line, high contrast for outdoor Android screens.
- **First-frame test:** if the thumbnail (frame 1) doesn't make a broker pause, re-shoot the hook.
- **Length discipline:** kill anything >45s unless it's a case/founder story earning the time (research §12: >3 min = ignored).
- **Logo bug** bottom-left except raw UGC selfies (`visual-system` non-negotiable).

## 6. Cover / Thumbnail Specs (`VP-THUMB-*`)

The grid is a shopfront. Covers must be coherent so a profile visit reads as one brand.

- **Layout:** big Hinglish phrase (top) + CH-* face (emotion: worried→relieved) + one number (₹20L, 100→12, 30 qualified).
- **Per content type:** `VP-THUMB-EDU` (desk + number), `VP-THUMB-DRAMA` (two-shot + reaction), `VP-THUMB-DEMO` (phone screen + face), `VP-THUMB-AI` ("AI 📞" badge).
- **Consistency:** same caption-bar style + logo bug across all covers → grid coherence (the same trick Highlights covers use in `stories.md §6`).
- **Pinned 3:** top-of-grid pinned reels = one franchise hook + one demo + one proof, so a new visitor instantly gets entertainment + product + trust.

## 8. Posting SOP

1. **Source** the OPP-* from `04-content-plan-500.csv` (filter by theme-day's CT + city + language).
2. **Run the content factory** Steps 1–9 (`10-content-factory.md`): hook → framework → CH-* dialogue → VO/subtitles → CTA-ID.
3. **Render** in `my-video/` via the CT's Higgsfield workflow (HF-DRAMA-DIALOGUE / HF-DEMO-SCREEN / HF-UGC-SELFIE / HF-TALKING-HEAD) using saved Soul IDs.
4. **Schedule** via Blotato to **IG + FB Reels + YouTube Shorts** at the peak slot (drama → 9–11 PM; edu/carousel → 7–8 AM; demo/BOFU → Fri/Sat 1–2 PM & 9 PM).
5. **Caption:** Hinglish mini-context + SEO keywords ("real estate CRM", "lead leakage", "AI calling for brokers") + the CTA line + 8–12 hashtags (`growth-loops.md §hashtags`).
6. **First 60 min:** reply to EVERY comment, pin the CTA comment, move buying-intent comments to DM (`comments.md`).
7. **Log:** OPP-id, CTA-id, posting time → feeds `growth-dashboard.md` + content attribution.

## 9. Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Hook + value in frame 1, sound-off readable | "Hi guys, aaj hum…" slow intros |
| One framework, one idea, one CTA | Stack demo + trial + community in one reel |
| ₹/lakh/crore concrete money | "improve efficiency", "digital transformation" |
| Reuse the 9 CH-* cast (Soul IDs) | Random faces / stock footage (brokers smell fake) |
| Marathi cut for Pune slots | One-size Mumbai reel forced on Pune |
| Subtitles burned in always | Rely on audio for comprehension |
| Reply in first 60 min | Post and ghost |

## 10. Metrics & Benchmarks

| Metric | What it tells you | Target (steady state) |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook rate** (3-sec views / plays) | Frame-1 stop power | **> 40%** |
| **Avg watch / completion** | Retention + framework strength | > 50% completion on <30s |
| **Reach** per reel | Cold distribution | drama/meme 10k+, 1–2/mo > 50k |
| **Saves** | Reference value (edu/case) | edu carousels-in-reel-form lead |
| **Shares** | Peer-WhatsApp pull (the #1 trust signal) | drama/meme lead; track sends |
| **Comments** | Algo + objection-mining | keyword reels spike this |
| **DMs per reel** | Conversation volume | log by OPP-id |
| **Reel → demo** | The objective | attribute via CTA-id (`follower-to-demo.md`) |

> A reel is judged by **DMs + saves + shares**, not raw views. Kill low-hook-rate formats fast; keep the cast, change the format. Winners get re-cut into UGC variants for Meta ads (ab-optimizer + media-buyer).
