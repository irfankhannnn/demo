# Distribution OS — Facebook

**Facebook = broker groups (trust seeding + lead listening) + page + lead ads.** Indian brokers live in FB groups for project leads and CP networking.

## Audience
Owners (35-55) and brokers in CP/broker groups; lead-ad audiences. Mood: community, deal-hunting, peer trust.

## Posting frequency
Page: 4–5/week (reels cross-post from IG). Groups: daily value participation (no spam). Ads: always-on lead-gen once content proven.

## Content mix (by CT-*)
| Surface | CT-* | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Page feed | CT-DRAMA/CT-EDU/CT-CASE | cross-post IG reels |
| Groups | CT-EDU/CT-AUTHORITY | give value, soft-mention |
| Lead ads | CT-DEMO/CT-CASE | winning organic → paid |

## CTA strategy
Page: comment/DM/demo. Groups: helpful answer → profile/DM (never hard pitch). Ads: instant-form lead capture → WhatsApp follow-up. CTA: DEMO, WHATSAPP, LEAD-MAGNET.

## Lead capture
FB Lead Ads (instant forms) + Messenger + group DMs. CAPI/Conversions setup (see media-buyer + Automation OS). Tag source = `facebook` / `facebook_ads`.

## Group strategy
Join top Mumbai/Pune broker + CP groups. Daily: answer a lead-management/follow-up question with genuine help; build name recognition; let the profile/bio do the selling. Occasionally share a free tool (lead-magnet).

## Repurposing
IG reels → FB reels/feed; carousels → FB image posts; case studies → group success stories (with permission).

## Publishing workflow
Content factory → Blotato (IG+FB) → group participation manual → winning posts promoted as lead ads.
