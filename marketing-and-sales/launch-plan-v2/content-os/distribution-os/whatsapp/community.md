# WhatsApp — Community Strategy

A WhatsApp Community / group for engaged brokers — **"RealEstateFlow Brokers Club"** — built as a retention + word-of-mouth + referral engine. Where followers become a tribe that defends the brand and refers peers. Pairs with `referral.md`, `customer-success.md`, `attention-os/retention-engine.md §5`, and the IG broadcast channel.

> **Why a community:** peer validation beats every other trust signal in this market (`02-market-research §2.2, §9.2`). Brokers already live in WhatsApp groups; a *better-run* group (value-first, zero spam, no poaching) becomes the room they don't want to leave — algorithm-proof retention even when IG reach dips.

---

## 1. Purpose & structure

WhatsApp **Community** (the umbrella) with structured sub-groups:

| Group | Type | Who posts | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **📢 Announcements** | admin-only | founder/team | tips, feature drops, RERA news, event invites |
| **💬 Broker Adda** | open, moderated | members | peer Q&A, follow-up help, deal-doubt resolution |
| **🏆 Wins & Case Studies** | semi-open | members + team | "closed via the app" wins, screenshots, proof |
| **🟠 Pune Group** | open, moderated | members | Marathi-first sub-group for Pune/PCMC brokers |

Start with Announcements + one discussion group; split by city/topic only once >150 active members.

## 4. Rules (pin these)
1. **Value first.** No "buy now" spam.
2. **No broker-vs-broker poaching** — koi kisi ke agent/client ko target nahi karega.
3. **No unrelated promos / chain forwards.**
4. **Respect** — koi sawaal chhota nahi.
5. Breaking → warning → remove. Light but firm moderation keeps signal high.

## 5. Growth tactics
- "Join the Club" CTA in IG bio + Stories (CTA-COMMUNITY-013), demo/trial follow-ups, founder broadcast.
- Members-only perks advertised publicly: early features, free lead-magnets, exclusive trial extensions (CTA-COMMUNITY-047), monthly AMA.
- Invite-a-peer nudge → ties to referral loop. Founder personally welcomes each new joiner by name.

## 6. Conversion mechanics
Community is TOFU/retention, **not** a pitch room — conversion is *earned* via value:
- Genuine help in Broker Adda → DM the most-engaged → soft DEMO offer.
- Wins group → social proof → "main bhi try karun?" inbound → route to `lead-nurture.md`.
- Members are the **prime referral source** (warm, trusting) → `referral.md`.
- Exclusive in-community trial offers (1×/month) convert lurkers.

## 7. Metrics (→ `growth-dashboard.md`)
Members (total + by city), **active %** (posted/reacted in 7d, target >25%), messages/week, new-joins/week, community→DEMO, community→referral, opt-out/leave rate. A quiet group = inject a ritual or a provocative doubt-thread; never let it die into a ghost town.
