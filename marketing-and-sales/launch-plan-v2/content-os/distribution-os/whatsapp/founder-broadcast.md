# WhatsApp — Founder Broadcast Strategy

A **consented broadcast list** of warm followers/leads the founder nurtures personally (WhatsApp Business broadcast list + WhatsApp Channel). This is the founder's owned, algorithm-proof audience — the one distribution surface that can't be throttled by Meta. Pairs with `founder-brand.md`, `founder-engine.md`, and the IG broadcast channel (`attention-os/retention-engine.md §5`).

> **Why it matters:** brokers spend 2–3 hrs/day on WhatsApp (`02-market-research §3.2`) and trust a real banda over a logo (`distribution-os/whatsapp.md §1`). A founder voice note at 8 AM out-converts any ad. Keep claims at the workflow level (business-memory §4 caveat) and respect WhatsApp Business policy.

---

## 1. Build the list (opt-in only)

| Source | How they opt in | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| IG DM handoffs (warm) | "WhatsApp pe broker tips bhej dun? Reply HAAN" | `bc-ig` |
| Demo no-shows / not-yet-ready | "Pressure nahi — weekly tips list pe rakh dun?" | `bc-demo` |
| Trial + paying users | auto-offer at onboarding | `bc-customer` |
| Community members | pinned join message | `bc-community` |
| Event / meetup contacts | QR → "Save number → reply START" | `bc-event` |

Rules: **explicit opt-in always** (never scrape a group). Save each contact (broadcast lists only deliver to people who saved your number). Cap list at ~256/broadcast list; spin multiple lists by segment + city (Mumbai / Pune) so language + examples fit. WhatsApp Channel runs in parallel for one-to-many at scale (no save needed, but lower intimacy).

## 2. Content mix (value : promo ≥ 4 : 1)

| Slot | Type | CT-* / source | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2–3×/wk | One sharp tip | CT-EDU repurposed | "Aaj ka 1 tip: har lead pe next-action date likho — warna woh follow-up reh jaata hai." |
| 1×/wk | Behind-the-scenes build | CT-FOUNDER | voice note: "Yeh hafta khata book ka naya settlement flow bana rahe hain. Aapko kya chahiye, batao." |
| 1×/wk | Quick win / mini case | CT-CASE / CT-PROOF | "Andheri ki ek 6-agent agency ne is hafte AI calling se 18 leads qualify kiye. Screenshot 👇" |
| weekly | Poll / question | engagement | "Reply with your #1 pain: 1) Lead leak 2) Follow-up 3) Commission hisaab" |
| 1 in 5 | Soft demo/trial nudge | CT-DEMO | "Jisko AI calling live dekhni ho, reply DEMO — Hindi mein dikha dunga." |

## 3. Verbatim broadcast templates

**Monday tip (hi-dominant):**
> "Subah ka 1 tip 👇 Jis lead se kal baat hui, usko *aaj ek line* bhejo — 'sir, woh flat available hai, dekhna chahenge?'. 90% deals follow-up se banti hain, lead se nahi. Try karke batao. — {{founder}}"

**Build-in-public (founder voice):**
> "Sach bataun? Pichhle mahine 3 agencies ne bola 'commission ka hisaab sabse bada sirdard hai'. Toh khata book mein settlement ka naya flow bana diya. Jo try karna chahe, reply KHATA 🙏"

**Pune Marathi handshake:**
> "Namaskar 🙌 Aajcha tip: pratyek lead la 'next follow-up date' lava — Excel athvun det nahi, system deto. Try kara, sanga kasа vatла. — {{founder}}"

**Promo (1-in-5, soft):**
> "Koi pressure nahi — par jisko 2-min AI-calling demo chahiye, reply DEMO. Main khud dikhata hoon, sales pitch nahi 😊"

## 4. Tone & timing
- **Tone:** Hinglish, personal, like a smart broker friend — first name where possible, one emoji max, never a marketing blast. Voice notes 1–2×/week (highest trust, hardest to fake).
- **Cadence:** 2–3 broadcasts/week. **Windows:** 8–9 AM (coffee scroll) or 8–9 PM (wind-down), IST (`02-market-research §3.2, §12`). Never overlap two broadcasts in a day — over-messaging kills opt-in.

## 6. Growth → conversion loop
Grow list from IG bio ("WhatsApp tips → link"), demos, trials, events. Convert: warm list → DEMO/TRIAL (1-in-5 nudges), and seed referrals (`referral.md`) off the highest-engagement repliers. Every reply is a 1:1 opening → route to `lead-nurture.md` if buying-intent.

## 7. Compliance
Opt-in required; easy opt-out ("STOP likho — turant hata dunga"); no unsolicited bulk; templates within WhatsApp Business policy. Honour STOP within 24h. Never add a number that didn't save you / reply-in.

## 8. Metrics (→ `growth-dashboard.md`)
List size (by segment/city), broadcast **read rate** (target >70%), **reply rate** (target >8%), broadcast→DEMO, broadcast→referral, opt-out rate (keep <2%/mo). A falling read rate = too much promo / wrong timing — rebalance to 4:1 value.
