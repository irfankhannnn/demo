# WhatsApp — Founder Broadcast Strategy

A consented broadcast list of warm followers/leads the founder nurtures personally (WhatsApp Business broadcast / channel).

## Build the list (opt-in only)
Source: IG DM handoffs, demo no-shows, trial users, event contacts, community members. Always explicit opt-in ("WhatsApp pe broker tips bhej dun?").

## Content mix (value:promo ≥ 4:1)
- 2–3/week: one sharp tip (follow-up, lead leakage, khata, AI calling).
- Behind-the-scenes building updates (human, founder voice).
- Quick wins / mini case study.
- Polls / "reply with your biggest pain".
- Occasional demo/trial nudge (1 in 5).

## Tone
Hinglish, personal, like a smart broker friend — never marketing-blast. First name where possible.

## Cadence & timing
2–3 broadcasts/week, 8–9 AM or 8–9 PM IST.

## Goal
Stay top-of-mind; convert warm list → demos; seed referrals. Track: list size, reply rate, broadcast→demo.

## Compliance
Opt-in, easy opt-out ("STOP likho"), no unsolicited bulk. Respect WhatsApp Business policy.
