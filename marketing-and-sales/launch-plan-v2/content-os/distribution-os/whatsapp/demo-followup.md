# WhatsApp — Demo Follow-up Strategy

Convert demos into trials/paid and rescue no-shows. Pairs with Sales OS demo-script + closing-script.

## Pre-demo
- T-24h: confirm + "kya dekhna chahoge? (AI calling / khata / team dashboard)".
- T-1h: reminder + 1-line "yeh aapki {pain} solve karega".

## No-show rescue
- +10 min: "Miss ho gaya? Koi baat nahi — reschedule karein? 2 slots: {a}/{b}".
- +1 day: send a 2-min recorded demo of their top pain.

## Post-demo (within 1 hour)
- Recap: 3 things that map to their pains + the price for their plan.
- Send trial link (app.realtyflow.in/signup) or onboarding next step.
- Address the one objection raised (route to objections.md).

## Follow-up cadence
T+1: "Setup mein help chahiye? main guide kar dun." · T+3: case study / ROI math · T+7: limited offer / decision nudge.

## Goal
Demo→trial/paid. Always end each message with a clear next step + slot.

## Metrics
Demo→trial, demo→paid, no-show rate, avg follow-ups to close. Feed growth-dashboard.
