# WhatsApp — Referral Strategy

Brokers trust brokers; word-of-mouth is the cheapest CAC. Turn happy customers + community into a referral engine.

## When to ask
After an activation win (deal closed, week-1 milestones hit, positive feedback). Never before value delivered.

## The ask (WhatsApp, personal)
> "{{name}}, khushi hui aapko RealEstateFlow pasand aaya 🙌 Aapke jaan-pehchaan mein koi broker/agency jo abhi WhatsApp/Excel pe struggle kar rahi ho? Main unhe ek free setup + extended trial de dunga — aur aapko bhi {reward}."

## Mechanics
- Referrer reward: free month / feature credit / extended plan.
- Referee reward: free setup + extended trial.
- Track referral code/source (Automation OS referral-tracking).

## Sources
Paying customers, community members, demo'd-but-not-yet (refer a peer), event contacts, founder network.

## Amplify
- Make sharing 1-tap: pre-written WhatsApp message + link.
- Public thanks (with consent) → social proof → more referrals.
- Tie to case studies: referred wins become CT-CASE content.

## Metrics
Referrals sent, referral→demo, referral→paid, referral as % of new customers. Feed growth-dashboard.
