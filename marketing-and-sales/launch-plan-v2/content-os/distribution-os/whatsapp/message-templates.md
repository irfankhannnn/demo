# WhatsApp — Message Templates

**The full copy-paste library** for every WhatsApp touch — 40+ templates organized by funnel stage. Hinglish (70/30) with **Marathi variants for Pune**. Personalise `{{name}}` / `{{city}}` / `{{slot}}` / `{{founder}}`. **One CTA each. Human tone, no bot-speak.** These power the sequences in `lead-nurture.md`, `demo-followup.md`, `customer-success.md`, `referral.md`.

> **WhatsApp Business policy / opt-in (read first):** broadcast & template messages require opt-in. Honour STOP within 24h. Outside the 24-hour customer-service window, only **approved template messages** may initiate. No cold blasting — it kills the channel and the number. Match the lead's tongue (Pune → Marathi handshake, then Hinglish for product mechanics, `03-language-strategy §8`). Money always ₹/lakh/crore. Reference real features only (`business-memory §3`).

## Usage rules
- **One CTA per message** — never stack two asks.
- **Match honorific:** owners → aap / tumhi; young brokers → tu/tum.
- **Speed:** lead templates fire <5 min; success/review are scheduled.
- **Approved templates** for out-of-window initiations (policy). In-window (24h since their last reply) = free-form OK.
- Log which template ID drove the action → `growth-dashboard.md` attribution.
