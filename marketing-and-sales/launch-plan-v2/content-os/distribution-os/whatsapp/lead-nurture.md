# WhatsApp — Lead Nurture Strategy

Move a warm WhatsApp lead from "interested" to "demo booked / trial started". Pairs with Sales OS qualification + nurture-bot.

## Entry
Lead arrives from IG DM handoff, FB/Google lead ad, event, referral. First message within 5 min (speed-to-lead).

## Nurture sequence (consented)
| Touch | Timing | Message intent |
|---|---|---|
| T0 | <5 min | Warm greeting + qualify (team size, current tool) |
| T1 | same day | Send AI-calling 60s clip or relevant pain reel |
| T2 | +1 day | Case study matching their segment (FW-CASE) |
| T3 | +2 days | Offer free 10-min pipeline audit / live demo |
| T4 | +4 days | Objection-buster (cost/time/team) + soft CTA |
| T5 | +7 days | "Koi pressure nahi" re-engage + best clip |

## Personalisation
Use name, city (Mumbai/Pune → language), segment (solo→trial, team→demo). Reference the content they engaged with.

## Rules
One CTA per message; value before ask; stop on request; book a concrete slot, don't leave open-ended.

## Handoff
On "yes to demo" → schedule + Sales OS `demo-script.md`. Tag lead stage. On trial start → customer-success.md.

## Metrics
Lead→demo rate, avg touches to demo, response time. Feed growth-dashboard.
