# WhatsApp — Customer Success Strategy

Onboard, activate, and retain paying customers via WhatsApp; reduce churn; create referral-ready fans. Pairs with Sales OS onboarding-script + retention-analyst.

## Onboarding (first 7 days = activation window)
- Day 0: welcome + "aaj ka 1 kaam: apne 10 leads daalo".
- Day 1: import existing leads/WhatsApp leads (workflow help).
- Day 2: set up AI calling on real leads (the aha moment).
- Day 3: team invite + RBAC (for agencies).
- Day 5: khata/settlement setup.
- Day 7: "first week win" check-in + show their dashboard numbers.

## Activation milestones (health signals)
Leads added · first follow-up reminder set · first AI call made · team member invited. Hitting 3/4 in week 1 → strong retention (see customer-health-scoring in Automation OS).

## Ongoing
- Weekly: proactive tip relevant to their usage.
- Monthly: usage review ("is mahine X leads, Y follow-ups, Z visits").
- Fast support response; celebrate their wins (deal closed via the app).

## Churn prevention
Inactivity (no login 7 days) → personal nudge + offer help. Low feature adoption → targeted mini-tutorial. Route at-risk to nurture-bot/founder.

## Turn into advocates
Happy + activated customers → referral ask (referral.md) + case-study request (CT-CASE/PROOF).

## Metrics
Activation rate, week-1 milestones hit, login frequency, churn, NPS. Feed growth-dashboard.
