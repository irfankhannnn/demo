# Distribution OS — Founder Distribution Engine

**The founder's operating cadence** (Phase 12): exactly what the founder does daily/weekly/monthly to compound authority, DMs, demos, community, and local presence. Pairs with `founder-brand.md`.

## Daily (45–75 min)
| Block | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| AM 20 min | Comment on 15–20 RE-owner/PropTech LinkedIn + IG posts | borrowed reach |
| AM 10 min | 10–15 targeted LinkedIn connects (Mumbai/Pune owners) w/ personal note | network |
| Midday 15 min | Reply to ALL comments + DMs (IG+LinkedIn) within the hour | conversations |
| PM 15 min | 1 founder story/post (IG story + 1 LinkedIn text) | presence |
| PM 10 min | Move warm DMs → WhatsApp → demo (Sales OS handoff) | pipeline |

## Weekly
- 1 founder reel (CT-FOUNDER) + 1 LinkedIn long-form (building-in-public).
- 1 broadcast/community drop (WhatsApp + IG broadcast) — a tip or behind-the-scenes.
- 1 outbound effort: 5 personalised DMs to ideal owners offering a free pipeline audit.
- Review: DMs started, demos booked, top content (feed `growth-dashboard.md`).
- 1 engagement in a Mumbai/Pune broker FB group (genuine value).

## Monthly
- 1 case-study collaboration with a happy customer (CT-CASE / CT-PROOF).
- 1 local touch: attend/host a Mumbai or Pune RE meetup / CREDAI-NAREDCO event / broker coffee.
- 1 podcast or collab appearance (PropTech/RE creator).
- 1 partnership conversation (portal, CP network, RE influencer).
- Refresh content pillars vs what drove demos.

## DM Strategy
Warm first (value/question), never cold-pitch. Flow: react to their content → DM insight → offer free audit → WhatsApp → demo. Log source = `founder`.

## Comment Strategy
Be the sharpest insight in RE-owner comment sections daily. Comments > posts for early-stage reach.

## Authority Building
Building-in-public numbers, contrarian truths, free frameworks (link to Content OS-style tools), consistency. 90-day goal: recognised voice in Mumbai/Pune broker circles.

## Community Building
Run the IG broadcast channel + WhatsApp community (see whatsapp/community.md). Weekly ritual + monthly live Q&A. Turn followers into a tribe that refers.

## Partnership Strategy
Target: property portals, CP aggregators, RE trainers/influencers, co-working/RE associations. Offer: co-content, webinars, referral deals.

## Local Networking — Mumbai
Andheri/Bandra/Thane broker hubs, CREDAI-MCHI events, project launch gatherings, CP meetups. Goal: 5 warm owner relationships/month.

## Local Networking — Pune
Hinjewadi/Wakad/Baner/Kharadi broker circles, CREDAI Pune, Marathi-first rapport. Goal: 5 warm owner relationships/month.
