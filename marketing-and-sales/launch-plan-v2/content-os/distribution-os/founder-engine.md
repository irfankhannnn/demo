# Distribution OS — Founder Distribution Engine

**The founder's operating cadence** (GTM Phase 12): exactly what the founder does daily/weekly/monthly to compound authority, DMs, demos, community, and local presence. Pairs with `founder-brand.md`. Every output is logged as `leadSource = founder`.

## Daily routine (time-blocked, ~60–90 min)
| Block | Time | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning engage | 20 min | Comment (real insight, not "great post") on 15–20 RE-owner / PropTech / CREDAI posts (LinkedIn + IG) | borrowed reach |
| Morning connect | 10 min | 10–15 targeted LinkedIn connects (Mumbai/Pune owners/managers) w/ personal note | network |
| Midday reply | 15 min | Reply to ALL comments + DMs (IG + LinkedIn) within the hour | conversations |
| Afternoon post | 15 min | 1 founder story (IG) + 1 LinkedIn text post | presence |
| Evening pipeline | 15 min | Move warm DMs → WhatsApp → demo (Sales OS handoff) | pipeline |

**Daily DM script (warm-first):** react to their content → "{{name}}, dekha aapne {{their post}} — sahi point. Aap real estate mein ho? Main brokers ke liye ek CRM bana raha hoon, AI calling ke saath. Aapki agency mein leads kaise track hote hain?"

## Weekly routine
- 1 founder reel (CT-FOUNDER) + 1 LinkedIn long-form (building-in-public, with a real number).
- 1 broadcast/community drop (WhatsApp + IG broadcast) — a tip or BTS.
- 1 outbound push: 5 personalised DMs to ideal owners offering a free pipeline audit.
- 1 genuine value-add in a Mumbai/Pune broker FB group.
- Weekly review: DMs started, demos booked, top/bottom content → feed `growth-dashboard.md`.

## Monthly routine
- 1 case-study collaboration with a happy customer (CT-CASE / CT-PROOF).
- 1 local touch: attend/host a Mumbai or Pune RE meetup / CREDAI-NAREDCO event / broker coffee.
- 1 podcast or collab appearance (PropTech/RE creator).
- 1 partnership conversation (portal, CP network, RE influencer/trainer).
- Refresh content pillars vs what actually drove demos.

## Comment strategy
Be the sharpest insight in RE-owner comment sections daily. Comments out-perform posts for early-stage reach. Formula: agree + add a non-obvious insight + a light question.

## Authority building
Building-in-public numbers + contrarian truths + free frameworks (link the Content OS-style calculators) + relentless consistency. 90-day goal: recognised name in Mumbai/Pune broker circles.

## Community building
Run the IG broadcast channel + WhatsApp community (`distribution-os/whatsapp/community.md`). Weekly ritual + monthly live Q&A. Convert followers → a tribe that refers.

## Partnership strategy
Targets: property portals (MagicBricks/Housing/99acres reseller angles), CP aggregators, RE trainers/influencers, co-working/RE associations, CREDAI/NAREDCO chapters. Offer: co-content, joint webinars, referral/revenue-share deals.

## Local networking — Mumbai
Andheri/Bandra/Thane broker hubs; CREDAI-MCHI events; project-launch gatherings; CP meetups. Cosmopolitan Hindi-English rapport. Goal: 5 warm owner relationships/month.

## Local networking — Pune
Hinjewadi/Wakad/Baner/Kharadi broker circles; CREDAI Pune; **Marathi-first rapport** (in-group trust). Goal: 5 warm owner relationships/month.

## Metrics
Comments made, connects, DMs started, founder-attributed demos, broadcast/community growth, local relationships. Feed `growth-dashboard.md`.
