# Distribution OS — LinkedIn (Founder Authority System)

**LinkedIn is the founder-led authority + B2B credibility channel** — where agency *owners* (the buyers), sales managers, RE developers, and PropTech peers form the "this is a serious, modern product" judgment that the sale needs (`03-language-strategy §4`, `02-market-research §2.2`). This file **IS the LinkedIn system** (Phase 5). It governs *what & why*; daily execution cadence lives in `founder-engine.md`. Integrates with `CT-AUTHORITY`, `CT-FOUNDER`, `CT-CASE`, and `FW-AUTHORITY / FW-CONTRARIAN / FW-CASE`.

> **Why LinkedIn matters despite low broker time-spend** (`02-market-research §9.1`, ~5%): the **buyer** (owner) needs an English, credibility signal before paying; LinkedIn is also the recruiting/partnership magnet. Low volume, high leverage. Language: **`en-leaning`** + the occasional Hinglish punch for warmth (never full-corporate; brand voice forbids it).

> **Handles:** founder profile (primary) + RealtyFlow company page (secondary) · featured link `demo.realtyflow.in`.

## 4. The Founder Authority System

- **Positioning:** "The operator-founder building the CRM Indian brokers *actually use* — in their language, for their chaos." Close to the problem (credible), not corporate (relatable).
- **5 content categories:** (1) Building-in-public, (2) RE industry hot-takes, (3) Broker pain → solution, (4) Data / case studies, (5) Personal story / mission.
- **Posting framework (per post):**
  1. **Hook line** — one scroll-stop sentence, often contrarian or a ₹-number.
  2. **Context** — 1–2 lines, why this matters now.
  3. **3–5 short insight lines / list** — generous line breaks, mobile-first.
  4. **Takeaway** — the one idea to remember.
  5. **Soft CTA** — comment / DM / demo. Never hard-sell.

**Verbatim long-form example (FW-CONTRARIAN):**
> "Indian brokerages lose 30–40% of leads. Everyone blames 'the market'.
>
> It's not the market. It's the follow-up.
>
> A lead that gets a reply in 15 minutes closes ~60% of the time. After an hour, ~20%.
>
> Most brokers I meet are drowning in 200 WhatsApp messages a day — the lead from this morning is buried by lunch.
>
> The fix isn't more leads. It's a system that never forgets a follow-up.
>
> That's the whole reason we built RealEstateFlow.
>
> What % of your leads do you think quietly leak? Comment your guess 👇"

**Verbatim short hot-take:**
> "'CRM choti agency ke liye nahi hai.' — every owner, right before a competitor with one steals their lead. A 3-person team needs it *more*, not less."

## 5. CTA strategy

Mostly **MOFU** — lead with insight, then a soft ask. Categories: **COMMENT · DM · DEMO · COMMUNITY**. "Comment X and I'll DM the breakdown" is the workhorse (comment→DM loop, `content-to-conversation.md`). Hard-sell kills authority here.

## 6. Lead capture

DM-to-demo, profile **Featured** link (`demo.realtyflow.in`), company-page lead-gen form ads, comment→DM. Tag leads source = `linkedin`. Owners who engage with a case-study post are the highest-intent — DM them a tailored case for their city.

## 7. Engagement strategy (the real growth engine)

- **Comment 15–20 min/day** on RE owners', CREDAI / NAREDCO, and PropTech posts with *real* insight — never "great post 👏". A sharp comment in an owner's thread earns borrowed reach → profile visit → connect → DM.
- **Reply to every comment on own posts within the hour** (algorithm + trust).
- **Build relationships in DMs before pitching** — react to their content, send an insight, *then* offer a free pipeline audit.

## 8. Connection strategy

Daily **10–15 targeted connects:** RE agency owners/managers in Mumbai/Pune (filter by title + geo). Personalised note referencing their actual work — **no instant pitch.** Example note:
> "Saw your post on channel-partner payouts — we obsess over the same commission-tracking headache for Mumbai brokerages. Would love to connect."

## 9. Commenting strategy

Be the **most insightful voice in RE-owner comment sections.** Comments out-reach posts for an early-stage profile. Daily target: 5 high-value comments in owner/CREDAI threads. The flywheel: insight → borrowed reach → profile visits → connects → DMs → demos.

## 12. Metrics (→ `growth-dashboard.md`)
Profile views, connection accept rate, post impressions, comment quality (replies that become DMs), DM→demo, `linkedin`-attributed demos, follower growth among owners/managers. Authority is a lagging metric — track *DMs started from comments* as the leading one.
