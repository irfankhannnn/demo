# Distribution OS — LinkedIn (Founder Authority System)

**LinkedIn is the founder-led authority + B2B credibility channel** (owners, sales managers, developers, investors). This file IS the LinkedIn system (Phase 5). Integrates with `CT-AUTHORITY`, `CT-FOUNDER`, `CT-CASE`, `FW-AUTHORITY/CONTRARIAN/CASE`.

## Audience
Agency owners, sales managers, RE developers, PropTech peers, potential hires/partners. Mood: professional, proof-driven, status-aware. Language: **en-leaning** + occasional Hinglish punch.

## Posting frequency
3–5 posts/week (founder profile primary, company page secondary). 1 long-form/week + 2–3 short text/carousel + daily commenting.

## Content mix (by CT-*)
| Pillar | CT-* | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Founder POV / lessons building RealEstateFlow | CT-FOUNDER | 30% |
| Industry authority / contrarian takes | CT-AUTHORITY | 25% |
| Case studies / data | CT-CASE | 20% |
| Product/feature insight (AI calling, khata) | CT-DEMO/CT-EDU | 15% |
| Behind-the-scenes / team / hiring | CT-FOUNDER | 10% |

## Founder Authority System
- **Positioning:** "The person building the CRM Indian brokers actually use." Operator-founder, not corporate.
- **5 content categories:** (1) Building-in-public, (2) RE industry hot-takes, (3) Broker pain → solution, (4) Data/case studies, (5) Personal story/mission.
- **Posting framework (per post):** Hook line (1 sentence, scroll-stop) → context → 3–5 short insight lines/list → takeaway → soft CTA (comment/DM/demo). Generous line breaks, mobile-first.

## CTA strategy
Mostly MOFU: "comment X", "DM to see the AI demo", book demo via link. Avoid hard-sell; lead with insight. CTA categories: COMMENT, DM, DEMO, COMMUNITY.

## Lead capture
DM-to-demo, profile featured link (demo.realtyflow.in), lead-gen form on company-page ads, comment→DM. Tag leads source = `linkedin`.

## Engagement strategy
- **Comment 15–20 mins/day** on RE owners', CREDAI/NAREDCO, PropTech posts with real insight (not "great post").
- Reply to every comment on own posts within the hour.
- Build relationships in DMs before pitching.

## Connection strategy
Daily 10–15 targeted connects: RE agency owners/managers in Mumbai/Pune (filter by title + geo). Personalised note referencing their work. No instant pitch.

## Commenting strategy
Be the most insightful voice in RE-owner comment sections → borrowed reach → profile visits → connects → DMs.

## Repurposing
LinkedIn long-form ← founder reel scripts (CT-FOUNDER) reworked en-leaning; case-study carousels ← IG case studies; hot-takes ← FW-CONTRARIAN hooks.

## Publishing workflow
Draft in content factory → schedule via Blotato → engage manually (commenting can't be automated for authenticity).
