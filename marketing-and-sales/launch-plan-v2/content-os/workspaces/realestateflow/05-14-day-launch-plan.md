# 05 — RealEstateFlow 14-Day Instagram Launch Sprint

> **What this is.** A day-by-day 14-day Instagram launch plan that **pulls specific OPP-ids from the top-scored bank** (`04-content-plan-500.md` / `.csv`). Every piece names its `OPP-`, title, content type (`CT-*`), framework (`FW-*`), characters (`CH-*`), language tag, platform, posting time (from market-research peak windows), and the goal. Built to convert the launch attention of ~62,000 Mumbai + Pune brokers into demos and trials — not just reach.

**Brand:** RealtyFlow · **Tagline:** "Agency ki Growth, Aapke Control Mein." · **Objective:** awareness → trust → demo/trial sign-ups in 14 days.
**Posting windows (research §3.3, §12):** 7:00–8:00 AM (coffee scroll), 12:30–1:00 PM (lunch), 6:30–8:00 PM (wind-down). Friday evenings strongest; Monday mornings weakest. All times IST.

## 2. Strategy: Week-1 vs Week-2 Themes

| | Theme | Goal | Content tilt |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Week 1 (Days 1–7) — "Naam aur Dard" (Name + Pain)** | Launch the brand + make the broker feel *seen*. Validate WhatsApp chaos, lead leakage, follow-up misses, commission jhagda. | **Reach + trust + first demos.** Get saved & forwarded; build the follower base. | Founder, Drama, Meme, Educational, WhatsApp-chaos. Reach-heavy, with 1–2 lead-gen anchors. |
| **Week 2 (Days 8–14) — "Proof aur Solution" (Proof + Solution)** | Convert the warmed audience. Show the product, the numbers, the AI wow, real outcomes. | **Lead-gen + conversion.** Demos, trials, DMs. Retarget Week-1 engagers with Meta ads (DEMO/TRIAL OPP rows). | Demo, Case study, AI, Authority, Social proof, Objection-killers. Lead-gen-heavy. |

**Why this order:** brokers buy on a 60–90 day cycle and trust peers over brands (research §2.3). Week 1 earns the right to sell by being relatable and shared; Week 2 cashes that trust into demos while the audience is warm and retargetable.
