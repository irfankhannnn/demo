# Sales OS — Follow-up Script

Most deals close on follow-up — ironic for a follow-up product, so we **model the behaviour**. Pairs with WhatsApp `lead-nurture.md` + `demo-followup.md` + `message-templates.md`. Grounded in research §2.3 (60–90 day cycle, peer-validation, speed-to-lead).

> **Principles:** One CTA per message · value before ask · reference their specific pain + the content they engaged · always propose a concrete next step (slot/trial), never "let me know" · stop on request.

---

## Channel rules
- **WhatsApp = primary.** It's where brokers live (3–4 hrs/day, research §2.2). Templates: `distribution-os/whatsapp/message-templates.md`.
- **Call** = high-value owners (Hot leads, ₹2,999+ plans) at Touch 3 and Touch 5.
- **Timing:** send in peak windows — 6–8 AM, 12–1 PM, 6–8 PM (research §12). Never 9 PM–11 PM (exhausted).
- **One CTA. One ask. One message.** No walls of text — brokers scroll past.

---

### Verbatim messages

**Touch 1 — Day 0, ≤1h (recap):**
> "Rajesh bhai, aaj demo ka maza aaya 🙌 Jaise dikhaya — aapki lead leakage AI calling se band ho jaayegi. Yeh raha trial link *(link)*. No card, main aapke pehle 10 leads khud import kar dunga. Kab time hai 10 min setup ke liye — aaj shaam ya kal subah?"

**Touch 2 — Day 1 (friction remover):**
> "Setup mein koi dikkat? Mujhe apni lead list (Excel/WhatsApp) bhej do — main import karke AI calling on kar deta hoon. Aapko sirf dekhna hai farak. 👍"

**Touch 3 — Day 3 (peer proof / ROI, `FW-CASE`):**
> "Ek cheez share kar raha hoon — {{similar broker, e.g. Andheri ke ek 6-agent owner}} ne yeh use karke 2 mahine mein ek leak hua ₹8L deal recover kiya. Aapke 100+ leads pe asar aur bada hoga. Maths dekhna chahoge? 2 min mein bhej dun."
> *(For Hot/₹2,999+ owner: call instead — "bhai 5 min baat karein, ROI samjha doon?")*

**Touch 4 — Day 5 (AI wow, `FW-AI`):**
> "Yeh 30-sec clip dekho — AI ne ek buyer ko khud call karke qualify kiya 👇 *(clip)*. Aap apne ek real lead pe abhi try kar sakte ho free mein. On kar dun?"

**Touch 5 — Day 7 (decision nudge):**
> "Bhai seedhi baat — abhi season hai, leads aa rahe hain. Har din wait = leak. Aaj trial on karo, main personally setup karunga aur ek hafta saath rahunga. Bolo, haan?"
> *(honest urgency only — festival/launch lead-flood or limited onboarding slot. Never fake scarcity.)*

**Touch 6 — Day 12 (soft re-engage):**
> "Koi pressure nahi 🙏 Bas yeh soch ke bheja — {{their pain, e.g. 'follow-up bhool jaana'}} pe ek choti cheez. *(best `FW-` content)*. Jab ready ho, main yahin hoon."

**Touch 7 — Day 21 (break-up):**
> "Rajesh bhai, main aapko baar-baar tang nahi karunga 🙂 Abhi timing nahi toh koi baat nahi — par jab woh agla lead haath se nikalne lage, ek message kar dena. Main turant set kar dunga. All the best! 🙌"

## Branch logic

**If REPLIED with interest →** jump to the matching next step:
- "kitna ka hai" → take pricing to DM, anchor on ROI → `closing-script.md`.
- "team se baat karni hai" → arm them (sales-enablement one-liner), set a follow-up slot.
- "trial on karo" → `onboarding-script.md` Day 0 immediately.

**If REPLIED with an objection →** route to `objections.md`, resolve, re-ask, then resume cadence.

**If NO REPLY →** continue cadence to Touch 7. After 2 consecutive no-replies, **switch channel once** (WhatsApp → one call) before continuing.

**If "abhi nahi / baad mein" →** move to long-term nurture (`community.md` / founder-broadcast); set a 30/60-day re-touch.

---

## Speed-to-lead cadence (NEW inbound, pre-demo — research §5: 15-min response wins)
For fresh `instagram_dm` / portal / ad leads (NOT yet demo'd):
- **≤5 min:** "Hi {{name}}! Aapne {{reel/'DEMO'}} pe DM kiya. Quick — solo ho ya team? 2 min mein bata dunga RealEstateFlow kaise fit hota hai."
- **+30 min (no reply):** "Bas ek line — abhi leads WhatsApp/Excel pe ya CRM pe? Iske hisaab se exact dikhaunga."
- **+1 day:** Value drop (`FW-LEAD-LEAKAGE` / leak calculator) + soft demo ask.
- **+3 day:** AI demo curiosity (`FW-AI`) → push to 2-min demo (`demo-script.md` short variant).
> Research: response within 15 min ≈ 60% engage; after 1 hr ≈ 20%. **Speed is the whole game.**

---

## Re-engagement sequence (gone-cold lead, 30+ days silent)
- **Touch A:** Pattern-interrupt value, no ask — "{{name}} bhai, ek naya feature aaya jo aapki {{pain}} pe seedha kaam karta hai 👇"
- **Touch B (+4d):** Peer win / `FW-CASE`.
- **Touch C (+8d):** Soft offer — "free pe AI calling try kar lo, kuch lagana nahi."
- No reply after C → move to broadcast list (founder-broadcast), stop 1:1.

---

## Break-up → nurture transition
On Touch 7 / break-up with no reply: stop personal follow-up, add to long-term nurture (broadcast + community), set a 60-day re-touch tied to a buying trigger ("season aane pe" / "naya project launch pe"). A break-up message often *triggers* a reply — that's the point.
