# Sales OS — Qualification Playbook

Qualify fast so effort goes to real buyers. Deliberately **lightweight** for SMB brokers — no heavy BANT interrogation; brokers smell a "sales process" and bounce. Pairs with Attention OS DM handoff (`content-to-conversation.md` §5) and WhatsApp `lead-nurture.md`. Grounded in market research §2 (buying behaviour) and §6 (pain ranking).

> **Philosophy:** Brokers don't fill forms — they have *conversations*. Qualify by listening, not quizzing. The four questions below should feel like a friend asking, not a CRM intake.

---

## 1. The discovery questions (with WHY)

Ask in DM/WhatsApp, woven into chat — not as a numbered list.

| # | Question (Hinglish) | WHY we ask |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Team kitni badi hai — aap solo ho ya kuch agents hain?" | Routes plan tier + path (self-serve vs demo) + decides if RBAC/hierarchy matters. |
| 2 | "Abhi leads kaise sambhalte ho — WhatsApp, Excel, ya koi CRM?" | Measures pain depth + switching cost + whether they've been burned before (objection #10). |
| 3 | "Mahine mein kitne leads aate hain roughly?" | Urgency signal + plan tier (Free 50 / Starter 500 / Growth unlimited) + ROI math input. |
| 4 | "Sabse badi headache kya hai — leads leak, follow-up bhool jaana, team visibility, commission ka hisaab, ya calling?" | Picks the **one pain** the demo will mirror. Never demo all features. |
| 5 (optional) | "Kuch hua recently jisse laga ki system chahiye?" | Surfaces the **buying trigger** — the emotional fuel for urgency. |

**Listening cues > answers:** A broker who says "abhi ek deal haath se nikal gaya" (#5) is hotter than one who answers all 4 neatly.

## 2. Scoring & routing matrix

Score each lead 0–2 on four axes; total drives the route.

| Axis | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pain** | "we're fine" | mild ("thoda chaos hai") | acute ("deal/agent kho diya") |
| **Authority** | agent, no say | manager/champion | owner / decision-maker |
| **Volume** | <20 leads/mo | 20–100 | 100+ / multi-agent |
| **Trigger** | none | competitor pressure | lost deal / agent left / dispute |

| Total | Tier | Route |
|---|---|---|
| 6–8 | **Hot** | Priority **demo** within 24h + ROI/leak math (`demo-script.md`, `CTA-DEMO`) |
| 3–5 | **Warm** | Demo or guided trial; nurture cadence (`followup-script.md`) |
| 1–2 | **Cool** | Self-serve **Free/Starter trial** (`CTA-TRIAL`); light WhatsApp nurture |
| 0 | **Cold/curious** | Community/broadcast nurture (`community.md`), low effort |

## 3. Persona-specific qualification

**Owner / Brokerage Boss (Rajesh Bhai — ADMIN, economic buyer)**
- Qualify on: time lost to admin, lead leakage ₹, team accountability gap, agent-churn fear.
- Key Q: "Aap khud kitne ghante admin mein jaate hain roz?" (4–6 hrs = hot).
- Sell: control + ROI. Closes fast if ROI clear. Wants peer proof.

**Sales Manager / Team Lead (Priya — ADMIN/elevated MEMBER, champion)**
- Qualify on: chasing agents for follow-ups, unfair lead-assignment fights, "no data on who's working."
- Key Q: "Team ke follow-ups kaise track karte ho abhi?"
- Sell: visibility-without-micromanagement. **Arm her to sell up to the owner** (sales-enablement); she rarely signs the cheque.

**Agent / Consultant (Arjun — MEMBER, user/influencer)**
- Qualify on: admin stealing selling time (20 hrs/wk), wants AI follow-up + call records + own metrics.
- Key Q: "AI tumhare boring follow-up calls kar de toh?"
- Sell: mobile + AI. Fast adopter (14–30 day decision). Often a *bottom-up* champion — gets the owner to buy.

## 5. Buying-trigger checklist (lean in hard when ANY present)

- [ ] Lost a big deal recently ("haath se nikal gaya")
- [ ] An agent left and took leads/relationships
- [ ] Scaling team (new hires incoming → need accountability)
- [ ] Commission dispute / "kiska kitna paisa" fight
- [ ] Competitor across the street using a system
- [ ] New project launch = lead flood incoming
- [ ] Family succession (son/junior taking over)

Each ticked box = move to **Hot**, compress timeline, lead with the matching pain story.

---

## 6. Disqualification criteria (gently exit)

Disqualify — or downgrade to long-term nurture — when **all** of these hold:
- No pain (genuinely "we're fine" + no trigger), AND
- No authority (can't decide, won't champion), AND
- No budget appetite (won't even start the Free plan).

Also park (not disqualify): **students/researchers**, **pure tyre-kickers**, **competitors snooping**. Be warm, send to community, move on. Never burn a brand impression — today's "no" is next quarter's "yes" after they lose a deal.

## 7. Sample qualifying conversations (Hinglish)

**WhatsApp — warm DM handoff (hot lead):**
> **You:** "Arre Rajesh bhai 🙌 aapne 'DEMO' kiya tha. Quick — team mein kitne agents ho?"
> **Lead:** "6 hain, par sab WhatsApp pe chalta hai, bahut chaos hai"
> **You:** "Samajh gaya. Mahine ka kitne leads aate hain?"
> **Lead:** "100-150, par half toh kahin kho jaate"
> **You:** "Yahi pakdunga main. Kuch hua recently jisse laga system chahiye?"
> **Lead:** "Haan ek Bandra deal nikal gaya, agent ne follow-up hi nahi kiya"
> → **Score: Pain 2 + Authority 2 + Volume 2 + Trigger 2 = 8 → HOT.** Book demo in 24h, mirror lead-leakage pain, lead with AI follow-up.

**DM — solo, low intent (cool lead):**
> **Lead:** "Kitne ka hai?"
> **You:** "Solo ho ya team? Aur abhi kaise manage karte ho?"
> **Lead:** "Akela hoon, Excel pe, thoda kaam hai bas"
> **You:** "Toh Free plan se start karo — ₹0, 50 leads. Link bhej dun? Pasand aaye toh aage badhna."
> → **Cool → self-serve Free.** No demo effort spent.

**Pune — Marathi rapport:**
> **You:** "Namaskar! Tumchi team kiti motthi aahe?" ("How big is your team?")
> Switch to Hinglish once rapport set. Pune = lead with *growth* narrative, not luxury.

## 8. Handoff contract (qualification → demo / trial)

When a lead is **Warm+** with team + current pain + intent to see it:
1. Create lead with: **source** (e.g. `instagram_dm`), **segment** (solo/team/agency), **persona** (owner/manager/agent), **primary pain**, **buying trigger**, **score**, **stage = qualified**.
2. Log **content attribution** (the originating `OPP-*`/reel/`FW-*`) — per `content-to-conversation.md` §5 + Automation OS attribution.
3. Route: Hot/Warm → `demo-script.md` (book slot). Cool → trial + `followup-script.md`.
4. Hand the **champion** a one-line internal pitch if buyer ≠ user (sales-enablement).

**Output is a lead record, not a vibe.** If it's not logged with source + segment + pain + stage, the funnel can't see it (Automation OS instrumentation).
