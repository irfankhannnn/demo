# Sales OS — Objections Playbook

The top objections Mumbai/Pune brokers raise against adopting a CRM, the emotional root of each, and the response. Mirror these in content (FW-OBJECTION/FW-MYTH) and DMs. Grounded in market research §objections.

| # | Objection | Emotional root | Response (Hinglish) | Proof/move |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "CRM mahanga hai" | Fear of wasted spend | "Ek missed deal se sasta hai. ₹999/mo, aur ek lead bach gaya toh paisa wasool." | ROI math, free plan |
| 2 | "Time nahi hai seekhne ka" | Overwhelm | "10 min mein setup. Aur AI calling toh aapka time bachata hai, leta nahi." | 2-min demo |
| 3 | "Meri team nahi seekhegi" | Past tech failure | "Hinglish UI, mobile-first — WhatsApp jitna asaan. Naya joiner 1 din mein chalata hai." | onboarding-script, RBAC |
| 4 | "Mera kaam toh chal raha hai" | Status-quo bias | "Chal raha hai aur badh raha hai — alag hai. Kitne leads silently leak ho rahe, pata hai?" | leak calculator |
| 5 | "WhatsApp/Excel se ho jaata hai" | Comfort | "WhatsApp communication ke liye, CRM business ke liye. Dono jodo — WhatsApp chhodo mat." | FW-WHATSAPP-CHAOS |
| 6 | "Data safe rahega?" | Trust/risk | "Cloud backup, RBAC — agent delete nahi kar sakta, phone khoye toh bhi data safe." | RBAC feature |
| 7 | "AI calling sach mein kaam karega?" | Skepticism | "Apne hi lead pe live try karo demo mein. 2 min mein khud dekh lo." | live AI demo |
| 8 | "Lock-in ho jayega" | Control fear | "Free plan, no card, kabhi bhi export. Try karke dekho." | free trial |
| 9 | "Abhi market down hai" | Budget anxiety | "Down market mein toh har lead aur important — leakage band karo, market ka wait mat karo." | FW-LEAD-LEAKAGE |
| 10 | "Pehle bhi try kiya, nahi chala" | Burned before | "Woh generic CRM tha. Yeh real estate ke liye bana — buyer matching, site visit, khata, AI." | RE-specific demo |

## Rules
Empathise first ("samajh sakta hoon"), never argue, reframe to ROI/risk, end with a low-friction next step (demo/trial). Take pricing detail to DM/WhatsApp, not public comments. Update this file from recurring comment/DM objections.
