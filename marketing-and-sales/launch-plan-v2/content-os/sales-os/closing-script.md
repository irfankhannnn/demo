# Sales OS — Closing Script

Convert a warm, demo'd lead into a paying customer. SMB brokers close **fast** when ROI + risk-reversal are clear (research §2.3 — decision phase wants references, trial, money-back). Pairs with `demo-script.md` (before) and `onboarding-script.md` (after). Plans/features per `01-business-memory.md` §5.

> **Mindset:** You're not "selling" — you're removing the last bit of risk so a clearly-painful problem gets solved today. Always close on a concrete action + the trial, never "soch ke batao."

---

## Pre-close checklist (all must be ✓)
- [ ] **Pain confirmed** — they nodded at the pain mirror.
- [ ] **Fix demo'd** — they saw the AI-calling wow + their one feature.
- [ ] **Objection handled** — no live blocker remaining (`objections.md`).
- [ ] **Right plan identified** — matched to team size (see Plan guidance).
- [ ] **Decision-maker engaged** — owner on the call, OR champion armed to pitch owner.

If any box is empty → don't close yet. Fill it first.

---

## Closing techniques (pick by read)

### 1. Assumptive (default — for Hot owners)
> "Aapko AI calling aur lead tracking pasand aaya. {{Plan}} aapke {{team size}} ke liye perfect hai — ₹{{price}}/mahina. Aaj shuru karein toh main khud aapke pehle 10 leads import kar deta hoon, aur AI calling on kar deta hoon — kal subah se hi farak dikhega. Bas apni list bhejo, theek hai?"

### 2. Summary close (for analytical/owner who wants the recap)
> "Toh seedha hisaab — abhi aapke 30-40% leads leak ho rahe, ₹{{X}}L/saal ka nuksaan. {{Plan}} ₹{{price}}/mo. AI follow-up karega, khata clear rahega, team dikhegi. Ek bhi bacha hua deal = saal bhar ka cost cover. Shuru karein?"

### 3. Risk-reversal close (for hesitant / burned-before)
> "Aise karo — main risk hi nikaal deta hoon. Free trial, no credit card, kabhi bhi cancel, data kabhi bhi export. Aapko kuch lagana nahi abhi. Bas ek hafta dekho — pasand na aaye toh chhod do, koi sawaal nahi. Itne mein toh haan ho sakti hai na?"

### 4. Urgency close (HONEST urgency only)
> "Bhai abhi {{season/Diwali/launch}} hai — leads ka flood aa raha hai. Har din bina system = leak. Aaj on karo, main personally setup karunga is hafte. Agle hafte bahut late ho jayega."
> *(Only real urgency: festival/launch lead-flood, limited onboarding-slot, time-bound extended-trial. **Never fake scarcity** — brokers smell it.)*

### 5. Alternative-choice close (assumes yes, picks the *how*)
> "Toh shuru karte hain — Starter se jaana chahoge ya seedha Growth (AI matching + 15 users)? Aur leads aaj import karun ya kal subah?"
> *(The choice is between two yeses, not yes/no.)*

## Handling "let me think about it" / "soch ke batata hoon"
Isolate the real blocker — "think about it" is never the real reason:
> "Bilkul, sochna chahiye. Bas yeh batao — soch kis cheez pe hai? Price, team adoption, ya kuch aur? Taaki main woh exact clear kar dun."

Then:
- **Price →** ROI math + Free plan ("paisa baad mein, abhi free pe shuru").
- **Team adoption →** "ek agent ko bhi trial pe dekhne do — woh khud bolega easy hai" (`objections.md` #3).
- **Authority ("partner se poochna hai") →** arm them + set a **specific** follow-up slot, don't leave open.
- **Genuinely not now →** "koi baat nahi — free pe leads daal ke rakho, jab ready ho AI on kar lena." (foot in door) → `followup-script.md`.

## Plan-selection guidance

| Signal | Plan | Price | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo, testing | **Free** | ₹0 | 50 leads, 2 users — zero-risk entry |
| Small team, WhatsApp leads | **Starter** | ₹999/mo | 500 leads, 5 users, WhatsApp workflow |
| Growing agency, wants AI matching | **Growth** | ₹2,999/mo | unlimited leads, 15 users, AI matching — **the sweet spot for most demo'd owners** |
| Large agency, needs API/analytics | **Pro** | ₹5,999/mo | API + advanced analytics |
| Developer / 25+ agents | **Enterprise** | Custom | dedicated conversation |

**Default recommendation for a demo'd team owner = Growth.** Start most solos on Free, upsell after value is proven (don't over-sell day one — `paywall-upgrade-cro` later).

---

## Negotiation / discount guardrails
- **Lead with value, not discount.** First answer to "kuch kam karo" = "Free plan se start karo, paisa tab jab faayda dikhe."
- **No random % off.** If you must move: offer **annual** (1–2 months free on yearly) or **extended trial**, never a permanent lower price.
- **Never discount below plan integrity** — it cheapens the product and trains brokers to haggle.
- **Trade, don't give:** any concession ↔ ask for something (annual commit, a testimonial, a referral intro).
- Anchor every number on ROI: "₹2,999 vs ek deal ka ₹5L commission — discount ki baat hi kahan."

---

## Post-close handoff (momentum is everything)
1. **Immediately** trigger `onboarding-script.md` Day 0 — import 10 leads together *on the same call/chat*. Don't let the gap kill the buzz.
2. Send WhatsApp welcome (`distribution-os/whatsapp/customer-success.md`) + onboarding slot confirmation.
3. Update lead → **stage = paid/trial-started**, plan, MRR; log to pipeline (pipeline-manager) + content attribution (`OPP-*`).
4. After the **first activation win** (first AI call / organized pipeline) → ask for **review + referral** (`referral.md`). Never before the win.
