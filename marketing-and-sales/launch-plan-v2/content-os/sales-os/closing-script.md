# Sales OS — Closing Script

Convert a warm, demo'd lead into a paying customer. SMB brokers close fast when ROI + risk-reversal are clear.

## Pre-close checklist
Pain confirmed · demo'd the fix · objection handled · right plan identified · decision-maker engaged.

## The close (assumptive + risk-reversed)
> "Aapko AI calling aur lead tracking pasand aaya. {{Plan}} aapke {{team size}} ke liye perfect hai — ₹{{price}}/mo. Aaj shuru karein toh main khud aapke pehle 10 leads import kar deta hoon, aur AI calling on kar deta hoon — kal se hi farak dikhega. Free trial se start, no card. Theek hai?"

## Risk reversal
"Free trial, no credit card, kabhi bhi cancel, data kabhi bhi export. Risk zero — bas ek hafta dekho."

## Urgency (honest only)
Festival/launch lead-flood ("abhi season hai, leak mat hone do"), limited onboarding slot, time-bound extended-trial offer. Never fake scarcity.

## Objection at close
Route to objections.md; isolate ("iske alawa aur koi cheez rok rahi hai?") then resolve and re-ask.

## Plan guidance
Solo→Free/Starter; growing team→Growth (AI matching, 15 users); large→Pro; developer→Enterprise. Upsell later via paywall-upgrade once value proven.

## After close
Trigger onboarding-script.md immediately (momentum). Ask for review/referral after first activation win.
