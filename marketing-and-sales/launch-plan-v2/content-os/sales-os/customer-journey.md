# Sales OS — Customer Journey

End-to-end map with drop-off points and improvements. The connective tissue across Attention, Distribution, Sales, and Automation OS.

## The journey
```
Instagram → DM → WhatsApp → Demo → Trial → Onboarding → Paid Customer → Referral
```

| Stage | Owner | Levers | Drop-off | Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Content OS / Attention OS | reels, hooks, series | weak hook / no follow reason | fix 1s hook; franchises; clear bio |
| **DM** | content-to-conversation | keyword loops, lead magnets | no CTA / slow reply | auto-replies; reply <60 min |
| **WhatsApp** | lead-nurture | speed-to-lead, AI clip, qualify | friction / no number | ask only number; offer live demo |
| **Demo** | demo-script | one pain → AI wow | no-show / feature dump | reminders; recorded fallback; tailor |
| **Trial** | onboarding-script | aha in 7 days | never activates | guided import; AI call day 2 |
| **Onboarding** | customer-success | week-1 milestones | stalls at setup | proactive WhatsApp; tutorials |
| **Paid** | customer-success | usage reviews, support | churn | health scoring; win-back |
| **Referral** | referral.md | post-win ask, rewards | never asked | trigger after activation win |

## Conversion targets (tune in growth-dashboard)
IG→DM ~1–2%/mo of followers · DM→WhatsApp 40% · WhatsApp→demo 30% · demo→trial 40% · trial→paid 25–35% · paid→referral 20%+.

## Biggest leverage points
1. Hook rate (top of funnel volume). 2. DM→WhatsApp friction. 3. Demo no-shows. 4. Trial week-1 activation. 5. Referral ask discipline.

## Instrumentation
Every stage needs tracking (lead source, content attribution, stage timestamps, activation events) — designed in Automation OS. Without it, you can't see drop-offs.
