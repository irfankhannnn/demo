# Sales OS — Demo Script (verbatim, 10–15 min + 2-min variant)

Goal: make the broker **FEEL** their #1 pain solved, land the AI-calling wow, then ask for the trial. Tailor to the pain captured in `qualification.md`. Show only the 2–3 features tied to their pain — **never dump features.** All features referenced are verified in `01-business-memory.md` §3.

> **The one rule:** One pain → one wow (almost always **AI calling**). Let them tap/drive if possible. Money in ₹/lakh/crore. Hinglish 70/30; Marathi rapport for Pune.

---

## Pre-demo (before the call)
- Re-read the lead record: persona, segment, primary pain, trigger, lead source.
- Pre-load a **sample lead with their real-ish data** (a name/number they'll recognise as plausible).
- Have AI calling ready to fire live. Test audio. Keep it to **one screen, mobile view**.
- Send WhatsApp 10 min before: *"Bhai 10 min mein judta hoon, link yeh raha. Apna ek real lead ready rakhna — usi pe live dikhaunga."*

---

## Minute-by-minute verbatim script

### 0:00–0:30 — Frame
> "Rajesh bhai, time ke liye shukriya. Main aaj feature dump nahi karunga — sirf woh dikhaunga jo aapki **{{pain}}** solve karta hai. 10 min, no fluff. Beech mein kabhi bhi rok ke sawaal pooch lena, theek hai?"

*(Pune Marathi opener: "Vel dilyabaddal dhanyavaad. Aaj fakt tumchi mukhya samasya solve karnari gosht dakhavto.")*

### 0:30–1:30 — Pain mirror (make them nod)
> "Aapne bola tha — leads WhatsApp pe aate hain, 200 chats, aur shaam tak yaad hi nahi rehta subah wala buyer kaun tha. Aur woh Bandra deal jo nikal gaya — agent ne follow-up hi nahi kiya. Sahi pakda na?"

*(Wait for the "haan, exactly." This nod is the whole demo. If they don't nod, re-qualify the pain before continuing.)*

### 1:30–4:00 — Lead Management (the foundation)
**What to click/show:** Lead List → add a lead → assign to an agent → set stage → open Lead Detail.
> "Dekho — yeh raha aapka pura lead. Naam, number, requirement, kaunsa agent owner hai, aur abhi kis stage pe hai. *(tap)* Yeh lead Rajesh ka, yeh Priya ka. Ab koi nahi bol sakta 'mujhe pata nahi tha yeh mera lead tha.' Har lead ka ek malik, ek next step. **Leak ka chance khatam.**"

Show the **Dashboard** glance: "Aur yahan se aapko ek nazar mein — kitne leads aaye, kahan atke. Office ka hisaab ek click mein."

### 4:00–7:30 — AI Calling (THE wow — scripted)
**What to click/show:** AICalling → Start Call modal → trigger a real/sample call live → show transcript building.
> "Ab woh cheez jiske liye aaye ho. Yeh lead jo abhi banaya — main ispe AI se call lagwata hoon. **Live. Abhi.** *(tap Start Call)*"
>
> *(Speaker on. AI dials, greets in Hinglish, asks qualifying questions, captures intent.)*
>
> "Suno — AI khud baat kar raha hai. Buyer ka budget pooch raha hai, possession timeline, area preference. Aur dekho — *(point to screen)* — saath ke saath transcript ban raha hai, lead auto-qualify ho rahi hai."
>
> *(Call ends.)*
>
> "Bas. Yeh ek call. Ab socho — **100 leads, AI raat ko bhi follow-up kar sakta hai. Aapko sirf qualified, garam leads milenge — closing ke liye.** Jo 10 ghante/hafta follow-up calls mein jaate the, woh wapas aapke."

**If they're stunned/silent →** that's the buying moment. Go straight to the ask (skip remaining features).

### 7:30–9:00 — The ONE more feature (pick by persona/pain)
Branch — show **only one**:

| If their pain / persona is… | Show this | Verbatim line |
|---|---|---|
| Forgotten follow-ups | **Follow-up reminders** (Calendar / ScheduleMeeting) | "Reminder set — ab koi follow-up nahi bhulega, system khud yaad dilayega." |
| Commission disputes / owner | **Khata Book / Settlement** | "Kiska kitna paisa — ek screen. Commission ka hisaab, zero jhagda. Audit ke time bhi ready." |
| Team visibility / manager | **Hierarchy + RBAC** | "Har agent dikhega — kisne kitne calls, follow-ups kiye. Bina micromanage. Aur naya banda delete nahi kar sakta." |
| Scattered inventory / agent | **Property + buyer matching** | "Client bola Bandra 2BHK — *(tap)* — turant property nikli, share kar do. Deal fast." |

### 9:00–10:00 — ROI moment
> "Maths simple hai. {{Plan}} ₹{{price}}/mahina. Aapka ek deal ₹2–20 lakh commission ka. Agar saal mein ek bhi leak hua lead bach gaya — yeh tool **free** ho gaya, baaki sab profit. Ek admin staff ₹20K/mo leta hai; yeh usse sasta aur 24×7 kaam karta hai."

### 10:00–11:00 — The ask (trial/close)
> "Chalo aise karte hain — aaj hi free trial shuru karte hain, no card. Main khud aapke pehle 10 leads import kar deta hoon abhi, aur AI calling on kar deta hoon — kal subah se hi farak dikhega. Theek hai? Bas mujhe woh Excel/WhatsApp list bhej do."

→ On yes: hand to `closing-script.md` plan selection + `onboarding-script.md` Day 0.
→ On objection: `objections.md`, isolate, resolve, **re-ask**.

## Discovery-led personalization branches

- **Owner (Rajesh):** Lead with leakage ₹ + AI calling + Khata + Dashboard (control). Close on ROI.
- **Manager (Priya):** Lead with Hierarchy/RBAC + follow-up tracking. Arm her to pitch the owner.
- **Agent (Arjun):** Lead with AI follow-up + call records + his own metrics dashboard. He sells upward.
- **Pune lead:** Growth framing ("scale your agency"), Marathi rapport, tech-forward tone.
- **Burned-before lead:** Open with khata + AI (features the old CRM never had) to break the "all CRM same" frame.

---

## Mid-demo objection handling (don't derail)
- Acknowledge in one line, park if not blocking: "Bilkul, woh main 1 min mein dikhata hoon — pehle yeh dekh lo."
- If it's a real blocker → jump to `objections.md`, resolve, return to flow.
- Common mid-demo ones: "kitna time lagega seekhne" (→ #2), "team seekhegi?" (→ #3), "data safe?" (→ #6).

---

## Short demo — 2-minute variant (DM/`CTA-DEMO` curiosity, cold-ish viewer)
For the "DM 'AI'" / `FW-AI` curiosity lead who isn't ready for a full call:
> **0:00–0:15 Frame:** "2 min mein dikhata hoon AI calling — apne hi lead pe."
> **0:15–1:30 Wow:** Fire ONE live AI call on their lead. Show transcript + auto-qualify.
> **1:30–2:00 Ask:** "Pasand aaya? Free trial on kar dun, no card — main setup kar deta hoon."

Use it as a **hook to earn the full demo or the trial.** No other features. Just the wow + the ask.

- If not closed on the call → start `followup-script.md` cadence (Touch 1 = the recap).
