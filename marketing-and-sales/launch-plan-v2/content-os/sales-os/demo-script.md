# Sales OS — Demo Script (2–15 min)

Goal: make the broker FEEL their pain solved, then ask for trial/paid. Tailor to the pain captured in qualification.

## Structure
1. **Frame (30s):** "Aaj main aapko sirf woh dikhaunga jo aapki {pain} solve karta hai — 10 min, no fluff."
2. **Pain mirror (1 min):** restate their world ("abhi WhatsApp pe 200 chats, kaun sa lead kahan pata nahi…").
3. **Core demo (5–8 min) — show, don't tell:**
   - **Lead Management:** capture → assign → stage → never-lost. "Yeh dekho, har lead ka owner aur next step."
   - **AI Calling (wow):** trigger a call on a real/sample lead live. "AI ne khud call kiya, qualify kiya, transcript diya."
   - **Follow-ups:** reminder set → "ab koi follow-up nahi bhulega."
   - **Khata/Settlement:** "commission ka hisaab — zero jhagda."
   - **Team dashboard/RBAC:** "aapko har agent dikhega, bina micromanage."
   - (Show only the 2–3 tied to their pain; don't dump features.)
4. **ROI moment (1 min):** "Agar mahine mein ek bhi leak hua lead bach gaya — yeh free ho gaya."
5. **Ask (1 min):** "Chalo aaj hi free trial shuru karte hain (no card), main aapke 10 leads daal deta hoon?" → trial or plan.

## Rules
One pain → one wow (usually AI calling). Let them tap/drive if possible. Handle objections via objections.md. Always end with a concrete next step + slot. Hinglish; Marathi rapport for Pune.

## After
Send post-demo recap (WhatsApp demo-followup.md) within 1 hour.
