# Sales OS — Onboarding Playbook (7-Day + 30-Day)

Activation in week 1 predicts retention. Drive to the **aha moment** (first live AI call + an organized pipeline) fast, then to habit, then to advocacy. Pairs with WhatsApp `customer-success.md` + Automation OS **activation-scoring**. Features per `01-business-memory.md` §3.

> **North-star aha:** The broker watches **AI make and qualify a real follow-up call** on their own lead. Everything in week 1 exists to reach that moment by Day 2 and make it a habit by Day 7.

---

## 7-Day playbook (day-by-day)

| Day | Action (exact) | Message (Hinglish) | Aha / milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | Import 10 real leads *together* on the close call; set up agency + admin login | "Welcome bhai! 🙌 Abhi aapke 10 leads daal dete hain — apni list bhejo." | "Sab ek jagah" |
| **1** | Set first follow-up reminders on those leads | "Aaj 3 leads pe reminder laga dete hain — ab koi follow-up nahi bhulega." | "Ab nahi bhulega" |
| **2** | **Turn on AI calling** on one real lead — the wow | "Ready? Ek real lead pe AI se call lagwate hain. Dekhna kamaal 👇" | 🌟 **THE aha — first AI call** |
| **3** | Invite team + set RBAC (agencies); solo → add properties | "Team ko invite kar do — Member role mein woh delete nahi kar sakte, control aapke paas." | Control / hierarchy |
| **4** | Add properties/inventory + match a buyer to a property | "Ek client ka requirement daalo — dekho property turant match ho jaati hai." | "Turant share" |
| **5** | Set up khata/settlement for one deal | "Commission ka hisaab daal do — kiska kitna paisa, ek screen, zero jhagda." | "Hisaab clear" |
| **6** | Light-touch check; nudge any unused core feature | "Kaisa chal raha hai? Koi cheez atki ho toh bata do, 5 min mein clear." | Friction removal |
| **7** | **Week-1 review call** — show their own dashboard numbers | "Ek hafte mein {{X}} leads organized, {{Y}} AI calls, {{Z}} follow-ups. Yeh raha aapka data 👇" | 📈 **Proof of value** |

**Solo vs agency split:** Solo skips Day 3 team-invite (instead: deeper property + buyer matching). Agency emphasises Day 3 (hierarchy/RBAC) + Day 7 team-adoption review.

---

## Activation milestones (target **3 of 4** in week 1)
1. ✅ **10+ leads added** (organized pipeline exists)
2. ✅ **First follow-up reminder set**
3. 🌟 **First AI call fired** (the aha — weight this heaviest)
4. ✅ **Team member invited** (agency) **OR** buyer matched to property (solo)

Feed these as events to Automation OS **activation-scoring**. 3/4 by Day 7 = "activated" = green health.

---

## Health signals (watch daily)

| Signal | Green | Yellow | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| Login frequency | daily | every 2–3 days | no login 48h+ |
| Leads added | 10+ | 3–9 | 0 |
| AI call fired | yes (Day 2–3) | by Day 5 | never |
| Team active (agency) | 2+ members | 1 | only owner |
| Replies to your nudges | warm | slow | silent |

Any **Red** → trigger the matching stall-recovery play below.

---

## Aha-moment engineering (the AI-calling moment)
The product lives or dies on Day 2. Make it foolproof:
- **Pre-stage the lead** with the broker so the call is on someone *real and recognisable*.
- **Do it live with them watching** — don't email instructions and hope. Screen-share or be on WhatsApp call.
- **Narrate the magic:** "Dekho — AI khud budget pooch raha hai, transcript ban raha hai, lead qualify ho rahi hai."
- **Immediately connect to ROI:** "Yeh 100 leads pe chala do — 10 ghante/hafta wapas aapke."
- **Capture the reaction** (with consent) → testimonial seed for `referral.md` + content (`FW-AI`).

If Day 2 AI call doesn't happen → **this is your #1 escalation**, ahead of everything else.

## Stall-recovery plays

| Stall | Trigger | Play |
|---|---|---|
| No login 48h | health = red | Personal WhatsApp nudge + offer a 10-min setup call: "Bhai 10 min do, main khud set kar deta hoon." |
| Leads imported but no AI call | Day 4, milestone 3 missing | "Aapne abhi tak AI calling try nahi kiya — yeh toh main feature hai. Abhi 2 min mein on kar dun?" |
| Owner active, team not (agency) | only owner logging in | Offer a **30-min team training**; address objection #3 ("team nahi seekhegi"). |
| Used once, then silent | usage dropped after Day 2 | Send their *own* early numbers as proof + one quick win they haven't tried (khata/matching). |
| Confusion / "samajh nahi aaya" | support messages | Send a short Loom/in-app tutorial for that exact feature; never reply with a wall of text. |
| At-risk by Day 5 (multi-red) | health score low | **Escalate to founder** for a personal call — high-touch save. |

Rule: **proactive, not reactive** — reach out *before* they get stuck, in peak windows (6–8 AM / 12–1 PM / 6–8 PM).

## 30-day playbook (habit → expansion)

| Week | Focus | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 1 (Day 0–7)** | Activation | 7-day playbook above → 3/4 milestones |
| **Week 2 (Day 8–14)** | Habit | Daily lead entry becomes default; AI calling on a *batch*; first khata settlement closed; show pipeline movement. |
| **Week 3 (Day 15–21)** | Depth | Full team active (agency); buyer–property matching in real deals; Dashboard/analytics review — "kahan leads atak rahe." |
| **Week 4 (Day 22–30)** | Value proof + expansion | **30-day ROI review:** show recovered/organized deals in ₹. If on Free/Starter and hitting limits → upsell to Growth (`paywall-upgrade-cro`). Ask for review + referral. |

**Day-30 ROI review verbatim:**
> "Ek mahina ho gaya 🙌 Dekho — {{X}} leads organized, {{Y}} AI calls, {{Z}} follow-ups jo warna bhool jaate. Ek deal jo leak hone wala tha, bach gaya. Ab yeh tool free ho gaya, baaki sab profit. Aapke ek do broker dost hain jinko yeh chahiye?"

---

## Success → advocacy transition
After the **first clear win** (recovered deal / "ab chaos nahi"):
1. **Capture the moment** → short testimonial / quote (research §2.3: peer proof is the #1 trust signal).
2. **Ask for review** (Google/peer WhatsApp groups) — `customer-success.md`.
3. **Ask for referral** with reward → `referral.md`. ("Aapke jaisa ek broker dost?")
4. Hand to ongoing **customer-success** cadence (monthly value review, expansion watch).

Never ask for the referral before the win. The win earns the ask.
