# LinkedIn — founder authority

**LinkedIn is the founder-led authority and B2B credibility channel** — where agency *owners* (the buyers), sales managers, developers and PropTech peers form the "this is a serious, modern product" judgment the sale needs (`../10-audience-and-voice/language-and-tone.md` §4, `../10-audience-and-voice/market-research.md` §2.2). This file governs what and why; the daily execution cadence lives in `founder-presence.md`. Works with `CT-AUTHORITY`, `FW-AUTHORITY` and `FW-CONTRARIAN`.

> **This is the one channel where the founder appears in person** — in text. The founder is never on camera anywhere, but LinkedIn posts, comments and DMs are written in the founder's own voice, not the AI presenter's.

> **Why LinkedIn matters despite low broker time-spend** (~5% of their content time): the **buyer** — the owner — wants an English credibility signal before paying, and LinkedIn is also the recruiting and partnership magnet. Low volume, high leverage. Language: **English-leaning**, with the occasional Hinglish punch for warmth. Never full-corporate; the brand voice forbids it.

> **Handles:** founder profile (primary) + a RealEstateFlow company page (secondary) · featured link `realestateflow.in/demo/`. Hold the link back until the domain serves HTTPS.

## 4. The Founder Authority System

- **Positioning:** "The operator-founder building the CRM Indian brokers *actually use* — in their language, for their chaos." Close to the problem (credible), not corporate (relatable).
- **5 content categories:** (1) Building-in-public, (2) RE industry hot-takes, (3) Broker pain → solution, (4) Data / case studies, (5) Personal story / mission.
- **Posting framework (per post):**
  1. **Hook line** — one scroll-stop sentence, often contrarian or a ₹-number.
  2. **Context** — 1–2 lines, why this matters now.
  3. **3–5 short insight lines / list** — generous line breaks, mobile-first.
  4. **Takeaway** — the one idea to remember.
  5. **Soft CTA** — comment / DM / demo. Never hard-sell.

**Verbatim long-form example (FW-CONTRARIAN):**
> "Most brokerages I talk to think they have a lead problem.
>
> Then I ask one question: of last month's leads, how many got a third follow-up?
>
> Almost nobody knows. That's the actual problem — not the market, not the lead volume.
>
> Most brokers are drowning in a couple of hundred WhatsApp messages a day. The lead from this morning is buried by lunch, and nothing anywhere remembers it.
>
> The fix isn't more leads. It's a system that never forgets a follow-up.
>
> That's the whole reason we built RealEstateFlow.
>
> Count yours this week. Comment the number 👇"

The original version of this post opened with "Indian brokerages lose 30–40% of leads" and cited "15 minutes closes ~60%, after an hour ~20%". Neither number has a source we can stand behind, and unsourced statistics are banned (`../10-audience-and-voice/claims-and-proof-policy.md`). The rewrite makes the same argument by getting the reader to produce the number themselves — which is also more persuasive, because it is *their* number.

**Verbatim short hot-take:**
> "'CRM choti agency ke liye nahi hai.' — every owner, right before a competitor with one steals their lead. A 3-person team needs it *more*, not less."

## 5. CTA strategy

Mostly mid-funnel — lead with insight, then a soft ask. Categories: **COMMENT · DM · DEMO · COMMUNITY**. "Comment X and I'll DM the breakdown" is the workhorse (`../40-sales-and-conversion/dm-to-demo.md`). Hard selling kills authority here.

## 6. Lead capture

DM to demo, the profile **Featured** link (`realestateflow.in/demo/`), and comment → DM. Company-page lead-gen ads are post-PMF; there are no paid ads in M1. Record `source` on the CRM lead and keep the post that drove it in the tracking sheet — the lead schema has no campaign field. Owners who engage with a product-insight post are the highest intent; DM them the walkthrough, not a case study we do not have.

## 7. Engagement strategy (the real growth engine)

- **Comment 15–20 minutes a day** on posts by agency owners, CREDAI/NAREDCO and PropTech people, with real insight — never "great post 👏". A sharp comment in an owner's thread earns borrowed reach → profile visit → connect → DM.
- **Reply to every comment on own posts within the hour** (algorithm + trust).
- **Build relationships in DMs before pitching** — react to their content, send an insight, *then* offer a free pipeline audit.

## 8. Connection strategy

Daily **10–15 targeted connects:** agency owners and managers in Mumbai, filtered by title and geography (Pune after the PMF gate — D24). A personalised note referencing their actual work — **no instant pitch.** Example:
> "Saw your post on channel-partner payouts — we obsess over the same commission-tracking headache for Mumbai brokerages. Would love to connect."

## 9. Commenting strategy

Be the **most insightful voice in owner comment sections.** Comments out-reach posts for an early-stage profile. Daily target: five high-value comments in owner and CREDAI threads. The flywheel: insight → borrowed reach → profile visits → connects → DMs → demos.
