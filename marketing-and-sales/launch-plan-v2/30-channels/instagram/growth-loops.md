# Instagram — growth loops

> **RealEstateFlow · `@realestateflow`**
> The **compounding engines** behind follower and reach growth. A loop is content where the *output becomes the next input* — a save trains the algorithm to show more, a share recruits a viewer who becomes a follower. Works with `../virality-and-retention.md`, `reels.md`, `carousels.md`, `comments.md` and `follower-to-demo.md`.

**Audience reality:** brokers **forward to peer WhatsApp groups** (the strongest trust signal in this market) and **save business-useful content**. Loops are built on those two behaviours. Brand copy 70% English / 30% romanized Hindi; Mumbai only in M1 (D24).

---

## 1. Save Loop
- **Mechanic:** reference content (frameworks, ₹-math, templates) → brokers save + re-open before client calls → saves train the algorithm → more cold reach → more saves.
- **Operationalize:** ship 2 CT-EDU carousels/week (`carousels.md`), every one ending `CTA-SAVE`. Tag "save this checklist." Lead-magnet keyword (AUDIT / KHATA) converts savers to DMs.
- **Fuel OPP-*:** OPP-013 (the 100-leads arithmetic the viewer does themselves), OPP-296 (the deal that got buried), OPP-511 (property share).
- **Metric:** save rate; saved→DM via keyword.

## 2. Share / Tag Loop
- **Mechanic:** relatable drama/meme + "tag a broker" → tagged brokers become viewers → some follow → they tag the next.
- **Operationalize:** CT-DRAMA/MEME with `CTA-SHARE-007` ("share with that one agent jo abhi bhi Excel pe hai") / `CTA-COMMENT-015` (tag). Validate a frustration they already share.
- **Fuel OPP-*:** OPP-001 (200 unread POV), OPP-435 (2026 Excel), OPP-430 (mera lead kahan).
- **Metric:** shares/sends + tag count.

## 3. Series Loop
- **Mechanic:** a recurring presenter and fixed franchises → followers return for the next episode → consistent follows and watch time.
- **Operationalize:** the franchises on fixed weekdays (`reels.md` §7, `content-cadence.md` §3). The cast is governed by `../../20-content-engine/cast-and-presenter.md`, and how many characters we actually run is open.
  > Open decision D25 — see marketing-and-sales/launch-plan-v2/00-OPEN-DECISIONS.md
- **Fuel OPP-*:** OPP-001/002/057 (drama), OPP-192 (AI Employee series).
- **Metric:** follower growth + returning-viewer rate per series.

## 4. Collab Loop
- **Mechanic:** co-create with broker influencers / CP-group admins → shared audiences → cross-follows.
- **Operationalize:** see §8 collab playbook. Founder guest spots are text or audio only — never on camera (`../founder-presence.md`).
- **Metric:** follows from collab posts; new-audience reach.

## 5. SEO Caption Loop
- **Mechanic:** keyword-rich Hinglish captions → IG search + Google discovery over time → evergreen long-tail reach.
- **Operationalize:** see §7 caption formulas. Seed "real estate CRM", "lead leakage", "WhatsApp AI Employee", "Mumbai broker" in captions + first comment.
- **Metric:** search-sourced reach (IG insights).

## 6. DM / Comment Relationship Loop
- **Mechanic:** 1:1 replies (`comments.md`, `dm-workflows.md`) → loyalty → these followers comment early on every new post → early engagement → boosted reach for everyone.
- **Operationalize:** the 60-minute reply rule and warm DM nurture. Do not re-share a DM or story reply as proof, even anonymised — a message sent to us is not consent to be published.
- **Metric:** repeat-commenter rate; first-60-min comment velocity.

## 8. Collab playbook

1. **Target list:** mid-size broker influencers (5–50k), CP-group admins, real-estate YouTubers, local-market pages — Mumbai (D24).
2. **Offer (value-first):** co-create a reel ("5 broker galtiyan" with their face), an extended free period on the terms in `../../pricing.json` (there is no lifetime plan), or a paid shoutout.
3. **Formats:** IG **Collab** post (shared to both grids), takeover story, audio podcast or written guest slot for the founder — no on-camera appearance — duet or stitch on their reel.
4. **Cadence:** 1 collab/week from Month 2 (`90-day-growth-strategy.md`).
5. **Attribution:** unique CTA keyword per collab ("DM 'ARJUN'") to measure follows + DMs.

## 9. SEO caption formulas

- **EDU:** `{Hinglish hook line}` → 2–3 sentence expansion with keyword → `{CTA-SAVE}` → hashtags.
  e.g. "100 leads aaye — aapke kitne convert hue? Yeh lead leakage hai; ginke dekho."
- **DEMO:** `{pain in keyword form}` → "dekho kaise RealEstateFlow {feature} karta hai" → `{CTA-DEMO}`. The claim has to be on the approved list (`../../10-audience-and-voice/claims-and-proof-policy.md`).
- **PRODUCT:** `{the moment it saves}` → "dekho kaise {feature} kaam karta hai" (real screen recording) → `{CTA-DEMO}`.
  There is no CASE formula. A case-study caption needs a real customer and their written permission.
- First line must carry the keyword AND the hook (only ~1 line shows before "…more").

## 10. The flywheel

```
   ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │  Reach (drama/meme) → Follows (series) → Saves/Shares (edu)
   │        ↑                                        ↓
   │  Build-in-public ← Product shipped ← Demos ← DMs (comment/DM loops)
   └────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
Each output feeds the next input: drama earns reach → the series converts reach to follows → edu earns saves and shares (more reach) → the loops open DMs → DMs become demos → what we learn in demos becomes the next thing shipped → shipping it is build-in-public content that earns reach again. Stories and the broadcast channel are the **retention layer** spinning the wheel between posts.

**Pre-launch, the loop closes at the waitlist.** The June draft closed it at "customers → case studies", which is the version of this flywheel that works once we have paying customers who have agreed to be named. Until then the product itself is the proof.

## 12. Metrics per loop

| Loop | Lead metric | Target signal |
|---|---|---|
| Save | save rate | edu beats reels on saves |
| Share/Tag | sends + tags | drama/meme lead |
| Series | returning-viewer % + follows | franchise retention |
| Collab | follows + DMs per collab | positive new-audience reach |
| SEO caption | search-sourced reach | grows month over month |
| DM/comment | first-60-min velocity | repeat commenters rise |

> Loops are judged by **compounding, not spikes**. A loop is working when this month's baseline reach/follows is higher than last month's *without* a viral outlier carrying it.
