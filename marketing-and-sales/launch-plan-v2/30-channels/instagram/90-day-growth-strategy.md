# Instagram — 90-day growth strategy

Week-by-week arc for the first three months on the account. Content comes from `../../20-content-engine/content-plan/content-plan-500.csv` (top `OPP-*`). Metrics are defined in `../../50-measurement/metric-dictionary.md` and reported in `../../50-measurement/weekly-scorecard.md`. Goal arc: **Foundation → Reach → Conversion.**

Months here are *relative* — Month 1 starts on the day the account goes live. No calendar dates, because the launch date is not fixed.

> Open decision D28 — see marketing-and-sales/launch-plan-v2/00-OPEN-DECISIONS.md

## Month 1 — Foundation — "establish the show"

**Goal:** brand, presenter and series live; learn which hooks land.

| Wk | Focus | Quotas | Milestones |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Setup + launch | profile/bio/pinned/highlights/broadcast set (bio carries the "AI presenter" disclosure); run the week-1 day files | profile live; first launch reel |
| 2 | Launch cont. | 6 reels, daily stories, 2 carousels | first 5 franchises seeded |
| 3 | Find winners | 6 reels (heavy CT-DRAMA + CT-EDU); daily founder engine | identify the top 1–2 franchises |
| 4 | Double down | scale the winning series; cut flops; 2 carousels | 10+ DM conversations |

**Month-1 KPIs (from the month-1 organic pack):** **150–300 waitlist signups** as the headline number, 1,200–2,500 followers, hook rate >40%, 10+ DM conversations, attribution logged (a manual sheet is fine — `/api/marketing/events` does not exist).

Demos and trials are *not* the month-1 KPI. The account is being built before the product is sold; counting demos this early pushes the content toward a hard sell it cannot support.

## Month 2 — Reach — "scale what works"

**Goal:** turn reach into DMs.

| Wk | Focus | Quotas | Milestones |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Scale winners | double the top 2 franchises; add a weekly collab/trend-jack | first reel over 25k reach |
| 6 | Differentiate | introduce a weekly product slot (`[REC]` screen recording of a real feature) | product series gets traction |
| 7 | Convert | tighten comment→DM and DM→WhatsApp; enable keyword rules if App Review has passed | DM rate rising |
| 8 | Seed | Facebook broker-group seeding | followers compounding |

**Paid ads stay off in M1.** From Month 2 onward, lead ads on proven organic reels are allowed **only if the PMF gate in `../../00-PLAN-OVERVIEW.md` §4 is met** (≥3 paying, activation, reply rate, ≥1 promoter). Until then, week 8 is organic seeding only.

**Month-2 KPIs:** follower growth, 1–2 reels over 50k reach, 40+ DM conversations, first demos booked.

## Month 3 — Conversion — "predictable demo engine"

**Goal:** make content a measurable pipeline.

| Wk | Focus | Quotas | Milestones |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Lock cadence | theme-day calendar locked; batch by presenter | consistent output |
| 10 | Proof | **only** from Founding customers who have given written permission — otherwise this week is more `[REC]` product proof | real proof live, or the slot stays product-led |
| 11 | Optimise | scale the winning creatives; retarget engagers (paid only behind the PMF gate) | cost per conversation understood |
| 12 | Community | launch the WhatsApp community; monthly text/voice-note AMA (no video, no founder on camera) | repeatable engine |

**Month-3 KPIs:** consistent demos per week, cost per conversation tracked, content→`OPP-*` attribution visible in the tracking sheet.

Week 10 is the week this plan used to fabricate. It said "weekly testimonial, case studies compound". We have no customers, so there is nothing to compound. A testimonial is publishable only when a paying customer has given written permission; see `../../10-audience-and-voice/claims-and-proof-policy.md`.

## Weekly milestones (every week, all months)

Ship the quota · reply to all comments and DMs within 60 minutes · one long-form founder text post · review top and bottom content by hook rate · update content attribution in the tracking sheet.

## What to double down on

Whatever drives DMs → conversations — usually CT-DRAMA for reach and CT-DEMO (real screen recordings) for conversion. Kill low-hook-rate formats fast; **keep the presenter, change the format.** Re-prioritise weekly from the scorecard.
