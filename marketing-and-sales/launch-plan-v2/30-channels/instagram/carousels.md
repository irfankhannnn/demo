# Instagram — carousels (the save engine)

> **RealEstateFlow · `@realestateflow`**
> Carousels win **saves and dwell time → algorithm boost and reference value**. Where reels grab cold reach and stories nurture, carousels are the bookmarkable utility a broker re-opens before a client call. Saves are the strongest Instagram ranking signal alongside comments. Best for `CT-EDU`, `CT-AUTHORITY` and `CT-DEMO`. Works with `FW-*`, `HK-*`, `CTA-*`, `VP-*` and the content plan (`../../20-content-engine/content-plan/content-plan-500.md`, `OPP-*`).

**Audience reality:** Android, scrolling at 6–8 AM and 12–1 PM, sound off. They save what helps their business (how-to, templates, ₹-math) and share what makes them look smart in a peer WhatsApp group. Brand copy is 70% English / 30% romanized Hindi (`../../10-audience-and-voice/brand-constants.md`); no Marathi in M1 (D24). Carousels also cross-post natively as **LinkedIn documents** and to Facebook.

## 1. The carousel is five slides

Brand kit v3 fixes the format: **5 slides — slide 1 gulal hook, slides 2–4 ink one-point slides, slide 5 marigold CTA.** The June draft called 6–9 slides "the sweet spot"; that is superseded. Anything longer is an explicit exception, agreed before production, not a default.

Pick ONE architecture per carousel; never blend.

### A. Mistake list — `FW-MISTAKE` (highest saves)

| Slide | Ground | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | gulal | "3 galtiyan jo har broker karta hai" + the number, big |
| 2 | ink | Galti 1 + what it costs in ₹ or time |
| 3 | ink | Galti 2 + its cost |
| 4 | ink | Galti 3 + the fix, shown in the product |
| 5 | marigold | `CTA-SAVE` + "save & share with your team" |

### B. How-to / steps — `FW-CRM`, `FW-DEMO`
Hook (promise) → why it matters → step 1–2 (screenshot insert) → step 3 → `CTA-SAVE` / `CTA-DEMO`.

### C. Myth-bust — `FW-MYTH`
Hook ("Myth: CRM mahanga hai") → why people believe it → the checkable truth → one reframe → `CTA-COMMENT` / `CTA-DEMO`.

### D. Data / math — `FW-LEAD-LEAKAGE`
Hook ("100 leads aaye. Aapke kitne convert hue?") → the three places leads go missing → how to count it from your own WhatsApp → what changes when it is tracked → `CTA-LEAD-MAGNET`.

### E. Product walkthrough — `FW-FEATURE` (replaces the old case-breakdown architecture)
Hook (the moment the feature saves) → the old way → the new way, screen-recorded → what it leaves behind (a record, a reminder) → `CTA-DEMO`.

The June draft's "Case breakdown" architecture opened on a result hook such as "8% → 12% close rate" attributed to a real agency. We have zero customers and no measured close rates, so that architecture is retired until a paying customer gives written permission to publish their numbers (`../../10-audience-and-voice/claims-and-proof-policy.md`).

## 2. Cover design rules (slide 1 does 80% of the work)

- Big Hinglish **promise or number**, Unbounded 800/900 on the **gulal** ground; body text Manrope.
- No hex values here — take them from `../../10-audience-and-voice/brand-constants.md`. One accent per card; marigold and gulal never share a card.
- A visible **"swipe →"** affordance and the slide count ("1/5").
- Use the `VP-THUMB-EDU` treatment so the cover matches the reel grid (one-brand look).
- The cover is also the **grid thumbnail** — it must stop a cold scroll exactly like a reel hook. Pull the line from `../../20-content-engine/hooks/hook-library.md`.
- Grid rule: no more than 3 of 9 visible tiles sit on the same ground.

---

## 3. Swipe-retention tactics

Each slide must *pull* to the next, or the swipe dies and saves drop.

- **Open the loop on the cover**, close it on slide 5 ("slide 5 pe asli number").
- **Number the slides** ("Galti #2 →") so progress is visible.
- **One idea per slide, big text** — no paragraph walls; nobody reads long blocks on a phone.
- **Cliffhanger slide-ends:** end a slide mid-thought so the thumb keeps moving.
- **Slide 4 is the most-saved frame** — make it screenshot-worthy.
- **Visual rhythm:** alternate text card / screenshot / presenter frame so it does not feel like a PDF.

## 4. Caption as mini-blog

The caption earns search discovery over time (the SEO caption loop, `growth-loops.md` §5).

- **Opener** repeats the hook (the first line shows in the feed before "…more").
- **Body:** 80–150 words expanding the carousel, seeded with keywords: *real estate CRM, lead leakage, lead management software, WhatsApp AI Employee, Mumbai broker*.
- **Ender:** the single CTA line (`CTA-SAVE` primary) plus a soft secondary ("share with your team").
- **Hashtags:** 8–12 from the pillar set (`growth-loops.md`).
- Log the `OPP-*` and `CTA-*` in the tracking sheet for attribution — there is no content-reference field on a CRM lead.

---

## 5. Design specs

- **Size:** 1080×1350 (4:5) or 1080×1080 — consistent across the whole carousel.
- **Type:** Unbounded (headlines) + Manrope (body). **Grounds and accents** per the brand constants: gulal hook card, ink body cards, marigold CTA card. Never both accents on one card.
- **Numbers:** ₹/lakh/crore, large. One idea per slide. Logo bug in the bottom corner of every slide.
- **Templates:** carousels are `[AI]` work — Higgsfield / HyperFrames / nano-banana-pro. Keep the master template under `marketing-and-sales/creative/` with `REF-<TYPE><NN>-<slug>-v<N>` naming. Remotion stills live in `marketing-and-sales/video-projects/my-video/` if a carousel needs one.
- **Cross-post:** export the same slides as a LinkedIn document (English-leaning caption) and to Facebook.

## 6. Carousel vs reel (when to choose)

| Use a CAROUSEL for | Use a REEL for |
|---|---|
| lists, frameworks, step-by-steps | emotion, drama, skits |
| data and ₹-math breakdowns | product demo in motion |
| reference content (save and re-open) | cold reach and shares |

Many top `OPP-*` ship **both** ways — OPP-296 (carousel) and OPP-443 (demo reel) are the same "₹5L deal gaya kahan" angle in two formats.

## 7. Five worked carousel outlines

Every worked example below is a **template the viewer fills with their own numbers**. None of them asserts a result we have measured.

**C1 — OPP-296 · "WhatsApp mein jo deal laga tha, woh gaya kahan?" · CT-EDU · FW-MISTAKE**
1. Hook (gulal): "Aapki sabse badi deal WhatsApp mein kahin dafan hai 💀"
2. Galti 1: lead WhatsApp pe hai, system pe nahi — 200 unread mein dhoondho.
3. Galti 2: koi reminder nahi → teen din baad lead thandi.
4. Galti 3: lead agent ke phone mein, owner ko dikhti hi nahi. Fix: ek tap mein WhatsApp se system mein (screen recording).
5. CTA (marigold): `CTA-SAVE-042` + "team ko bhejo."

**C2 — OPP-013 · "100 leads aaye — kitne convert hue?" · CT-EDU · FW-CRM**
1. Hook: "100 leads aaye. Aap ginke batao — kitne band hue?"
2. Teen jagah lead girti hai: WhatsApp mein dab gayi / follow-up hi nahi hua / galat agent ke paas chali gayi.
3. Apna hisaab: pichhle mahine ke leads × aapka average commission. Calculator nahi, aapke apne numbers.
4. Jo track hota hai woh dikhta hai: har lead pe date, owner, next step.
5. CTA: `CTA-LEAD-MAGNET-005` ("DM 'AUDIT'").

**C3 — OPP-240 · "CRM mahanga hai?" · CT-AUTHORITY · FW-AUTHORITY**
1. Hook: "'CRM mahanga hai' — chalo hisaab karte hain."
2. The objection, stated fairly.
3. `{{price_line}}` from `../../pricing.json` — Solo, Team, Team+ and the AI Employee add-on with its `{{ai_employee_disclosure}}` (no trial, no refund). Never type a price into this file.
4. Aapka apna hisaab: ek bachi hui deal ka commission vs mahine ka plan. Aap bharo.
5. CTA: `CTA-DEMO-002`.

> Prices come only from `../../pricing.json`. The replacement pricing proposal is `docs/realestateflow-vision/38-pricing-plan-contacts-and-credits.md`. The June draft's "Free / ₹999 / ₹2,999 / ₹5,999" tiers exist in no source, and its "₹20L/yr leakage" and "₹25L recovered" figures are banned unsourced claims.

**C4 — OPP-163 · "Apni leak khud nikalo" · CT-EDU · FW-LEAD-LEAKAGE**
1. Hook: "60 second mein apni lead leak nikalo."
2. Teen inputs: leads per month, close rate, average commission — **your** numbers.
3. Kahan girti hai: no reply / no second follow-up / no record.
4. Ab wahi hisaab tracked leads ke saath. Farak aap dekhoge.
5. CTA: `CTA-COMMENT-002` ("comment 'AUDIT'").

> The Leakage Audit asset must exist before this carousel runs — the on-site calculator at `agency-app/landing-pages/agency-owners/index.html` is still a placeholder.

**C5 — OPP-511 · "Property turant share karo" · CT-EDU · FW-PROPERTY**
1. Hook: "Client ne 2BHK manga — aap file dhoondh rahe ho?"
2. Purana tarika: 30 WhatsApp chats, ek PDF, aadha data.
3. Naya tarika: teen tap mein property page share (screen recording).
4. Client ko link, aapko booking — site visit seedha CRM mein.
5. CTA: `CTA-SAVE-028`.

The June draft also carried a "RealEstateFlow vs Salesforce" carousel. The month-1 rule is never to name a competitor, and that outline also claimed a "Hinglish UI" (the CRM UI is English) and an AI-calling row that is not an approved claim. Use the category framing — "built for how an Indian broking agency actually works" — without naming anyone.

## 8. Cadence and metrics

**Cadence:** 2 carousels a week (`content-cadence.md` weekly quota), typically Monday (Broker ki Galti) and Thursday (product walkthrough), posted 7–8 AM. Caption is the mini-blog. Cross-post to LinkedIn and Facebook the same day.

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| **Save rate** (saves ÷ reach) | the #1 carousel metric — beat reels on this |
| **Swipe-through** (slide-5 views ÷ cover views) | > 45% reach the CTA |
| **Comments** (keyword triggers) | spike on FW-MYTH and FW-LEAD-LEAKAGE covers |
| **Shares** to peer groups | "send to team" CTAs drive this |
| **Carousel → DM** (lead-magnet keywords) | log by `OPP-*` and `CTA-*` (`follower-to-demo.md`) |

> Judge carousels by **saves and lead-magnet DMs**, not reach. A carousel that earns 400 saves and 12 "AUDIT" DMs beats one with 20k reach and zero saves. Metric definitions live in `../../50-measurement/metric-dictionary.md`.
