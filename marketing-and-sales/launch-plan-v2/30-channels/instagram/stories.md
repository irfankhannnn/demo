# Instagram — stories

> **RealEstateFlow · `@realestateflow`**
> Stories are the **warm layer and the funnel-to-DM engine**. Reels and carousels win cold reach; stories nurture the audience already earned and convert it into 1:1 conversations. This is where a follower becomes a DM and a DM becomes a demo (`follower-to-demo.md`, `dm-workflows.md`).

**Audience reality (`../../10-audience-and-voice/market-research.md`):** Mumbai brokers scroll at **6–8 AM (chai), 12–1 PM (lunch), 6–8 PM (wind-down)**, on Android, sound mostly off, between hundreds of WhatsApp messages. Stories must be **glanceable in 2 seconds and tappable**. Brand copy 70% English / 30% romanized Hindi; Mumbai only in M1 (D24).

**Any face on screen is the AI presenter speaking for the brand**, never a broker, owner or customer. Free-form DM replies work only inside Meta's 24-hour window.

## 2. Daily Story Slots (the fixed grid)

Run **4–7 frames/day**, batched, posted across the 3 peak windows. Don't dump 7 at once — space them.

| Slot | Window | Frame type | Goal | Sticker | CTA category |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | 6–8 AM | **Re-share today's Reel** ("naya reel live 👇") | Drive reel views | Reaction slider / "watch" tap | — (internal) |
| S2 | 8–10 AM | **Poll / This-or-That** (broker pain) | Engagement signal | Poll | CTA-COMMENT energy |
| S3 | 12–1 PM | **BTS / cast moment** | Warmth, recall | Quiz or emoji slider | CTA-FOLLOW / COMMUNITY |
| S4 | 4–6 PM | **Mini-tip / 1 stat card** | Save + authority | Quiz | CTA-SAVE / DM keyword |
| S5 | 6–8 PM | **Question sticker** ("biggest lead-pain?") | Open DMs | Question | CTA-DM |
| S6 | 6–8 PM | **Funnel frame** (demo/trial link sticker) | Lead-gen | Link sticker | CTA-DEMO / TRIAL / WHATSAPP |
| S7 | flex | **Build-in-public drop** (what shipped this week, screen recording) | Trust | "DM us" | CTA-DM / DEMO |

> Minimum viable day = **S1 + S5 + S6** (re-share the reel, open a question, drop the funnel). Friday evening: a heavier funnel push, since Friday evening over-indexes.
>
> S7 used to be a proof drop — a testimonial screenshot or a customer count. We have neither. Build-in-public does the same job honestly: show what actually shipped this week.

## 3. Interactive Sticker Playbook

Stickers are the engagement currency of Stories. Each maps to a goal and feeds a downstream funnel.

### 3.1 Poll (binary) — fastest engagement
- **Use for:** validating a pain, soft objection surfacing, reach warm-up.
- **Frame formula:** Hinglish question + 2 options + a presenter face reacting.
- **Examples:**
  - "Leads kahan rakhte ho? 👉 **WhatsApp** / **Excel**" (presenter, confused)
  - "Follow-up yaad rehta hai? 👉 **Haan** / **Bhool jaata hoon**"
- **Funnel:** whoever taps "Excel / bhool jaata hoon" = warm pain → next frame offers the fix + link sticker.

### 3.2 Quiz (multi-choice, has a "right" answer) — teach + save
- **Use for:** CT-EDU micro-lessons, myth-busting (FW-MYTH), stat reveals.
- **Frame formula:** "Guess karo: aapke 100 leads mein se kitne convert hue?" → options 5 / 12 / 30 → reveal frame does **not** name a number for them; it names the three places leads go missing and asks them to count their own (OPP-013).
- **Funnel:** the reveal frame ends with CTA-LEAD-MAGNET ("Leakage Audit — DM 'AUDIT'"), and only once that asset exists.

### 3.3 Emoji Slider — emotion + low-friction tap
- **Use for:** reactions to drama/BTS, "kitna relate kiya" gauge.
- **Frame formula:** "WhatsApp chaos kitna relatable? 🔥 slide karo" over a drama still.
- **Funnel:** a high slide → re-surface the matching reel; feeds the engagement-signal read in `comments.md`.

### 3.4 Question sticker — the DM opener (most important)
- **Use for:** opening conversations, sourcing content ideas, surfacing objections.
- **Frame formula (prompts):**
  - "Apni sabse badi lead-problem batao — main agle reel mein solve karunga." (CTA-COMMENT-003 energy)
  - "Konsa CRM use karte ho abhi? Sach bolo, judge nahi karunga."
  - "Ek sawaal poocho RealEstateFlow ke baare mein — sab answer karunga."
- **Funnel (critical):** **every answer is a warm DM thread.** Reply privately → run the DM script in `dm-workflows.md`. Do not re-share someone's answer as proof, even anonymised — a reply to us is not consent to be published.

### 3.5 Link sticker — the conversion frame
- **Use for:** demo, trial, WhatsApp, lead-magnet, community.
- **Rule:** one clear link per frame, with the presenter pointing and a 3-word Hinglish label.
  - "Walkthrough dekho 👆 90 sec" → `realestateflow.in/demo/` (CTA-DEMO-001)
  - "{{trial_line}} 👆" → `app.realestateflow.in/signup` (CTA-TRIAL-001)
  - "WhatsApp pe baat 👆" → WhatsApp link (CTA-WHATSAPP-001)
  - "Leakage Audit 👆 free" → lead magnet, once it exists (CTA-LEAD-MAGNET-001)

  Hold every link back until `realestateflow.in` serves HTTPS.

> **One primary action per frame.** Never stack demo + trial + community on the same story.

## 4. Story → DM Funnels (the money path)

Three repeatable funnels. Each is a 2–3 frame sequence ending in a DM thread that hands to `dm-workflows.md`.

### Funnel A — "Pain Poll → Fix → DM"
1. **Poll frame:** "Leads WhatsApp mein kho jaate hain? Haan / Nahi" (FW-WHATSAPP-CHAOS).
2. **Fix frame:** "Jinhone 'Haan' kiya — yeh 30-second walkthrough dekho 👇" + link sticker (CTA-DEMO).
3. **DM bait frame:** "DM mein 'AUDIT' likho, leakage audit bhej deta hoon" (CTA-DM-001 / LEAD-MAGNET).
→ The keyword rule replies once App Review passes; until then a person does, within 15 minutes (`dm-workflows.md` §1).

### Funnel B — "Question Sticker → Private Reply → Demo"
1. **Question frame:** "Konsi cheez sabse zyada time khaati hai?"
2. Broker answers → **reply privately**, empathize, mini-value, soft demo offer.
3. Hand to DM qualify→demo flow.

### Funnel C — "Audit score → Curiosity → WhatsApp"

The old Funnel C opened on "200+ brokerages already on RealtyFlow" and an Andheri broker who cut leakage to near zero in 60 days. Both are fabrications — we have zero customers — so the funnel is rebuilt on something the viewer produces themselves.

1. **Audit frame:** "Pichhle mahine ki leads mein se kitno ko teesra follow-up gaya? Ginke batao." (quiz or question sticker)
2. **Curiosity frame:** "Jo number aaya — usko zero kaise karte hain? 90 second mein dikhata hoon."
3. **WhatsApp frame:** "Poori baat WhatsApp pe — 👆 tap" (CTA-WHATSAPP).

## 5. Behind-the-Scenes (BTS) System

BTS builds the parasocial familiarity brokers need before they DM. Use the recurring cast so the universe feels continuous — the same trick reels use — while keeping the "AI presenter" disclosure visible on the profile.

> Open decision D25 — see marketing-and-sales/launch-plan-v2/00-OPEN-DECISIONS.md

| BTS type | Cast | Frame idea | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Office life | fictional office cast | "Monday standup — leaderboard reveal" | 2×/wk |
| Founder voice | text or voice note from the founder, no face | "Aaj kya seekha" (FW-FOUNDER) | 1–2×/wk |
| Newbie POV | fictional newbie character | "Day 3 — pehla lead add kiya 🎉" | 1×/wk |
| Field/UGC | presenter, handheld | "scene yeh hai bro…", speaking for the brand | 2×/wk |
| Build-in-public | screen recording `[REC]` | "Naya feature aa raha hai — kya chahiye?" (question sticker) | 1×/wk |

> BTS frames keep stickers light (emoji slider or question) — the goal is warmth and recall, not conversion. Logo bug bottom-left (`../../20-content-engine/visual-system.md`), except raw handheld selfies.
>
> There is no "customer love" row. Reposting a customer story needs a customer, and an AI character playing a happy customer is exactly what the claims policy forbids.

## 6. Story Highlights Architecture (evergreen funnels)

Highlights are the **pinned, always-on mini-funnels** a new profile visitor taps after a reel hooks them. Design the covers in brand-kit v3 (`VP-THUMB` style, tokens from `../../10-audience-and-voice/brand-constants.md`). Order them left to right as a funnel.

| # | Highlight | Cover label | Contents | Funnel job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Shuru Karo** | "START 👋" | who we are, what RealEstateFlow does in 3 frames, the AI-presenter disclosure, link | Orient a cold visitor |
| 2 | **Demo** | "DEMO 🎬" | 90-second walkthrough clips, link sticker | MOFU → demo (CTA-DEMO) |
| 3 | **Leakage** | "AUDIT ₹" | the Leakage Audit, once built (OPP-163) | Lead-magnet capture |
| 4 | **AI Employee** | "AI 🤖" | screen recordings of the WhatsApp AI Employee (FW-AI, OPP-192) | Curiosity + demo |
| 5 | **Pricing** | "PRICE 💸" | `{{price_line}}` and `{{ai_employee_disclosure}}` from `../../pricing.json` | BOFU objection |
| 6 | **Community** | "AGENTS 🤝" | community invite (CTA-COMMUNITY) | Belonging / nurture |
| 7 | **FAQ** | "SAWAAL ❓" | top 10 objections answered (`../../40-sales-and-conversion/objections.md`) | De-risk |
| 8 | **Trial** | "FREE TRIAL ✅" | `{{trial_line}}` + setup help | BOFU (CTA-TRIAL) |

The Proof and Pune highlights are gone: no customers to prove anything with, and no Pune scope in M1. Proof returns when a paying customer permits it in writing.

> Refresh Highlights monthly; keep covers visually identical for the one-brand look. Every Highlight ends on a link or DM frame so it always exits to an action.

## 7. Frequency & Production SOP

- **Volume:** 4–7 frames/day, 6 days/week (Sunday lighter). ~30–40 frames/week.
- **Batch:** produce a week of stories in one sitting. Stories are posted manually whatever we decide about scheduling — Blotato schedules feed posts, not stories.
  > Open decision D22 — see marketing-and-sales/launch-plan-v2/00-OPEN-DECISIONS.md
- **Language mix:** the brand rule, 70% English / 30% romanized Hindi, and 0% Marathi in M1 (`../../10-audience-and-voice/brand-constants.md`). Subtitles burned in for sound-off.
- **Rule of one:** one primary CTA per frame; log the `CTA-*` used in the tracking sheet (`../../20-content-engine/ctas/cta-library.md`).

### Daily checklist
1. [ ] Re-share today's reel/carousel (S1).
2. [ ] 1 interactive sticker frame (poll/quiz/slider).
3. [ ] 1 question sticker (open DMs).
4. [ ] 1 funnel frame (link sticker → demo/trial/WA).
5. [ ] Reply to **every** sticker response and DM inside the 24-hour window → hand to `dm-workflows.md`.
6. [ ] Note which frame drove the most taps and DMs → feed `growth-loops.md` and `content-cadence.md`.

## 8. KPIs (Stories)

| Metric | What it tells you | Target (steady state) |
|---|---|---|
| Story reach / follower base | Recall + algorithm warmth | ≥ 25% of followers |
| Tap-forward rate | Frame quality (lower = better) | < 12% |
| Sticker interaction rate | Engagement-signal strength | ≥ 8% of viewers |
| Question/DM replies per day | Top-of-conversation volume | ≥ 10/day by Month 2 |
| Link sticker taps | Funnel pull | track per `CTA-*` |
| Story → DM → demo conversions | The actual objective | `follower-to-demo.md` |

> Stories are measured by **conversations started**, not views. A story day that produced 12 DM threads beats one with 5k views and zero replies. Definitions live in `../../50-measurement/metric-dictionary.md`.
