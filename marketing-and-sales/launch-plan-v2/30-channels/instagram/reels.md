# Instagram — reels (the reach engine)

> **RealEstateFlow · `@realestateflow`**
> Reels carry most of the cold reach and most broker content consumption (`../../10-audience-and-voice/market-research.md` §9.1). This is the **scroll-stop → follow → save → DM** engine. Carousels earn saves and Stories nurture, but reels are how a Mumbai broker who has never heard of us first meets the brand at 9 PM on the couch. Works with `CT-DRAMA/EDU/DEMO/AI/UGC/MEME/AUTHORITY`, `FW-*`, `HK-*`, `CTA-*`, visual presets `VP-*` and the content plan (`../../20-content-engine/content-plan/content-plan-500.md`, `OPP-*`).

**Audience reality:** Android, **sound OFF**, scrolling between hundreds of WhatsApp messages at **9–11 PM (drama), 6–8 AM (chai/edu), 12–1 PM (lunch/meme)**. They share to peer WhatsApp groups when content validates a frustration or makes them look smart. Brand copy 70% English / 30% romanized Hindi; Mumbai only in M1 (D24). Money in ₹/lakh/crore, never "improve close rate by 50%".

**Every face in a reel is the AI presenter speaking for the brand.** The founder is never on camera, and the presenter never claims to be a broker, an owner or a customer. See `../../10-audience-and-voice/claims-and-proof-policy.md` and `../../20-content-engine/cast-and-presenter.md`.

## 1. Reel Anatomy (second-by-second, sound-off-first)

A 30-second reel has a fixed skeleton. Every second has a job. Subtitles are **always burned in** (sound off).

| Time | Job | What's on screen | Mapped to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0.0–1.0s** | Scroll-stop. Hook line + a face mid-reaction | Big Hinglish text (top third) + presenter face/motion | `HK-*` (line 1) |
| **1.0–3.0s** | "This is me" — name the pain in their words | presenter doing the painful thing (scrolling WhatsApp, fumbling) | the pain list in `../../10-audience-and-voice/icp-and-personas.md` |
| **3.0–6.0s** | Agitate / open the loop | cost in ₹, or "kaise?" tease | `FW-*` setup beat |
| **6.0–22.0s** | One idea, delivered via the framework structure | drama escalation, or 3 teaching beats, or 2–3 real screen taps | `FW-*` body |
| **22.0–27.0s** | Payoff / twist / result | product brings order; before→after flip | `FW-*` resolution |
| **27.0–30.0s** | ONE CTA + end-card | spoken + on-screen CTA, logo bug, handle | `CTA-*` (single) |

**The 3 non-negotiables:**
1. **Hook in frame 1** — text must be readable before the audio loads. Pull a hook from `../../20-content-engine/hooks/hook-library.md` matching the framework (every hook is tagged `best_frameworks`).
2. **One idea per reel** — combining two frameworks dilutes; the hook may borrow a second framework's *energy*, the structure follows one (`../../20-content-engine/framework-library.md` pairing rule).
3. **One CTA** — log the `CTA-*` used in the tracking sheet so attribution knows which line drove the DM (`../../40-sales-and-conversion/dm-to-demo.md`).

## 2. Reel Format Templates (by content type)

Each template = a repeatable shoot recipe. Default character, framework, length, primary metric, and a worked OPP-* example.

### 2.1 Drama / Skit — `CT-DRAMA` (the flagship reach engine)
- **Frameworks:** FW-DRAMA, FW-WHATSAPP-CHAOS, FW-SKIT, FW-CONVO · **Cast:** clearly fictional duos, labelled "dramatised" on screen · **Length:** 20–45s (up to 60s) · **Win:** reach + follows + shares.
  > Open decision D25 — see marketing-and-sales/launch-plan-v2/00-OPEN-DECISIONS.md
- **Structure:** relatable office scene → conflict (pain bites) → twist (system saves) → punchline → CTA-FOLLOW/COMMENT.
- **Example — OPP-001** "POV broker scrolling 200 unread WhatsApp, dhoondh raha ek lead": frantic scrolling (0–6s) → the message is buried → "buyer already bought" (twist) → cut to the dashboard, calm → `CTA-FOLLOW`. Hook: `HK-WHATSAPP-CHAOS-*`.

### 2.2 Educational — `CT-EDU` (the save engine in reel form)
- **Frameworks:** FW-MISTAKE, FW-CRM, FW-MYTH, FW-LEAD-LEAKAGE · **Cast:** presenter talking + screen inserts · **Length:** 20–35s · **Win:** saves + authority.
- **Structure:** "3 galtiyan jo har broker karta hai" → mistake+consequence ×3 → fix → CTA-SAVE.
- **Example — OPP-013** "100 leads aaye — aapke kitne convert hue?": presenter at a desk, an on-screen counter the viewer fills with their own number, the three places leads go missing, then the plug → `CTA-SAVE-014`. Hook: `HK-LEAD-MANAGEMENT-*` / `HK-REVENUE-*`. Never assert the conversion rate for them.

### 2.3 Product Demo — `CT-DEMO` (the conversion engine)
- **Frameworks:** FW-DEMO, FW-AI, FW-CRM · **Cast:** hands on a phone, `[REC]` screen recording of the real product · **Length:** 15–30s · **Win:** demo clicks + trials.
- **Structure:** pain in 1 line → "dekho kaise" → 2–3 taps screen-record → result → CTA-DEMO.
- **Example — OPP-443** "WhatsApp mein jo deal laga thi woh gayi kahan (demo)": pain line → one-tap WhatsApp-lead-to-system capture → reminder fires → `CTA-DEMO-001`. Hook: `HK-CURIOSITY-*`.

### 2.4 AI Employee — `CT-AI` (differentiation reach)
- **Frameworks:** FW-AI, FW-CURIOSITY · **Cast:** presenter + `[REC]` screen recording · **Length:** 15–30s · **Win:** curiosity + demos.
- **Structure:** name the moment → show it happening on a real screen → what it leaves behind → CTA-DEMO/DM.
- **Example — OPP-192** "WhatsApp pe likho 'aaj ke meetings' — AI Employee list bhej deta hai": record the actual exchange, with no narration of a result nobody can check.
- **What this slot may not say.** The June draft's example was "AI ne mere 100 buyers ko call kiya, 30 qualified nikle" — a first-person result claim about AI calling. AI calling is not on the approved-claims list, the automated follow-up agent only calls inside the tenant's business hours, and the presenter may not speak as a broker reporting their own results.
  > Open decision D26 — see marketing-and-sales/launch-plan-v2/00-OPEN-DECISIONS.md

### 2.5 Handheld / UGC style — `CT-UGC` (relatability)
- **Frameworks:** FW-UGC, FW-PROPERTY, FW-OBJECTION · **Cast:** AI presenter, selfie framing · **Length:** 20–40s · **Win:** relatability.
- **Structure:** handheld, the presenter speaks **for RealEstateFlow** — never "main ek broker hoon" — one benefit shown on a real screen → casual CTA.
- **Example — OPP-436** "Follow-up ka reminder khud aa jaye toh?" The June draft's example asserted "response within 15 min = 60% close, after 1 hr = 20%", which is unsourced; replace it with a question the viewer answers from their own inbox.
- Paid variants are out of scope while M1 runs no paid ads.

### 2.6 Meme / relatable — `CT-MEME` (cheap reach + shares)
- **Frameworks:** FW-SKIT, FW-CURIOSITY, FW-WHATSAPP-CHAOS · **Cast:** any, exaggerated, clearly fictional · **Length:** 8–20s · **Win:** shares.
- **Example — OPP-435** "2026 aa gaya bhai aur tu abhi bhi Excel pe" · **OPP-430** "POV jab client puche mera lead kahan hai." CTA-SHARE.

### 2.7 Authority — `CT-AUTHORITY` (brand trust)
- **Frameworks:** FW-FOUNDER, FW-AUTHORITY, FW-CONTRARIAN · **Cast:** AI presenter speaking for the brand. The founder's own voice lives in LinkedIn text and WhatsApp messages, never on camera · **Length:** 30–60s · **Win:** follows + community.
- **Example — OPP-383** "Launch day, RealEstateFlow ab live hai" · **OPP-088** "Built for how an Indian broking agency actually works." CTA-COMMUNITY/FOLLOW. Do not name a competitor.

### 2.8 Case study — `CT-CASE` · **disabled**

Disabled until a paying customer gives written permission to publish their numbers. The June draft cast AI characters as "the customer" (OPP-280 "Arjun 5→12", OPP-372 "Andheri ~0 leak"). An AI face presenting as a real customer is the thing the claims policy forbids most explicitly, and the underlying results were invented anyway.

> Master recipe (CT → FW → presenter → CTA → Higgsfield → VP) lives in `../../20-content-engine/content-types.md`. These templates inherit those defaults.

## 3. Hook · Retention · CTA Mechanics

**Hook (0–1.5s):** Pull from `../../20-content-engine/hooks/hook-library.md` by category matching the angle — FEAR/REVENUE for leakage, WHATSAPP-CHAOS for drama, AI for the AI Employee, CURIOSITY for demos. The **number/loss** and **POV/story** patterns stop best. A number in a hook must be the viewer's own ("100 leads aaye — kitne ka pata hai?"), never one we assert. Burn the hook in as on-screen text.

**Retention levers (1s–27s):**
- **Open loops:** tease the payoff ("dikhau kahan?") and resolve only at the end.
- **Pattern interrupts every 3–5s:** cut, zoom, caption-pop, b-roll insert — sound-off scrollers need visual motion.
- **Visual progress:** counters, "Galti #1 / #2 / #3" labels, before→after split.
- **No slow intro:** never "Hi, aaj hum baat karenge…" — the value starts in frame 1.

**CTA (27–30s):** ONE category, matched to the funnel stage (`../../20-content-engine/ctas/cta-library.md`). TOFU reels → FOLLOW/SAVE/SHARE/COMMENT; MOFU → COMMENT-keyword (SYSTEM / KHATA / AUDIT / DEMO) or DEMO; BOFU → TRIAL. The pinned comment restates it (`comments.md`).

## 4. Audio Strategy

| Reel type | Audio | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Drama / Meme | **Trending audio** (lip-synced or background) | algorithm boost + culturally current; sound-on viewers get bonus |
| Educational / Demo | **Clear VO** — ElevenLabs only, per the voice spec in `../../20-content-engine/cast-and-presenter.md` and `../../20-content-engine/higgsfield-guide.md` | clarity beats trend; teaching needs a voice |
| AI Employee | VO over a real screen recording | the recording *is* the proof |

**Rules:** subtitles ALWAYS (sound-off default). For trending audio, check it's not falling (use within first ~2 weeks of trend). VO in the piece's `language` tag. Keep music under the VO at about 15% volume.

## 5. Editing & Pacing Rules

- **Cut rhythm:** a cut/zoom/text-change every **2–4 seconds**. Dead air = scroll.
- **Aspect:** 9:16, 1080×1920. Safe zone per the month-1 pack: the top 0–220px and 1500–1920px are dead, and keep the right 140px clear.
- **Text:** Unbounded 800 for hooks, Manrope for captions, marigold highlight on ink; max ~6 words per line, high contrast for outdoor Android screens. Tokens live in `../../10-audience-and-voice/brand-constants.md` — no hex values in this file.
- **First-frame test:** if the thumbnail (frame 1) doesn't make a broker pause, re-shoot the hook.
- **Length discipline:** kill anything over 45s unless it is an authority piece earning the time.
- **Logo bug** bottom-left except raw handheld selfies (`../../20-content-engine/visual-system.md` non-negotiable).

## 6. Cover / Thumbnail Specs (`VP-THUMB-*`)

The grid is a shopfront. Covers must be coherent so a profile visit reads as one brand.

- **Layout:** big Hinglish phrase (top) + presenter face (worried → relieved) + at most one number, and only one the viewer supplies or one we can point at on a real screen.
- **Per content type:** `VP-THUMB-EDU` (desk + number), `VP-THUMB-DRAMA` (two-shot + reaction), `VP-THUMB-DEMO` (phone screen + face), `VP-THUMB-AI` ("AI 📞" badge).
- **Consistency:** same caption-bar style + logo bug across all covers → grid coherence (the same trick Highlights covers use in `stories.md §6`).
- **Pinned 3:** top-of-grid pinned reels = one franchise hook + one walkthrough + one build-in-public, so a new visitor immediately gets entertainment, product and honesty. The pinned post carries the "AI presenter" disclosure.

## 8. Posting SOP

1. **Source** the `OPP-*` from `../../20-content-engine/content-plan/content-plan-500.csv` (filter by the theme-day's CT and language). Check it against the claims policy before producing it.
2. **Route** `[AI]` / `[REC]` / `[ED]` per `../../20-content-engine/README.md`; the editor is capped at about 3 videos a week.
3. **Run the pipeline** (`../../20-content-engine/production-pipeline.md`): hook → framework → dialogue → VO/subtitles → `CTA-*`.
4. **Render** in `marketing-and-sales/video-projects/my-video/` where a render is needed, or generate via the CT's Higgsfield workflow (HF-DRAMA-DIALOGUE / HF-DEMO-SCREEN / HF-UGC-SELFIE / HF-TALKING-HEAD) using saved Soul IDs. Name the output `REF-<TYPE><NN>-<slug>-v<N>` under `marketing-and-sales/creative/`.
5. **Publish** to **Instagram + Facebook Reels** at the peak slot (drama → 9–11 PM; edu → 7–8 AM; demo → Fri/Sat 1–2 PM and 9 PM). Blotato can schedule feed posts; YouTube Shorts is parked.
   > Open decision D22 — see marketing-and-sales/launch-plan-v2/00-OPEN-DECISIONS.md
6. **Caption:** Hinglish mini-context + SEO keywords ("real estate CRM", "lead leakage", "WhatsApp AI Employee") + the CTA line + 8–12 hashtags (`growth-loops.md` §7).
7. **First 60 min:** reply to every comment, pin the CTA comment, move buying-intent comments to DM (`comments.md`).
8. **Log:** `OPP-*`, `CTA-*`, posting time → the tracking sheet, which is what attribution runs on today (`../../50-measurement/attribution-today.md`).

## 9. Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Hook + value in frame 1, sound-off readable | "Hi guys, aaj hum…" slow intros |
| One framework, one idea, one CTA | Stack demo + trial + community in one reel |
| ₹/lakh/crore concrete money | "improve efficiency", "digital transformation" |
| Reuse the same presenter and Soul IDs | Random faces or stock footage (brokers smell fake) |
| Disclose "AI presenter" where a face appears | Let an AI face pass as a broker, owner or customer |
| Subtitles burned in always | Rely on audio for comprehension |
| Reply in first 60 min | Post and ghost |

## 10. Metrics & Benchmarks

| Metric | What it tells you | Target (steady state) |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook rate** (3-sec views / plays) | Frame-1 stop power | **> 40%** |
| **Avg watch / completion** | Retention + framework strength | > 50% completion on <30s |
| **Reach** per reel | Cold distribution | drama/meme 10k+, 1–2/mo > 50k |
| **Saves** | Reference value | edu carousels-in-reel-form lead |
| **Shares** | Peer-WhatsApp pull (the strongest trust signal) | drama/meme lead; track sends |
| **Comments** | Algo + objection-mining | keyword reels spike this |
| **DMs per reel** | Conversation volume | log by `OPP-*` |
| **Reel → demo** | The objective | attribute via `CTA-*` in the tracking sheet (`follower-to-demo.md`) |

> A reel is judged by **DMs, saves and shares**, not raw views. Kill low-hook-rate formats fast; keep the presenter, change the format. Winners are the candidates for paid creative later — there are no paid ads in M1, and paid only opens behind the PMF gate. Metric definitions live in `../../50-measurement/metric-dictionary.md`.
