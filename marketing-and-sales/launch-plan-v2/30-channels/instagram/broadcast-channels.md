# Instagram — broadcast channel

> **RealEstateFlow · `@realestateflow`**
> A **1-to-many warm-retention layer** that survives algorithm dips. Reels and carousels chase cold reach; the broadcast channel is a push line straight to the brokers who already raised their hand — no feed, no algorithm gatekeeping. Channel name: **"RealEstateFlow Brokers"**. Works with `../virality-and-retention.md`, `content-cadence.md`, `follower-to-demo.md`, and the WhatsApp layer.

**Audience reality:** brokers already get hundreds of WhatsApp messages a day — a broadcast must be glanceable, useful, low-frequency and never pure promo. Value:promo ratio ≥ 4:1. Language per `../../10-audience-and-voice/brand-constants.md` (70% English / 30% romanized Hindi for brand copy).

## 2. Launch plan

1. **Create** the channel "RealEstateFlow Brokers" (Meta broadcast).
2. **Seed:** announce across 3 reels and daily stories for the first week — "Daily broker tips + early access to new features — join 👇" (`CTA-COMMUNITY` energy).
3. **Pin** the channel link in the bio and in a permanent Highlight ("AGENTS 🤝", `stories.md` §6).
4. **First message (welcome):** "Swagat hai 🙌 yahan roz ek quick tip (follow-up, lead, khata) + naye features sabse pehle. Pakka useful, zero spam. Reply karke apni biggest lead-problem batao."
5. **Convert existing DMs:** invite every nurture-stage lead from `dm-workflows.md` §6.

## 4. Message templates

- **Quick tip:** "💡 {one-line tip}. {why it matters, in ₹ or time}. Try karke batao 👇"
- **Poll:** "Aaj ka sawaal: {binary/triple option}? Vote karo — kal ka tip uspe banayenge."
- **BTS / build-in-public:** "Behind the scenes: {what we are building}. Aapko kya chahiye iss feature mein?"
- **Product drop:** "{feature} — 40 second ka screen recording. Aapki agency mein kaam aayega? Reply 'DEMO'."
- **Soft funnel (1 in 5 max):** "Demo dekhna hai? 👉 realestateflow.in/demo/. Ya WhatsApp community join karo: {link}."

No "proof drop" template. Until a paying customer gives written permission there is no count and no testimonial to send — the product drop replaces it. No Pune template for M1 (D24); there is no Pune team to connect anyone to.

## 5. Growth tactics

- **Reel/story CTA:** end value-heavy posts with "full tips channel mein — join (link in bio)."
- **Exclusivity:** drop lead magnets and beta access **channel-first** ("yeh sirf channel pe hai").
- **DM rescue:** invite cold DM leads (the +7d nudge) instead of dropping them.
- **Cross-channel:** mention it in the WhatsApp community and on LinkedIn for owners.
- **Reply prompts:** ask a question in about one drop in three to keep reactions and reach high.

---

## 6. Conversion to demo / community

The channel is a **slow trust drip**, not a hard funnel. Periodically (≤1 in 5):

- **To demo:** "Demo dekhna hai? 👉 {link}" (`CTA-DEMO`).
- **To community:** invite to the WhatsApp community (`CTA-COMMUNITY`, `../whatsapp/community.md`).
- **To live:** a monthly AMA in Month 3 — text or voice note, no video (`90-day-growth-strategy.md`).

Hand any "reply 'DEMO'" to `dm-workflows.md`, then `../../40-sales-and-conversion/dm-to-demo.md`.

---

## 7. Cadence

2–3 messages a week minimum; never pure promo; **value:promo ≥ 4:1**. Drop at peak windows (morning tip 7–8 AM, evening drop 6–8 PM). Do not over-post — an over-active channel gets muted, and a muted channel is dead.

## 8. Metrics

| Metric | What it tells you | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Subscribers | warm-audience size | grows with each reel CTA week |
| Open / reaction rate | message relevance | reactions matter more than opens |
| Reply rate | conversation pull | rises with poll/question drops |
| Channel → demo clicks | funnel pull | track per drop |
| Mute / leave rate | over-posting signal | keep low (the 4:1 ratio protects this) |

Reported in `../../50-measurement/weekly-scorecard.md`. Judge the channel by **demo clicks and replies**, not subscriber count.
