# Instagram — channel strategy

> **The primary channel for RealEstateFlow.** Mumbai brokers consume most of their professional content here (`../../10-audience-and-voice/market-research.md` §9.1), sound-off, one-handed, between site visits. Instagram is where reach, relatability and warm-lead capture happen. This file is the **strategy layer**; the execution system (grid plan, Reels engine, Stories engine, highlight architecture, hashtag banks, DM flows, growth playbook) lives in the sibling files in this folder.

>
> **Bio and pinned post must disclose "AI presenter."** Every video is fronted by an AI presenter speaking for the brand, never as a broker, owner or customer. See `../../10-audience-and-voice/claims-and-proof-policy.md`.

## 1. Audience

| Segment | Who | Language posture | What stops their scroll |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Agency Owner / Boss** (the buyer) | 35–45, 3–8+ agents, Android, control-anxious, ROI-driven | `hi-dominant` + `en-leaning` proof, **aap** | ₹-quantified pain, checkable product behaviour, "team accountability" |
| **Sales Manager / Team Lead** | 28–40, runs the floor | `hi-dominant`, **aap/tum** | lead-assignment fairness, visibility-without-micromanagement |
| **Young Broker / Consultant** | 26–35, field hustler, FOMO | `mixed`, **tu/bhai** | drama, humour, hustle, "AI handles admin" |
| **New Joiner** | new MEMBER | simple `hi`, **tum** | "ghabrao mat", onboarding ease |

Persona names are deliberately omitted here — the cast in `../../20-content-engine/cast-and-presenter.md` already owns the names, and reusing them for real personas caused a collision (the old draft called the owner "Priya", which is the sales-manager character).

- **Geo:** Mumbai only for M1 (Bambaiyya Hinglish base). Pune and the Marathi handshake are deferred until after the PMF gate.
  > Open decision D24 — see marketing-and-sales/launch-plan-v2/00-OPEN-DECISIONS.md
- **Behaviour:** passive scroll, shares to WhatsApp broker groups (the key viral signal), saves educational content and templates. They rarely create — they **forward**. Mostly Android, sound-off, one-handed → **burned-in subtitles are non-negotiable**.
- **Mental state:** they scroll to feel seen, feel ahead of rivals and feel safe about money — not to learn. Content that names their exact Tuesday-afternoon pain wins; "transform your workflow" loses.
- **India specifics:** quantify pain in ₹/lakh/crore (not %), reference real lead sources (MagicBricks, 99acres, WhatsApp), RERA where relevant, and prefer **specific, checkable product behaviour over claims**. We have no customers and therefore no peer proof; see the claims policy.

## 3. Content mix (by CT-*)

Weekly target ≈ 7 Reels + 3 Carousels + ~15 Stories. Map each slot to a content type:

| CT-* | Share of feed | Format | Why on IG |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CT-DRAMA** | ~30% (flagship reach) | Reel 20–45s | WhatsApp-loss skits get shared to broker groups — the viral lane |
| **CT-EDU** | ~30% (highest-volume saves) | Reel / Carousel | "3 mistakes in follow-up" → saves + authority |
| **CT-DEMO** | ~20% | Reel 15–30s, screen recording `[REC]` | feature spotlight on the real product; de-risks before trial |
| **CT-UGC** | ~10% | Reel handheld | AI presenter speaking for the brand, handheld style; **never presented as a broker** |
| **CT-MEME** | ~7% | image / Reel | cheap reach, validation humour |
| **CT-NEWS** | reactive | Reel 20–40s | Maharashtra RERA / ready-reckoner newsjacking |
| **CT-AUTHORITY** | ~3% | Reel + cross-post LinkedIn | brand trust; see `../founder-presence.md` |
| **CT-PROOF / CT-CASE** | **0% until real customers** | — | reinstated only with a paying customer's written permission |

The old draft gave CT-PROOF/CT-CASE ~10% of the feed on the theory that peer testimonials are the highest trust signal in this market. That is probably true, and it is unavailable to us: we have zero customers. Those slots go to CT-DEMO (screen recordings of the real product) and CT-EDU until we have proof we are allowed to show.

**Attention budgeting:** per 10 pieces ≈ 5 reach-grabbers (fear/drama TOFU), 3 desire/product-proof (MOFU), 2 conversion (BOFU). Keeps reach high while still producing conversations. See `../../20-content-engine/attention-model.md` §6.

## 4. CTA strategy (by CTA category)

One primary CTA per piece, placed as the last spoken line **and** the caption-ender. A soft secondary (Save/Follow) rides in the caption.

| Funnel | Format pairing | Categories | Example IDs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TOFU** (reach/engagement) | Reels, memes, Stories | **FOLLOW · SAVE · SHARE · COMMENT** | CTA-SAVE-014, CTA-SHARE-007, CTA-COMMENT-006 |
| **MOFU** (capture/conversation) | Carousels, EDU Reels, Stories | **COMMENT (keyword) · DM · WHATSAPP · LEAD-MAGNET · DEMO** | CTA-DM-001, CTA-COMMENT-002, CTA-LEAD-MAGNET-005 |
| **BOFU** (convert) | Stories (link sticker), DEMO Reels | **DEMO · TRIAL · WHATSAPP** | CTA-DEMO-007, CTA-TRIAL-001, CTA-WHATSAPP-016 |

Keyword-comment CTAs ("comment 'SYSTEM'") are the workhorse. The month-1 keyword set is **SYSTEM / KHATA / AUDIT / DEMO** — see `comments.md`. They feed the DM flow in `dm-workflows.md`.

Marathi-led CTAs are out of scope for M1 (D24).

## 5. Lead capture

1. **Comment-to-DM keyword** (primary): "comment 'SYSTEM'" → private reply with the link. Automated by the RealEstateFlow Instagram service's keyword rules (`apps/instagram/backend_insta_sol_ms/services/ruleMatcher.js`) **once Meta App Review passes**; the service is still in Development Mode with `INSTA_DRY_RUN_SENDS` on in dev, so until then a person sends the reply within 15 minutes.
2. **DM keyword triggers** (CTA-DM-*): "DM 'TRIAL'" → signup link.
3. **Story link sticker** → demo / signup / WhatsApp.
4. **Lead magnets** (CTA-LEAD-MAGNET-*): Leakage Audit, Khata tracker, Follow-up SOP. **Each of these must be built before it is offered** — the lead-leakage calculator on the site is still a placeholder (`apps/landing-pages/agency-owners/index.html`).
5. **Bio link** → `realestateflow.in` (demo video, WhatsApp button, signup).
6. **WhatsApp hand-off:** warm IG leads move to WhatsApp for objection handling and pricing — see `../whatsapp/README.md` and `../../40-sales-and-conversion/dm-to-demo.md`.

**Meta limits that bind all of the above:** free-form DMs only inside the 24-hour window, and one private reply per comment within 7 days. Replies are drafted first and never auto-sent without review.

**What the CRM actually records.** A lead created from Instagram carries `source: 'Instagram'`, `sourceAdapter: 'instagram'`, `externalRef` and `reelRef` (`apps/crm/server/leadIngestion.js`). There is no `leadSource`, `contentRef` or `utm` field on a lead, and `/api/marketing/events` is not built. Log the `OPP-*` that drove a lead manually in the tracking sheet until a content-reference field exists.

## 6. Repurposing

**Instagram is the content source of truth.** Most assets originate as 9:16 Reels and flow outward:

| From → To | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| **IG Reel → Facebook Reel** | same 9:16, same caption | cross-post per `../README.md` publishing SOP |
| **IG Reel → YouTube Shorts** | same vertical cut, SEO title/desc | `../youtube.md` — **parked**, see D23 |
| **IG Reel → WhatsApp status / broadcast** | top drama/product clips | `../whatsapp/README.md` |
| **IG Carousel → LinkedIn document/carousel** | EDU/Authority slides, English-leaned | `../linkedin.md` |
| **IG Authority Reel → LinkedIn native video** | recut, English caption | `../founder-presence.md` |
| **FB group post → IG meme/Reel** | best-performing pain points harvested back | reverse flow |

## 7. Publishing workflow

```
Pick OPP/CT-* → FW-* → presenter → HK-* → script (lang tag) → scene/VP-* →
Higgsfield (HF-*) gen or [REC] screen recording → stitch + burned-in subtitles →
caption + hashtags → assign CTA-* → publish at the peak slot →
cross-post (IG + FB Reels) → Recipe Log
```

- **Caption:** hook restated → 2–4 value lines in the language tag → 1 CTA line → 5–10 hashtags (broad `#realestate #realtor` + niche `#mumbairealestate #realestatecrm` + Hinglish `#propertydealsindia`).
- **Publishing:** the Blotato MCP is configured (`.mcp.json`) and can schedule IG and FB feed posts; Stories, the broadcast channel and every comment/DM reply stay manual either way.
  > Open decision D22 — see marketing-and-sales/launch-plan-v2/00-OPEN-DECISIONS.md
- **Quality gate:** claim is on the approved list (`../../10-audience-and-voice/claims-and-proof-policy.md`), hook visual ≤1s, subtitles present, language tag matches the persona, presenter consistency prompt used, brand-kit v3 tokens and 9:16 framing (`../../10-audience-and-voice/brand-constants.md`), single CTA, ₹/lakh/crore, RERA-safe, AI-presenter disclosure present where a face appears, Recipe logged.
- **Deep system:** the sibling files in this folder govern *how at scale*; this file governs *what and why*.

## 8. Metrics

Definitions live in `../../50-measurement/metric-dictionary.md`; do not redefine them here.

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook rate** | 3s views ÷ reach | >45% |
| **Hold rate** | avg watch ÷ length | >55% |
| **Action rate** | (saves+shares+comments+DMs) ÷ reach | track + grow |
| **IG→DM** | DMs ÷ followers/mo | ~1–2% |
| **DM→WhatsApp** | hand-offs ÷ DMs | 40% (`../../40-sales-and-conversion/customer-journey.md`) |
| **Save/share split** | which CT-* drives each | rebalance mix |

The month-1 headline KPI is **waitlist signups**, not demos or trials. Low hook rate = fix the first second, not the topic. Reach without DMs is vanity; keep one CTA on every piece.
