# Virality and retention

Two engines that used to live in separate files and were always the same argument in two halves: **virality is how a stranger arrives, retention is why they stay.** Reach without retention is a leaky bucket; retention without reach is a very cosy dead end.

Applies mainly to Instagram (`instagram/`), with the broadcast and community layers in `instagram/broadcast-channels.md` and `whatsapp/community.md`.

Mumbai only in M1; brand copy 70% English / 30% romanized Hindi.

# Part A — Virality

## 1. The two reach multipliers — shares and saves

**Shares** come from sending something to someone else:

- "This is SO us" recognition (office drama).
- Status flex — "send this to your team / your boss".
- Useful to a peer — "send to a broker who forgets follow-ups".
- Humour (skits, memes).
- Local pride — Mumbai and Bambaiyya specificity. (Regional-language pride was the Pune/Marathi play; parked with the rest of the Pune scope.)

**Saves** come from keeping something for yourself:

- Lists ("3 galtiyan"), how-tos, step-by-steps.
- Templates, frameworks, scripts.
- Arithmetic the viewer runs on their own numbers.
- "I'll need this later" reference content.

**Design rule:** engineer every reel for **either** a share trigger **or** a save trigger, and name it in the script — "send this to…", "save this for…".

## 2. The "tag a broker" mechanic

Every relatable-pain piece ends with a tag prompt:

> "Tag a broker jo aaj bhi WhatsApp pe leads sambhalta hai 👇"

Each tag is a free new viewer, plus a notification that pulls the tagged person in. Works best on `CT-DRAMA`, `CT-MEME` and `FW-WHATSAPP-CHAOS`. Rotate the tag target: the forgetful-follow-up broker, the Excel-loyal owner, the AI sceptic.

## 3. Repeatable viral formats (franchises)

Series compound, because a recurring cast plus a fixed format gives predictable reach and return visits.

| Franchise | Engine | Cadence | Sample episodes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"Apna Properties" office drama** | FW-DRAMA | 2–3/wk | "Lead kaun bhool gaya?", "Commission ka jhagda" |
| **"Broker ki Galti #N"** | FW-MISTAKE | weekly | numbered mistakes |
| **"AI Employee ne kya kiya aaj"** | FW-AI | weekly | real screen recordings of the WhatsApp AI Employee |
| **"WhatsApp Chaos Files"** | FW-WHATSAPP-CHAOS | weekly | lost-lead stories |
| **"Owner vs Reality"** | FW-CONTRARIAN | weekly | hard owner truths |
| **"New Joiner Diaries"** | FW-CONVO | bi-weekly | onboarding comedy |

Cast is governed by `../20-content-engine/cast-and-presenter.md`; how many recurring characters we actually run is open.

Fictional characters are fine in clearly fictional skits, labelled as dramatised. What they may never do is play a **customer**, an **owner giving a testimonial**, or **the founder**.

## 4. Contrarian angles (comments and reach)

Comments signal the algorithm, and contrarian truths spark comments:

- "CRM choti agency ke liye nahi" — myth bust.
- "Aur leads nahi, better follow-up chahiye."
- "Tumhari team ek mehnga WhatsApp group hai."
- "AI tumhe replace nahi karega — AI-wala broker karega."

**Rule:** attack the *belief* or the *old way*, never the viewer. Stay inside `../10-audience-and-voice/product-truth.md` and `../10-audience-and-voice/claims-and-proof-policy.md`. Never name a competitor.

## 5. Trend-jacking SOP

1. **Spot** a trending audio or format, or real-estate news (a RERA rule, a portal price hike, market data, a festival).
2. **Map** it to a `FW-*` — usually FW-NEWS or FW-SKIT — within 24 to 48 hours.
3. **Localise** to Mumbai and to our pain angle.
4. **Ship fast.** Timely beats perfect. Keep a trend-ready presenter shoot on hand, and remember the editor's three-videos-a-week cap — trend-jacks are `[AI]` work, not `[ED]`.

## 6. Collab and cross-pollination loops

- Co-create with broker influencers and CP-community admins for shared audiences.
- **Founder guest spots in text or audio only** — Twitter Spaces, audio podcasts, LinkedIn. Any on-camera slot uses the AI presenter or a product screen recording.
- Duet or stitch broker hot-takes.
- Seed the best reels into Mumbai broker Facebook groups (`facebook.md`).

## 7. Virality checklist (score before posting — ship at ≥9/16)

Score 0–2 each:

- [ ] Hook lands in under a second
- [ ] One clear emotion
- [ ] Relatable to broker daily life
- [ ] Explicit share or tag trigger
- [ ] Save trigger or quotable line
- [ ] Recurring character or format (familiarity)
- [ ] Subtitles (sound-off)
- [ ] Strong, single CTA

Add one gate that is pass/fail rather than scored: **every claim in the piece is on the approved list.** A viral piece built on a fabricated number is worse than no piece.

# Part B — Retention

## 8. Profile as a landing page

A viral reel sends thousands to the profile in three seconds, and it has to convert them to followers.

- **Bio:** "Real estate CRM jo samjhe aapka kaam | Leads · WhatsApp AI Employee · Khata | Mumbai 🏙️", the **"AI presenter" disclosure**, and the CTA link (`realestateflow.in/demo/`, once HTTPS works).
- **Pinned 3 reels:** (1) the best drama — reach and "what is this", (2) an AI Employee screen recording — differentiation, (3) a build-in-public piece — honesty. The third slot used to be a case study; we have none.
- **Highlights:** Shuru Karo · Demo · AUDIT · AI Employee · Pricing · Community · FAQ · Free Trial (`instagram/stories.md` §6). No "Reviews" highlight — there are no reviews.
- **Grid coherence:** consistent covers (`VP-THUMB-*`), brand-kit v3 four-ground system, Unbounded headlines and Manrope body, logo bug. Tokens live in `../10-audience-and-voice/brand-constants.md`; do not hardcode a hex value here. The blue-and-Inter spec the June draft carried is the v1 palette and is superseded.

## 9. Series and recurring characters bring return visits

A recurring cast makes content a *show*, and people follow people rather than products. Each franchise in §3 trains the audience to expect the next episode, which turns viewing into a habit. The presenter is the constant; the format is the variable.

## 10. The binge path

Engineer a path a curious visitor can binge in one sitting:

```
Viral reel → profile → pinned drama → a named highlight
→ a save-worthy carousel → DM CTA
```

Cross-reference inside the content itself: "Part 2 profile pe", "iska full breakdown highlights mein".

## 11. Posting rhythm (consistency beats intensity)

- Reels 5–6/wk · Stories daily (3–5 frames) · Carousels 2/wk · Broadcast 2–3/wk.
- **Theme days are appointment viewing** (`instagram/content-cadence.md` §3): Galti Monday, Demo Tuesday, Drama Wednesday, Walkthrough Thursday, AI Friday, Meme Saturday, Authority Sunday. Thursday used to be "Proof Thursday"; without customers there is no proof to run, so it became a second product walkthrough.
- Same series, same day — the audience learns the schedule and comes back.

## 12. Broadcast channel and community rituals

- **Instagram broadcast channel "RealEstateFlow Brokers":** behind the scenes, tips, early features, polls — off-feed retention that survives algorithm dips (`instagram/broadcast-channels.md`).
- **WhatsApp community** for warm followers (`whatsapp/community.md`).
- **Weekly rituals:** "Monday Pipeline Cleanup" and a Friday build-in-public drop. Predictability is retention.

## 13. Story consistency (the daily touch)

Stories keep the account in the top tray. Daily mix: one value tip, one BTS or poll, one **product** proof — a screen recording or a build-in-public note, not social proof — and on some days one CTA. Interactive stickers create micro-conversations that become DMs.

## 14. Retention loops

| Loop | Mechanism | Fuel |
|---|---|---|
| **Save loop** | reference content re-opened and shared | CT-EDU carousels |
| **Series loop** | "next episode" expectation | franchises |
| **Reply loop** | 1:1 DM and comment replies → loyalty → early engagement on new posts | `instagram/comments.md` |
| **Community loop** | broadcast and WhatsApp → off-feed retention | rituals |
| **Streak loop** | daily stories → habitual check-ins | story cadence |

## 15. Metrics

| Metric | Why | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Shares ÷ reach | the share multiplier | drama and meme lead |
| Saves ÷ reach | the save multiplier | edu leads |
| Tag count | borrowed reach | rising |
| Follower retention (unfollow rate) | are we keeping them? | low and falling |
| Returning-viewer % | is a habit forming? | rising |
| Story completion rate | daily hold | >70% |
| Broadcast open rate | off-feed retention | >30% |
| Profile → link CTR | is the profile converting? | rising |

Definitions in `../50-measurement/metric-dictionary.md`; reported in `../50-measurement/weekly-scorecard.md`.

A viral piece with no CTA is vanity — every reach play still carries one and feeds `../40-sales-and-conversion/dm-to-demo.md`. Falling hold on a series means refreshing the **format** while keeping the **presenter**.
