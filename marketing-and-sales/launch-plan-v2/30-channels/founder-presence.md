# Founder presence

Merged from the two founder docs in the old Content OS: the brand thesis (`founder-brand.md`) and the operating cadence (`founder-engine.md`). The thesis is §1–§4; the cadence, which is the half that gets used daily, is §5 onward.

> ## The rule that shapes this whole file
>
> **The founder is never on camera.** Not on Instagram, not on YouTube, not on a podcast video, not as a "proxy" played by an AI character. Video is fronted by the AI presenter speaking **for the brand** — never as the founder, never as a broker, never as a customer (`../10-audience-and-voice/claims-and-proof-policy.md`, `../20-content-engine/cast-and-presenter.md`).
>
> The founder's own voice lives in **text and audio**: LinkedIn posts, comments, DMs, WhatsApp broadcasts, voice notes, audio podcasts, and in person at meetups. That is not a lesser channel — for the buyer persona it is the main one.

---

## 1. Thesis

"An operator-founder building the real estate CRM Indian brokers actually use — in their language, for their chaos." Credible because it is close to the problem; relatable because it is not corporate.

## 2. Positioning pillars

1. **Builder** — building in public: lessons, real numbers from our own work, the why, the roadmap.
2. **Industry insider** — fluent in Mumbai broker reality (lead leakage, WhatsApp chaos, khata disputes, RERA).
3. **Contrarian truth-teller** — "more leads isn't the fix; follow-up is", "your team is an expensive WhatsApp group."
4. **Generous teacher** — free frameworks, audits and templates, no gatekeeping. Build the asset before promising it.
5. **Human** — wins, failures, the grind.

Note what pillar 1 can and cannot say. Numbers about *our own* build — what shipped, how long it took, what broke — are ours to publish. Numbers about customer outcomes are not, because there are no customers yet.

## 3. Voice

Confident, warm, street-smart. Hinglish on Instagram, English-leaning on LinkedIn, per `../10-audience-and-voice/brand-constants.md`. Money in ₹/lakh/crore. Marathi rapport is parked with the rest of the Pune scope.

## 5. Daily routine (time-blocked, ~60–90 min)

| Block | Time | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning engage | 20 min | Comment with real insight — not "great post" — on 15–20 owner, PropTech and CREDAI posts (LinkedIn + Instagram) | borrowed reach |
| Morning connect | 10 min | 10–15 targeted LinkedIn connects (Mumbai owners and managers) with a personal note | network |
| Midday reply | 15 min | Reply to every comment and DM (Instagram + LinkedIn) within the hour | conversations |
| Afternoon post | 15 min | One founder story on Instagram + one LinkedIn text post | presence |
| Evening pipeline | 15 min | Move warm DMs → WhatsApp → demo (`../40-sales-and-conversion/dm-to-demo.md`) | pipeline |

This daily routine is the most valuable thing in the two source documents, and it survives unchanged.

**Daily DM script (warm first):** react to their content, then —
> "{{name}}, dekha aapne {{their post}} — sahi point. Aap real estate mein ho? Main ek system bana raha hoon jo WhatsApp ke peeche kaam karta hai — leads, follow-up, khata ek jagah. Aapki agency mein leads kaise track hote hain?"

The old script pitched "ek CRM bana raha hoon, AI calling ke saath". AI calling is not on the approved-claims list and the M1 wedge is the WhatsApp AI Employee, so the opener leads with the thing we are actually selling.

## 6. Weekly routine

- One authority reel (AI presenter or screen recording) and one LinkedIn long-form post — building in public, with a real number from our own work.
- One broadcast or community drop (WhatsApp + Instagram broadcast) — a tip or a BTS note.
- One outbound push: five personalised DMs to ideal owners offering a free pipeline audit.
- One genuine value-add in a Mumbai broker Facebook group (`facebook.md`).
- Weekly review: DMs started, demos booked, best and worst content → `../50-measurement/weekly-scorecard.md`.

## 7. Monthly routine

- One local touch: attend or host a Mumbai real-estate meetup, a CREDAI/NAREDCO event, or a broker coffee.
- One **audio-only** podcast or a written collaboration. No video appearances.
- One partnership conversation (portal, CP network, trainer or influencer).
- Refresh the content pillars against what actually drove demos.
- **Case-study collaboration — post-launch only.** The old monthly routine included "one case study with a happy customer". We have no customers; when we do, it needs their written permission before anything is published.

## 8. Comment strategy

Be the sharpest insight in owner comment sections, daily. Comments out-perform posts for an early-stage profile. Formula: agree, add a non-obvious insight, ask a light question.

## 9. Authority building

Building-in-public numbers, contrarian truths, free frameworks and relentless consistency. Ninety-day goal: a recognised name in Mumbai broker circles.

## 10. Community

Run the Instagram broadcast channel and the WhatsApp community (`whatsapp/community.md`). Weekly ritual, monthly AMA by text or voice note. Convert followers into a group that refers — though the referral programme itself is gated (`whatsapp/referral.md`).

## 11. Partnerships

Targets: property portals (MagicBricks, Housing, 99acres reseller angles), CP aggregators, trainers and influencers, co-working and real-estate associations, CREDAI/NAREDCO chapters. Offer: co-created content, joint sessions, referral or revenue-share deals.

## 12. Local networking — Mumbai

Andheri, Bandra and Thane broker hubs; CREDAI-MCHI events; project-launch gatherings; CP meetups. Cosmopolitan Hindi-English rapport. Goal: five warm owner relationships a month.

**Pune** (Hinjewadi, Wakad, Baner, Kharadi; CREDAI Pune; Marathi-first rapport) is deferred until after the PMF gate — M1 is Mumbai only.

## 13. Measurement

The founder profile should become a lead source in its own right — followers → DMs → demos — and a recruiting and partnership magnet.

Track: comments made, connects, DMs started, founder-attributed demos, broadcast and community growth, local relationships. A lead's `source` is recorded on the CRM record; there is no `leadSource` field, so founder-sourced leads are marked in the tracking sheet. Definitions in `../50-measurement/metric-dictionary.md`.

Ninety-day target: a recognised voice in Mumbai broker circles with a steady DM → demo flow.
