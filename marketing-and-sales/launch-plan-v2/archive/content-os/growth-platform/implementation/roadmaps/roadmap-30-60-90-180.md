# Growth Platform — 30 / 60 / 90 / 180-Day Roadmap (Phase 14)

> **Archived (17 Sep 2026):** June 2026 design record, kept for history. Not the current plan. Current source: `marketing-and-sales/launch-plan-v2/00-PLAN-OVERVIEW.md`.

The canonical roadmap. **Extends** `marketing-and-sales/launch-plan-v2/month-2-plus/README.md` (0–90 kept, now adds 90–180) — that doc points here. Prioritized by **business impact × engineering effort × time-to-value × revenue impact × adoption impact**. Workstreams: **Content · Distribution · Sales · Engineering.** KPI gates per phase use `M-*` from `../../analytics/metric-dictionary.md`. Maps to sprints in `../coding-backlog/coding-backlog.md` and epics in `../epics/epics.md`.

**Principle:** cheapest compounding first (content + founder engine + referral), then conversion automation, then optimization, then scale. **Never build scoring/dashboards before attribution + events exist** (gap-analysis §7).

## Days 0–30 — Launch & Measure
**Theme:** content live, conversations started, funnel visible.

| Workstream | Actions |
|---|---|
| Content | 14-day launch plan → 5–6 reels/wk; 9 characters + 5 franchises |
| Distribution | IG daily + stories; founder-engine (comments/connects/DMs); WhatsApp nurture; LinkedIn authority |
| Sales | objections + qualification + demo-script in use; first demos |
| Engineering | **Sprint 1: EP-1 attribution + EP-2 events + minimal dashboard**; UTM capture; submit Meta/WhatsApp app review (Day 1) |

**KPI gate:** 1k IG followers · M-E1 hook>40% · 10+ DM convos · 3–5 demos · attribution live (M-AT1; sheet ok).

---

## Days 31–60 — Convert & Automate
**Theme:** turn reach into demos; remove manual friction.

| Workstream | Actions |
|---|---|
| Content | double down winning franchises; CT-AI + CT-CASE weekly; IG broadcast |
| Distribution | FB-group seeding; light paid on proven reels; YouTube Shorts repurpose |
| Sales | follow-up + closing + onboarding scripts; demo-followup automation |
| Engineering | **Sprint 2: EP-3 inbound capture** (IG/FB webhooks, keyword reply, Meta Lead Ads, AI auto-qualify) + **Sprint 3: EP-4 sequences** + WhatsApp API + EP-14 consent |

**KPI gate:** 5k followers · M-O1 speed-to-lead<5min · 15–20 demos · 5+ trials · M-C7 no-show<30% · M-O4 DLQ=0.

---

## Days 61–90 — Optimize & Score
**Theme:** predictable, measured, prioritized engine.

| Workstream | Actions |
|---|---|
| Content | lock theme-day calendar; batch by character; weekly testimonial |
| Distribution | scale winning paid; WhatsApp community + monthly founder live |
| Sales | hottest-first queue live; referral motion post-activation; win-back for inactive trials |
| Engineering | **Sprint 4: EP-5 scoring + EP-8 activation + EP-6 referral** + **Sprint 5 start: EP-10 campaigns/CAC + EP-7 full dashboard** |

**KPI gate:** 10k+ followers · predictable demos/wk (M-NS1≥20) · CAC defined + M-EF6 LTV:CAC≥3:1 · M-CR6 content-`OPP-*` ROI visible · M-F8 referral% tracked · scores on every lead (M-S1/S2).

---

## Days 91–180 — Scale & Self-Operate
**Theme:** retention, expansion, and an engine that runs itself.

| Workstream | Actions |
|---|---|
| Content | content engine on autopilot; oracle/ab-optimizer pick winners from M-CR6; localization (Marathi) |
| Distribution | scale proven channels by effective-CAC (M-EF3); referral as primary cheap channel; co-marketing |
| Sales | expansion plays for M-S4 accounts; structured win-back; NRR motion |
| Engineering | **Sprint 5 complete: EP-12 6-dashboard suite + EP-9 health/expansion scoring** + **Sprint 6: EP-11 AI agent operating model** (weekly brief, agent actions) + EP-13 observability hardening |

**KPI gate (Day 180):**
- M-NS1 qualified demos/wk **steady ≥20**, M-NS2 new-paid/mo predictable & growing
- M-EF6 LTV:CAC **≥3:1**, M-EF7 payback **<6 mo**, M-EF3 effective-CAC ranked per channel
- M-R6 NRR **>100%**, M-A5 churn **<5%**, M-A1 activation **>40%**
- M-F8 referral **≥20%** of new customers
- All 6 dashboards live + RBAC-gated; full loop **traceable reel→…→expansion** (gap-analysis §8)
- growth-strategist weekly brief auto-generated; system self-operates

## Dependencies & risks
- Meta/WhatsApp app review takes weeks → **submit Day 1** (gates EP-3/EP-4).
- WhatsApp template approval gates sequences → submit in Phase 0–30.
- Content quality gates everything downstream → protect production capacity.
- Scoring/dashboards depend on attribution+events → enforce sprint order.
