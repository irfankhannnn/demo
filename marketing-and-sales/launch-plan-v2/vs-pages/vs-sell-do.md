### Section 3 — Feature-by-feature breakdown

**H2:** Detailed feature comparison

#### Lead management
- **Sell.do**: deep CP (channel partner) module, multi-source lead capture, complex lead-scoring rules, manual assignment
- **RealEstateFlow**: WhatsApp-native lead capture (forwarded message → AI extracts), simpler scoring optimised for broker workflow, auto-assignment via AI Employee
- **Verdict**: Sell.do wins for developer-CP scale; RealEstateFlow wins for broker-on-WhatsApp speed

#### CRM core (contacts, properties, deals)
- **Sell.do**: enterprise-grade, multi-tenant developer CRM
- **RealEstateFlow**: tenant-scoped, broker-flavoured (Buyers + Owners + Properties + Khata)
- **Verdict**: Both fine; RealEstateFlow's broker-specific schema (Khata, locality tagging) saves time

#### AI / automation
- **Sell.do**: rule-based automations + integrations with external AI
- **RealEstateFlow**: native AI Employee on WhatsApp/Telegram, qualifies + follows up + updates CRM autonomously
- **Verdict**: RealEstateFlow's AI Employee is a categorical difference, not just a feature

#### WhatsApp integration
- **Sell.do**: webhook + API, requires setup
- **RealEstateFlow**: native, primary interface, no setup
- **Verdict**: RealEstateFlow wins for WhatsApp-first brokers

#### Reporting + analytics
- **Sell.do**: comprehensive enterprise dashboards
- **RealEstateFlow**: focused broker-relevant dashboards (active leads, properties, Khata settlement)
- **Verdict**: Sell.do wins for depth; RealEstateFlow wins for actionability

#### Pricing transparency
- **Sell.do**: per-user, annual contracts, custom quotes — opaque to most brokers
- **RealEstateFlow**: ₹999/₹1,999/₹500 per seat / ₹7,999 AI Employee — transparent, monthly + annual options, no hidden costs
- **Verdict**: RealEstateFlow wins for the ICP; Sell.do is fine if you have an annual budget already

#### Onboarding
- **Sell.do**: 7-14 days with their team, paid implementation
- **RealEstateFlow**: <30 min self-serve, founder-personal call optional
- **Verdict**: RealEstateFlow wins for speed-to-value; Sell.do wins for hand-holding for large teams
