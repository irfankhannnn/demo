### Section 1 — When Excel is enough

**H2:** Honest take: when Excel/Sheets is the right choice

- You're a hobby broker (1-3 deals/year)
- Less than 20 active buyers + properties total
- No team — just you
- No follow-up complexity (each lead is a one-shot conversation)
- Budget is ₹0
- WhatsApp groups feel manageable

For pure beginners or part-timers, Excel/Google Sheets is genuinely fine. We won't try to convince you otherwise.

---

### Section 2 — When Excel breaks

**H2:** Signs Excel is costing you more than ₹999/mo

If any of these apply, you're paying for Excel in time + lost deals:

1. **You forget to follow up** — leads go cold because no system reminds you. Industry data: 80% of leads die from missed follow-up.
2. **The file crashes weekly** — and one Friday you'll lose a sheet that has 6 months of contacts.
3. **You can't share with your team** — every agent has a slightly different version. Conflicts compound.
4. **You can't use it on mobile** — Mumbai brokers spend 8h/day in cars + sites; Excel mobile is unreadable.
5. **You re-type the same buyer's number 3-5 times** — every channel, every WhatsApp message, every site visit.
6. **You don't know which leads are hot** — the file shows everything, not what to focus on today.
7. **You can't query** — "Show me all 3BHK buyers in Andheri ₹2-3Cr" requires manual filter every time.
8. **Khata is in a separate notebook** — settlement disputes happen because numbers don't match.

If you have 4+ of these, Excel is the bottleneck. Time to switch.
