## Final draft (210 words)

Some real numbers from the last 60 days of building a SaaS company solo in Mumbai:

5 cancelled meetings (3 because Bandra-Worli Sea Link was shut)
2 power outages mid-deploy
3 moments where I almost gave up
1 conversation with a CA who didn't believe a "subscription" could replace "annual maintenance"
14 chai breaks where I rewrote the wedge
0 paid ads run (and not running any in M1 either)

The hardest thing wasn't writing the code. It was figuring out:
- Which broker actually needs this (answer: 1-3 person agencies in Mumbai metro)
- What to charge (₹999 felt too cheap; ₹3,000 felt presumptuous; landed at ₹999 with reasoning)
- How to talk about AI without sounding like a parody
- Why my Razorpay account asked for 14 documents

I shipped the MVP last week. Beta launches in 9 days. 8 Mumbai brokers I've talked to are testing it.

If you're an Indian SaaS founder reading this and wondering whether shipping in India is worth it: yes. The market is there. The buyers are there. The hard part is talking like a Mumbai broker, not a Silicon Valley founder.

DMs open if you're building in this space.
