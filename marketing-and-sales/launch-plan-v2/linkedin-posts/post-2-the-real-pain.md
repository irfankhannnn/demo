## Final draft (180 words)

Every CRM company tells brokers: "Get more leads."

Every broker I talked to said the opposite.

"I have too many leads. I can't follow up with the ones I already have."

A 3-agent agency in Powai showed me their lead pile: 240 leads in the last 90 days. They'd contacted maybe 60 of them. The other 180? Cold. Lost. Dead.

Not because the leads were bad. Because the agents couldn't keep up.

The bottleneck isn't lead generation. It's follow-up speed. And follow-up speed is bottlenecked by humans.

This is why I'm building RealEstateFlow — not as another lead-gen tool. As an AI Employee that takes over the follow-up.

Lead comes in on WhatsApp → AI qualifies it → sends details → schedules site visit → updates the broker.

The broker shows up at the site visit. The broker closes the deal. The AI does the 80% of the work that wasn't getting done.
