> **Schedule:** T-14 (founder publishes ~2 weeks before public launch)

## Final draft (200 words)

Most Mumbai brokers I've met run their entire agency on three things: a WhatsApp chat, a paper diary, and one Excel sheet that crashes every Friday.

I shadowed 12 of them in Andheri, Bandra, Powai, and Thane over the last 60 days.

What I saw broke my brain a little.

A solo broker in Bandra was managing 80 active buyers — by hand. Every follow-up was a calendar reminder, every property listing a copy-paste from MagicBricks into 4 separate WhatsApp groups, every Khata entry a rough number scribbled into a notebook.

She'd worked 14 hours that day. Closed two deals. Slept four hours.

We've all heard "real estate is relationship-driven." Yes. But these brokers aren't drowning in relationships. They're drowning in admin.

For 60 days I've been quietly building something: an AI Employee that runs the broking agency on WhatsApp + Telegram. Qualifies leads. Follows up buyers. Updates the Khata book. So the broker can do what brokers do — show properties, close deals, sleep more than four hours.

Beta launch in 14 days for 8-10 Mumbai brokers.

If that's you — DM me. No pitch, just a 15-min chat.
