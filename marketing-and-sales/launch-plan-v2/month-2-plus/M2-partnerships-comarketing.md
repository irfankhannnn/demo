# M2 — Partnerships + Co-Marketing

> **Type:** 🧍 MANUAL + 🤖
> **Phase:** Month 2-3
> **Skill(s):** `co-marketing` + `cold-email` + `community-marketing`

## Objective
Identify + onboard 3-5 strategic partners (real-estate adjacent: photographers, home-loan brokers, property-management firms, virtual-tour vendors, RERA consultants) for cross-promotion + revenue-share where applicable.

## Why partnerships?
Mumbai broker ICP overlaps with multiple adjacent service-providers. Cross-referrals + bundled offers expand reach without paid acquisition. Plus: partner content (joint webinars, co-blog posts) compounds.

## Plan

### M2 Week 1-2 — Partner discovery

Target categories:
1. **Real-estate photographers / virtual tour vendors** — every Mumbai broker uses 1-3
2. **Home-loan brokers** — every Mumbai property buyer needs one
3. **Property management firms** — overlap on rental/property workflows
4. **RERA / GST / legal consultants** — recurring need
5. **Property portals (MagicBricks/Housing.com individual relationships)** — long-shot, but try

Source 15-20 candidate partners via:
- LinkedIn search by category + Mumbai
- Cold email with explicit ask: "I'm building RealEstateFlow; we share the same broker ICP; want a 30-min chat about cross-promotion?"
- Indian SaaS Slack #IndianFounders for warm intros

### M2 Week 3-4 — Partner conversations + 3 deals

- Run 8-10 partner exploratory calls
- Close 3-5 partnerships with clear cross-promo terms:
  - **Cross-referral**: each partner gives the other 1 customer reference letter / case study / shared booth at trade event
  - **Revenue share** (specific deals): 10-15% of first-year revenue if partner introduces a paying customer
  - **Joint webinar**: 1 partner + RealEstateFlow co-host a Mumbai broker webinar in M2 Week 4
  - **Joint LinkedIn co-post**: 1 monthly cross-promo post

### M2 Week 4 — First joint asset

- 1 webinar (45 min): "Mumbai broker workflow modernisation" with a home-loan partner
- 50-100 broker registrations target (founder + partner promote)
- Recording becomes evergreen lead-magnet for both

## Success metrics

| Metric | M2 Target |
|---|---|
| Partner exploratory calls | 8-10 |
| Closed partnerships | 3-5 |
| Joint webinar attendees | 50-100 |
| Cross-referrals (partners → us) | 5-10 trial signups |
| Cross-referrals (us → partners) | 5-10 referrals out |

## Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Partnerships become founder-time-heavy | Cap at 5 partners M2; M3 evaluate ROI |
| Partner intent unclear (just exchange logos) | Term sheet specifies actions + metrics |
| Webinar low attendance | 200+ targeted invites with founder-personal outreach |
| Revenue share dispute | Clear written terms + 1 trusted reference per quarter |
