# M2 — Hiring Plan (First 1-2 Hires)

> **Type:** 🧍 MANUAL
> **Phase:** Month 2-3
> **Skill(s):** `revops` + `customer-research`
> **Pre-requisites:** MRR ≥ ₹2L (decision threshold from `00-DECISIONS-LOG.md`)

## Objective
Plan + execute first 1-2 hires — likely a part-time SDR / outbound contractor + a part-time community/content person — to take routine load off founder and unlock M3-M6 scale.

## Why hire now?
Founder bandwidth is the M2 bottleneck. Demos cap at 4/day; cold sequence quality drops without dedicated time; content engine demands 5h/week. Without 1-2 hires, M2-M6 hits a ceiling.

## Plan

### M2 Week 1-2 — Decision

- Verify MRR threshold (₹2L) from Day-29 audit
- Confirm hiring is right vs vendor (contractor / agency / freelancer per task)
- Determine hire type:
  - **Hire 1 (Priority)**: Part-time SDR / outbound ops contractor — owns cold sequence ops + reply triage 4-6h/day
  - **Hire 2 (Optional)**: Part-time content writer / social media operator — owns LinkedIn cadence + blog drafts

### M2 Week 3 — Sourcing

- LinkedIn job post: "Part-time SDR / outbound ops for Mumbai SaaS — flexible hours, ₹40k/mo"
- Indian SaaS Slack post + Wellfound (Indian remote)
- Founder network referrals
- Target: 10-15 applicants

### M2 Week 4 — Hiring

- 5-7 first-round 30-min calls
- 2-3 second-round paid trial: 5-day sprint (₹5k stipend) doing actual cold sequence work + reply handling
- 1 hire selected by Day 60
- Onboarding: 5-day shadow founder; week 2 fully on cold sequence + reply ops; founder reviews weekly

## Success metrics

| Metric | M2 Target |
|---|---|
| MRR threshold met (₹2L) | ✅ to greenlight |
| Applicants screened | 10-15 |
| Final hire onboarded by Day 60 | ✅ |
| Founder hours freed up by M3 Week 1 | ≥10/week |
| Cold sequence volume scaled | 100/day → 200/day |

## Hiring spec (Hire 1: SDR/Outbound Ops)

### Job description
**Title:** Part-time SDR + Outbound Ops Specialist
**Compensation:** ₹40k/mo + ₹500/qualified-lead bonus
**Hours:** 4-6h/day, flexible Mon-Sat
**Location:** Remote (Mumbai timezone)

**Responsibilities:**
- Run daily cold email + WhatsApp + LinkedIn sequences (using founder-built templates)
- Reply triage: respond to 80%+ of replies within 60 min during business hours
- Maintain CRM data hygiene: every interaction logged
- Daily report to founder
- Weekly performance review

**Requirements:**
- 1+ years B2B SDR or outbound experience (Indian market)
- English + Hindi proficient
- Experience with Instantly / Apollo / similar tools
- Self-managed, no micromanagement
- WhatsApp Business + LinkedIn presence

**Nice to have:**
- Real-estate domain knowledge
- Mumbai market familiarity
- Comfortable with AI-assisted workflows

### Interview process
- Round 1 (30 min Zoom): culture + experience fit
- Round 2 (paid 5-day trial): real cold sequence work
- Final: 30-min with founder; offer

### Onboarding
- Week 1: shadow founder + read all M1 docs
- Week 2: take over 50% of cold sequence ops
- Week 3+: full ownership

### Performance metrics
- Reply rate ≥3% (M1 baseline)
- Demos booked ≥10/week
- Reply SLA <60 min during business hours
- Data hygiene 95%+ accuracy
- Founder satisfaction (weekly check-in)

## Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| MRR threshold not met | Defer hire to M3; focus M2 on revenue |
| Bad hire (low quality) | 5-day paid trial = real signal; 30-day probation |
| Founder over-delegates | Daily report + weekly review; founder still owns strategy |
| Replacement cost (if hire leaves) | Keep cold sequence templates + tooling owned by founder |
| Compensation too low | Open to bonus structure if base ₹40k feels light |
