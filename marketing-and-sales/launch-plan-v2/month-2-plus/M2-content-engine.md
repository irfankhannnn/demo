# M2 — Content Engine (SEO + AEO + Social)

> **Type:** 🤖 AUTO + 🤝
> **Phase:** Month 2-6
> **Skill(s):** `content-strategy` + `seo-blog` + `ai-seo` + `social-content` + `schema-markup`
> **Pre-requisites:** Day-25 case study + SEO blog + 12-week calendar live

## Objective
Activate the 12-week content calendar from Day-25 — ship 1 SEO blog/week + 2 LinkedIn posts/week + 1 Twitter thread/week + AEO-optimised Q&A pages, building organic traffic + AI citations + topical authority for Mumbai broker SEO.

## Why content engine?
Days 1-30 was outbound + organic via founder. Content engine is the long-tail asset: each post compounds in Google + AI search results, becoming a permanent acquisition channel that doesn't depend on founder hours weekly.

## Plan

### M2 Week 1-4 — SEO blogs (4 posts)

Use the Day-25 12-week content calendar. Sample Week 5-8 topics:

| Wk | Topic | Target keyword | Word count |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | "How to manage Mumbai broker WhatsApp leads (2026 guide)" | "Mumbai broker WhatsApp leads" (50-100/mo) | 1,800 |
| 6 | "RERA-compliance for Mumbai brokers — quick guide" | "RERA Mumbai broker" (200-500/mo) | 2,000 |
| 7 | "AI Employee for real estate: what it actually does" | "AI employee real estate" (100-200/mo) | 1,500 |
| 8 | "Solo broker vs agency: when to switch tools" | "solo real estate broker tools India" (50-100/mo) | 1,400 |

Each post:
- Use `seo-blog` skill
- Internal links: 5+ to /pricing, /demo, /vs/*, case studies, prior posts
- 1 schema (Article + FAQPage where applicable)
- 1 unique image (Nano-Banana-Pro)
- Promotion: 1 LinkedIn post + 1 Twitter thread + 1 WhatsApp share + 1 community group share

### M2 Week 1-4 — AEO Q&A pages (5 pages)

Use `ai-seo` skill to author Q&A-format pages targeting LLM citations:
- "What is the best CRM for real estate brokers in Mumbai?"
- "How does AI Employee work on WhatsApp for real estate?"
- "Is Sell.do or RealEstateFlow better for solo brokers?"
- "What is Khata book in real estate context?"
- "How much does real estate CRM cost in India?"

Each page: 800-1,200 words + structured data + clear answer in first paragraph + JSON-LD `Article` + `FAQPage`.

- Adapt M3 calendar based on performance

## Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| AI-generated content feels generic | Founder edits each post heavily; weave in real M1 stories |
| Cannibalised keywords | Internal linking + canonical tags; topic clusters not isolated posts |
| Slow organic growth | Realistic — months 3-6 is when SEO compounds; trust the engine |
| Content drift from wedge | All posts cycle to /pricing or /demo; 1 wedge per 4 posts |
