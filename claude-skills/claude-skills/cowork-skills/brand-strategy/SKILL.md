# Brand Strategy Skill

## Overview
This skill generates comprehensive brand strategy assets for RealtyFlow targeting Indian real estate agents. All outputs use **Hinglish** (70% English + 30% Hindi romanized) with emotion-driven Hindi and technical English.

## Hinglish Writing Rules

### 70/30 Rule
- **English (70%):** Product features, technical terms, business concepts, verbs
- **Hindi (30%):** Emotions, cultural references, everyday language, adjectives

### Tone Characteristics
- **Emotion (Hindi):** Aapka, mera, hum, pyaar, sapna, shakti, aarzoo
- **Technology (English):** platform, analytics, automation, growth, leads
- **Mix:** "Apne dreams ko reality mein convert karenge" (Technical English verb + Hindi emotion)

### Hindi Romanization Guide
| Hindi Word | Romanized | Meaning |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| अपना | Apna | Own/your |
| सपना | Sapna | Dream |
| शक्ति | Shakti | Power |
| आशा | Aasha | Hope |
| बदलाव | Badlav | Change |
| जीवन | Jeevan | Life |
| सफलता | Safalta | Success |
| बिजनेस | Bijness | Business |
| ग्राहक | Graahak | Customer |

## Brand Positioning Framework

### Problem → Solution → Differentiator Pattern
```
PROBLEM (Hindi + English):
"Real estate agents lose 60% leads because they're drowning in WhatsApp messages,
missed calls, aur manual follow-ups."

SOLUTION (English):
"RealtyFlow automates lead management, qualification, and follow-up—giving agents
their time back."

DIFFERENTIATOR (Hinglish):
"Only platform for Indian agents jo samjhta hai local market complexity.
Built by Indians, for Indians. Apna CRM."
```

### 3. Tone-of-Voice Guide

```markdown
# RealtyFlow Tone of Voice

## Core Personality
**Approachable. Empowering. Honest.**

| Dimension | DO | DON'T |
|-----------|----|----|
| **Language** | Mix Hinglish naturally; use "aapka" | Don't use pure Hindi; avoid Indianized English |
| **Emotion** | Use Hindi for feelings (dil, sapna, shakti) | Don't be fake or overly formal |
| **Speed** | Fast, action-oriented language | Don't be slow or academic |
| **Trust** | Show real results, numbers, testimonials | Don't make false promises |
| **Humor** | Local market jokes, relatable frustrations | Don't be corporate-funny |

## Writing Examples

### ❌ WRONG (Generic CRM tone)
"Leverage our cutting-edge platform to optimize your sales pipeline efficiency metrics."

### ✅ RIGHT (Hinglish, empowering)
"Apne leads ko automate karo aur real deals close karo. Spreadsheets ko trash mein daalo."

### ❌ WRONG (Too Hindi, alienating)
"Aapka khwaabon ko hum sach karte hain"

### ✅ RIGHT (Balanced Hinglish)
"Close more deals. Karo zyada paise. Apna time lelo back."

## Vocabulary Keywords

### Emotion/Aspiration (Hindi)
- Sapna (dream)
- Shakti (power)
- Aapka (your/own)
- Safalta (success)
- Jeevan (life)
- Badlav (change)
- Hum (we/us - inclusive)

### Technology (English)
- Platform
- Automation
- Analytics
- Intelligence
- Integration
- Growth
- Scale

### Business (Mix)
- Leads, Deals, Closing
- "Zyada leads" (more leads - casual Hindi)
- "Real results" (English guarantee)
```

```markdown
- Real Indian agents, real offices, real deals

**Helpful. Powerful. Honest. Indian.**
- Speak like a trusted advisor, not a pushy salesman
- Use Hinglish naturally (70/30 rule)
- Lead with results, support with features
- Celebrate customer wins loudly

*"We give Indian real estate agents the technology to focus on what they do best: closing deals and building relationships."*
```
