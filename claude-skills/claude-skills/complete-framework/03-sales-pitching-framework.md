# Sales Pitching Framework (Demo Call & Conversion)
## For Product Demos, Sales Calls, and Lead-to-Customer Conversion

---

## The Framework: RAPPORT → DISCOVER → DEMONSTRATE → DIFFERENTIATE → CLOSE → FOLLOW-UP

### 🎯 Purpose
A structured sales conversation framework for converting leads into paying customers through product demos, sales calls, and trial-to-paid conversions.

---

## 📞 The RealtyFlow Sales Call Structure (30-45 Minutes)

### Phase 1: RAPPORT BUILDING (3 minutes)

**Goal:** Establish trust and make them comfortable

**Opening Script (English):**
```
"Hi [Name], thanks for taking the time today! How's your day going?

Before we dive in, I saw you're based in [City]—how's the real estate
market there these days? Still competitive?"

[Let them talk for 1-2 minutes]

"Got it. Well, I'm excited to show you RealtyFlow today. We've been
working with agents just like you across India, and I think you'll find
this really valuable. Sound good?"
```

**Opening Script (Hinglish):**
```
"Hi [Name], thank you for joining today! Aaj ka din kaisa chal raha hai?

Show karne se pehle, I saw you're in [City]—wahin ka market kaisa hai
요즈음? Still competitive ya thoda slow hai?"

[Let them talk for 1-2 minutes]

"Samajh gaya. Dekho, aaj main aapko RealtyFlow dikhaunga, and I think
this will be super helpful for you. Chalein?"
```

**Pro Tips:**
- ✅ Use their name 2-3 times in the first 3 minutes
- ✅ Mirror their energy (if they're formal, be formal; if casual, be casual)
- ✅ Find one personal connection point (city, common contact, industry news)
- ❌ Don't jump straight into pitch—it feels salesy

### Phase 2: DISCOVERY (5-7 minutes)

**Goal:** Understand their pain points deeply so you can customize the demo

**The 7 Discovery Questions:**

#### 1. Current Process Question
```
"Walk me through how you manage your leads today. Where do you track
buyers, sellers, properties—Excel, WhatsApp, notebook?"
```

**What you're listening for:** Chaos, frustration, manual work

---

#### 2. Pain Point Question
```
"What's the biggest challenge you face with your current system?
Like, what makes you pull your hair out?"
```

**What you're listening for:** Specific pain (lost leads, time waste, missed follow-ups)

---

#### 3. Consequence Question
```
"And what happens when [pain point] occurs? Like, has it ever cost you
a deal or commission?"
```

**What you're listening for:** Financial impact, emotional cost

---

#### 4. Volume Question
```
"How many active leads are you managing right now? And how many
properties do you have in your inventory?"
```

**What you're listening for:** Scale (helps position the right plan)

---

#### 5. Team Question
```
"Are you a solo agent, or do you have a team? Any plans to grow in
the next 6-12 months?"
```

**What you're listening for:** Growth intent (upsell opportunity)

---

#### 6. Tool Stack Question
```
"Have you tried any CRM or software before? What did you like or
dislike about it?"
```

**What you're listening for:** Previous objections, feature gaps, price sensitivity

---

#### 7. Ideal Outcome Question
```
"If you had a magic wand and could design the perfect system, what
would it do for you?"
```

**What you're listening for:** Aspirations, dream features (shows what they value most)

### Phase 3: DEMONSTRATE (15-20 minutes)

**Goal:** Show the product in action, solving THEIR specific problems

**The Golden Rule of Demos:**
> "Don't show features. Show outcomes. Don't say 'This button does X.'
> Say 'This is how you'll save 2 hours every day.'"

---

**Step 4: Show Additional Modules (5 minutes)**

**Quickly tour the remaining features:**
- Sellers module


---

**Demo Best Practices:**

✅ **DO:**
- Use real, populated data (not empty screens)
- Solve their problem first, then show extra features
- Pause and ask "Does this make sense?" every 5 minutes
- Let them ask questions—it shows engagement

❌ **DON'T:**
- Click through every single button (boring)
- Use technical jargon ("This is the API integration...")
- Rush through—they'll feel overwhelmed
- Show features they don't care about

### Phase 5: HANDLE OBJECTIONS (5 minutes)

**Common Objections & Responses:**

**Response:**
```

RealtyFlow costs ₹18,000/year. If it helps you close even one deal,
it's already paid for itself 5x over.

Plus, we have a 14-day free trial. Zero risk. Try it, and if you don't
see value, walk away. Fair?"
```

---

#### Objection 2: "I'm already using Excel and WhatsApp. It's working fine."

**Response:**
```
"I hear you. And Excel is great—it's flexible, it's free. But here's

How many hours do you spend every week updating spreadsheets? Probably

10-15 hours, and you won't lose any more leads.

Think of it this way: Excel is a bicycle. RealtyFlow is a car. Both get

```

## 🎯 Sales Call Best Practices

### Before the Call:
- [ ] Research the lead (LinkedIn, website, mutual connections)
- [ ] Prepare 3 customized talking points based on their profile
- [ ] Have the demo environment ready (pre-populated with sample data)
- [ ] Test your mic, camera, screen share

### During the Call:
- [ ] Smile (they can hear it in your voice, even on audio calls)
- [ ] Take notes on their pain points (shows you're listening)
- [ ] Use their name frequently
- [ ] Ask "Does this make sense?" every 5 minutes
- [ ] Let them drive the conversation (don't monologue)

### After the Call:
- [ ] Send follow-up email within 1 hour
- [ ] Add them to CRM with detailed notes
- [ ] Schedule next touchpoint in calendar
- [ ] If they signed up, send onboarding resources immediately

## 🧪 Testing Your Sales Pitch

### The 5-Sign Test (Good signs during the call):
1. ✅ They ask detailed questions about features
2. ✅ They mention specific use cases ("I could use this for...")
3. ✅ They ask about pricing without hesitation
4. ✅ They say "This is exactly what I need"
5. ✅ They ask about the signup process

### The Red Flags (Bad signs):
1. ❌ They're multitasking (you hear typing, talking to someone else)
2. ❌ They don't ask any questions
3. ❌ They say "Send me more info" without committing to next steps
4. ❌ They focus only on price, not value
5. ❌ They rush through the call ("I have another meeting in 5 minutes")
