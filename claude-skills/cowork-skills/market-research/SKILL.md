## Market Sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM)

### TAM: Total Addressable Market

```

Total Real Estate Agents in India:
  - RERA-registered agents: 1.2M
  - Active agents (conducting 5+ transactions/year): 800K
  - Agents earning ₹5L+/year: 500K

TAM = 500K agents × ₹99/month × 12 months
TAM = 500K × ₹1,188
TAM = ₹594 Crore annually

Alternative TAM (Franchise/Agency):
  - Real estate franchises in India: 5,000+
  - Avg agents per franchise: 10-50
  - Franchises with 5+ agents (our target): 2,000
  - Avg team size: 12 agents
  - Team license: ₹1,500-2,000/month

Agency TAM = 2,000 × 12 agents × ₹1,800/month × 12
Agency TAM = ₹518 Crore annually

TOTAL TAM = ₹594Cr (individual) + ₹518Cr (agencies) = ₹1,112 Crore
```

## Feature Impact Scoring Model

### Framework: RICE Prioritization

```
Score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort

Where:
  Reach = # customers affected (0-100 scale)
  Impact = Value to each customer (1-3: low/med/high)
  Confidence = Certainty of impact (0.5-1.0)
  Effort = Dev weeks required (1-20)
```

### Feature Scoring Examples

```
Feature 1: WhatsApp Integration (Auto-sync messages to CRM)
  Reach: 95 (nearly all agents use WhatsApp)
  Impact: 3 (solves #1 pain point: lead chaos)
  Confidence: 0.95 (clear customer feedback)
  Effort: 8 weeks
  SCORE = (95 × 3 × 0.95) / 8 = 33.8 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ HIGH PRIORITY

Feature 2: AI Lead Scoring
  Reach: 60 (appeals to data-driven agents)
  Impact: 2 (helpful but not critical)
  Confidence: 0.7 (some customer feedback)
  Effort: 12 weeks
  SCORE = (60 × 2 × 0.7) / 12 = 7.0 ⭐ LOW PRIORITY (for now)

Feature 3: Automated Follow-up Reminders
  Reach: 85 (most agents struggle with this)
  Impact: 3 (saves 2+ hours/day)
  Confidence: 0.9 (customer validated)
  Effort: 4 weeks
  SCORE = (85 × 3 × 0.9) / 4 = 57.4 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ HIGHEST PRIORITY

Feature 4: Demo Video Library (Education)
  Reach: 50 (helps with adoption)
  Impact: 2 (nice-to-have)
  Confidence: 0.8
  Effort: 3 weeks
  SCORE = (50 × 2 × 0.8) / 3 = 26.7 ⭐⭐⭐ MEDIUM PRIORITY

Feature 5: Custom Reports Builder
  Reach: 40 (only franchises need)
  Impact: 2 (valuable for reporting)
  Confidence: 0.7
  Effort: 16 weeks
  SCORE = (40 × 2 × 0.7) / 16 = 3.5 ⭐ VERY LOW PRIORITY
```

**Recommendation:** Focus on WhatsApp Integration + Automated Reminders first (both 50+ score).

## Pain Point Scoring Formula

### Model: Symptom + Impact + Frequency

```
Pain Score = (Symptom Severity × Impact × Frequency × Addressability) / 10

Where:
  Symptom Severity: 1-10 (how much it hurts daily)
  Impact: 1-5 (revenue impact if solved)
  Frequency: 1-10 (how often it happens)
  Addressability: 0.5-1.0 (can RealtyFlow solve it?)

─────────────────────────────────────────

Pain Point 1: Lead Follow-up Chaos
  Symptom Severity: 9 ("I lose sleep over missed messages")
  Impact: 5 ("Costs me 2-3 deals/month = ₹50K+ lost")
  Frequency: 9 ("Happens every single day")
  Addressability: 1.0 (RealtyFlow directly solves)
  SCORE = (9 × 5 × 9 × 1.0) / 10 = 40.5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ CRITICAL

Pain Point 2: Team Coordination (for agencies)
  Symptom Severity: 8 ("Agents don't know about hot leads")
  Impact: 4 ("One agent left because of this")
  Frequency: 8 ("Multiple times per week")
  Addressability: 0.95
  SCORE = (8 × 4 × 8 × 0.95) / 10 = 24.3 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ HIGH

Pain Point 3: No Analytics
  Symptom Severity: 5 ("I wish I had better data")
  Impact: 3 ("Would help planning, but not urgent")
  Frequency: 5 ("Monthly reporting cycle")
  Addressability: 0.7 (nice-to-have, not must-have)
  SCORE = (5 × 3 × 5 × 0.7) / 10 = 5.2 ⭐ LOW

Pain Point 4: Integration with Existing Tools
  Symptom Severity: 7 ("Current tools don't talk to each other")
  Impact: 3 ("Creates manual work")
  Frequency: 7 ("Daily friction")
  Addressability: 0.8 (RealtyFlow can integrate)
  SCORE = (7 × 3 × 7 × 0.8) / 10 = 11.8 ⭐⭐ MEDIUM

IMPLICATION:
Focus messaging on Pain #1 (Lead Chaos) - highest score.
Feature development should prioritize addressing Pain #1 & #2.
Acknowledge but deprioritize Pain #3 (analytics can come later).
```
