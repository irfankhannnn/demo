# Social Pitching Framework (30-Second Pitch)
## Based on Daniel Priestley's Name-Same-Fame-Pain-Aim-Game Model

---

## The Framework: NAME → SAME → FAME → PAIN → AIM → GAME

### 🎯 Purpose
A 30-second elevator pitch designed for networking events, casual encounters, social media bios, and quick introductions where you have limited attention span.

---

## RealtyFlow Social Pitch Templates

### Template 1: Full Framework (30 seconds)

**"Hi, I'm [YOUR NAME] from RealtyFlow.**

**[SAME]** You know how real estate agents use basic spreadsheets or generic CRMs?

**[FAME]** We've built the first CRM specifically designed for Indian real estate agents—complete with AI calling agents that handle follow-ups automatically.

**[PAIN]** Most agents lose 60% of their leads because they can't keep track of buyers, sellers, tenants, and owners all in one place.

**[AIM]** We're helping 3,000 agents in India and Dubai manage their entire real estate lifecycle—from enquiry to closing.

**[GAME]** Our bigger vision? To become the operating system for every real estate transaction in India."

---

### Template 2: Pain-First Approach (30 seconds)

**"I'm [YOUR NAME], founder of RealtyFlow.**

**[PAIN]** Real estate agents waste 15+ hours every week on manual follow-ups, and they still lose half their leads.

**[SAME]** It's like using a hammer when you need a power tool—most CRMs aren't built for real estate.

**[FAME]** RealtyFlow is the only CRM with built-in AI calling agents that automatically nurture leads while agents focus on closing deals.

**[AIM]** We're onboarding 3,000 agents this quarter across India and Dubai.

**[GAME]** We're building the future where every real estate agent has a digital team working 24/7."

---

### Template 3: Vision-First Approach (30 seconds)

**"I'm [YOUR NAME] with RealtyFlow.**

**[GAME]** We're building the world's first AI-powered real estate CRM designed specifically for Indian agents.

**[PAIN]** Right now, agents are drowning in spreadsheets—tracking buyers, sellers, tenants, owners, and they're losing crores in missed follow-ups.

**[SAME]** Think of us like Salesforce, but built from the ground up for real estate.

**[FAME]** We have AI agents that make calls, send WhatsApp messages, and qualify leads automatically.

**[AIM]** We're launching a 3,000-agent campaign across India and Dubai this quarter."

## 🎯 How to Use This Framework

### When to Use the 30-Second Social Pitch:
- ✅ Networking events and startup meetups
- ✅ LinkedIn connection requests
- ✅ Twitter/X bio and intro threads
- ✅ Casual encounters ("What do you do?")
- ✅ Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts scripts
- ✅ Cold outreach opening lines

### Delivery Tips:
1. **Speak at normal pace**: Don't rush. 30 seconds is longer than you think.
2. **Pause after PAIN**: Let them feel the problem.
3. **End with a question**: "Sound interesting?" or "Mind if I send you more info?"
4. **Match energy**: Professional events → formal. Startup events → casual.
5. **Use Hinglish for Indian audiences**: Builds instant rapport and relatability.

---

## 🧪 Testing Your Social Pitch

### The 3-Question Test:
After your pitch, if they ask ANY of these questions, you nailed it:
1. "How does the AI calling work?"
2. "What's your pricing?"
3. "Can I try it?"

### The Bad Sign:
If they say "Oh, interesting" and immediately change the subject, your pitch was too vague or boring.

## 🔄 Iteration Checklist

Before finalizing your social pitch, ensure:
- [ ] It's under 30 seconds when spoken aloud
- [ ] It mentions ONE specific pain point
- [ ] It includes a tangible number (3,000 agents, 60% leads, 15+ hours)
- [ ] The "fame" differentiator is crystal clear (AI calling agents)
- [ ] It ends with forward momentum (aim/game)
- [ ] Hinglish version feels natural, not forced

---

## 🚀 Next Steps

1. **Memorize** one English + one Hinglish version
2. **Practice** with a timer (actually record yourself)
3. **Test** at 5 networking events
4. **Iterate** based on the 3-question test
5. **Scale** by training your team to use the same frameworks
