## Core Positioning Statement

```
For:            Indian real estate agents and agencies (Mumbai, Pune, Delhi, Bangalore)
Who:            Struggle with lead management, manual follow-ups, and zero tech control
RealtyFlow is:  A CRM built specifically for real estate workflows
That:           Gives agencies complete control over pipeline, clients, and growth
Unlike:         Generic CRMs (Salesforce, Zoho) that require heavy customization
Key Difference: Built by real estate people, in your language, for your workflow
```

## Competitive Positioning

| Competitor | Their Weakness | Our Advantage |
|---|---|---|
| Salesforce | Too expensive, not real estate focused | Affordable, RE-specific features |
| HubSpot | Generic, needs heavy customization | Ready out-of-box for RE agents |
| Zoho CRM | Complex setup, not RE workflow | Simple onboarding, RE terminology |
| Spreadsheets | No automation, no mobile | Full mobile app, automation |
| Sell.Do | Limited features, older UI | Modern UI, Hinglish, better UX |
| WhatsApp Groups | Chaotic, no tracking | Organized pipeline, full visibility |

### Key Differentiators
1. **Hinglish UI** — the only CRM that speaks the user's language
2. **Real estate specific** — buyer requirements, property matching, site visits built-in
3. **India-first** — RERA compliance, INR, crore/lakh, Indian property portals
4. **Mobile first** — 80% of Indian users work on mobile
5. **WhatsApp integration** — works with India's most used communication tool
6. **Affordable pricing** — designed for SMB agencies, not enterprises
