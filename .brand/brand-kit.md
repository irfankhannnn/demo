## Visual Style

- **Aesthetic:** Clean modern SaaS — generous whitespace, rounded corners (8-12px), subtle shadows
- **Imagery:** Indian real estate context — Mumbai/Pune offices, property sites, agent lifestyle
- **Icons:** Lucide or Heroicons (line style, 24px default)
- **Gradients:** Blue-to-purple for premium feel; green accents for success states
- **Photography:** Real Indian professionals, natural lighting, warm tones

## Tone of Voice

```
Personality:   Casual + Expert + Supportive
Feel:          Smart friend who understands real estate
Avoid:         Corporate jargon, overly formal English, condescending tone
Embrace:       Street-smart confidence, practical advice, relatable frustration
```

### Hinglish Writing Rules
1. **Natural code-switching** — switch Hindi/English as Indians naturally speak
2. **Romanized Hindi only** — "aapka" NOT "आपका"
3. **Hindi for emotion** — "tension mat lo", "bahut badhiya", "bilkul sahi"
4. **English for tech terms** — CRM, leads, pipeline, dashboard always in English
5. **Conversational fillers** — "yaar", "bhai", "dekho", "suno", "bas"
6. **Indian numbers** — ₹, crore, lakh (never million/billion)
