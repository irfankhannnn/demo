Where this data comes from

Three stages. Anything a machine can decide exactly is done in Python and is reproducible. Anything needing reading comprehension is done by a model. Neither stage does the other's job, which is why every field below carries an origin tag.

1

Parse

parse_dm_export.py splits the raw export on its separator lines and pulls out handles, timestamps, Indian mobile numbers and reel links. No judgement.

2

Analyse

The insta-lead-analyst agent reads each thread and writes the summary, next action, Hinglish reply, score and lead type.

3

Upsert

upsert_leads_excel.py merges on the handle, updates existing rows in place, appends new ones, and logs every change.

4

This page

build_dashboard_data.py exports the workbook to lead-dashboard-data.js. This page only reads it.

Every field, its type and its meaning

The origin tag tells you who can change a field. A parser field is mechanical. An analyst field is a judgement and can be argued with. A computed field is derived by a rule and cannot be edited by hand, it will be recalculated. A you field is yours to set in Excel.
