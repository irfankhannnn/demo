## Article Writing Framework

### Blog Post Structure

```markdown
# [H1: Primary Keyword + Hinglish Hook — 60 chars max]

**Meta Title:** [Primary keyword + benefit — 60 chars]
**Meta Description:** [Primary keyword + Hinglish value prop — 155 chars]
**Target Keyword:** [primary]
**Secondary Keywords:** [3-5 related terms]
**Word Count:** 800-1200
**Reading Time:** 4-6 min

---

## Introduction (100-150 words)
- Hook: Relatable Hinglish problem statement
- Context: Why this matters for real estate agents
- Promise: What they'll learn
- CTA preview: "Aur end mein, ek free tool jo sab solve karega"

## [H2: First Major Point — include keyword]
### [H3: Sub-point if needed]
- Practical advice with Hinglish examples
- Use bullet lists for scan-ability
- Include data/stats where possible

## [H2: Second Major Point]
- More value, more Hinglish
- Real-world examples from Indian real estate

## [H2: Third Major Point]
- Actionable takeaways
- "Yeh karo, aaj hi"

## [H2: How RealtyFlow Solves This] (soft sell)
- Brief product mention, tied to article topic
- Screenshot or feature highlight
- "Try karo free mein" with CTA link

## Conclusion (50-100 words)
- Summarize key points
- Strong Hinglish CTA
- Internal link to related article

---

**CTA Box:**
"RealtyFlow — India ka Real Estate CRM. Free trial shuru karo →"
```

### Hinglish Blog Writing Rules

1. **Headlines in Hinglish** — "5 Galtiyan Jo Real Estate Agents Ko Leads Cost Karti Hain"
2. **Natural mixing** — Don't force Hindi. Use it where it feels conversational
3. **First paragraph hooks** — Start with a question or relatable scenario
4. **Bold key phrases** — Important Hinglish phrases in bold for scanning
5. **Short paragraphs** — Max 3-4 lines per paragraph (mobile readability)
6. **Bullet lists** — Use extensively; agents skim, not read
7. **Numbers and stats** — Use Indian formats: ₹, crore, lakh
8. **Internal links** — Link to 2-3 other blog posts + 1 product page
9. **Image placeholders** — `[IMAGE: description]` with alt text suggestions
10. **Schema markup** — Include FAQ schema for relevant posts
