# UGC Script Writing

Create UGC-style video ad scripts for Cloudberry. Brief: $ARGUMENTS

## Script Frameworks

### 1. Problem-Agitate-Solution (PAS)
- Hook (0-3s): Selfie-style, attention-grabbing statement
- Problem (3-8s): Over-the-shoulder showing the pain
- Agitate (8-15s): Back to face, frustration emphasis
- Solution (15-25s): Screen recording of Cloudberry
- Proof (25-35s): Back to face, results/metrics
- CTA (35-40s): Close-up, direct to camera

### 2. Before-After-Bridge (BAB)
- Hook (0-3s): POV of chaotic morning
- Before (3-12s): Time-lapse of manual work
- After (12-25s): Calm workflow with Cloudberry
- Bridge (25-35s): Why Cloudberry is different
- CTA (35-40s): Phone showing signup

### 3. Hero's Journey (Story-Based)
- Hook (0-3s): Walking in project, relatable opener
- Struggle (3-15s): Authentic pain story
- Discovery (15-20s): Finding Cloudberry
- Transformation (20-35s): Screen + reaction shots
- CTA (35-45s): Direct, genuine recommendation

## Hook Library (20 hooks by category)
Generate 5 hooks for each: Pain Point, Curiosity, Result, Contrarian

## Camera Directions
- Selfie/Talking Head → Hook, CTA, personal story
- Over-the-Shoulder → Screen demos
- Screen Recording → Product features
- POV → Immersive moments
- Handheld/Walking → Authentic feel

Include: overview, full script, tone/feel guide, do's and don'ts, deliverables
