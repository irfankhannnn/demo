## Intent

The **first demo/launch video** for RealEstateFlow — a pre-launch product with zero customers.
It must do three jobs in 29 seconds: land the reframe ("WhatsApp rahega. Chaos jayega."),
show the breadth of the Real Estate OS so an agency owner understands this is a whole system
and not another chatbot, and close on the Founding-50 waitlist.

Tone: street-smart, blunt, confident, operator-to-operator. Never corporate SaaS. The account
must read as *the operator's account*, not another glossy broker feed — dark ink ground, huge
Unbounded display type, marigold signal. Visually the opposite of every white-and-blue Indian
real-estate account.

Motion-graphics-first: kinetic type + UI abstractions. No AI-generated human faces, no stock
photography, no invented testimonials.

## Notes

### Claims safety — the hard constraint on this video

RealEstateFlow is pre-launch with **zero customers**. Per
`realestateflow/content-strategy-first-month/14-METRICS-CLAIMS-REVIEW.md` §2, these are
**BANNED from this video**:

- "200+ Agencies · Mumbai · Delhi · Pune · Dubai" — false, we have no customers
- "32% of leads lost in WhatsApp" — unsourced
- "₹20 lakh khoti hai har saal" — unsourced, most legally exposed claim in the set
- "80% of deals need 5+ follow-ups" — sales folklore, no primary source
- Any testimonial, any customer quote, any "trusted by", any AI face presented as a customer

Note: the existing Instagram asset `post-02-stat-32-percent.png` uses a banned claim. **Do not
reuse it or its number.**

**The replacement rule:** never assert a loss figure — make the viewer produce their own.

### Verified feature claims (the ONLY features this video may show)

All checked against the codebase in `14-METRICS-CLAIMS-REVIEW.md` §2.3 and the CRM app's own
page modules:

- Leads — buyer, seller, owner, tenant · enquiries · B2B leads
- People — buyers, owners, tenants, customers, contacts
- Inventory — properties, projects, developers, areas, rented properties
- Khata Book — To Give / To Take, per party, per property, settlement status, with reminders.
  Categories: Brokerage, Maintenance, Deep Cleaning, Repair, Security Deposit, Rent, Utility Bills
- Calendar / meetings · notes · documents
- Call recordings
- Business analytics · team hierarchy
- WhatsApp Inbox + AI Employee: runs real CRM operations from inside WhatsApp, understands
  Hinglish (`kharidar`, `kirayedar`, `makan`, `malik`, `sampark`, `milan`, `kitne`, `dikhao`)
- Daily brief · pipeline summary · priority leads · follow-up summary · business health ·
  next-action suggestions
- Connect your own WhatsApp number by QR — no Meta Business API needed
