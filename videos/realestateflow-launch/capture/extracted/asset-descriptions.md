## Visual language reference (from the shipped Instagram set)

Observed in `post-01`, `post-03`, `post-05` — the established look this video must extend:

- One flat saturated ground per card: ink, marigold, gulal, or paper. Never two accents on one card.
- Display type set enormous and tight, filling the lower half, ranged left.
- A small uppercase category tag with a filled dot, top-left.
- A small uppercase label bottom-left.
- WhatsApp is drawn as an **ink-grounded card with rounded chat bubbles** — incoming bubble in
  paper cream, outgoing bubble in marigold with a tick. Not a screenshot, an abstraction.
- Hinglish set inline with English at the same weight, never italicised or othered.
